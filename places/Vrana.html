Vrana

Old Croatian district by Vransko jezero. After 1540 partly retained by Venice; 1549 settler Rade-Siljanovic-b???? led peasants here as zakup Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre. Old Catholic church Gospa od Karmela. Conquered by Ottomans during Cyprus-War-1570-1573 along with Tinj and Zemunik.

In Desnica II (1684–1749)

Major site (docs 8, 13, 16, 22, 34–36, 51) Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II. Vrana — Morlak settlement and uprising (1683–1684): Vrana Morlachs expelled their Sanjak-beg (refusing to fight at Vienna), then sought Venetian protection; fra Grgur Šutić convinced them to seize Vrana and start war on Turks. Morpolača (torre de Morlacchi Palazzo) — tower 3 miles before Vrana (doc 51 footnote). Bortulačić governor; killed at Vrana 1692-01-16 by Biograđani and Pakoštanci. Spingaroli later sopraintendente. Pertinenza HQ from 1689 Cornaro Liga (with Banjci, Polača, two Jagodne, Vrčevo, Tinj, Lerage o Ceragne, Banjana, Pristeg, Radasinovac, Raštane, Gorizza). Bunjevac/Vrana petition for Smiljanić as capo and Radašinović as captain (Sept 1684, in Cyrillic — “idioma illirico”, “caratteri illirici”; docs 34–35). Bili Vučić, Francesco Mirčetić, Ilija Radašinović, Đure Đekić, Mattio Pop, Grgur Lepur, Radivoj Najerlović, Mattio Pančić, Mattio Bajčić, Parišić — all Vrana harambase/knezovi acting collectively 1684.
