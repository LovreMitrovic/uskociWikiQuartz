Stojan Janković (c. 1635 – ?1687)

The most decorated kotarski uskok of his generation. Son of Janko-Mitrovic-b1605-Zegar. Born in Žegar (Ottoman zaleđe), came over with his father’s uskakanje in the late 1640s and lived in Posedarje. Captured in slavery by Ottomans 1666–1667 (14 months in Constantinople); released; named kavalier of the Collegio of San Marco 1670; capo de Morlacchi 1681. The dispatches of Foscolo, Foscarini, Dolfin, Cornaro, Priuli, Valier, Donato, and the dukal of Marc’ Antonio Giustinian (1684) document a near-continuous half-century of military service.

Timeline (events documented in this source — 1646 to 1684 only)

  • c. 1635 — Born in Žegar (estimated; not directly attested. Father’s age in 1649 doc. 49 suggests father was born c. 1614 → son c. 1635 is the conventional date).
  • 1650-02-01 — As “Stojan, figlio di harambassa Gianco Mitrouich, in età tenera esistente,” granted 4 ducati/month (doc. 52). Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I
  • 1652-11-10 — peso di biscotto added to pay (doc. 65). Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I
  • 1653-08-01 — Pay raised to 6 ducati/month “seben in tenera età esistente” (doc. 73). Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I
  • 1666-04-05 — Captured by Turks at the Cetina rotta (battle near Obrovac na Cetini): “harambassa di Possidaria” missing, “il figliolo del Giancovich”; Cornaro’s letter from Split to Senate (doc. 148). 14 months as slave in Constantinople. Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I
  • 1666-05-06 — During his captivity, the Posedarje Morlachi unanimously elect Pavle Vukčević as their vice-harambaša (doc. 152). Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I
  • 1668-12-09 — Free again. Together with serdar Smoljan Smiljanić and governator Giovan Battista Soppe, he ambushed and crushed a 120-strong Turkish raiding party at the Bribir torrent — only 15 Turks escaped, all the rest killed (doc. 163, Priuli to Senate). Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I
  • 1669-01-02 — Senate approves Priuli’s distribution of golden rings to Soppe, Smiljanić and Mitrović (doc. 165). Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I
  • 1669-11-25 — Priuli’s attestato recapitulates his azioni: in person took the head of Reep-aga Filipović in Kotari, 70 heads + 30 schiavi at Cetina, 9 heads + 7 schiavi at Udbina, beheaded aga Velagić at Otres, beheaded aga Pajalitović at Kosovo polje (Kosulovo polje), 30 schiavi + 1 head at Gračac, copious herds at Rudopolje (doc. 168). Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I
  • 1670-01-15 — Stojan’s supplica to the Doge (doc. 170): names his uncles (the five Mitrović brothers) and recapitulates his deeds; requests cavaliere status. Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I
  • 1670-03-13 — Senate makes him kavalier nel collegio di San Marco, golden chain + medaglia + dolama (doc. 171). Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I
  • 1670-08-10 — Provveditore Barbaro grants him the houses of Jusuf-aga Tunić in Islam + 400 gognali of land (doc. 173 — original investitura in Arhiv B. Desnice u Obrovcu). Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I
  • 1674-04-23 — Birth of son Zorzi-Alvise, baptised Sv. Ilija Zadar by sacromonaco Athanasio Troilo. Mother donna Vinca (his first wife). Compare: signor Battista Sopino. (Doc. 207). Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I
  • 1675-01-17 (st. v.) — Sister Janja Janković marries Dimitri Nikolić (Bajo Pivljanin’s brother) at Sv. Ilija (doc. 210). Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I
  • 1675-08-24 — Senate orders Janko (= one of his brothers, perhaps Zaviša or Ilija; the patronymic confused the chancery) and his son Nikola enrolled in cavalry (doc. 215). Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I
  • 1676-08-16 (st. v.) — Wife Vinka/Vuka having died, marries Antonia Reci (Greek of Latin rite of Zadar), at Sv. Ilija (doc. 228). Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I
  • 1677-09-30 (st. v.) — Birth of son Josip Janković (mother Antonia) baptised Sv. Ilija (doc. 235). Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I
  • 1679-02-09 — His son Konstantin named capitano of the company of 50 fanti raised by Stojan (doc. 255). Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I
  • 1681-06-26 — Senate names him capo di tutti i Morlacchi with 25 ducati/month, replacing the deceased Zorzi Posedarski (doc. 275 — original ducal on parchment in Arhiv B. Desnice u Obrovcu). Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I
  • 1683-09-17 — Provveditore Donà interns Stojan and his brother Zaviša to Venice “per degni rispetti” (doc. 315). Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I
  • 1683-12-09 — Returns from Venice; immediately deployed by Donà to suppress the Vrana uprising; intercepts the senjska četa under Vukanović and his brother Ilija at Kruševo (doc. 348). Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I
  • 1684-02-08 — Pay raised to 40 ducati/month (doc. 369, ducal of Doge Marc’Antonio Giustinian). Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I
  • 1684-03-01 — Donà appoints him sopraintendente of the upper-Kotari villages (Ljuba, Ražanac, Radovin, Castel Venier, Posedarje, Budin, Bagnj, Starigrad) — doc. 381. Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I

Timeline (continued — 1684 to 1687, from Desnica II)

  • 1684-05-13 — Daughter Magdalena dies, buried at Sv. Ilija (Zadar) in rito greco (doc 3). Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II
  • 1684-06-02 — Battle near Knin: leads ~2000 Kotari Morlachs with Smiljanić and Bortulačić; ~120 Turks killed, 90 horses taken; lightly wounded in hand (doc 9). Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II
  • 1684-06-02 — Receives 13 brazza of crimson damask from Mocenigo’s fiscal chamber (doc 10). Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II
  • 1684-07-13 — Mocenigo assigns him sopraintendenza of I due Obrovazzi — Stari Grado — Budin e tutte le genti…ultimamente capitate alla divotione de Morlacchi (doc 19). Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II
  • 1684-07-15 — Mocenigo’s terminacija granting brother Ilija 15 ducats/month references “valorose attioni” of father Janko and Stojan’s own continuing service (doc 20). Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II
  • 1684-07-28 — Co-signs petition (with Posedarski, Bortulačić, Smiljanić) protesting Mocenigo’s appointment of four “new” capi (doc 22). Senate ruled in their favor (doc 23). Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II
  • 1684-08 — Glamoč–Grahovo campaign: ~5000 Morlachs raid Glamoč (palaces of alajbeg Filipović burned), then ambush Mazut-paša at Grahovo on return (doc 27, 30, 32). Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II
  • 1684-08-31 — Awarded 10 ducats/month durante bello (doc 29). Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II
  • 1685-01 — Cavalry pay; recruits 50-man Oltremarini company through brother Zaviša (doc 44, 51). Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II
  • 1685-04-07 — Battle of Sinj: Venetian disaster; Stojan one of few Morlach capi who fought through with bravery (doc 60, 61). Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II
  • 1685-05 — Brings 70 families / 900 souls from Lika under Polo Michiel (doc 70). Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II
  • 1685-06 — Settles 316 families / 2,561 souls from Lika at the territory between Kožulovo polje and Ostrovica (doc 73, 90). Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II
  • 1685-07-22 — Lika joint raid: with Posedarski, Smiljanić, Bortulačić; sacks Rebac, Ribnik, Medak, Parčići, Tepsincich, Pavlocich, Komić; ~300 Morlach houses transferred to Venetian side (doc 86, 87, 89, 90). Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II
  • 1685-07-25 — Son Janko dies, buried at Sv. Ilija (Zadar) (doc 94). Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II
  • 1685-09 — Bjelaj/Vakuf raid with Pietro Tartaglia + Geliseo + Razzetini; brings ~500 musketeer-strong Orthodox community from Srb (docs 97, 98). Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II
  • 1685-09 (after) — Sardar “Peraica” killed at Rogoznica by Turkish corsair fusta; pension passed to brother Frane Peraica (doc 104, 112). The identity of “Peraica” with Ilija Mitrović is contested; see Ilija-Mitrovic-b????-Zegar notes. Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II
  • 1686-01 — Lapac raid with 1,250 Morlachs; burns Pašić palazzo near Boričevac, captures Idris-aga of Bihać (doc 118). Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II
  • 1686-01 — Posts bail for Lika settler Ostoja Gagić (doc 124). Confirmed illiterate; Ivan Bortolazzi signs for him. Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II
  • 1686-02-01 — Anonymous Lapac complaint by new-settler Morlachs accuses him and Bortulačić of extortion, Turkish bribery, killing 15+ men, monopolising Kožulovo polje (doc 126). Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II
  • 1686-02-02 — Daughter Marietta dies, buried Sv. Ilija (Zadar) in rito greco (doc 127). Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II
  • 1686-06 — Destroys ~25–29 Krka watermills + tower (doc 135). Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II
  • 1686-07-30 — Receives the Iljanović letter from Vienna (doc 139), claiming descent from “signori de Illyanova” — flagged by Cornaro and reviewed by State Inquisitors as Ragusan intrigue (docs 147, 154). Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II
  • 1686-08-23 — Son Liberal dies, buried Sv. Ilija (Zadar) in rito greco (doc 140). Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II
  • 1686-09 — Second Livno raid (with Smiljanić); famous četobaša Karalija killed on return (doc 141, 142). Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II
  • 1686-09-23 — Buys redemption-right of house at S. Simeone (Zadar) from Caterina Grizogono (doc 143). Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II
  • 1686-11-07 — Doge Marc’Antonio Giustinian authorises a 500-man oltramarino regiment under his command, son Nikola as colonnello, son Konstantin to lead a cavalry company (doc 148). Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II
  • 1686-11-12 to 19 — Bosnian pasha invades Kotari with 16,000 Turks under 5 pashas; Stojan commands Bribir defence (doc 149–157); Bribir abandoned by ~700 men in panic; Vrana and Perušić successfully defended.
  • 1687-01-29 — Wife Antonia dies, buried Sv. Šime (Zadar) (doc 166). Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II
  • 1687-04 — Sinj relief from Atlagić’s siege with Cornaro and Zen (doc 169). Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II
  • 1687-08-23 — Killed at Duvno, struck down by musket and sword while only 8–10 companions followed; the larger Morlach body refused to advance (doc 174). Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II

Family

  • Father: Janko-Mitrovic-b1605-Zegar (d. 1659).
  • Brothers: Sabba (m. Maria Lase 1676-08-17, doc. 229); Ilija Mitrović (the bandit).
  • Sister: Janja (d. 1680-01-09 by fall, broke neck — Desnica’s footnote on doc. 211; Janja was 25, so b. ~1655; m. Dimitri Nikolić-Pivljanin).
  • Wife 1: Vinka (also documented as Vuka, doc. 211 fn.). Acted as kuma at the baptism of Bojana Novaković 1672-11-28 (doc. 203). Last attested (alive) Feb 1675. Children with V: Zorzi-Alvise (1674), Anastazija (1675), Nikola.
  • Wife 2: Antonia Reci (m. 1676-08-16, doc. 228). The Reci/Rezzi were Greci of Latin rite resident in Zadar (Desnica’s footnote). She was the daughter of donna Nicolotta and the q. Apostolo Rezzi. Children with A: Josip (1677), Konstantin (named capitan 1679 in tenera età).

Notes

  • His pay-roll, original terminacije on parchment, and the family genealogical Trassunto survived in Boško Desnica’s family archive in Obrovac — Desnica is Stojan’s lateral descendant.
  • The legend of “Stojan Janković” in epic poetry collapses Janko (father, d. 1659), Stojan (son, d. 1687), and the brigand Ilija into a single figure. The dispatches separate them clearly; on the kotarska epopeja Desnica preface §I treats this conflation explicitly.
