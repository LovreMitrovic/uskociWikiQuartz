# Boško Desnica, *Istorija kotarskih uskoka 1646–1684, sveska I* (1950)

## Citation
Desnica, Boško. *Istorija kotarskih uskoka 1646—1684. Sveska I.* Srpska akademija nauka, Zbornik za istoriju, jezik i književnost srpskog naroda, treće odeljenje, knjiga XIII (*Historia Uscoccorum Cotarensium 1646—1684, Fasciculus I*). Beograd: Izdavačko preduzeće Narodne Republike Srbije, 1950. Edited by Svetozar Brkić. 359 pp.

Compiler: **Dr. Boško Desnica** of Obrovac (1886–1945) — Serbian Dalmatian historian, last bearer of the **Janković-Mitrović** line through his maternal lineage; the family archive in Obrovac is one of the source-fonds for this volume.

## Period covered
**1646 — 1 May 1684.** From Foscolo's correspondence at the start of the Candian War (rat 1645–1669) through the post-Karlovački-mir interbellum to the outbreak of Vrana uprising and the eve of the Morean War.

## Form, language, transmission
- **Form**: primary archival anthology — 399 numbered documents transcribed (per Desnica's preface, intentionally) "tačno onako kako stoje u originalu, bez ikakvih bilo gramatičkih bilo ortografskih ispravaka ili izmena."
- **Languages**: Italian (most provveditorial dispatches and terminacije), Latin (ducali), Croatian/Serbian (a minority of letters from Krajiški Turci and Morlachi, both in bosanska ćirilica and latinica), Turkish-via-translation (a few sandžak letters in Italian translation; one Bosnian-Cyrillic original from Mehmed-beg Atlagić).
- **Document types**: dispatches of the *provveditore generale* to the Senate; *terminacije* (decrees) granting pay/land; *patenti* and *dukali*; lettere of the *conti* of Nin, Šibenik, Trogir, Split, Klis, Novigrad; notarial instrumenti (Šibenik, Zadar); parish registers (Sv. Stošija and Sv. Ilija in Zadar, Sv. Zulijan in Šibenik, Filipjakov, Novigrad, Ražanac, Posedarje); inquisitions/preslušavanja of harambaše and confidenti; letters from Krajiški Turci (Atlagić, Filipović, Durakbegović, Mehmed-aga); reports of confidenti spying in the Ottoman zaleđe.

## Archival fonds drawn upon
1. **Mletački državni arhiv** (Venice State Archive): *Senato — Dispacci provveditore generale in Dalmazia*, filze 50–111 (the spine of the volume — almost every dispatch is appended with multiple in-document originals).
2. **Zadarski stari arhiv** (Zadar State Archive): *Spisi* of provveditori Foscolo, Foscarini, Dolfin, A. Zen, Bernardo, Cornaro (Andrea & Caterino), Contarini, Priuli, A. Barbaro, Morosini, Civran, Grimani, Valier, Cornaro G., Donato; the *Arhiv mletačkog dragomana* (filze for Turkish-language correspondence).
3. **Šibenski bilježnički arhiv** (held with the Šibenik Okružni Sud): notarial protocols of Bernardo Skoci and Petar Verancio.
4. **Family archives**: 
   - **Posedarski Zbornik** in the *Arhiv kneza Antona Benje-Posedarskog u Zadru* (the Posedarski "zlatna knjiga").
   - **Šurićev zbornik** in the family of P. Šurić in Split.
   - **Smiljanić zbornik** ("Summario di ducali, pubbliche fedi ed attestazioni meritate dall'antica, benemerita famiglia Smiglanich", 1786) in the *Bihać* society in Split.
   - **Janković-Mitrović archive** ("Trassunto delle benemerenze della famiglia Mitrovich") in the possession of B. Desnica, Obrovac — including original *terminacije* on parchment.
5. **Parish/church archives**: Sv. Stošija (cathedral) Zadar, Sv. Ilija (Greek-rite Orthodox) Zadar, Sv. Zulijan (Greek-rite) Šibenik, parohijska crkva u Filipjakovu (with Kašićev Ritual), and minor records of Novigrad, Ražanac, Posedarje.
6. **Library archives**: *Zadarska gradska biblioteka Paravia*, *Biblioteka Papafava* (Zadar), *Biblioteka kninskog muzeja* (for the Kašićev Ritual with the glagolska zabeleška about Močivuna).

## Reliability and biases
- **Primary**: original Venetian administrative correspondence; near-real-time dispatches generally written within hours/days of events.
- **Strengths**:
  - Provides exact dates of births/marriages/deaths for the central families (Janković, Smiljanić, Sorić, Mandušić, Šarić, Močivuna, Pivljanin, Posedarski) via parish-register transcriptions otherwise lost or hard to access.
  - Preserves 14 mass-rosters of harambaše with **village of origin → settlement village**, the single richest documentary base for late-17th-c. *uskočka* migration mapping in Ravni Kotari and Bukovica.
  - Includes the cardinal genealogical correction in **doc. 20** (footnote): *"Smiljanići poreklom iz Udbine, a ne kako se priča i veruje, iz Smiljana u Lici."*
- **Biases**:
  - Venetian-administrative perspective. The Morlachi are described from above, by their Venetian patrons; their own voices appear chiefly in mediated *costituti* (interrogations).
  - Numbers (Turkish casualties, captured animals) are partisan and often inflated by the harambaše who depose them — Desnica frequently flags this in footnotes.
  - **Desnica's own footnotes** are interpretive (e.g., on the Smiljanić origin, on the Pivljanin–Janković kinship, on toponym identifications). Treat the document body as primary; treat Desnica's footnotes as carefully-argued secondary commentary.
  - Place-name identifications in footnotes are usually solid; a few ("Notina"/Popina, "Vavina"/Udbina) are explicit conjectural emendations.
- **Reliability**: **high** for the bare facts (names, dates, places, kin-links, attestations); **medium-high** for numerical claims; **high** for the chains of kinship Desnica reconstructs in his footnotes (he had access to family archives no longer in private hands).

## Key facts extracted (summary)
- Persons added or substantially expanded: ~140 (major Janković-Mitrović and Smiljanić clusters, harambaše of every named *uskočka grupa*, Venetian provveditori 1646–84, key Krajiški Turci).
- Surnames added or expanded: ~150, with place-attestation for every single-mention bearer.
- Places added or updated: ~80 — the full geography of Ravni Kotari, Bukovica, Lika–Krbava perimeter, Šibenski zaleđe, Bosanska Krajina edge cities (Knin, Drniš, Banja Luka, Livno, Glamoč, Sinj).
- Events documented: Battle of Ribnik (Jul 1648), Battle of Zečevo I (Aug 1648), Battle of Zečevo II (May 1666), Conquest of Klis (1648), Cetina rotta (Apr 1666), Bribir ambush (Dec 1668), Vrana uprising (Oct 1683 – May 1684), Grusi krajiški statut (5 Aug 1654).
- Critical genealogical corrections: **Smiljanići from Udbina, not Smiljan**.
