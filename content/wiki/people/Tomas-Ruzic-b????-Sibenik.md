# Tomaš Ružić (Tomaso Rozic)

Born in the [[Sibenik]] area; served as a soldier in [[Klis]] and [[Senj]] (kraljevski Vlasi); fled to Vodice na Krasu 1518 [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. 1524: contacted [[Gazi-Husrev-beg]] proposing to lead 100 Vlach families to Ottoman territory; captured by Venetians ([[Zuane-Minoto-b????-Venice]]) on Habsburg-Venetian border. Subsequently led his kindred back to nahija Kličevac (Miranje, Jagodnja, 26 houses) by 1528. After 1538/40 returned to Šibenik area.
