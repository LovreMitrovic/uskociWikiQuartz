# Frane (Francesco) Peraica

Brother of serdar **Ilija Peraica**, who in earlier years had brought 1,600 souls from Ottoman territory under Venetian devotion (Mocenigo's pay-grant of 10 ducati/month, 15 July 1684). Ilija was killed in combat with an Ottoman corsair *fusta* off **Rogoznica** while transporting his family to Zadar (autumn 1685).

## Timeline (this source)
- **1685-10-01** — *Terminacija* of generalni providur Petar Valier in Split (doc. 104): assigns to **Francesco Peraizza** the 10 ducati/month previously paid to his deceased brother Ilija, *"come quello che elletto dalle genti d'Elia in capo, promette eguale puntualità, fede et rassegnatione ai pubblici cenni"* (Zad. drž. arh., Spisi P. Valier, knj. II, str. 156) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **1685** — His followers ("Peraizza's people") counted in the 1685 settlement census (doc. 107): 400 fighters / 1,400 souls scattered across the territories of Zadar and Šibenik per the parish priest's lists.

## Notes
The 1685 raid on Bjelaj-Vakuf (early Sept 1685) reports the bassà of Bosnia trying to recall *"li morlacchi di Peraiza e Nachich"* (doc. 74), so the family's people were a known migration cohort.
