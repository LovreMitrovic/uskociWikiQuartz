# Mate Rojić (Matije Rožića)

Primićur and head of the **largest** Vlasi Istre dzemat in 1528–1530 — 80 houses in nahija [[Podgorje]] [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. (Šarić's text and tables alternate "Mate Rojić" and "Matija Rožić".)
