# Gazi Husrev-beg

Bosnian sanjakbeg and conqueror of central Croatia. Patron and superior of [[Murat-beg-Gajdic-b????-Sibenik]] [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Initiated the *istimâlet* return migration of the Vlasi Istre 1528. Founded the Kožulova polje vakuf in 1531 (later [[Kozulova-Polje]] nahija). His Bosnia *hasovi* in 1530 covered 10 cities, 158 villages, 64 čiftluks (annual revenue 643,979 akči). 1524: addressed by [[Tomas-Ruzic-b????-Sibenik]] proposing 100 Vlach families' transfer.
