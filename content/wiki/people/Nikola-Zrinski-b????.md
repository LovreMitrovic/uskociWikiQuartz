# Nikola Zrinski

Croatian magnate. 25 June 1544: granted hereditary *libertini* status to 25 Mušković Vlach families settled at [[Prilisce]] and Rosopajnik (Vukova Gorica) on his ribničko vlastelinstvo [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].
