# Antun (Antonio) Bortulačić, dottor

Zadar patrician; *dottor*. Brother of [[Simun-Bortulacic-b????-Zadar|Šimun Bortulačić]] (one of the four Morlak capi). Drafted petitions on behalf of the four Morlak capi (Posedarski, Bortulačić, Janković, Smiljanić) to the Venetian Senate against Mocenigo's appointment of the four "new" sub-leaders, July 1684 (doc 22) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Also referenced doc 58.
