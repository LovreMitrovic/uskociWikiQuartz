# Vid (Francesco) Katalinić

Capitano of Starigrad on the Velebit (Morlacca) coast. Reported on Karlobag developments to Molin in January 1690 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 228). **Appointed captain of a new 50-man Starigrad company by Dolfin in November 1696** [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (docs 290–291, 294).

## Notes
- Likely the same "Frane Katalinić" attested as knez of Starigrad in 1678 (succeeding his father Žorži, [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]] doc. 253) — the volume-II "Vid" / "Francesco" appears to be the same person; no daughter/son fork in the source.
