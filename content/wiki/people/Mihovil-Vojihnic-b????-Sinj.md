# Mihovil, sin Radoja Vojihnića

Knez of a Vojnić Catholic Vlach (later "Bunjevac") dzemat in nahija Sinj in 1550 [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Vojnić families later resettled the [[Tinj]] vakuf after the [[Cyprus-War-1570-1573]].
