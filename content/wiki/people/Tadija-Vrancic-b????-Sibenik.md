# Tadija Vrančić

Sister's son of [[Vuk-Mandusic-b????-Sibenik|Vuk Mandušić]]. **1648-08-03** — Foscolo names him *capitan* of his uncle's vacated 50-man company *"privo d' altri più congiunti"* (doc. 45) — i.e. Vuk had no closer relatives. By 1652 the family-name is recorded as **Mandušić alias Vrančić** (doc. 60, 4 ducati + peso di biscotto/month for him and his father Žorži Mandušić-Vrančić). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
