# Šain-aga Mandić

Aga of [[Sinj]]; head of a prominent Mandić family there. Father of [[Hasan-aga-Mandic-b????-Sinj|Hasan-aga Mandić]] (captured at Kaštela, prisoner in Zadar autumn 1685).

## Timeline (this source)
- **Autumn 1685, undated** (doc. 108) — Letter from Sinj to provveditor Valier asking that his son be brought to Split so accounts can be reconciled with **Rados** (the Venetian colonel/governor holding the captives), and pleading on behalf of his slave **Mihael Milinović**, whom **Mattio Strmić** allegedly took from him deceitfully despite a written *fede*. Mandić had taken captain **Ivan Marušić** (vojvoda) prisoner and treated his case as a parallel exchange [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Autumn 1685** (docs. 109–113) — Series of intercepted letters between Šain-aga, his son Hasan-aga and others, recording his ransom negotiations and reporting Venetian/Ottoman war news.

## Notes
The Mandić ransom-correspondence is one of the most personal Ottoman-side documentary sets in the volume; preserved in *Spisi P. Valier*, knj. I (Zadar State Archive).
