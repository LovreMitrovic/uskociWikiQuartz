# Giacomo (Jakov) Sinobad

Cornetta riformato; cousin ("cugino") of the deceased [[Andrija-Sinobad-b????-Posedarje|Andrea Sinobad]]; described as "ultimo superstite della famiglia." Supplicated 1727 to be made serdar of Knin per family tradition; **appointed serdar of Knin 1 December 1729** by ducal of Doge Mocenigo (replacing [[Petar-Mitrovic-Stojisavljevic-b????-Vrlika|Petar Mitrović-Stojisavljević]]) (docs 378, 380) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
