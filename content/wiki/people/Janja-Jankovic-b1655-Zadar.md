# Janja Janković (c. 1655 – 9 January 1680)

Sister of Stojan Janković. **Married Dmitar Nikolić** (brother of Bajo Pivljanin) on 17 January 1675 at Sv. Ilija (Orthodox), Zadar — *"sposato Dimitri Nicolich con la donna Anna Giancovichi. Compare illustrissimo signor Battista Sopino."* (doc. 210). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]

- **1677-10-01** (st. v.) — Birth of son **Nikola Nikolić** baptised at Sv. Ilija (doc. 236). Compare: gov. Battista Sopini. [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1680-01-09** — Died, age 25, fell and broke her neck — *zabilješka u matici umrlih parohije Sv. Stošije u Zadru*, cited in Desnica's footnote on doc. 211. Birth therefore c. 1655.

## Notes
- The kinship-link **Janković ↔ Pivljanin** through her marriage anchors the brothers Bajo and Mitar Nikolić-Pivljanin in Zadar from the end of the Candian War to the Morean War (Desnica, fn. doc. 211).
