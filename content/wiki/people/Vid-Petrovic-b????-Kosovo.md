# Vid Petrović (Kosovo near Sinj)

Haiduk leader on Mt. Prolog. In 1702, with [[Jovan-Sinobad-b????-Posedarje|Jovan Sinobad's nephew]] and 60 men, attacked Ottoman travellers and a village near the [[Kula-Atlagica|Atlagić tower]] (doc 346) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Repeatedly described in source as "*scellerato Vido Petrouich*" — a notorious bandit at the confines.

In 1706 raided Ottomans carrying a public flag (his ensign was captured). Sought to avenge the death of a [[Kresovic|Kresović]] famous bandit and to oppress the [[Raskovic|Rasković]] family (doc 365) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. **Killed in ambush by conte [[Antonio-Canagetti-b????.md|Antonio Canagetti]] at [[Bruvno]] in 1707**, together with a son of conte [[Obradovic|Obradović of Bruvno]] (doc 365).
