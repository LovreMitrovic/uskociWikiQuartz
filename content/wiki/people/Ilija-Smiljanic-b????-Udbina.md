# Ilija Smiljanić (c. 1622 – 8 September 1654)

Son of [[Petar-Smiljanic-b????-Udbina|Petar Smiljanić]]. *"basso, bruno, segnato da varoli, occhi varii, giovane, mostachi castagni"* (doc. 36, terminacija 1648-07-14). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]

## Timeline
- **1648-07-14** — 4 ducati/month assigned by Foscolo (doc. 36). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1648-07-17** — Deposes about Ribnik: lost his father, "di questi ne sono periti, compresa la persona di mio padre, nove" out of 100 men (doc. 38). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1648-07-31** — Bukovčani re-elect him as their capo in Foscolo's presence after Ribnik (doc. 41). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1648-08-01** — At the **Battle of Zečevo I**, fights alongside Vuk Mandušić (whose corpse he helps bury); Vuk killed (doc. 42). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1653-04-06** — Senate gives him a house in Zadar (the *quondam Girolamo Capello* house; if redeemed by Capello heirs the State will provide a substitute) — doc. 71. [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1653-09-20** — Dolfin's *attestato* recapitulates his deeds: twice under Knin "facendo alcuni schiavi de principali"; in Glamoč campaign 6 grosse terre burned; in two attacks on Udbina captured a banner (doc. 75). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1654-09-08** — **Killed at Vučjak** (a mountain pass between Krupa and Gračac, on the Lika–Dalmacija border). On a 70-strong horse-raid with Janko Mitrović into Lika, attacked the village of Ondić near Udbina, killed 30 and took 15 schiavi. On retreat over Velebit, ambushed at Vučjak — Ilija the first wounded, dragged from the saddle, killed by an *archibugiata* in the back (docs. 86, 87). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]

## Family
- Wife: **Kata** — *Turca di nascita*, baptised Christian on her marriage to Ilija. After his death, *priva delle facoltà paterne* (her wealthy Turkish family disowned her). Provveditore Dolfin granted her 12 ducati + 2 pesi di pane / month (doc. 89). She remarried harambaša **[[Milivoj-Zubicevic-b????-Sibenik|Milivoj Zubičević]]** of Šibenik in 1655-11-01 (doc. 97 — full inventory of her *dote*: 432 ducati 4 lire). On 1665-10-15 she had a son with Zubičević, **Lukas**, baptised at Sv. Zulijan Šibenik (doc. 144). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
