# Filip Smiljanić (c. 1633 – January 1656)

Younger son of [[Petar-Smiljanic-b????-Udbina|Petar]]. *"basso, occhi uarii, pelcastagno, prima lanugine, d' anni venti in circa"* (doc. 53, terminacija 1650-03-01). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]

## Timeline
- **1650-03-01** — 4 ducati/month (doc. 53). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1653-05-03** — Provveditore Dolfin replaces him into the *compagnia di corazze* of the banished Frane Jeremija; Dolfin reduces it to *cavalleria leggera capelletta* (doc. 72). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1654-09-08** — Eyewitness deposition of his brother Ilija's death at Vučjak (doc. 86). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1654-09-16** — Universal Morlachi petition gets him named principal harambaša after Ilija; Dolfin grants it but warns him about *"ferocità del suo ardire, che dal bollore della gioventù suole ricevere dannose instigationi"* (doc. 88). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1656-01-29** — Buried at the cathedral (Sv. Stošija) Zadar, alongside his *alfier* **Frane Marininić** (doc. 99). Killed in Šibenik in a brawl with a Venetian *plaćeni vojnik* of the šibenska posada — Desnica's footnote (doc. 99). The deaths caused major Morlach unrest in Šibenik; provveditore Zen sent col. Davila to restore order (doc. 98). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
