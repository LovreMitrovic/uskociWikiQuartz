# Filippo Tintor

Cavalry harambaša from the Šibenik–Skradin area. Arrives at Bribir 12 Nov 1686 with 10 horse to join Stojan Janković at the Bosnian-pasha alarm; immediately re-dispatched to all the *capi* and karambaše of the Kotari to drive the muster (doc. 155) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
