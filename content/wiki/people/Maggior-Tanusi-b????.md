# Maggior Tanusi

Major in the Oltramarini. In July 1684 commanded 100 oltramarini sent to support Janković and Bortulačić (doc 17) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Killed at the Sinj battle April 1685, doc 60.
