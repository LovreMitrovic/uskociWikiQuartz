# Mihajlo Radmilović (Mihailo Radmilovich)

Morlak who escaped from [[Zegar|Žegar]] to Venetian territory in 1684. His goods were stolen by Venetian subjects on the way; Mocenigo ordered restitution, doc 31 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
