# Vukota Bunčić (1419)

Vlach katunska glava in eastern Hum, 1419 [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]] (Hrabak 1981:188). The patronymic Bunčić = "son or descendant of Bun" — concrete evidence of the medieval Bun-stem onomastic family from which the etnik **Bunjevac** ultimately derives.

Likely connected to selo [[Buncici]] in Bilećke Rudine, where a stećak names **[[Bunac-Rusovic-b????-Buncici]]** — the latter probably a starešina from the **Vlasi Banjani** aglomeracija to which the Bunčići belonged.
