# Bože Milković

Karambaša heading the *guardia estraordinaria* of 50 cavalrymen in [[Bukovica]] in spring 1686. Tasked by provveditor Antonio Zen with watching the Knin Turks; on a raid by ~30 Knin Turks toward Skradin (April 1686), he occupied the passes, ambushed them on the return, captured 2, took a banner, killed 7 — five heads + the banner brought to Zadar; Bože received a medal forwarded from Cattaro (doc. 133, 12 April 1686) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
