# Dragić Avramović (Draghich Abramovich)

Morlak *suddito* (newcomer). Petitioner for relief for the widow and children of his deceased brother [[Dragoja-Avramovic-b????|Dragoja]], who killed Čejvan-aga Lopušić of Knin in a skirmish and was himself killed. Awarded 1 *peso* of biscuit/month by Mocenigo February 1685, doc 49 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
