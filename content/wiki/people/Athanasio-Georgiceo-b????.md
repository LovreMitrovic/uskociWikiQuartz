# Athanasio Georgiceo (1626)

Author of a 1626 izvješće on demographic conditions in [[Sandzak-Klis]] and the Vlach migration to Podunavlje (Batinić 1885:116–150) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

Key data points:
- The seoba "preko Dunava" was driven "ponajviše iz straha od Mehmed-bega i njegova oca" (i.e. [[Mustafa-beg-Klis-b????]] and [[Mehmed-beg-Klis-b????]]).
- Sandžak Klis raja population dropped from ~30,000 houses to ~20,000 between 1600 and 1624 — *25 godina nestajanja*.
