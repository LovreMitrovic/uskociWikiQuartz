# Grgur Milohanović

Returnee colonist from [[Nova-Vas]] near Poreč; in 1561 founded selo Miljacki in present-day Zemunik Gornji [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. After Ottoman conquest of Zemunik in 1570 led 40 families back to Istria, settling [[Zbandalj]] near Poreč with [[Ivan-Filipini-b????]].
