# Kojadin Žepina (Coiadin Zepina)

Morlak *alfiere* (standard-bearer). Lost an arm under Durak-beg's tower at Plavno in 1684. Granted 1 ducat/month for one year by Mocenigo, doc 2 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
