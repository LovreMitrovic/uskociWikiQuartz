# knez Vraneš Ivanović (1581)

Vlach knez recorded with his **knežina** in the area of [[Kaloca]] in 1581 (Fermedžin 1892:319) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

One of the earliest concrete attestations of dinarska sociokulturna structure in the Segedinski/Budimski beglerbegluk after the 1530s deportations. The personal name **Vraneš** is itself characteristic of the Hercegovinian-vlach onomastic stock.
