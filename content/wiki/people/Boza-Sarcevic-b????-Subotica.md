# Boza (Ambrozije) Šarčević (1868)

Subotica intellectual; in **1868** launches the **Bunjevački kalendar** — the founding publication of the 19th-c. Bunjevac narodni preporod in [[Backa]] [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]]. Counterpart in Subotica to [[Ivan-Antunovic-b1815-Backa]] in Kaloča.
