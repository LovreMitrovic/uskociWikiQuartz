# Marija (Maria) Smiljanić née Bolić

Widow of *qu. sardar* Smoljan Smiljanić. Wrote her testament at Zadar **5 October 1693**; died before **13 April 1694** (the date of *apertura* of the will); buried at San Domenico, Zadar [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 267).

## Will
- Heir: son [[Lazaro-Smiljanic-b????-Bukovica|Lazaro Smiljanić]].
- Daughter Margarita (living 1693).
- Son-in-law: governor **Salentich** (received green velvet jacket).
- Nephews **Aloisi** (silver buttons) and **Giovanni / Zuanne** (silk sash).
- *Compare* (godparent): **quartier mastro Luca Sbizza** — executor and legatee.
- Recipients: **Caterina Pizzocara** (twice mentioned, one residing near terra ferma); **Vincenza Pastrović** and her son **Antonio**; **Ellena daughter of dottor Cassio** (diamond ring); **padre Scutari** (Dominican provinciale); **don Gregorio Begna** (deceased; debtor to her for 4 zecchini); the late servant **Ellena**; **Angiolina Dominis** (mentioned in scritto).
- *Commissari* of the will: **Casseretti**, **dottor Biagio Soppe** (consigliere of corte di Zara), **Antonio Canaletti**.
- Notary recording and opening the will: **Andreas de Toris**, dottor and cancelliere mag. com.
- Testimon at apertura: **Zorzi Smirich**.
- Apertura witnessed by curato **don Andrea Caldana**.

## Family

## Notes
- Distinct from her son Lazaro's wife, **also** Maria Bolić ([[Maria-Bolic-Lazaro-b????-Zadar|Maria Bolich miga legitima consorte]] of Lazaro, 1696 testament). The two Marije Bolić across one generation suggest cousin-marriage but are not linked in the source.
