# Johann Joseph grof von Rabatta (1709–1730)

Habsburg commander of the Karlovački generalat for two decades. His izvješća — analyzed in detail by Lazanin 2002 — divide the population of Hrvatska / Primorska krajina + Lika–Krbava into two etnije by **etnokulturni ključ** [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]]:
- **Hrvati** = old Habsburg Catholic subjects, Krajina starosjedioci, doseljenici from provincial Hrvatska (Gornje Pokuplje, Vinodol) and Kranjska.
- **Vlasi** = all bearers of patrijarhalno-pastoralna kultura of the Dinaric arc, irrespective of confession; both older and newer prebjega from Ottoman/Venetian areas.

Striking detail: bivši "lički Turci" who were Catholicized in late 17th c. are called **"novokrštenim Vlasima"** in one Rabatta report. Confessional differences are secondary in his framework.
