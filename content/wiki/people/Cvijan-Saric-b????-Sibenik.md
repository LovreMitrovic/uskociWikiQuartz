# Cvijan Šarić — serdar šibenskih pravoslavnih Morlaka

The Cvitko / Cvijan Šarić of Evlija Çelebi (1660, "hrvatski razbojnik") and of the Sv. Zulijan Šibenik baptismal registers is the same man who appears across the Desnica corpus 1647–66 as harambaša → serdar of the Šibenik Orthodox Morlachi.

## Family
- **Wife: Dessa** (Sv. Zulijan baptismal registers, doc. 81, 83, 144).
- **Sons**:
  - **Iuan/Ivan** — baptised 1654-07-02 at Sv. Zulijan, Šibenik; compare *Prodan Zubićević* (docs. 81, 83). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
  - **Žorži/Zorzi Šarić** — *harambassa* in the 1654 Sv. Zulijan parish protest (doc. 84).
  - **Zuanne/Jovan Šarić** — Cvijan's pay (4 ducati + peso di biscotto) transferred to him 1662-03-14 by provveditore Andrea Korner (doc. 123). Jovan still alive 1682-06-03, when Doge orders the back-pay arrears settled (doc. 285).

## Timeline
- **1652-12** / **1653-01-03** — Among the *carambasse* who captured the alaj-beg **Filipović** at Duare; pay renewed (docs. 68, 69).
- **1653-04-04** — Provveditore Foscarini grants 4 ducati + peso di biscotto/month for capturing alaj-beg Filipović (doc. 69 — the man transferred is in fact a leader from this same operation).
- **1654-07-02** — Son **Ivan/Iuan Šarić** baptised at Sv. Zulijan (orthodox), Šibenik (docs. 81, 83). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1654-07-26** — *Cuitho Sarich harambassa* heads a 37-name list of the šibenski Orthodox Morlachi (mostly *harambasse* — see [[Saric-Sibenik-Pravoslavni-Morlaci-1654]]) who collectively defend their parohijski sveštenik **fra Cirilo Gabrieli** against Jovan Vuković Obrad (doc. 84). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1662-03-14** — Pay transferred to son **Jovan** "in attentione di esser augmentate per la fruttuose operatione contribute anco nel corso di questa nostra carica" (doc. 123) — i.e. Cvijan was still alive but stepping back. [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1664-01-07** — Provveditore Contarini frees the galiot Jakov Vučić "ricercato in gratia da esso Sarich et altri Morlacchi di Šibenik" — recompense for burning all the Turkish hay reserves around Knin (doc. 136). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1665-10-15** — Cvijan now **serdar Cuitco Sarich**: his wife **Dessa** is *comare* at the baptism of Lukas Zubičević (whose mother Catarina was widow of Ilija Smiljanić — doc. 144). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1666-04-05** — Cetina rotta: Cornaro to Senate names "harambassa di Sebenico Sarich, che vecchio, ardito e pieno d' avidità, niente cura i pericoli" as the principal *concertatore* of the doomed Cetina raid in which Stojan Janković was captured (doc. 148). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1666-05-21** — Cornaro signs Cvijan's pay-renewal (date *terminus post quem* of his death; transferred to brother Vučen 1666-05-21, doc. 155 — but doc. 155 concerns *Močivuna* not Šarić; Cvijan's son was already collecting since 1662).
- **1668-06-13** — Provveditore Priuli orders the *camera fiscal di Veglia* to pay Cvijan 10 ducati/month back-pay from October 1667 — *therefore Cvijan was still alive in 1668* (doc. 159). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1668-10-06** — *"a nome anco di tutti i Morlacchi che seguono il rito vecchio"* he and pop Cirilo press provveditor Priuli for action against Mihovil Kapuano of Šibenik who throws garbage at the Orthodox church and verbally abuses the parish priest (doc. 162). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]

## Notes
- **Religious profile**: explicitly *rito greco* (Orthodox); curate is *fra Cirilo Gabrieli*, parish church Sv. Zulijan, Šibenik (an old Orthodox enclave). Šarić is its de facto *patron* — leads the parish's collective defence in 1654, 1668. The Ottoman administrative label *"Hrvat"* used by Evlija Çelebi for him is, per Šarić 2008, an example of how the *Vilajet-Hrvati* (abolished 1537) designation persisted across confessional boundaries.
