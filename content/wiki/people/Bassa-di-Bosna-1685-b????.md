# Bassa di Bosna (1685, unnamed)

Unnamed Pasha of Bosnia in 1685. Led the Sinj relief and Venetian rout April 1685; took [[Zuanne-Alberti-b????-Split|Ivan Alberti]] as his personal slave; offered to return Valier's silver, docs 60, 61, 62, 65 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].

## Notes
- Distinct from the Bosnian Pasha of 1684.
