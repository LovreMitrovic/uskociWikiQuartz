# Capitanio Segna

Senjski (from [[Senj]]) captain, of Hungarian nationality. At the Sinj battle April 1685, his *alfier* (standard-bearer) gave [[Marin-Michiel-b????-Venice|Marin Michiel]] his horse to escape, doc 61 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].

## Notes
- "Segna" is the Italian for Senj — used as descriptor in the source rather than a strict surname.
