# Nikola Milokanić (Osman)

70-year-old former Muslim from Banpolje in [[Lika]]; born c. 1521 in Istria, fled to Ottoman territory c. 1550 where he married, and in old age returned to Istria with 184 kin [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Testified before dalmatinski providur Ferigo Nani 16 April 1591.
