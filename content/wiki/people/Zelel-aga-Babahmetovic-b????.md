# Zelel-aga Babahmetović

Former Venetian state slave; exchanged for [[Conte-Sabbini-b????-Venice|conte Sabbini]] during the last (Cretan/Candian) war. His daughter (a widow returning to him) was in Mehmed-beg Durakbegović's escort during the March 1685 ambush, doc 56 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].

## Family
- Daughter: unnamed widow, returning to her father in 1685 (in Durakbegović's escort).
