# Mehmed Terzibašić, drniški aga (1608)

Local Ottoman aga of [[Drnis]]. Led the **1608 Drniš revolt** (drniški muslimani + Vlasi) against provincijska upravljačka elita of [[Sandzak-Klis]] [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

Suppression of the revolt directly triggered the [[Bunjevac-Migration-to-Backa-c1606|1608 transfer]] of **2,000 morlach houses across the Danube** (Šibenik knez to Venetian senate) — the largest single demographic event in early Bunjevac history.
