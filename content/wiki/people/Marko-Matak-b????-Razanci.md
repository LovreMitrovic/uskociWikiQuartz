# Marko Matak (Marco Mattach)

Villager of [[Razanci]] (Rasanze). Took a *gecerma* (woolen vest) of crimson-violet from Mihajlo Radmilović as he was escaping from Žegar to Venetian territory, doc 31 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
