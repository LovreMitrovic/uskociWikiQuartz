# Polo (Paolo) Michiel

Venetian nobleman, *venturiero* (volunteer) under Provveditor Mocenigo in 1684; later *Provveditor General della Cavalleria* in 1685.

## Timeline
- **1684** — Sent by Mocenigo to mediate disputes between Vrana and Zadar Vecchia, and between Obrovac and Novigrad (docs 8, 15) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **1684** — Asked to mediate the Sinobad–Vukčević vs. Mitrović feud (krvarina) among Budin Morlaks (doc 15) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **1685** — Provveditor General della Cavalleria; assumes authority over the 70 Lika families (~900 souls) brought in by Stojan Janković in May 1685 (doc 71) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
