# Tomo Sinovčić (Tomas Sinovcich)

From [[Novigrad-Dalmatia|Novigrad]]. Rewarded by Mocenigo on 5 September 1684 for killing 5 Turks at Grahovo, including the *kjehaja* (lieutenant) of Mazut-paša, doc 32 footnote [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
