# Vukašin Rošić

Harambaša. In February 1685 led 20 Morlak families from Čitluk near Novi (Lika) to the Starigrad (Velebit) area on the Venetian side; petitioned for the [[Perusic|Perušić]] castle as residence, doc 45 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
