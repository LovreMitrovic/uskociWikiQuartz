# Palikuća

One of the three *capi* of the Šibenik–Skradin Morlachs (with Goreta and Žinović) at the November 1686 Bribir defence. Paired with them in the desertion of the post when the Bosnian pasha approached (doc. 155) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
