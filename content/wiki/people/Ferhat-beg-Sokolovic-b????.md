# Ferhat-beg Sokolović

Klis sanjakbeg active 1568–1574+. 1568: settled ~200 "nevjernika Istre" families from Venetian territory near Knin (Plavno?), authorized by Porte ferman 21 November 1568 [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. 1574: uvakufio [[Tinj]] for Zemunik vakuf (six villages) and Hrvace; resettled with [[Vojnic]] Bunjevac colonists from nahija Sinj.
