# Zaviša / Sabba Janković

Brother of [[Stojan-Jankovic-b1635-Zegar|Stojan]]. Recorded as *Sabba* in the Sv. Ilija registers.

- **1676-08-17** (st. v.) — Married **Maria Lase** at Sv. Ilija Zadar; compare: *illustrissimo signor Simon Bortolazzi* (doc. 229). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1683-09-17** — Interned to Venice with Stojan by Donà (doc. 315). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]

## Timeline (continued — 1684 onwards, from Desnica II)
- **1684** — Mediates Novigrad-Obrovac robbery dispute; orders restitution of plundered goods (doc 15). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **1685-02** — Recruits 50-man Oltremarini company (advance 150 ducats + 2,000 biscuits) on behalf of brother Stojan (doc 51). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **1685–1688** — Capitano of Obrovac. [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **1686-04** — Captures the Udbina capitano (overseeing his fields), the disdar Ibrahim-aga, the spahija [[Muhamed-beg-b????-Udbina|Muhamed-beg]] of Udbina (nephew of [[Ibrahim-Pasic-b????-Ripac|Ibrahim Pašić]] of Ripač), and Aga Kumalić; bought Muhamed-beg at auction for 650 reali, Ibrahim-aga for 260 reali (docs 79, 124, 126, 133, 134). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **1687-06** — Raids Rebac with 500 men: 20 horses, 200 cattle, 8 captives (doc 170). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **1687-09** — Wounded in Varcar Vakuf raid; 4,000 Morlachs penetrated 6 days into Bosnia (doc 179). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **1689** — Attestation by Cornaro (doc 225). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **1693–96** — Documented raids on Bilaj, Skopje (Vakup), Mostar (bridge), Goražde, Gabella, Cyclut, Klivno, Kupres, Bihać/Ripač (1696 attestation). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **1697** — Doge Silvestro Valier raises his pay to 50 ducats/month (doc 303). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **post-1700** — Redeployed during War of Spanish Succession to Italy. [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
