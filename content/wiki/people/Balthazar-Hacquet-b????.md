# Balthazar Hacquet (1802–05)

French physician in Austrian service, author of *Abbildung und Beschreibung der südwest und östlichen Wenden, Illyrer und Slaven* (1802–05). Records the Bunjevci as part of the *Likaner Volk* — the bravest of all Ličani (whom he called "austrijski Crnogorci"), poludivlji, prone to pljačka, with women of greatest virtue [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]] (Hacquet 1802–05:165, 177).
