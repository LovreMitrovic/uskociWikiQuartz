# Bijovče, sin Vučkov

Knez of an 86-house dzemat in nahija [[Ostrovica]] in 1550 [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Likely orthodox; gives his name to today's **Biovičino Selo** kod Kistanja. Dzemat included Međare, Žaževiž (Žažvić), Putičane, Dubravice, Dobra Voda, Bratiškovac, Čulišić.
