# Jovan Sudarović

Morlach harambaša from **Parčići** in Lika, of *fede vecchia* (Orthodox). After scoring with the Kotarani in Lika 1685, retreated his 150-house / 300-fighter cohort partly to Karlobag, partly to Obrovac.

Interrogated in Split 23 Nov 1685 (doc. 114) together with his companion **Rade Rkać** (in original *Rado Grego* — "Greek" in pejorative for Orthodox). They report that the **Habsburg imperial commissar of the Karlović family** had tried to fix them at Karlobag with promises of a captaincy and privileges, but they preferred Venetian devotion if granted the **kaštel at Žegar** (across the Zrmanja from Sebenico, key to closing the Knin–Skradin axis). They had a small cannon taken from the Turks at Udbina that they would bring to Žegar; needed only a 100-man company, food until next harvest, and habitations. Valier rewarded them with 4 cecchini for the journey and sent them back to the cavalry commander and Janković [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
