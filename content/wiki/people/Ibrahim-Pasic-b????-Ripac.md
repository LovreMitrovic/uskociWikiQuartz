# Ibrahim Pašić of Ripač

Major Bosnian beg of **Ripač** (on the Una). Owner of a fortified palace near [[Boricevac|Boričevac]] on the Lapac plain, burned in Stojan Janković's January 1686 raid (doc. 118). Uncle of [[Muhamed-beg-b????-Udbina|Muhamed-beg]], *spahija* of [[Udbina]], whom provveditor Antonio Zen describes as *"di nascita nobile; è nipote d'Imbrahim Passich da Ripaz, ch'è signore di stima e facoltà"* (doc. 134, 18 June 1687) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
