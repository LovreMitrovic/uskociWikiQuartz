# Domenico Mocenigo (Domeniko Moćenigo)

Venetian noble; *Provveditor general estraordinario dell'armi in Dalmatia et Albania*. Arrived in Dalmatia May 1684 to replace the ailing Lorenzo Donà, doc 1 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Main correspondent in docs 1–43 of this volume.

## Timeline
- **1684-05** — Arrives in Dalmatia; finds outgoing Provveditor Donà ill and bedridden at *scoglio del Lantana* near Zadar (doc 1) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **1684-07** — Awards 15 ducats/month pension to [[Ilija-Mitrovic-b????-Zegar]] in recognition of Janko Mitrović's deeds in the past war and Stojan Janković's continuing service (doc 20) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **1684-07** — Appoints four "new" sub-leaders (Alberti, Soppe, Soppe Fortezza, Ventura) over the Kotari, in parallel with the four Morlak capi (doc 19); subsequently abolished by Senate (doc 23) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **1684-08-31** — Awards Stojan Janković 10 ducats/month *durante bello* (doc 29) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **1684** — Petition by the four Morlak capi (Posedarski, Bortulačić, Janković, Smiljanić) to Senate against his appointments; Senate rules in favor of the capi (docs 22, 23) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **1684 (late)** — Replaced; eventually scapegoated for the subsequent Sinj defeat [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
