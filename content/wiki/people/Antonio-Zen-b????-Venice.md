# Antonio Zen (Zeno)

Venetian provveditor extraordinario at **[[Cattaro]]** in 1686 (doc. 131, dispatching Korponež's *galeotta* to relieve the Pastrovići siege).

Subsequently *provveditor general della cavalleria*. Reports from Zadar 12 April 1686 and 18 June 1687 (docs. 133, 134) on Janković's 1,600-man command, on the cavalry's incursions, on harambaša Boža Milković's ambush of Knin Turks at Bukovica, and on Zaviša Janković's capture of the Udbina disdar **Ibrahim-aga** and the *spahija* **Muhamed-beg** (nephew of Ibrahim Pašić of Ripač) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
