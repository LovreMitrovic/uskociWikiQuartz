# Ivan (Zuanne) Alberti

Split nobleman (*splitski plemić*); *governator*; one of four "new" sub-leaders Mocenigo appointed in 1684 for the Kotari sub-region [[Novigrad-Dalmatia|Novigrad]]–[[Karin]]–[[Badun|Bađun]]–[[Pridraga]]–[[Kloster|Klošter]], doc 19 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].

## Timeline
- **1684** — Appointed sub-leader (doc 19); position abolished by Senate (doc 23) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **1685-04-07** — Captured at the Sinj battle by the Bosnian Pasha as personal slave (docs 60–62, 65) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **1685** — Wrote ransom letter from Klis (Livno) to Venice (doc 65) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **1685** — Named successor to conte Marianović (doc 65) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
