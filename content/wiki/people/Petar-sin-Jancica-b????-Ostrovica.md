# Petar, sin Jančića (Ostrovica)

Primićur recorded in the 1530 sumarni defter as head of a 30-house Vlasi Istre dzemat (1,365 akči) in nahija [[Ostrovica]] [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Likely the same person as [[Petar-Najcinovic-b????-Dobruca-Vas]], whose family name is recorded interchangeably as "Jančić" / "Najčinović".
