# Marko Smiljanić (c. 1647 – 23 February 1693)

Capitan; brother of [[Smoljan-Smiljanic-b????-Bukovica|Smoljan Smiljanić]]. Awarded **10 ducats/month** by Doge Francesco Morosini (*ducale* 21 May 1690) for service in the Morean War — at **Calamata, Navarino, Modon, Napoli di Romania, Negroponte**, and at **Malvasia** with his company on 12 Aug 1689 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (docs 226, 233).

Died **23 February 1693**, age ~46; buried in San Domenico, Zadar; given last unction by curato don Andrea Caldana of the Zadar duomo (S. Stošija) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 264).

## Family
- Brother: [[Smoljan-Smiljanic-b????-Bukovica|Smoljan Smiljanić]] (sardar; deceased by 1693).
- Sister-in-law: [[Maria-Bolic-Smiljanic-b????-Zadar|Marija Smiljanić née Bolić]] (Smoljan's widow; testament 1693).

## Notes
- His extensive Morean War record fixes him on the eastern Mediterranean front during the early 1690s, away from the Bukovica/Kotari theatre.
