# Antonio Molin

Venetian *provveditor estraordinario commissario* (and at times styled *vice commissario*) in Dalmatia from autumn 1686, succeeding Marin Michiel at Zadar while Cornaro held overall command from Split/Šibenik.

## Timeline (this source)
- **1686-09-17** — Cornaro from Salona (doc. 144) records that Molin "rimasto ultimamente a Zara" with multiple charges relating to that piazza.
- **1686-10-07** — Issues the alert to *giudici e capitanii* of the Kotari villages to remove non-combatants, livestock and goods to safe places, and to keep all able-bodied men under arms; mandate handed to Stojan Janković for execution (doc. 146; Zad. st. arh. Spisi Molin, knj. jedina, str. 24).
- **1686-11-10** — Reports to Cornaro on the slow Morlach response, the dispatch of Janković to Nadin and Bortulačić to Tinj, and the cavalry's logistics issues at Malpaga (doc. 149).
