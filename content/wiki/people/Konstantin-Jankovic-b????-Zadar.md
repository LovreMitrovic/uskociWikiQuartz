# Konstantin Janković

Son of [[Stojan-Jankovic-b1635-Zegar|Stojan Janković]] and [[Antonia-Reci-b????-Zadar|Antonia Reci]]. Already named *capitano* of a 50-fanti company on 1679-02-09 *"in tenera età"* ([[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]] doc. 255).

## Timeline (this source)
- **1686-11-07** — Ducal of Marc'Antonio Giustinian (doc. 148): once Stojan's regiment is fully assembled, Konstantin is to be commander of an *oltramarino* cavalry company raised at Stojan's own expense, "d'esser commandata dal tenente della medesma sin a tanto che si renda habile il figliolo stesso al commando". Original ducal on parchment in *Arhiv B. Desnice* [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
