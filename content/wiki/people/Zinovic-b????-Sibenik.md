# Žinović (Xinovich)

One of the three *capi* of the Šibenik–Skradin Morlachs (with Goreta and Palikuća), paired with them in the November 1686 Bribir abandonment (doc. 155) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
