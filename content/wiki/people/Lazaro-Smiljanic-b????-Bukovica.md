# Lazaro (Lazzaro) Smiljanić

Son of [[Smoljan-Smiljanic-b????-Bukovica|Smoljan Smiljanić]] and [[Maria-Bolic-Smiljanic-b????-Zadar|Marija née Bolić]]. *In tenera età* in 1679 when named capitan of his father's 50-fanti company ([[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]] doc. 255).

## In this source
- Heir per his mother Marija's testament 5 Oct 1693 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 267).
- Wrote his own will **28 July 1696**, leaving his estate to his wife **Maria Bolić** (then pregnant) and the unborn child — or, failing them, to Christian slaves and the Dominicans [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 289). Commissario: **Sebastian Matiazi**.
- Commended at the **siege of Ulcinj** in 1696 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 299).
