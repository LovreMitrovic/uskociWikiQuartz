# P. santo Capuzino (unnamed Capuchin friar)

Unnamed Capuchin friar at the Sinj battle April 1685, carrying the banner of the Virgin and a *brandistocco* (boar-spear); presumed enslaved after the defeat, doc 60 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
