# Giulio (Julije) Soppe

Zadar noble (Soppe family); sub-leader appointed by Mocenigo in 1684 for the Kotari sub-region of [[Diklo]]–[[Kozino|Kožino]]–[[Petrcane|Petrčane]]–[[Bokanjac]]–[[Gruhe]]–[[Dracevac]], doc 19 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Position abolished by Senate (doc 23).
