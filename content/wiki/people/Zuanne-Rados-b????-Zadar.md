# Ivan (Zuanne) Radoš

*Conte cavalier*; *sergente maggior di battaglia*. Submitted petition for a cavalry company in 1684 (doc 5) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Member of the consortium owning the slave Hasan-beg/Ivisa Mandić (son of Šain-aga Mandić of Sinj), with ransom demand of 2,000 reali plus 4 horses (800 paid) (docs 65, 68). His brother — colonel/*governator* Radoš — killed or missing after the Sinj battle April 1685 (docs 60, 61); another brother held captive in Constantinople (doc 65).

## Family
- Brother: *governator / colonnello* Radoš — killed/missing at Sinj April 1685.
- Brother: unnamed, captive in Constantinople (1685).
