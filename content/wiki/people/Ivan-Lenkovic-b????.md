# Ivan Lenković

Senjski kapetan in 1560. Venetian Senate sought his intervention through the Habsburg ambassador to release Venetian Morlach subjects captured by Senj uskoks on Velebit summer pastures (June 1560) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].
