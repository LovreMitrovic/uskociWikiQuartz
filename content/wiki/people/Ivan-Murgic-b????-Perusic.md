# Ivan Murgić († 1881)

Born in [[Perusic]] ([[Lika]]). Career officer in the Vojna krajina from 1828 onward; served at posts in Primorje and Lika. Author of *Uspomene na gornju Krajinu s osobitim obzirom na Bunjevce*, published posthumously in **Vienac** (Zagreb, 1882, br. 2–8) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]] (Murgić 1882:2, 29).

Records what Šarić considers **the last izvorna svjedočanstva** of self-identifying Bunjevci in Lika–Primorje:
- *"Svi se ovi još i sada sami zovu Bunjevci i kažu: mi smo vridna braća Bunjevci."*
- *"Ako bi jih tko upitao, koje su vjere... a Bunjevac kazao je... vaviek 'ja sam pravi Bunjevac'."*

By Murgić's own framing, the Bunjevci were already "ništa drugo nego Hrvati" — recording the moment of disappearance of the autonomous Bunjevac identity in the Lika–Primorje branch.

Erdeljanović 1930 mistakenly assumed Murgić was himself of Bunjevac origin (he was not).
