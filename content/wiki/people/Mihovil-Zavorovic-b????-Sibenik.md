# Mihovil Zavorović (Michiel Zavoreo)

*Capitano del contado* of [[Sibenik|Šibenik]]. Co-led the Cetina expedition with Stojan Janković in March 1685 (docs 42, 51, 54, 59); present at the Sinj battle April 1685 (docs 61) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
