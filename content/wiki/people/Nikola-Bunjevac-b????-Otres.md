# Nikola Bunjevac (1662)

**Earliest known written attestation of the surname "Bunjevac"** [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]] (Desnica 1950:I/106).

Recorded among Morlaci who fell in a clash with Turks at Otres during the Candian War. He had defected (prebjeg) from Ottoman territory. Note in matičnih knjiga of the novigradska i ražanačka župa for 1662.

The surname Bunjevac as etnik attaches in non-Bunjevac milieux — typically among Orthodox Vlach neighbors, who differentiated incoming Catholic Vlachs by this name.
