# Vučić Oluić

New Morlach settler at **Bilišani**, above Obrovac. In late January 1686 (doc. 123) he comes to Stojan Janković at Zadar and reports that the Croatian ban (Erdődy) has crossed into Bosnia four days earlier with great destruction, and that 600 Morlach families have been moved by the ban to Croatia. He had been on a *četa* expedition to **Sitna Gora** (Siva Gora) on the Lika–Krbava montagne when this news reached them through the smoke of burning villages [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
