# Marko Perpić de Krmpoti (1681)

Starešina (Vlach Krmpotian elder) registered as **"Marcus Perpich de Kernpothi Rascianus"** in a 13 May 1681 zapisnik of the Dvorska komora inquiry into Zrinski estate obligations [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]] (Dabić 2000:107).

Together with [[Stephanus-Duich-b????-Krmpote]], summoned to testify about the prior obligations of the Krmpotians in [[Lic]], [[Krmpote-Senj]], and [[Ledenice]] toward the Zrinski grofovi.

The "Rascianus" attribution in the Habsburg register is, in Šarić's reading, an **administrative classification, not a self-identification** — it captures the Habsburg perception of all Catholic Vlach prebjega from Ottoman territory as part of a "racka nacija" overlapping with vlach-Orthodox neighbors. Vojin Dabić 2000 reads the same record as evidence the Krmpotians "smatrali sebe Srbima"; Šarić rejects this as anachronistic flattening of pre-modern administrative attribution.
