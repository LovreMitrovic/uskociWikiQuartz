# Marin Michiel (Marin Mikiel)

Venetian nobleman; *Provveditor estraordinario commissario* in Dalmatia 1685. Distinct from [[Marin-Cavalli-b????-Venice]] (different family, 1560) and from [[Polo-Michiel-b????-Venice]].

## Timeline
- **1685 (Feb)** — Conducts a reconnaissance tour of the Knin frontier; with [[Stojan-Jankovic-b1635-Zegar]] and [[Smoljan-Smiljanic-b????-Bukovica]] in a 600-strong force burned outlying Knin houses while only 8 Turkish horses were watering; 4 Turks captured (doc 51) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **1685-04 (7 April)** — At the Sinj battle; horse killed under him; saved by an *alfier* (standard-bearer) of *capitanio Segna* (Senjski Hungarian-nationality captain) who gave him his own horse (docs 44, 51, 61) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Karambaša Nakić also offered him a horse (doc 61).
