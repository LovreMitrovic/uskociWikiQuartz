# vojvoda Gvozden Sladović

Starešina of the [[Sladovic|Sladović]] (sub)katun in the early 17th c., active across [[Bukovica]] and [[Podgorje]] [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]]. The Sladović group of this period is known as **"Gvozdenovi ljudi"** after him.

In **1605** Gvozdenovi ljudi (20 families) joined the [[Krmpote-Migration-Lic-1605|first Krmpote migration to Lič]] on the Zrinski [[Hreljin]] estate. Surnames in the 1605 group: Balinović, Brošković, Ilnić, Karanović, Lovrić, Martinović, Mihovilović, Mikulić, Sladović, Veljanić, Vilenica.

After 1605 the Sladović fate intertwines with the Krmpotić branch.
