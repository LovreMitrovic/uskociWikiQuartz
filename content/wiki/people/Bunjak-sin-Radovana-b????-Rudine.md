# Bunjak sin Radovanov (1477)

Member of the džemat of Poblah sin Radovana in nahija Rudine, 1477 Hercegovina defter (Aličić 1985:68) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]]. **One of only two Bun-stem personal names** found among 7,822 Vlach-household-heads + comparable patronymic-fathers in the entire Hercegovina defter (the other being [[Bunus-sin-Petra-b????-Bjelopavlici]]).

Onomastic significance: by the late 15th c. Bun-stem names had become very rare in Hercegovinian vlach society — even when, by Šarić's argument, the Bunjevci themselves were forming there. Šarić reads this as evidence that vlach personal-name stocks were already heavily slavicized (rad-, vuk-, drag-, mil-) by 1477 and the romanic Bun-stem was archaic.
