# Filippo (Filip) Mitrović

Capitano of a 56-foot company at Obrovac from 1688; **dismissed by Molin in January 1690** [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 227). Identified in the source as **nephew of Zaviša Mitrović** — possibly a son of a third Mitrović brother (otherwise unattested in Janko's documented sons Stojan, Zaviša, Ilija). Distinct from [[Filip-Smiljanic-b????-Udbina|Filip Smiljanić]].

## Notes
- Confidence medium because no parental link to the wider Mitrović-Janković kuća is explicitly named; Desnica records only the "nephew of Zaviša" formula.
