# Ante Starčević (1823–1896)

Born in [[Veliki-Zitnik]] near [[Pazariste]] in [[Lika]] — direct descendant of the Bunjevac porkulab [[Milos-Starcevic-b1656-Veliki-Zitnik]], šukununuk-level. **Founder of modern Croatian etnonacionalni identity** through the Hrvatska stranka prava (HSP).

Šarić cites the Starčević lineage as the **paradigmatic case** of the transformation: in just one century, an "influential bunjevačka obitelj" went from Bunjevac to "snažan hrvatski identitet." Cousins **David Starčević** and **Mile Starčević** continued the political project [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

Šime Starčević was Ante's stric (uncle).
