# Bastić, pasha of Zvornik

Newly-named pasha of [[Zvornik]] (sandžak Zvornik) in 1686. Sent to **[[Cliuno|Livno]]** in early September 1686 with a *kapidžibaša* and the new Bosnian *alajbeg* **Filipović** plus 600 Turks, to repair the towers and houses burned in Janković's first Livno raid (mid-July 1686) and to reinforce the castle. Routed in the **second Livno raid** (5 Sept 1686, doc. 141) when Janković and Smiljanić attacked with 6,000 men [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
