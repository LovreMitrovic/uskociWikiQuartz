# Karalija (četobaša)

A celebrated Ottoman *četobaša* ("famous Caralia"). Killed by Janković's returning party from the **second Livno raid** in early September 1686, together with two other principal companions; their heads taken (doc. 141, attested by Stojan Janković and Smoljan Smiljanić in Zadar, 7 Sept 1686) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
