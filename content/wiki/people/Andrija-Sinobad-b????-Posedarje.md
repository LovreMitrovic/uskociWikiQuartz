# Andrea (Andrija) Sinobad

Son of kavalier [[Jovan-Sinobad-b????-Posedarje|Jovan Sinobad]]. Appointed captain of the [[Zvonigrad]] company in 1697 **as a child** (with a tutor *governatore* assigned), on his father's merit (doc 304) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Later served as **serdar of [[Knin]]**; died in 1725 (referenced 1726, docs 376, 378). Cousin [[Giacomo-Sinobad-b????-Posedarje|Giacomo Sinobad]] later succeeded as serdar of Knin in 1729.
