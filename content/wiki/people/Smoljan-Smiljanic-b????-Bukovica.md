# Smoljan Smiljanić (Smoglianovich)

Nephew of Ilija and Filip Smiljanić — *"ad imitazione degli quondam Elia et Filippo Smilianich suoi zii"* (doc. 139). The dukal of 13 July 1669 (doc. 166) names him Smiljanić from a family that *"abbandonarono il paese turchesco li progentiori"* — this is the same Udbinski uskakanje as his uncles.

He had **uterine half-brothers** Šimun and Marko **Brajinović** (sons of his mother **Anka** by her first husband **q. Frane Brajinović**, son of q. **Toma Brajinović**). When Šimun was a slave, Smoljan held the brothers' inherited goods in trust per provveditore Contarini's mandate of 22 February 1664 (doc. 184, 1671 inventory of restored goods). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]

## Timeline
- **1656-06-15** — *Capitan Smiglianich, "come herede e bona tenente delli quondam ... Elia e Filippo Smiglianich suoi zii"* — sentenced to pay medic Mistakieli for treatments (doc. 103). Listed in the document as *"Smoglian Smiglianich o Mihalevich"* — alternate paternal patronymic. [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1664-10-01** — Provveditore Contarini names him **third serdar** alongside the two existing serdari Mirković and Kiakić; pay 20 ducati/month, all subordinated to col. Frane Posedarski (doc. 139). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1665-10-15** — Witness/godparent: a son **Lukas** of harambaša Milivoj Zubičević and donna Catarina (= widow of Ilija Smiljanić!) baptised; comare = Dessa wife of serdar Cuitco Sarich (doc. 144). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1668-12-09** — Co-leader with Janko Mitrović of the Bribir ambush — ring + reward (doc. 163, 165). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1669-07-13** — Dukal of Doge Domenico Contarini: 30 ducati/month + 100-ducat gold chain + dolama (doc. 166). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1670-01-22** — Provveditore Barbaro names him **commandant of Nadin** (doc. 190). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1671** — Restores the Brajinović inheritance to half-brothers Šimun and Marko (doc. 184 — full inventory of mother's house in Spianata behind Sv. Marko, including silver-bridled saddlery, brocaded vests, jewellery, kilims, weapons; original document in *Arhiv B. Desnice u Obrovcu*). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1679-02-09** — His son **Lazaro** (in tenera età) named *capitan* of a 50-man company of Croata fanti raised by Smoljan (doc. 255). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1684** — Co-signatory with Stojan Janković, Frane Posedarski, Šimun Bortulačić of the proposal to raise 4 paid cavalry companies of 50 to discipline the Morlachi (doc. 395). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]

- **1684-08** — Glamoč–Grahovo campaign with Janković and Bortulačić; lightly wounded in hand at Grahovo (docs 27, 30, 32). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]

- **1685-09** — Bjelaj/Vakuf raid; brings 500 Orthodox musketeers from Srb to Venetian side (docs 97, 98). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
