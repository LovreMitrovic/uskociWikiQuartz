# Bassa di Bosna (1684, unnamed)

Unnamed Pasha of Bosnia, May 1684; reported to be heading to Belgrade and onward to Hungary [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].

## Notes
- Distinct from the later Bosnian Pasha of April 1685 (who led the Sinj relief and took Alberti as personal slave).
