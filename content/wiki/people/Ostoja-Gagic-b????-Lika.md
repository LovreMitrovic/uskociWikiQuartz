# Ostoja Gagić (Osta Gahich)

Lika-born Morlach who *uskočio* with the 1685 wave; settled at **Biljane** in the Kotari. In Jan 1686 (doc. 124) detained in Zadar prison on the suit of [[Rade-Tepsic-b????-Lika|Rade Tepšić]] over an unliquidated debt; **Stojan Janković** stood as personal *pieggio* (surety) for his release, the bond being signed on Janković's behalf by Ivan Bortulačić *"per non saper lui [Janković] scriver"* — a notable confirmation that Stojan was illiterate. Document in *Spisi Mikiel*, knj. jedina, str. 5 verso [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
