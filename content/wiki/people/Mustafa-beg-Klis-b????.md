# Mustafa-beg, kliški sandžak-beg

**Tatar-origin** Klis sandžak-beg of the early 17th c. Father of [[Mehmed-beg-Klis-b????]] [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

Together they constituted the "tatarska dinastija" against whose extortive provincial rule the Bunjevac Vlachs of [[Sandzak-Klis]] rebelled and from which they fled to [[Backa]]. Athanasio Georgiceo (1626) noted that the seoba "preko Dunava" was "ponajviše iz straha od Mehmed-bega i njegova oca" (Batinić 1885:116–150).
