# Petar Mitrović (born Stojisavljević)

Born to [[Vucen-Stojisavljevic-b????-Vrlika|Vučen Stojisavljević]] and [[Marija-Mitrovic-b????-Zegar|Marija Mitrović]] in a village under [[Vrlika]]. After his father's death raised in the Mitrović household and assumed the **Mitrović cognomen**.

## Timeline
- **1716** — Appointed captain of an overseas company by provveditor [[Andelo-Emo-b????-Venice|Anđelo Emo]] (doc 379). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **1726-02-23** — Named **serdar of Knin Krajina** by [[Niccolo-Erizzo-b????-Venice|Niccolo Erizzo]] under the cognomen Mitrović (docs 376, 379). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **1729** — Investigation under provveditor [[Pietro-Vendramin-b????-Venice|Pietro Vendramin]] establishes his actual paternity (Stojisavljević) (doc 379). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **1729-12-01** — Removed by ducal of Doge Mocenigo II in favor of [[Giacomo-Sinobad-b????-Posedarje|Giacomo Sinobad]] (doc 380). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
