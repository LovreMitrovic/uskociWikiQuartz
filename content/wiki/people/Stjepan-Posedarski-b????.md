# Stjepan Posedarski

In 1519 a chaplain of [[Ivan-Karlovic-b????-Krbava]] under this name gave a dramatic anti-Ottoman speech before Pope Leo X in Rome, decrying Ottoman *istimâlet* tactics and intermarriage [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Šarić also lists a Stjepan Posedarski (knez) among 16th-c. cases of Vlach/noble defection to Islam — possibly the same person, possibly a kinsman.
