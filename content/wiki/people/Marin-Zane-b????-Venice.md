# Marin Zane

Venetian provveditor general of Dalmatia 1703–1705. **Suppressed the 1704 Bukovica revolt** led by [[Petar-Jagodic-Kuridza-b1666-Bukovica|pop Petar Jagodić-Kuridža]], [[Ilija-Nanic-b????-Zegar|Ilija Nanić]] and [[Mattio-Zabetic-b????-Zaravecchia|Mattio Žabetić]] (docs 351–357). Later "ritornato di provveditor general" (doc 389) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
