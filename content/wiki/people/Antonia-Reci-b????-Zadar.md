# Antonia Reci (?–1687)

Second wife of [[Stojan-Jankovic-b1635-Zegar|Stojan Janković]]; from a Greek-of-Latin-rite Zadar family (daughter of donna Nicolotta and *q. Apostolo Rezzi*; see [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]] doc. 228 for marriage 16 Aug 1676).

Died 29 Jan 1687 in Zadar; buried at **Sv. Šime, Zadar** [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].

Mother of Stojan's surviving sons Josip, Konstantin and of the children Marietta (d. 2 Feb 1686) and Liberal (d. 23 Aug 1686), all buried in Zadar in 1686–87.
