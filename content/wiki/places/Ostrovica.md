# Ostrovica

Ottoman fortress and Vlach *nahija* (29 houses, 4 villages) in 1528–1530, with primićuri [[Petar-sin-Jancica-b????-Ostrovica]], [[Grgur-Bratic-b????-Ostrovica]], later [[Milan-sin-Radka-b????-Ostrovica]] (1540 — 154 houses). By 1550 a major hub (352 houses) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Site of villages Bjeline (Stara Bilina), Lišane Ostrovičke, Vrbljani (timar of [[Marko-sin-Lipovica-b????]]).

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
Repeated muster point [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 19, 51). Zaviša Janković sent here; Milković's base. Pertinenza HQ from 1689 Cornaro Liga (with Vukšić, Morlat Polača, Zazvić, [[Zecevo|Žečevo]], [[Kistanje]], [[Suplja-Gornja|Šuplja Gornja]], [[Piramatovci]], [[Stankovci]], [[Banjevci]], [[Dobra-Voda]]). Vušić, Morlač, Zavišić, [[Dobropoglie|Dobropolje]], Šuplja Crikva listed under Ostrovica pertinenza.
