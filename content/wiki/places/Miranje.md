# Miranje

Village in [[Ravni-Kotari]]. Settled 1528 with [[Jagodnja]] as the dzemat of [[Tomas-Ruzic-b????-Sibenik]] (26 houses, fertile land yielding 1143 akčes) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Donje and Gornje Miranje were also a joint *timar* of [[Ivanis-Poropat-b????-Mirko]] and Ivaniš sin Mirka (2000 akčes split). Later orthodox church of Sv. Mihajlo built on site of medieval Catholic predecessor.
