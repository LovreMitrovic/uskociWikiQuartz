# Ulcinj

Adriatic port; origin of "Fuste Dalcignotte" pirates threatening the Adriatic (doc 373) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Dolfin at Ulcinj while Selman pasha raided Mokro Polje (~1696).
