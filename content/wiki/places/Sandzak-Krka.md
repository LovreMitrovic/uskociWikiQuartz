# Sandzak Krka (Lika)

Ottoman *sandžak* with seat at [[Knin]], created from western parts of [[Sandzak-Klis]] in the late 16th c. 1585 register here marks the disappearance of the **Vlasi Istre** name; surviving "raja" pay former Vlach *filurija* in reduced amounts [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].
