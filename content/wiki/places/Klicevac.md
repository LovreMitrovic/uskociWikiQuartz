# Kličevac

Ottoman Vlach *nahija* (16 villages, 120 houses) in 1528–1530 — the largest by households. Included villages [[Miranje]], [[Jagodnja]], headed by [[Tomas-Ruzic-b????-Sibenik]] (26 houses) and Radič (123 houses) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Dissolved by 1540, villages absorbed into [[Kozulova-Polje]] vakuf.
