# Žejane

Village on [[Cicarija]]. One of the last remaining communities of the Istro-Romanian / "istrorumunjski" idiom into the modern era — last relict of the bilingual Slavic-Romance Vlach speech of the [[Vlasi-Istre]] [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].
