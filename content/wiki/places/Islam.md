# Islam

Village; pertinenza HQ from 1689 Cornaro Liga, including [[Policnik|Poličnik]], Stošije, Budin, Kašić, Veljani, Suhovare [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Mehmed beg's name attached to the village. Under Zaviša Mitrović 1691 (doc 309). Captain Ilija Nanić of Kašić & Islam. Mitrović family had houses and 400 gognali there from 1670 (Barbaro investiture). Conflict with Posedarci (docs 311, 312, 329, 330, 358, 359, 377). Note: 70 Žegarski porodice settled at Islam after Sorić's 1648 raid (per [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]).
