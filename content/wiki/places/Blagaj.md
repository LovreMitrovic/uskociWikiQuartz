# Blagaj

Old fortress above the [[Buna]] spring; medieval seat of the Bosnian rulers, then of oblasni gospodari **Sandalj Hranić** and **herceg Stjepan Vukčić Kosača** (after whom Humska zemlja was renamed Hercegovina from 1448). After Ottoman conquest, seat of the nahija Blagaj in [[Sandzak-Hercegovina]].

In Bosnian-Franciscan literary tradition, [[Buna]] under Blagaj came to function as a **symbolic origin point of Hercegovina** ("Blagaj šeher, a kasaba Mostar" — old proverb). Šarić: this symbolic charge underpins the constructed [[Buna]]-river origin legend of the Bunjevci, deployed by the Bosanska franjevačka provincija in 16th–17th-c. jurisdictional disputes with Dalmatian dioceses [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].
