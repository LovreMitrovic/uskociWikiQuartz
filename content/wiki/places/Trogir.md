# Trogir (Traù)

Venetian port and territory (docs 9, 16, 18, 51, 56, 59, 61) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Cetina Morlak migration March 1685: Ilija Peraica brought 600 armed men + families + 30,000 small animals + 10,000 cattle to Trogir territory. Castelli di Trau attacked by Turks 7 April 1685 (docs 56, 62) — defeated by 3000 castellani + Morlachs.
