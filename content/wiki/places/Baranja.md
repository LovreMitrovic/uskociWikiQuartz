# Baranja

Region between the Drava and Danube; under Ottoman [[Sandzak-Mohac]] from 1543. Receiver of Bunjevac and Orthodox vlach migrations from [[Sandzak-Klis]] / [[Sandzak-Krka]] in the 16th–17th c. (see [[Bunjevac-Migration-to-Backa-c1606]]).

Notable settlement: **[[Grabovac]]** — Orthodox monastery founded 1585 by monks from manastir Dragović near [[Cetina]]. In 1619 reinforced by Klis-area Orthodox Vlachs [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].
