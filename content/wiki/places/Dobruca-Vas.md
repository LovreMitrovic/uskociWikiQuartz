# Dobruča Vas (Dobrudža)

Village near [[Benkovac]]. 1528: 7 non-Muslim houses, residence of [[Petar-Najcinovic-b????-Dobruca-Vas]] (knez, head of Benković dzemat) and his relative [[Nenad-Jancic-b????-Dobruca-Vas]] [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. 1574 vakuf register: 15 Christian houses on Husrev-beg vakuf. Najčinović returned here after 1540 (1533 Zadar grain contract).
