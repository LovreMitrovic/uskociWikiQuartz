# Dicmo

Vallone near [[Sinj]]; Ali camped here April 1687 (doc 169); later place where settlers from [[Duvno]] + [[Rama]] placed; Orthodox enclave (docs 169, 184) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
