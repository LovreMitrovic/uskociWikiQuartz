# Radovin

Village in Zadar district. 16th–17th c. concentration of [[Rudelic]] Vlachs absorbed into Catholic Bunjevac stream; in 1683 relocate to [[Karlobag]] and Šugarje in Podgorje, with some to Smiljan in [[Lika]] (1686 [[Pavo-Rudelic-b????-Smiljan]]) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
In Ventura's sub-region (docs 19, 31) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]; under [[Posedarje]] pertinenza in 1689 Cornaro Liga.
