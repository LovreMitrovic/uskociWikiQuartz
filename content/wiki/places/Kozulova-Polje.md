# Kožulova Polje

Most fertile area of the Vilajet Hrvati between [[Klicevac]] and [[Ostrovica]] fortresses, named after the pre-Ottoman noble Vlach family Kožul (Kožulić) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Endowed as the *vakuf* of [[Gazi-Husrev-beg]] in 1531 (first as čiftluk, later nahija Kožulova). 1574 register lists 97 "Istarska raja" houses across ~20 villages and *mezre*. Today only the village Kožlovac east of Benkovac preserves the name.
