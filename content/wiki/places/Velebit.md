# Velebit

Mountain range separating [[Lika]] from the Adriatic coast and [[Podgorje]]. Site of major *yaylak* summer pastures (Veliko/Malo Rujno, Oglavinovac, Javornik, Štirovac, Bunjevac, Sveto Brdo, Veliki/Mali Golić, Libinje, Male/Duboke jasle) used by [[Vlasi-Istre]] and other transhumant Vlachs [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Timber from these slopes was used for the Ottoman Obrovac fleet 1530.

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
"Morlacca / Velebich" — [[Vinjerac]] villagers guarded passes here in last war [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 173).
