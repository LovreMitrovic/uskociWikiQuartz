# Pazinska Grofovija (County of Pazin)

Inland Habsburg Istrian county, seat at [[Pazin]]. Captain in 1571: [[Gaspar-Mauric-b????]]. Site of the 1570 [[Pazin-Peasant-Revolt-1570]] which led to ~2,000 "Ćići" subjects fleeing to Ottoman territory [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Hosted Vlach settlements at Tinjan, [[Milohanic-i]], and others.
