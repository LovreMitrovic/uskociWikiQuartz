# Šibenik

Venetian Dalmatian city. Birth area of [[Murat-beg-Gajdic-b????-Sibenik]] and of [[Tomas-Ruzic-b????-Sibenik]] (before his flight to Istria 1518). Šibenik *knez* in 1532: [[Bernardo-Balbi-b????-Venice]] [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Hub for trade contacts of Vlasi Istre on the Ottoman side (e.g. [[Petar-Najcinovic-b????-Dobruca-Vas]] 1533).

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
Multiple staging events; recruiting area, pertinenza border [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (docs 8, 16, 17, 25, 28, 38, 39, 48, 49, 50, 51). Battista Contarini conte. Vranjska zaseda 28–29 May 1684: Šibenik Morlachs ambushed Turkish raiding party returning from Šibenik area; 40 Turks killed. 500 picked men from Šibenik / [[Skradin]] / [[Velim]] raided [[Bilaj]] Dec 1687. 200 men from Šibenik to garrison Drniš. Crnice brothers died in prison there (doc 343). Bishop Calegari. Filip Smiljanić actually killed in Šibenik brawl (per source footnote, doc 320 — corrects the family supplica's claim he died fighting Turks).
