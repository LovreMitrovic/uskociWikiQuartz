# Makarska

Adriatic port and Riviera center, Hercegovina relazione (doc 7, 33) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Listed Sept 1687 with [[Omis|Almissa]] and [[Poljica]] for Morlach captain assemblies (doc 177). Receiving area for Ivan Rašić's group 1692 (200 souls / 25 families settled at [[Zavojane]], Stigli, Hadži Lukovo).
