# Knin

Strategic Croatian fortress town. Pre-Ottoman seat of the Knin diocese (reduced to titular see after 1522). Later seat of [[Sandzak-Krka]] [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Surroundings absorbed [[Vlasi-Uskoplja]] settlement c. 1528; later receives Vlachs Bjelajaca from Bosnia 1690.

## Per Desnica (1646–1684)
Throughout the corpus the operational base of the Krčki sandžak-beg (Durakbegović 1670s–80s), the kapetan (Atlagić, then Firdusović 1680s+), and the disdar (Mehmed-aga). Recurring base of Ottoman counter-raids against the Kotari.

- 1648-04 — Conquered by Foscolo's force after Klis (doc. 26); held only briefly.
- 1653-10-22 — Stojan Janković under Knin's walls beheads aga Asan Cuna (Grgur Galiotović as alfier; doc. 77).
- 1666-04-05 — Knin Turks form the chief Ottoman muster behind the Cetina rotta.
- 1675-08-29 — Mehmed-beg Atlagić's letter in **Bosnian Cyrillic** (doc. 214).
- 1683-12-16 — Pađen battle: 24 Kotarska zastave under Knin's walls (docs. 353, 355). 10 Turks killed; 3 Kotarci dead/missing (Martin Suskovich da Budin, Bose Sudanović Bužanin, one from Ljuba).
- 1683 (Christmas) — Turks abandon Knin briefly, return; 1500-strong Ottoman base by Feb 1684 (doc. 384).

## People (Ottoman side)
- **Mehmed-beg Atlagić** — kapetan kninski.
- **Mustaj-beg Firdusović** — kapetan kninski 1683 (doc. 380).
- **Mehmed-aga** — disdar.
- **Asan Cuna** — aga, beheaded 1653.
- **Omer-aga Vidimlić** — 1679 (doc. 261).

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
Major fortress, multiple raids and skirmishes 1684–1685 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Mehmed-beg Durakbegović sandžak-beg until early 1685. Sandžak-beg Mehmed Beg Cernicich (sangiacco of Krka, Lika, Krbava); his son Mahmut Beg disdar/castellan of Knin fortress.

**Knin Battle 2 June 1684** (doc 9): 2000 Kotari Morlachs under Janković, Smiljanić, Bortulačić; victory; ~120 Turks killed; 90 horses captured; Janković lightly wounded.

**Mikiel's reconnaissance (Feb 1685, doc 51)**: Mikiel + Janković + Smiljanić, 600-strong; burned Knin's outlying houses; 4 Turks captured.

**Knin Conquest (29 August – 11 September 1688)** [docs 213–220]: Cornaro's 15-day siege; assault Sep 2 (city); siege of inner fortresses; surrender Sep 11 by Atlagić to discretion. First/second recinto; fortress on Grebano; mosque converted to Catholic church; 11 bronze cannons captured; 600+ defenders, 300 perished, 1000 souls left, 150 Venetian subjects in chains freed. Atlagić, his son Mehmed Beg, nephew Alibeg captured. Convoy from [[Banja-Luka]] failed to relieve.

Krajina post-conquest: Sinobad governor/sopraintendente; Bulin Most + Aqua Radeglievaz lands; many Turkish houses (Hadži-Kurtović, Hadži-Emorić, Acmet-aga Livaković); Cossovo plain ([[Kosovo-Polje-Knin]]) full of new subjects; Juka Vukmirović Morlach habitant. Cadi-grande of Knin oversaw Skradin, Krka river, Strmica, Bošnjake, Komazlić, Golubić, Starčević, Otton, Pađene, [[Mokro-Polje]], Bribir, Ivosenze, Barivode, Radučić, [[Zegar]] (doc 315). 1689 conquest details: Bjelajci settled with Pirić in Durakbegović tower; Selman pasha threatened Knin from Mostar.
