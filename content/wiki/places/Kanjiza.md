# Kanjiža

"Five long days into Bosnia"; Kotarci raided here April 1688 — 300 men under Kalinić from [[Korlat]]; took 50 captives, 4000 small animals, 500 large; defeated by Dukajtar's 100 cavalry; 15-20 dead (doc 202) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Likely Bosanska Kanjiža/Kanjiža in Slavonia or Hungarian Kanjiža.
