# Bunčići

Village in Radmilović-Dubrava near Bileća, [[Hercegovina]]. Site of a tall medieval **stećak** with Cyrillic inscription naming **[[Bunac-Rusovic-b????-Buncici]]** — likely a vlach starešina from the Banjani Vlach aglomeracija (Bešlagić 1971:390) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

Connected to the medieval Bun-stem Vlachs ([[Vukota-Buncic-b1419]] in the 1419 katun documents).
