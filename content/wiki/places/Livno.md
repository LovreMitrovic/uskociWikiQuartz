# Livno

Bosnian frontier town (docs 9, 16, 27, 28, 30, 33, 38, 40, 53, 56, 60–62, 65) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Atlagić based here. Ali pasha of Hercegovina arrived with cavalry 1688. Burned by Zaviša (Klivuc / Cliuz). Bosnian Pasha's stop March 1685 (doc 53).
