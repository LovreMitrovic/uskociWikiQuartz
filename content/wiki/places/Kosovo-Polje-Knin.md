# Kosovo Polje (kraj Knina)

Dalmatian Kosovo polje near [[Knin]] (doc 19, in Soppe Fortezza's sub-region) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Pianura full of new subjects post-1688 conquest. NOT the Kosovo of Serbia. Vid Petrović "da Cossouo" — Kosovo near Sinj area, not this Knin Kosovo nor Serbian Kosovo.
