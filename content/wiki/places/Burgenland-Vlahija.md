# Burgenland Vlahija

Cluster of 12 Vlach-settled villages on the slopes of Kiseg gorje in southern Burgenland, on Batthyány and Erdődy estates [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. First mention 1541; settlement likely between 1517 and 1541 (perhaps c. 1538). Inhabitants were "libertini" (free peasants in military service); old Štokavian-Ikavian Croatian speakers, Catholic. Possibly drawn from same Vlach pool as the [[Vlasi-Istre]] migration cycle.
