# Kašić

Village under [[Islam]] pertinenza in 1689 Cornaro Liga [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (docs 309, 370, 377). Ilija Nanić captain of Kašić & Islam. Gvozden Radasović had property there.
