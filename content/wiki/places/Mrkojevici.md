# Mrkojevići

Vlach montenegrin region near Bar in [[Sandzak-Skadar]]. In **1622**, pop **Jovan iz Mrkojevića** led 14 Orthodox Crnogorac families to selo **Jural** in [[Limska-Draga]] in [[Mletacka-Istra]]. They were accompanied by 12 Catholic families under fra **Franjo Poruba** — the Catholic group separated, citing "uiuer essi sotto il rito latino, et gli altri sotto il Greco; onde non stariano mai bene insieme" — an early concrete instance of intra-Vlach rite-based separation [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]] (Bertoša 1986:169).
