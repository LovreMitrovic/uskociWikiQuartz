# Krbava

Karst plateau region, pre-Ottoman seat of the Krbavski princes ([[Ivan-Karlovic-b????-Krbava]]). Krbava-Modruš diocese. Ottoman conquest 1522–1527 destroyed the *knežstvo*; some inhabitants migrated north or were taken in Ottoman raids (3000 captives in 1514) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
Mehmed Beg Cernicich was sandžak-beg [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (docs 17, 33, 55). Stojan Janković destroyed the area Aug 1687. Imperial reconquest 1689 from [[Karlovac]] under Erbestein. Castles: [[Komic]] (Cornich), Bunich (Bunić), Bidach, Curiach (uninhabited per Peri), Coremill (Korenica per footnote), Suignaraz (only millet guard).
