# Sandzak Hercegovina

Ottoman *sandžak* covering Hercegovina from c. 1470. 1477 Vlach defter records *istimâlet* settlement contracts (Vladimir, Radoje vojvode) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Source area for major Vlach migrations into Croatia, including [[Vlasi-Uskoplja]] and Catholic Bunjevac streams.
