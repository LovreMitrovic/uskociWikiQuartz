# Krmpote (Senj area)

Habsburg-territory village in the [[Senjska-Kapetanija]], settled by Krmpote families starting in the 1605/1627 migrations from Hercegovinian/Ottoman Krmpote (today [[Medvidja]]). Center of the **Sv. Jakov** parish ([[Sv-Jakov]]), with adjacent settlement clusters [[Krivi-Put]], [[Ledenice]], [[Senjska-Draga]] [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

Eponymous transfer of the Hercegovinian katun name onto a new homeland — characteristic of Krmpote toponymy throughout the Tromeđa.

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
Luka Njegovan formerly lived here; brought 40–50 families to [[Vinjerac]] in last war [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 186). Distinct from [[Krmpote-Bukovica]] (Bukovica village near Zelengrad).
