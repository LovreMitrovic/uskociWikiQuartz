# Senj

Habsburg Adriatic port and base of the Senj uskoks (corsair-warriors targeting Ottoman territory). Captain in 1560: [[Ivan-Lenkovic-b????]] [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Hosted [[Vlasi-Istre]] defectors among uskoks; notable were [[Andrija-Frletic-b????-Dracevac]] ("Andre kapetan" of epic tradition) and his brother Vuk. Many uskok captains were former Ottoman subjects.

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
Capitanio of Segna mentioned (doc 61) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Senj statute confine extends only to [[Lukovo]] (10 Italian miles south of Karlobag). Senjani occupied Frankopan estates. [[Sv-Juraj-Senj|Sv. Juraj]] near Senj served as refuge for Bukovčani during the Candian war.
