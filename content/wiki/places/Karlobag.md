# Karlobag

Adriatic coastal town under [[Velebit]]. Habsburg fortress rebuilt 1574, becoming a new flashpoint between the three powers [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Receiving area for [[Rudelic]] families relocating from [[Radovin]] in 1683.

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
"Carlobago, ancient Scrisia" — fortress on Adriatic; 200 archibuggieri 1690, palace built; salt scale opened 1696 to compete with [[Obrovac]] [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Imperial subjects retreated here 1687 toward Senj (doc 186). Senj statute confine extends to [[Lukovo]] (10 Italian miles south). Karlobag people in encroachment dispute with [[Sibuljina|Sibuljina/Tribanj]] (2 miles from Starigrad). 1592 Senj uskoks raided Karlobag/Pago → Venetian capture; 1615 Senj/Uskok War: Venetians destroyed Karlobag.
