# Vodice na Krasu

Village in the [[Kras]] / Ćićarija. Refuge of [[Tomas-Ruzic-b????-Sibenik]] from 1518; site of his 1524 contact with [[Gazi-Husrev-beg]] and subsequent capture by Venetians [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Part of the Rašpor estate transferred to Habsburg control in 1523, administered by [[Nikola-Rauber-b????]] from [[Trst]].
