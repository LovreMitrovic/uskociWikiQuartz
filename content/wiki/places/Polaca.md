# Polača

Village near [[Benkovac]]. 1528: 7 non-Muslim houses; record of "Ivan, sin Hrvata" and (in nearby Marijan Polača) "Radonja, sin Morlaka". Župnik in 1591: [[Matej-Danilovic-b????-Polaca]], whose deposition before the Nin bishop documented widespread konkubinat among glagoljaški priests in *Banadego* and Lika [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Old Catholic churches Sv. Cecilija, Sv. Kuzma i Damjan.

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
Under [[Vrana]] pertinenza in 1689 Cornaro Liga [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Morlat Polača under [[Ostrovica]] pertinenza.
