# Drniš

Town in Petrovo polje, [[Sandzak-Klis]]. Site of the **1608 Drniš revolt** of muslim and Vlach population under drniški aga **[[Mehmed-Terzibasic-b????-Drnis]]** — its suppression triggered the transfer of 2,000 morlach houses across the Danube reported by the Šibenik knez to the Venetian Senate [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]] (see [[Bunjevac-Migration-to-Backa-c1606]]).

Drniš/Petrovo polje was one of the documented emigration sources for Bunjevac flight to Bačka, alongside Vrlika and Livno.

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
Captured/contested area; staging point March 1685 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 51). Fenzi the governor; threatened multiple times; Selman pasha's defeat here; 200 men from Šibenik to garrison. Tomaso Zavoreo governator (doc 314). [[Petrovo-Polje]] near Drniš. Petrova Crkva (Petrovo Crichva) — church near Drniš/Petrovo polje (doc 195).
