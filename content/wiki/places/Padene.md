# Pađene

Village near [[Knin]]. 1636 Forza report records ~3,200 "Morlaci del rito Greco" (orthodox) populating Pađene, [[Plavna]], Žagrović, Butišnica, Žegar, Krupa, Mokropolje, Kistanje [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Settled by Vlachs Bjelajaca from Bosnia in 1690.
