# Lič

Village in Gorski kotar on the former Zrinski estate of [[Hreljin]]. Primary Habsburg-side Bunjevac settlement nucleus in the early 17th c. — destination of two reconstructed Krmpote migration waves:
- **1605** — [[Krmpote-Migration-Lic-1605|first migration]]: 24+ Krmpote families, 15 Vojnić families, 20 Sladović "Gvozdenovi ljudi".
- **1627** — [[Krmpote-Migration-Lic-1627|second migration]]: 16 reconstructed families incl. [[Pavelic]], [[Starcevic]], [[Vukelic]], [[Tomljanovic]], [[Prpic]].

Family lists per Pavličević 1989, **medium confidence** [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]]. From Lič and the Senj coast, these families further dispersed into Lika (1690+) and Bačka.
