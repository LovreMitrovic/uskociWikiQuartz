# Beč / Vienna

Capital of the Habsburg Monarchy. "Guerra sotto Vienna" (1683 siege) is the political backdrop for the entire 1684–1699 Morean War / Holy League conflict that frames Desnica II [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (docs 36, 315). 1731 Lika border cordon difficulties — Imperial commander dependent on Vienna for resources.
