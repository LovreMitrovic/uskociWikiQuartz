# Habsburška Istra (Habsburg Istria)

Inland and northeastern Istria under Habsburg rule, including [[Pazinska-Grofovija]] and parts of [[Kras]]. Refuge for Croatian Vlach migrants c. 1518–1523 [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Source area for the 1528 return migration to Ottoman territory and the 1570 Pazin revolt-driven defection.
