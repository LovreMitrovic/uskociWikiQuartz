# Duvno (Tomislavgrad)

Hercegovina town/region, scene of the 23 Aug 1687 raid where Stojan Janković was killed by musket and sword while only 8–10 companions followed (doc 174) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Zeno burned the mosque-fortress.
