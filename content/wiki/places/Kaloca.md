# Kalocsa (Kaloča)

Catholic archdiocesan see in southern Hungary. Locus of the **first explicit "parochia Bunievczi"** (1622), requested by don [[Simun-Matkovic-b????]] of the Congregatio Propaganda Fide [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

In 1581 in the Kaloča area, knez **[[Vranes-Ivanovic-b????-Kaloca]]** with his knežina is recorded — early evidence of a Dinaric vlach presence pre-dating the main Bunjevac wave to Bačka.

In 1870 [[Ivan-Antunovic-b1815-Backa]] launched *Bunjevačke i šokačke novine* from Kaloča.
