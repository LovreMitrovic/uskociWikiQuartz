# Korlat

Village near [[Benkovac]] (1528: "Korlatović" with mezra Tihlić, 5 non-Muslim houses) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Old Catholic churches Sv. Nediljica, Sv. Jere, Uznesenja BDM. Adjacent Tihlić = today's [[Kula-Atlagica]].

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
Under [[Nadin]] pertinenza in 1689 Cornaro Liga (with [[Biljane]], Sopot, [[Benkovac]], Perušić, [[Kozulovac]], [[Kula-Atlagica]], [[Kozulova-Polje|Cossulovo Polje]], [[Ostrovica]]) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Zaviša Janković's holdings (oxen 1699/1700). 300 men under Kalinić from Korlat raided [[Kanjiza|Kanjiža]] April 1688 (doc 202).
