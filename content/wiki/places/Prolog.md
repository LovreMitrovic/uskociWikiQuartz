# Prolog (Mt.)

Mountain pass between Cetina and Bosnia, route of Turkish reinforcements to Sinj (docs 60, 61) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. "Bukova Klavica" (Bucova Clauizza) is a location on Mt. Prolog where Vid Petrović and 60 haiduks ambushed Ottoman travellers 1702 (doc 346); also where Nikola Žanković was killed 1699.
