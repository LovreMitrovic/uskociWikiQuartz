# Krupa (na Zrmanji)

Village in nahija [[Zrmanja]]. 1528–1530: 1 Muslim + 5 non-Muslim houses; residence of [[Husein-sin-Poropata-b????-Krupa]] (early Muslim convert) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Later site of orthodox manastir Krupa (Uspenja Bogorodice).
