# Lika

Karst plateau region east of [[Velebit]]. Pre-Ottoman home of "kraljevski Vlasi" (royal Vlachs); post-conquest part of Ottoman territory with major *yaylak* pastures (Mazinska planina, Čemernica) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Place of *timar* holdings for [[Petar-Prejic-b????]] and [[Culina-sin-Milica-b????]] (1528–1530). Toponym "Istrijanska Glavica" near Raduč preserves memory of [[Vlasi-Istre]] summer grazing.

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
Major source of new Morlak immigrants 1684–1685 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 8: 152 families/2300 souls; doc 70: 70 families/900 souls). Lika migration May 1685: Janković brought 70 Lika families (900 souls) under Polo Michiel's authority. Atlagić wanted to escape Hungarian campaign; raids on [[Bjelaj|Bilaj]], [[Rebac]], [[Udbina]]. Imperial conquest 1689 from [[Karlovac]] under Erbestein. 1731 Lika border cordon difficulties — communication halted. Many fortresses: [[Bjelaj]], Sotkolač, Krug, Ostrosach, Presnić, [[Trzac]], Krekovica, Mutuica, Kurlić, Zudurovo, Peć (Lika), Posmid, Vranograč, [[Cazin]], Bila Stena, [[Buzim]], Sgodagraski, Korica, Pedalj, Mali/Veliki Gračac, Mali/Veliki Obrovac (Lika), [[Bihac|Bihać]], [[Pazariste|Pazarišta]], Bilai, Grabenec, Široka Kula, Budach, Brlog, [[Rebac]], [[Perusic|Perušić]], [[Udbina]] (300 cav + 800 foot). 1684 Venetian raid burned [[Medak]] and other villages.
