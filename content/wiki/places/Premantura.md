# Premantura

Village south of [[Pula]] founded 1585 by Vlach colonists from [[Sandzak-Krka]] (Zvonigrad, Nadin, Velim) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Marks the inflection point from "Vlasi Istre" cycle to new Štokavian "dalmatinsko-transdinarska" migration streams in southern Istra (17th c.).
