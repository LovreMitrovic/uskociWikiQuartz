# Sandzak Klis

Ottoman frontier *sandžak* established 1537 after the conquest of [[Klis]] fortress, succeeding the [[Vilajet-Hrvati]]. Seat at Klis, later [[Knin]]. First sanjakbeg: [[Murat-beg-Gajdic-b????-Sibenik]]. Surveyed in opširni defteri of 1540, 1550, 1574, 1585 [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Held the western Tromeđa frontier with Venice and Habsburgs.
