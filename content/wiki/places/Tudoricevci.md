# Tudoričevci

Medieval vlach katun on the Dečansko vlastelinstvo (today's Metohija). Recorded in the 1330 **Dečanska povelja** of Stefan Uroš III (hrisovulja for manastir Dečani). 29 ognjišta; the **Bunovići** were one of five major (proširene/zadružne) families in it — descendants of [[Bun-praotac-b????-Tudoricevci]] [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]] (Ivanović 1951–52).

Summer pastures on Visitor near Dobra Rijeka and Andrijevica; winter "altinska zemlja" near Dečani. Onomastic relic: **Bunova livada / Livadhi i Bon's** in selo Shaptej near Dečani (Gashi 1982).
