# Krka (manastir Sv. Arhangela)

Greek-rite (Orthodox) monastery on [[Krka]] river, granted to Greek-rite monks 1690 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Bounded by Lačani (vignale), Migliaska (three mills), Karslac (boschetto), Atlum Greb, Biela Vlaha, Giama Colubinca, Skradin road, Zerveni stine od Celine — 100 fields total.
