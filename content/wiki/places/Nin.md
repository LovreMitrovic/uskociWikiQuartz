# Nin

Venetian-Croatian episcopal town. Resettlement zone for some Vlach defectors during [[Cyprus-War-1570-1573]] (April 1572 group via [[Nikola-Klacic-b????]]) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Ninska biskupija extended pastoral care into Ottoman territory through arhiprezbiter [[Jure-Matasovic-b????-Nin]] (1579).

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
Pertinenza HQ from 1689 Cornaro Liga (with [[Zaton]], [[Privlaka|Bevilacqua]], [[Pontadura]], [[Vrcevo|Vrče/Verche]], [[Poljica-Brig-Vrana|Poljica]], [[Dracevac|Dračevac]], [[Mahurci]]) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Cassio governor; Sebastiano Ponte noble of Nin (Liga 1696). Žegarani Morlachs settled below Nin after Ribnik 1648 (per [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]).
