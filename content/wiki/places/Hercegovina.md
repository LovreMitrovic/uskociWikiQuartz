# Hercegovina

Region south of Bosnia. Origin point of major Vlach migration streams into 16th-c. Croatia. Renamed "Hercegovina" from Humska zemlja from 1448 (after herceg Stjepan Vukčić Kosača, seat at [[Blagaj]]).

## 1477 defter
The 1477 Ottoman defter records *istimâlet* contracts with Vlach voivodes [[Vladimir-vojvoda-b????-Hercegovina]] (nahija Mostar) and [[Dadoje-vojvoda-b????-Hercegovina]] / Sinan-beg [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. The defter shows three core katuns of the future Bunjevac population [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]]:
- **Krmpote** — [[Vukic-Krmpotic-b????-Hercegovina]], nahija Humska zemlja, 11 houses; pastures [[Buhovo]]/[[Borajna]]/[[Ledinac]]/[[Veseli-Vstak]] (winter) and [[Cvrsnica]]/[[Vran]]/[[Ljubusa]] (summer).
- **Vojnići** — [[Vukasin-sin-Vojihne-b????-Mostar]], nahija Mostar, 10 houses + 3 unmarried; eponymous selo [[Vojnici-Ljubuski]].
- **Sladovići** — [[Milosav-sin-Sladica-b????-Mostar]], nahija Mostar, 19 houses; villages [[Podaca]]/[[Igrane]]/[[Kozica]].

In the same defter, nahija Blagaj has 36 vlach džemats (none Bunjevac); the dominant figure is **vojvoda [[Petar-Hrabren-b????-Hercegovina]]** of Donji Vlasi — predominantly Orthodox population around the [[Buna]] river.

## Migration source area
Source area of later Bunjevac (Catholic) and Orthodox Vlach migrations into [[Bukovica]], [[Ravni-Kotari]], [[Lika]], [[Backa]]. Earliest Bun-stem onomastic attestations in the 1477 defter: only [[Bunjak-sin-Radovana-b????-Rudine]] (nahija Rudine) and [[Bunus-sin-Petra-b????-Bjelopavlici]] (nahija Bjelopavlići) among 7,822 households + their fathers — the romance Bun-stem is already archaic.

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
Full description by fra Mijo Radonić May 1684 (doc 7) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Ahmed-paša commanded Hercegovina troops at [[Sinj]] 1685. Sandžak alaibeg there; Selman pasha threatened Knin (~1696). Brochno-Blato-Goranci migration 1693–1694 (5000 souls) settled at [[Zadvarje]] and [[Vrgorac]]. Towns/regions enumerated: [[Trebinje]], [[Stolac]], [[Konjic]], [[Prozor]], [[Pocitelj|Počitelj]], [[Gabela]], [[Ljubuski|Ljubuški]], [[Mostar]], [[Blagaj]], Ranza ([[Rama]]), [[Glamocko-Polje|Glamoč]], Hliuno ([[Livno]]), [[Sinj]], [[Drnis|Drniš]], [[Skradin]], [[Knin]], [[Vrlika]], [[Sidraga]], [[Vrana]], [[Obrovac]], [[Grahovo]] (Bos.), [[Novi-Hercegovina|Novi]], [[Nevesinje]], [[Gacko]], [[Imotski]], [[Vrgorac]], [[Makarska]], Sandžak Hercegovina (existing).
