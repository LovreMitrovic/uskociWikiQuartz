# Pula

Major Venetian Istrian port. Reception zone for late-16th-c. Vlach colonization of southern Istra, including [[Premantura]] (1585), [[Marcana]] (1583), and the [[Sikici]] hamlet (1586) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].
