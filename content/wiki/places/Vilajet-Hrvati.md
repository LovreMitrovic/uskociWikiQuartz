# Vilajet Hrvati (Vilâyet-i Hirvat)

Provisional Ottoman military-administrative unit on the western frontier of Sandzak Bosna, covering ~12,000 km² from the lower [[Cetina]] to [[Lika]] and [[Krbava]], including SW Bosnian karst polja and upper Pounje [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Created after Ottoman conquest 1522–1527; replaced 1537 by the [[Sandzak-Klis]]. First subaša/voivode: [[Murat-beg-Gajdic-b????-Sibenik]], under Bosnian sanjakbeg [[Gazi-Husrev-beg]]. ~4,220 taxable households in 1530.
