# Posedarje

Coastal village/county on Novigrad more, ancestral seat of the [[Posedarski|Posedarski (Benja-Posedarski)]] noble line. The principal Venetian-side settlement-village for the Žegar Mitrović *uskakanje* and a major staging-area for Morlachi assaults into Bukovica.

## Genealogical/military summary (per Desnica 1646–84)
- 1647 — Knez Frano Posedarski (the Elder) is the local governator collaborating with Foscolo (docs. 5–10) on Morlachi receiving operations.
- 1648-05-15 — Janko Mitrović given judicial first-instance authority over Bukovački uskoci based at Posedarje (doc. 29).
- 1654-01-10 — Janko Mitrović's home village from his uskakanje base; he repels a Turkish raid at the Posedarje *guazzo*; pay raised to 20 ducati (doc. 78).
- 1656-08-04 — Bigliane (a Posedarje sub-village) is where the Morlach harambaše signed the petition refusing Posedarski as colonnello (doc. 105).
- 1666-05-06 — During Stojan Janković's slavery the Posedarci elect Pavle Vukčević as vice-harambaša (doc. 152).
- 1671-01 — Greek-merchant svaleggio in the Posedarje area: 2 Greek merchants killed/wounded; Stojan Janković investigates (docs. 186, 188).
- 1674 — Petar Ćivran investitures Posedarci with additional Budin lands (doc. 209). 36+8+44 families: Possedarja old + Erveničani + Budinjani.
- 1683 — Frane Posedarski the Younger (governator of Nin) one of the four direttori (doc. 395).
