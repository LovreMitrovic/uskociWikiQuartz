# Buhovo

Western Hercegovina locality near today's eastern edge of Bekija. Recorded in the 1477 Hercegovina defter as a **winter pasture (zimište) of the Krmpote katun** (alongside [[Borajna]], [[Ledinac]], [[Veseli-Vstak]]) — about 22 km west of the [[Buna]] river [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]] (Aličić 1985:130–131).
