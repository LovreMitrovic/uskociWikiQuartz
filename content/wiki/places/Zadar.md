# Zadar

Major Venetian Adriatic port and seat of the Zadar contado. Reception zone for [[Vlasi-Istre]] migrations 1538 and 1540 [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Documented Venetian rectors include [[Vittorio-Barbadico-b????-Venice]] (1528), [[Antonio-de-Mula-b????-Venice]] (1540), [[Antonio-Michielo-b????-Venice]] (1557). Hub of notarial records, Senate correspondence, and franjevački pastoral activity.

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
Administrative center throughout [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Base of Mocenigo, Pasqualigo, Valier as provveditori generali. Parishes: [[Sv-Stosija-Zadar|Sv. Stošija]] cathedral; Greek-rite [[Sv-Ilija-Zadar|Sv. Ilija]] church (Magdalena Janković burial 13 May 1684 — doc 3; Croatian/Albanian-Vlach cavalry confraternity dispute 12 Feb 1685 — doc 47); Latin-rite [[Sv-Sime-Zadar|Sv. Šime]] (Antonia Janković burial 29 Jan 1687 — doc 166). Site of Janković regiment recruitment 1686 (300+ oltramarini sent to Venice). Kotari Pertinenze Reform 11 March 1689 (doc 224) divided Zadar territory into 9 administrative pertinenze (Nin, Posedarje, Islam, Novigrad, Nadin, Zemunik, Vrana, Ostrovica, Zaravecchia). 1689 census: 16,273 souls; 4,470 fit for arms (893 cavalry); 28 working mills.
