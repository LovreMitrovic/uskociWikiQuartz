# Kotor (Cattaro)

Bay of Kotor naval base and Venetian provveditore headquarters [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 72, 174). Threatened by Skadar Sandžak May 1685 (Grbalj/Zuppa contado at risk). Boka where [[Lalizic|Laližić]] was exarch 1695. Levant career station of Ottaviano Lazzari (doc 345).
