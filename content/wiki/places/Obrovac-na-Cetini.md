# Obrovac (na Cetini)

Distinct from [[Obrovac]] (Obrovac na Zrmanji). Tower in the Cetina valley near Sinj. Tower burned by Morlachs April 1685 (doc 61) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].

## Cross-reference
Two Obrovacs in Desnica II: this Cetina-valley one (Sinj area, doc 61), distinct from the Zrmanja-river [[Obrovac]] in Mitrović sub-region (docs 11, 15, 19, 51).
