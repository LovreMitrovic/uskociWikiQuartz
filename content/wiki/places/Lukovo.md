# Lukovo

Village 10 Italian miles from [[Karlobag]]; the Senj statute extends only to here [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Site of duel between Mesić and Mitrović (doc 364 footnote — likely Saviša Janković).
