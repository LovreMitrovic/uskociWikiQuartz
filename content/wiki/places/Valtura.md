# Valtura

Village near [[Pula]] in southern Istria. Settled in 1647 by a Vojnić group under knez **[[Filip-Zupanovic-b????-Tinj]]** during the Candian War — separating the Vojnići from the Tinj/Županovići area as Ottoman territory [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]]. Sister settlement to [[Premantura]] and [[Marcana]] in the broader 16th–17th-c. Vlach colonization of S. Istria.
