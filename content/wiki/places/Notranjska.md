# Notranjska

Habsburg Inner Carniola; reception area for Croatian Vlach refugees c. 1518–1523, alongside [[Habsburska-Istra]] and [[Kras]] [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Krsto I. Frankapan held lands here pre-1527.
