# Poljica (brig Vrane)

Village in the Zadar contado near [[Vrana]] / [[Nin]], in Posedarski's sub-region (doc 19) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Distinct from [[Poljica]] (Poljičko kneštvo, between Split and Cetina). Later listed under Nin pertinenza in Cornaro/Dolfin Liga (1689, 1696) alongside Zaton, Bevilacqua/Privlaka, Pontadura, Vrčevo, Dračevac, Mahurci.
