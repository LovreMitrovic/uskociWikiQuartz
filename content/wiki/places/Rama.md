# Rama

River entering [[Neretva]] (doc 7) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Site of Sv. Petar in Rama Franciscan monastery; abandoned by friars Oct 1687; 80+ friars + 500 armed family members fled to Sinj/Dicmo, then to Sustipan abbey near Split (docs 181, 184).
