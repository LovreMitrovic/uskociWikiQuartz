# Bosansko Grahovo

Site of the 26 Aug 1684 Morlak victory: 5000 Morlachs under Janković, Smiljanić, Bortulačić ambushed two Bosnian-Hercegovinian pashas; 150 Turks killed (incl. 30 court aga, 2 "Bugliuch bassé") [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (docs 7, 27, 56). Mazut-paša's kjehaja killed by Sinovčić (doc 32 footnote). Smiljanić lightly wounded in hand.
