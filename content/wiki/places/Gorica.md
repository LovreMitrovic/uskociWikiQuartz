# Gorica (Tinj)

Catholic Vlach Bunjevac village in the Vojnić-Tinj corridor. Pre-1647 was within **Sandžak Klis**, *kadiluk* of Halil-beg of Vrana. Pop **don Stjepan Sorić** was *župnik* here from at least 1636 (per Šarić 2024/2008).

## Source-village of major uskakanje
- 1647-05-29 — knez Frano Posedarski receives Cordo, Filipac and other Goričani who have crossed; Foscolo writes to receive them (doc. 5 [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]).
- 1647-06-17 — Foscolo's proclama protects "Morlacchi da Gorizza, Percos et altri luoghi circonvicini venuti alla devotione del prencipe" (doc. 12).
- 1647 onward — **don Stjepan Sorić of Gorica** leads the *uskakanje* of the Goričani Catholic Vlachs into Venetian territory; mass-raids on Udbina (1648); killed at Ribnik 1648-07-17.
- 1648-07-31 — After Sorić's death the Goričani elect his brother **Mate Sorić** as their new capo (doc. 41).
