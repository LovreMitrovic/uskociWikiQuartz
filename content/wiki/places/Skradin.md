# Skradin

Ottoman fortress and seat of the *kadiluk* under which the nine Vlasi Istre nahijas were registered in 1528–1530 [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Site of Ottoman fleet construction alongside [[Obrovac]] under [[Murat-beg-Gajdic-b????-Sibenik]].

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
Repeated reference (docs 19, 28, 33, 37, 38, 50, 51, 58) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Cadi-jurisdiction subordinate to [[Knin]]. Staging area for Knin campaign Aug 1688 (bridge planned). Depopulated post-conquest; Dolfin ordered Morlachs to return; 50 fields granted. 500 picked men from Šibenik / Skradin / [[Velim]] raided [[Bilaj]] Dec 1687 (doc 184).
