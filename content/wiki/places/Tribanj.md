# Tribanj / Šibuljina

Krajnje dalmatinsko selo na Morlačkom kanalu prema Hrvatskom primorju [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 41 footnote 1). Šibuljina je zaselak. 131 souls newly settled (doc 41). 1702 Morlach families settled but ordered to disperse to Ražance/Starigrad; 25 families re-petitioned to resettle 1704 (docs 337, 354). 2 miles from Starigrad; encroachment dispute with Karlobag people. Decima collected from inhabitants (doc 355).
