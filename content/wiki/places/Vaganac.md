# Vaganac

Lika locality where porkulab [[Milos-Starcevic-b1656-Veliki-Zitnik]] held part of his 168 štrikova zemlje per the 1712 popis [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]]. Distinct from the Vaganac in Ogulinska regimenta (Ogulin parish), which had Rukavina settlers (1712 popis).
