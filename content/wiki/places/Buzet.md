# Buzet

Town in northern Venetian Istria. Buzetski Kras was a refuge for the [[Poropat]] kindred c. 1518–1528 before their return to [[Zrmanja]] [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Long-term concentration of Poropat (Poropatići) on the Venetian side at Trstenik, Dane, Lanišće.
