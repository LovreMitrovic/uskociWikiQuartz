# Bukovica

Karst region in northern Dalmatia between [[Zadar]], [[Knin]], and [[Velebit]]. Core settlement area of the [[Vlasi-Istre]] in the 16th c. Forms an Ottoman *nahija* in the 1528–1530 register (60 houses, 11 villages); reduced to a single village (Bruška) by 1540 [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Later receives Hercegovinian Catholic Vlachs (Bunjevci) and Orthodox Vlach migrants.

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
Central focus throughout [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]; tax/herd zone. Smiljanić family origin. Pasha's incursion April 1688 plundered it. Villages of [[Krmpote-Bukovica]], [[Parcic|Parčić]], [[Modrino-Selo]], Erveničani. Under Novigrad pertinenza 1689 Cornaro Liga (with [[Karin]], two Obrovci, [[Krusevo]], [[Zegar|Žegar]], [[Ervenik]], [[Zelengrad]], Drinjci, Otavac, [[Popovici]], Lussichi). 1704 Bukovica Revolt (Pop Petar Jagodić-Kuridža Uprising).
