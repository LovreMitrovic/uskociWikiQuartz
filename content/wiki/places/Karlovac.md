# Karlovac

Habsburg general's seat; Erbestein's command [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Imperial reconquest of [[Lika]] and [[Krbava]] launched from here 1689. Location of Vojnović feud, 1706 (doc 362).
