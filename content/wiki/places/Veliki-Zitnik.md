# Veliki Žitnik

Sub-locality of [[Pazariste]] in Lika. Family seat of the Bunjevac **[[Starcevic]]** lineage from 1690. Birthplace of **[[Ante-Starcevic-b1823-Veliki-Zitnik]]** (1823–1896), founder of the Hrvatska stranka prava [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].
