# Ledenice

Coastal hinterland village on the former Zrinski estate; one of the Krmpote Habsburg-side colony cluster villages alongside [[Lic]], [[Krmpote-Senj]], [[Krivi-Put]], [[Senjska-Draga]] [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

In the 1681 Dvorska komora inquiry into Zrinski obligations, Krmpote starešine ([[Marko-Perpich-b????-Krmpote]] and Stephanus Duich) testified about earlier Ledenice/Lič/Krmpote duties [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].
