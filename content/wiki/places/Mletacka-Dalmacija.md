# Mletačka Dalmacija (Venetian Dalmatia)

Coastal Dalmatia under Venetian rule: [[Zadar]], [[Sibenik]], [[Trogir]], [[Split]], islands. Reception area for [[Vlasi-Istre]] crossing 1538 (~5,000 to Zadar contado, of whom ~1,000+ retained by [[Antonio-de-Mula-b????-Venice]]) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Border with Ottoman Klis sandžak unstable until late 16th c.
