# Vinjerac (Castel Venier)

Coastal village/castle on Novigrad more, Italian name *Castel Venier* from the patrician Venier family.

## 1647 — Krmpote re-settlement
**Harambaša [[Harambasa-Njegovan-b????-Medvidja]]** settled 40–50 Krmpote families in 1647 after their final exodus from Ottoman [[Medvidja]] (selo Krmpote in nahija Obrovac) during the Candian War — see [[Krmpote-Migration-Vinjerac-1647]] [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

## Per Desnica (1647–84)
- 1656-03-04 — Janko Mitrović orders castel-Venier villagers to assemble at the Obrovac superiore pass-guard (doc. 101 footnote: *"Stanovnici Krmpota u Bukovici, koji su 1648 uskočili u kraj između Posedarja i Vinjerca"* — i.e. **the Krmpoćani settled in the district between Posedarje and Vinjerac**).
- 1663-02 — kapetan **Ivan Čačić** of castel-Venier killed **Murat-aga Salihagić** (Perušić *disdar*) and exhibited his head on Vinjerac walls (doc. 127).
- 1664-05-21 — Ivan Čačić named *serdar* (doc. 137).
- 1678 — Sv. Juraj uskoks contested for *montagna* lands with castel-Venier residents (docs. 239, 240).
- 1684-03-01 — Jovo (Giuco) Garguricić named *capitano* (doc. 381).
- The kotari knez **Tadić** owned a *malin* near Vinjerac (the *Malin del conte Tadijch* of doc. 258).

## People associated
- [[Janko-Mitrovic-b1605-Zegar|Janko Mitrović]] (post-1647 partly stationed here)
- Ivan Čačić — kapetan / serdar

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
Village of 80 houses [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (docs 173, 203). Grguričić captain, Knežević judge; 6 robbers of Zagreb Bishop included 2 from here. Stefano Knežević resident; under [[Posedarje]] pertinenza in 1689 Cornaro Liga. Tadia Knežević captain 1700 (doc 321). Luka Njegovan brought 40–50 families from [[Krmpote-Senj]] to Vinjerac in last war (Cretan War era). Velebit (Morlacca / Velebich) — Vinjerac villagers guarded passes here in last war.
