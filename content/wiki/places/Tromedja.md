# Tromeđa / Triplex Confinium

The "tripoint" between Ottoman, Venetian, and Habsburg territories on the Eastern Adriatic. The legal-geopolitical Triplex Confinium was defined only by the 1699 Karlowitz peace, but a *de facto* tripartite borderland already formed in the 1520s in the contact zone of Novigradsko more, Karinsko more, and the Velebit channel — Šarić's "**original Tromeđa**" [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Bounded approximately by [[Zadar]], [[Knin]], [[Senj]]. Tightly linked to the imagological region [[Morlakija]].
