# Danilović

Don [[Matej-Danilovic-b????-Polaca]] was župnik in Polača in 1591; his deposition before the Nin bishop documented widespread konkubinat among glagoljaški priests in *Banadego* and Lika [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].
