# Krešović

Single attestation: **harambaša Plavša Krešović** — 1649-08-04, "giovine sbarbato, mustacchi e pelo biondi, statura comun" — 4 ducati/month assigned by Foscolo (doc. 50).

(Note: the *quondam carambassa Plavza* in doc. 69 — pay transferred to *Giacomo Braicovich suo cognato* — is the same Plavša now deceased by January 1653.)

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)

- **Radoš Kresović (Radas Cressevich)** — brother of Sava; took over Sinobad's casale and lands at Modro near St. Peter's church in Bukovica; **refused to return them** to Sinobad, Zadar 1697 (doc. 302) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Sava Kresović (Sava Cressevich)** — brother of Radoš; same Modro selo dispute 1697 (doc. 302) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Kresović (anonymous, "Cresseuich famoso bandito")** — bandit whose death Vid Petrović sought to avenge in 1707 (doc. 365) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
