# Sinobad

Budin (Ravni Kotari) capitanska family. Per Desnica's footnote on doc. 181: in 1684-07-01 Mocenigo granted Jovan Sinobad the kula at [[Karin]] when he settled the area with Morlaci from **Modrino Selo**; the kula is still standing and known as *"Sinobaduša."*

## Bearers
- **Pera (Periza, Petar) Sinobad** — kapetan di Budin (per doc. 279 his son Jovan is called "figlia [sic, figliolo] del capitano di Budin"). Killed by Ilija Mitrović with his band; houses burned in Budin (doc. 396).
- **Jovan (Zuanne) Sinobadović** = "Zuan Sinobad" — son of Pera; alive 1682-02-17 (doc. 279, deposing about Ćosić's sheep); 1683-12-26 attests in the murder/plunder by Ilija Mitrović and others of his father and brother (doc. 391, 396); subsequently received the Karin kula.
- (1684 unnamed brother) — also killed by Ilija Mitrović's band.
- **uncle of Jovan** — 1684 prevented from cultivating the family lands by Ilija Mitrović (doc. 396).

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)

The Sinobad family rises across the Morean War period to become the principal Venetian Morlach kapetanska family of the Knin–Zvonigrad region. Major figures span four generations.

### Genealogy (per [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]])
- **Petar (Pietro/Pera) Sinobad** of Posedarje — killed at Brgud at start of Morean War by **Radan Matić** of Brgud (doc. 331); blood vendetta carried by son Jovan into 1701 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Jovan / Zuanne Sinobad** (kavalier) — son of Petar; appointed governor of Zvonigrad 1690-05-24 with company of 50 paesani, salary 14 ducats (doc. 234); appointed governor of Knin krajina 1691-06-20 with 60 fields and vineyards near Bulin Most plus houses in Knin from late Turks Hadži-Kurtović, Hadži-Emorić, Acmet-aga Livaković (docs. 239, 241); reconciled with Mitrović family 1692 (doc. 247); house in Modrino selo, Bukovica, near St. Peter's church 1692 (doc. 259, casale of Turk Modre, his *congiunto*); appointed sopraintendente of Knin krajina 1696-10-16 (doc. 293); cavalier of San Marco **1696-01-30** by Doge Valier (Sinj, Čitluk, Klivno) (doc. 283). Died **1715 in Glamoč clash** (doc. 372). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **Filippo Sinobad** — alfier then capitano of Zvonigrad; brother of Jovan; appointed June 1691 to replace Jovan; died before Aug 1694 (docs. 240, 277).
- **Mihail (Michiel) Sinobad** — nephew of Jovan and Filippo; previously held tower of Topolje; appointed captain of Zvonigrad **1694-08-21** to succeed late Filippo (doc. 277); died ~1697, replaced by Andrea (doc. 304). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **Andrea (Andrija) Sinobad** — son of kavalier Jovan; appointed captain of Zvonigrad company 1697 as a child with tutor "governatore" on father's merit (doc. 304); later serdar of Knin; died 1725 (referenced 1726, doc. 376). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **Giacomo (Jakov) Sinobad** — cornetta riformato; cousin of deceased Andrea Sinobad; "ultimo superstite della famiglia"; supplicated 1727 to be made serdar of Knin per family tradition; appointed serdar Knin **1729-12-01** by Vendramin replacing Petar Mitrović-Stojisavljević (docs. 378, 380). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **Gvozden Sinobad** — uncle ("zio") of kavalier Jovan; ordered 1699 to return cloak with silver to Anđelija Krišanova Kolundžija (doc. 313). Distinct from [[Radasovic|Gvozden Radasović]]. [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **The unnamed nephew (sister's son)** of Jovan — leader of haiduks on Mt. Prolog with Vid Petrović and 60 men; attacked Ottoman travellers and a village near Atlagić tower 1702 (doc. 346). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]

### Houses, lands, slaves
- **Modrino selo, Bukovica** — village named after Turk *Modre*, congiunto of Sinobad; casale assigned to Jovan 1692; later disputed 1697 with Radoš and Sava Kresović who refused to return (docs. 259, 302). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **Knin Turkish properties** — houses of Acmet-aga Livaković, Hadži-Kurtović, Hadži-Emorić; 60 fields near Bulin Most + Aqua Radeglievaz (doc. 241).
- **Slave Seifulah of Travnik** — confessed in 1702 ransom of 200 reali, silver saber, silver horse-tack (doc. 343).

### Notes
- The old feud between casa Mitrović and casa Sinobad ("engami implacabili") was reconciled by Dolfin in 1692; Sinobad moved to Knin to physically separate the two houses [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc. 247).
- Family origin: Posedarje. Ancestors had worked under Turks at Modrino selo (doc. 259).
