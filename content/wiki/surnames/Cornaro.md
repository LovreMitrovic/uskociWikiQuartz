# Cornaro

Venetian patrician.

## Bearers in Desnica II
- **Girolamo Cornaro (Đirolamo Kornaro)** — provveditor generale in Dalmatia 1689; issued attestation for Zaviša Janković doc. 225; **issued the 1689-03-11 Liga regulation** dividing Kotari into 9 pertinenze with governators and judges (doc. 224). Was at conquest of Knin (doc. 321) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Alessandro Corner** — Turk-converted Christian from Knin; witness 1700 in Sfakioto's Grimani-commission protocols (doc. 321) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
