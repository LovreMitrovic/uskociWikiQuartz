# Nakić

## Bearers
- **harambaša Matija Nakić** — 1683-12-09 with the Petropolje conti pleads via fra Petar Tintić for protection (doc. 349). 1684-01-04 with Frane Mirčetić petitions for *uskakanje* of 1000+ households (doc. 362). 1684-01-05 Donà gives him a panno-suit and 2 cechini (doc. 363). Sent to Venice; awaited at Šibenik with hostages 1684-03-14 (doc. 382).

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
- **karambaša Nakić** — leader of 500 Trogir-territory Morlaks; July 1684 mentioned in dispatches (docs 17, 18); at the Sinj defeat April 1685 he gave his own horse to the wounded provveditor Marin Michiel, saving him (doc 61); January 1686 reports the Bosnian Pasha's march from Banja Luka toward Livno (doc 125); November 1686 sent by Stojan Janković with Vuconie Cvitanović to warn the Zadar contado of the Turkish irruption (doc 155) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
