# Ivančević

Wide-spread Morlach surname. Multiple unrelated bearers across Ravni Kotari and the Šibenik zaleđe.

## Bearers
- **Ivan Ivančević da Polje (Petropolje)** — 1647-08-31 capitan in pop Sorić's force (doc. 16).
- **Mattio Ivancevich da Velim** — 1653-01-16 co-signs proxy authorising Smiljanić to plead in Venice (doc. 70).
- **Zaviša (Zavissa) Ivancevich con figlio et nipote** — 1671-12-17 settled in Dračevac as one of the 20 Morlach families repopulating it (doc. 199).
- **Michiel Ivancevich col nipote** — 1671-12-17 same Dračevac roster (doc. 199).
- **Pavissa Ivancevich** — 1684-03-01 named *capitano* of Dračevac (doc. 381).
- **Margarita Ivančević** — 1683-09-02 in Zadar; ordered by Donà to surrender pop Sorić's preserved hand (which she kept as relic) and his papers (doc. 311).
