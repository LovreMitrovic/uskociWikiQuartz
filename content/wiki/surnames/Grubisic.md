# Grubišić

Already in Šarić's continuity-list of Zadar-hinterland anthroponyms. Multiple distinct bearers across Dalmatia and Senj.

## Bearers
- **kapetan Piero Grubišić iz Senja** — 1647-05-15 with Morlak Elia Kositezić accused of inciting Pag Morlachi to switch to imperial service (doc. 9).
- **Tomaso Grubišić** + **Ilija Grubišić** — 1654-07-26 in 1654 Sv. Zulijan list, harambašas (doc. 84).
- **carambassà Andrea Grubišić** — 1653-01-04, pay 4 ducati/month (doc. 69).
- **kapetan Marko Grubišić iz Novigrada** — 1670-08-12 named to Mokro Polje guard (doc. 174).
- **Francesco Grubissich** — 1680-05-03 lessor near Sovare in Suhovare (doc. 265).
- **Grubissa Sincevich** — 1684-01-24 in possession of jewelry left by Turks with Bernardo Krivelari (doc. 366).
