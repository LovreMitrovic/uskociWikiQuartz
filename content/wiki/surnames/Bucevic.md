# Bućević

Major Dračevac/Komorce re-settlement family (doc. 199, 1671-12-17):

- **Raina Bucevich et fratello** — 2 souls.
- **Mattio Bucevich** — 1.
- **Michiel Bucevich** — 1.
- **Tommaso Bucevich con due germani** — 3.
- **Vido Bucevich col fratello** — 2.

Five Bućević household-heads alongside the Harlić cluster — characteristic of compact-village migration patterns.
