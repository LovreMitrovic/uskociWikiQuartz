# Busović

## Bearers
- **Spahija Busović** — 1648-05-11 *uskočio* with 30 Morlach houses to Janko Mitrović's banner from Lika (doc. 28).
- **Dragosav Busović** + **Todora** — 1663-04-21 parents of **Đurđe (Zorzi) Busović**, baptised at Sv. Zulijan Šibenik (doc. 129). Đurđe is the brother of vladika **Nikodim Busović** (per Desnica's footnote: a separate baptismal record for Nikodim was published in *Prilozi*, knj. XVII, sv. 1).

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)

- **monsignor Nikodim Busović (Nicodemo Buscovich)** — Greek-rite bishop ("vescovo del rito greco / vescovo di Stratonika"); native subject; **ordained 1693-06-24** by Melentije bishop of Philadelphia (doc. 317 footnote). Petitioned 1694 to restore monastery of **Dragović** between two arms of the river Cetina (doc. 276). Sept 1699 received 50 kanapa of land at **Međupuće** for Dragović monks (doc. 317). Troubled Nikanor Stanković of Vrlika 1698 (doc. 308). 1701 pledged for Petar Banković of Pakoštane (doc. 332) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
