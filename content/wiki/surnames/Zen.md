# Zen

Venetian patrician house.

## Bearers
- **Girolamo Zen** — *procurator*; signatory of doc 22 (1684) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Antonio Zeno** — Provveditor General della cavalleria 1686–1687; reports on Sinj siege (doc 133, doc 134) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **provveditor estraordinario Zen** — based at Cattaro, sends Korponež with galeotta to Pastrovići March 1686 (doc 131) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
