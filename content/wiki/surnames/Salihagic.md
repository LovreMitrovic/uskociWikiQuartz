# Salihagić

## Bearers (Muslim Lika)
- **Murat-aga Salihagić** — *disdar perušićki*; killed February 1663 by Ivan Čačić of Vinjerac on the *cestmma* of Perušić-area; head exhibited at Castel Venier (doc. 127).
- **njegov otac** (anonymous) — 1663 unsuccessfully offered ransom for his son's head (doc. 127).
- **Salihagini ćato** (1682) — killed on the road from Sukošan to Zadar; the slave Handan was abducted (doc. 281, Mehmed-beg Filipović's letter).
