# Pop

Likely an occupational surname from *pop* (priest). The same name in this period frequently appears as part of double surnames like *Pop-X* among the Bukovica/Lika Orthodox population.

## Bearers
- **Mattio Pop** — Vrana harambaša; signatory of the 1684 Vrana petition (doc 35) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
