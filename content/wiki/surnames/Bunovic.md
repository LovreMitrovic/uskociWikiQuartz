# Bunović

Medieval vlach clan in the [[Tudoricevci]] katun on the Dečansko vlastelinstvo. Recorded in the **1330 Dečanska povelja** (Stefan Uroš III's hrisovulja for manastir Dečani) — one of five major (proširene/zadružne) families in a 29-ognjište katun [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

Named ancestor: **[[Bun-praotac-b????-Tudoricevci]]** with sons Nikola, Tvrtko, Tvrde, Tudorica, brother Rade, Rade's son Vojisil (Ivanović 1951–52:260). Onomastic relic: **Bunova livada / Livadhi i Bon's** in selo Shaptej (Gashi 1982).

Together with [[Buncic]] and [[Bunislavic]], a key 14th-c. attestation in the Bun-stem onomastic family from which the etnik [[Bunjevac]] derives.
