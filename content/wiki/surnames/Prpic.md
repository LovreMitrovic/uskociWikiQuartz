# Prpić

Bunjevac surname; among the 16 reconstructed families of the [[Krmpote-Migration-Lic-1627|1627 Krmpote migration]] (Pavličević 1989) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]] (medium confidence). 1712 popis: present in Švica and Kompolje parishes in Otočka regimenta.
