# Kašić (Cassio)

## Bearers
- **dr. Ivan (Giovanni) Kašić** — 1678-04-15 named by Valier among the four sopraintendentini (Sope, Bortulačić, Kašić, Pasini) of the Liga (doc. 242).

## Notes
- The **Kašićev Ritual** at the Filipjakov parohijska crkva (now in Knin museum) is annotated with a 1666 glagolska zabeleška by Nikola Kužinović about the death of Vučko Močivuna at Zečevo (doc. 151).

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)

- **Francesco Cassio** — Zadar/Nin noble; appointed **governor of pertinenza 1 (Nin)** by Cornaro in 1689 Liga reform; later one of Liga giudici under Dolfin 1696 (docs. 224, 286) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **dottor Cassio** — father of **Ellena**, who received a diamond ring bequest in Marija Smiljanić's 1693 testament (doc. 267) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Giovanni (Ivan) Cassio dottore** — witness alongside *alfier* Antonio Bardi to the 14 September 1686 Stojan-Janković purchase contract for the San Simeone-parish house in Zadar (doc 143, line 6305) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
