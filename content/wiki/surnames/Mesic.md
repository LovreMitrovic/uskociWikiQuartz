# Mesić

Lika surname. **[[Marko-Mesic-b????-Lika]]** led katoličko svećenstvo during the 1712 Lika–Krbava popis [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)

- **prete Marko Mesić** — imperial subject from Otočac; brought Lika subjects under imperial control; advanced toward Zvonigrad June 1699 (doc. 316); allegedly fought a duel at [[Lukovo]] with a Mitrović — likely Zaviša Janković (doc. 364, footnote) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Simone Bisaković (Simon Bisacouich)** — from Otočac, imperial subject, kinsman ("germano") of priest Mesić; 1699 witness in Sfakioto's Grimani-commission protocols (doc. 316) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
