# Civaleli

Zadar patrician house. Active throughout the corpus as Venetian envoys/colonelli.

## Bearers
- **Gregorio Civaleli** — 1647-07-21 already with command-rank — Foscolo cites him among the *capitani de Crovati et Albanesi* killed at the Diklo–Bokanjac fight (doc. 13). Surviving Civaleli active 1648-07-14 sent with kapetan Begna to console the Morlachi after Ribnik (doc. 37).
- **col. Civaleli** — 1658-06-28 sent by Bernardo to Petar Zrinski (doc. 106).
- **Civalelli** — 1655 witness to Smiljanić-Zubičević dote (doc. 97).
