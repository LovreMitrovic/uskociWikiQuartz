# Krivelari

Single attestation: **Bernardo Krivelari iz Šibenika** — 1684-01-24, holder of jewellery left in pawn by Turks (later raided by Filipjakov Morlachi) — list includes 4 gold rings, silver buttons, *susach* worth 5 reali, *sustach* worth 2.5 reali, gold *koncier* / *sotuf-luh* with 660 ounces of pearls (doc. 366).
