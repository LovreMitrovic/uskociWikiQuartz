# Dragović

Single attestation: **pop (sveštenik *rito greco*) Lazar Dragović** — 1683-12-26 envoy of 1500 Morlach families from **Kolasaz, Parčić, Krmpote, Rodaljica, Kosulovo Polje, Skradin, Kistanje, Perušić, Benkovac, Brkljača Polača, Mor Polača** who had occupied the kašteli **Raslina, Račevica, Dobri Dolac, Dejanović, Putičane, Skonotić, Vrana**. Asks Donà to assign them a director (doc. 358). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
