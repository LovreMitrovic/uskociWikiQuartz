# Atlagić

Bosnian beg family centered in [[Knin]]; "Kula Atlagića" is a major locality on the Knin–Drniš edge.

## Bearers
- **Mehmed-beg Atlagić** kapetan kninski — 1675-end-Aug forwards letters of the Bosnian *teftedar* Omer-paša and the Venetian bailo to provveditor Marin Zorzi; objects to Sandžak-beg Durakbegović trying to collect *travarina* on Žemunik/Poličnik/Islam lands (doc. 214). Letter in **Bosnian Cyrillic**.
- **Atlaghich** ("il Turco Atlagich") — 1662-02-18 in the Ottoman raid party that struck the Kotari (doc. 121). Capitano-rank.
- **Atlaghich** — captain of Knin 1677-02-26 sells castrati and grain to Zadar merchants Deanović, Ponte, Tomasoni for 500 cechini (doc. 234).
- **alaijmegh Atlagich** — killed at the **Cetina rotta** April 1666 — *"soggetto dal grido et auttorita ch' é ben nota a questi confini"* (doc. 148).
- **figliolo et nipote del Atlaghich con Omerizza suo di casa** — 1682 came from Belgrade to Knin (doc. 280).
- **case dell' Atlaghich** near Cliuno — 1684 the Bosnian alaj-beg encamps there with 1000 spahi (doc. 384).
- **Kula Atlagića** — Salihagini family attestation: 1680 *Jussuf e Suglio Pervichio dalla Cula dell' Atlaghich* (doc. 263) — Pervichi being the residential Atlagić sub-line.

## Notes
- Despite being adversaries of the Janković, the Atlagići maintained corresponding-business and even sheep-fattening contracts with Zadar burghers in peacetime.

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)

- **bassa Atlaghich** (paša Atlagić) — tried to bombard Zadar 1689; defeated by Zaviša Janković actions [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (docs. 224, 288).
- **Atlagić, commandant of Knin** — pre-1690s, oversaw disdar Mehmed of Zvonigrad (doc. 321 — Jusuf aga Derviševčić's testimony 1700) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Casa dei Atlaghich** (tower) — 1702 referenced near a village attacked by haiduks of Vid Petrović and Sinobad's nephew on Mt. Prolog [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc. 346).
