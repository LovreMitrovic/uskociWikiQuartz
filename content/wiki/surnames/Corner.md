# Corner / Cornaro

Venetian patrician house. The same family appears in Italian sources as **Corner** and Latinized as **Cornaro**.

## Bearers
- **Nicolò Corner** — Venetian senator, signatory of doc 22 (1684) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Zorzi Corner** — Venetian senator (doc 144 / line 4509) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- [[Girolamo-Cornaro-b????-Venice|Girolamo Cornaro kavalier]] — Provveditor general in Dalmazia et Albania from 1686; conducted the 1686 Sinj campaign and the 1688 Knin conquest; correspondent of dozens of docs from 1686–88; brother of the late kavalier Catterino Cornaro (doc 138) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
