# Capelletto / Kapelet

The italianized military epithet "Capelletto" (= soldier in the Cavalleria Capelletta, a Venetian Croatian-cavalry corps) became a hereditary surname for the family of Dragosav of Budin.

## Bearers
- **Dragosav Capelletto da Budin** — 1678 *capitan*; with kapetan Ivesko Jović of Ražanac among the four arbitri who divided the Sv. Juraj/Castel Venier lands (docs. 239, 240). 1679-05-16 — Vukčević matter: ordered not to harm Radojica Garković in retaliation for his slave Milovan being sold to Turks (doc. 247). Father of **Vukadin** and uncle of **Bogoje** Vukčević.
