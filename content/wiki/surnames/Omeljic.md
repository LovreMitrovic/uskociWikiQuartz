# Omeljić

## Bearers
- **Marko Omeljić (Omelich, Homeglich) da Čitluk** — 1647-12-17 signed (with X) the Petropolje uskakanje contract (doc. 18). 1648-11-03 listed as one of the harambaše who took *pieggiarija* for the slave-girl Zahire (doc. 48).
