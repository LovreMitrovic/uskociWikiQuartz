# Đerverščović

Demonymic surname (= "of/from Đevrske"). Possibly the same as **Smoljan Miković of Đevrske** (cf. [[Mikovic]]) under a different patronymic.

## Bearers
- **Smoljan Đerverščović da Đevrske** (Smoglian Gerverscovich) — 1653-01-16 co-signs Smiljanić proxy (doc. 70). 1671-06-21 with [[Milisav-Mocivuna|Milisav Močivuna]] and Luka Kožulović represents the *pravoslavni Morlaci* over the Sv. Ivan church in Spianata (doc. 193).
