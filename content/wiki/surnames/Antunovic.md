# Antunović

Bunjevac surname of [[Backa]]. Foremost member: **[[Ivan-Antunovic-b1815-Backa]]** (1815–1888), naslovni biskup, central figure of the 19th-c. Bunjevac narodni preporod through the *Razprava* (1882) and the *Bunjevačke i šokačke novine* (1870) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].
