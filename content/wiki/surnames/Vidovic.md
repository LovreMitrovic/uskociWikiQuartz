# Vidović

## Bearers in Desnica II
- **Mirko Vidović (Mirco Vidovich)** — from Gorica; killed by raiders night of 1693-11-04/05 with multiple wounds including arquebus shot to neck (doc. 268) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Bosizza (Božica) Vidović** — widow of Mirko Vidović; gave testimony 1693 (doc. 268) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Gabrilo (Gavrilo) Vidović** — from Koslovac; brother of Mirko; **forgave killers 1695-12-23** (doc. 268) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
