# Hrkač / Rkać

Counter-etnonym to [[Bunjevac]] in the [[Tromedja]] vlach milieu. The two together formed a confessional-onomastic dichotomy ([[Bunjevac]] = Catholic vlach :: Hrkač = Orthodox vlach) emerging on the Klis-Lika frontier around 1550 — at the core of Šarić's argument that **the Bunjevac etnik formed in interaction with its Orthodox-vlach neighbor** [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

## Surname attestations
1743 biskupska vizitacija registers Catholic surname-bearers (Pribinovići u Mostarskom blatu, Duvno, Stipanići, Prisoje):
- Rkać, Arkać, Arnjkaš.
- Near Ljubuški: Catholics with surname Rkač.
- Catholic rodovi Džajkići and Draškići near Ljubuški preserve oral tradition that they were once "Hrkač" coming from "od Vrljike" (Čulinović Konstantinović 1989:114).

The pattern matches that of [[Bunjevac]] — the surname attaches in the *opposite* confessional milieu to which it points (Catholic Hrkač = former Orthodox; Orthodox Bunjevac = former Catholic), as a marker of integration through inter-confessional movement.
