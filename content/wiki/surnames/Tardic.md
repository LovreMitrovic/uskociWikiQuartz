# Tardić (Gajdić)

Croatian-Šibenik lineage of [[Murat-beg-Gajdic-b????-Sibenik]] (formerly Tardić), the first sanjakbeg of [[Sandzak-Klis]]. Old surname Tardić; the Ottoman variant "Gajdić" appears alongside it in sources. Origin and Šibenik kin documented in Juran 2016 [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].
