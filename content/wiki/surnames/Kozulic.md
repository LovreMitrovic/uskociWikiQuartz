# Kožulić (Kožul)

Croatian noble family of Vlach origin, late 15th–early 16th c. owners of Kožulovo polje in present-day [[Ravni-Kotari]]. After Ottoman conquest, the area became the Husrev-beg vakuf and the family name persisted only in the toponym Kožlovac east of Benkovac [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].
