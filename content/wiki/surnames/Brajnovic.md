# Brajnović (Brainović / Brajinović)

Surname listed by Šarić among Zadar-hinterland anthroponyms with medieval-modern continuity [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Two distinct 17th-c. kin-groups appear in the corpus:

1. **Petropolje Brainović** — Franciscan clergy line.
2. **Zadar Brajinović** — settled Morlach family in the Spianata (Predgrađe Sv. Marka, future Borgo Erizzo / Arbanasi).

## Bearers (Desnica)
- **fra Šimun (Simon) Brainović** — 1647-12-17 capelan of Petropolje; signs the uskakanje contract on behalf of the conti (doc. 18).
- **fra Pavao (Paulo) Brainović** — Šimun's brother (doc. 18) — Petropolje pop.
- **fra Frane (Francesco) Brainović** — Šimun's brother, also Petropolje paroh (doc. 18).
- **q. Toma Brajinović** — d. before 1664; his estate held in escrow.
- **q. Frane Brajinović** (son of q. Toma) — first husband of Anka, who later remarried into the Smiljanić.
- **Anka** — widow of q. Frane Brajinović; mother of Šimun and Marko Brajinović + Smoljan Smiljanić (uterine).
- **Šimun Brajinović** (son of Frane & Anka, uterine half-brother of Smoljan Smiljanić) — 1664 was a slave (doc. 184); 1671 inheritance restored.
- **Marko Brajinović** — Šimun's brother (doc. 184).
- **Paolo Brainovich** of Malpaga — 1673-05-01 in the bandits-list of those who plundered the Klis caravan (doc. 204).

## Note on the Sv. Marko connection
The Brajinović house was on the **Spianata behind Sv. Marko** in Zadar — the same predgrađe later renamed *Borgo Erizzo* and now **Arbanasi**, named after the 1726 Albanian settlement. Pre-Albanian, this was the Bukovica-uskočka Morlach predgrađe.
