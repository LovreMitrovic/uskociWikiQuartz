# Vukobratović

Single attestation: **harambaša Ivanko (Ivanco) Vukobratović** *barjaktar (alfier) serdara Ilije Smiljanovića* — 1654-01-12, "di statura comune, mostacchi pel castagno, barba rasa, d' anni trentadue in circa" (b. ~1622) — pay 4 ducati/month assigned by Dolfin (doc. 79).
