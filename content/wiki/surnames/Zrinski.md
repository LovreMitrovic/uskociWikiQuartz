# Zrinski

Croatian magnatial house. [[Nikola-Zrinski-b????]] settled 25 Mušković Vlach families on his ribničko vlastelinstvo at [[Prilisce]] and Rosopajnik (Vukova Gorica) in 1544, granting them hereditary *libertini* status (deed 25 June 1544) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].
