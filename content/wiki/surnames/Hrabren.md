# Hrabren

Notable medieval Hercegovinian vlach lineage. Foremost member: **vojvoda [[Petar-Hrabren-b????-Hercegovina]]** of Donji Vlasi, ktitor of Hercegovinian Orthodox monasteries [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

The Hrabreni 1477 katun aglomeracija in nahija Blagaj (Aličić 1985) is one of the strongest pieces of evidence that the **Buna-river area was predominantly Orthodox-Vlach in the late 15th c.**, contrary to the constructed Bunjevac-from-Buna legend.
