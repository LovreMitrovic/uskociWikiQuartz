# Mirčetić

## Bearers
- **Frane (Francesco) Mirčetić** — 1684-01-04 leads 400 households of Zagorje Morlachi to settle in Vrana; counterpart of Matija Nakić (doc. 362). Brings them in with all family destroyed houses behind them.
- **Rade Mirčetić** — capo de Morlacchi 1684-02-22 (doc. 377) — among Frane's lieutenants.
- **Radovan Mitrichievich da Budin** — 1680-05-10 named as one of the six Morlachi who kidnapped Alil & Camera near Gračac (doc. 266); spelling variant of Mirčetić.

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
- [[Frane-Mircetic-b????-Vrana|Frane Mirčetić]] — continues to appear as harambaša of Vrana; signatory of the 1684 Vrana petitions to Mocenigo (docs 35, 36) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
