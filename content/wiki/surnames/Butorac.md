# Butorac (Butorčić)

Bunjevac surname. Originally **Butorčić** (patronymic) in the 1605 Lič migration, simplified to Butorac by 18th c. [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

## Attestations
- **1605** — among Krmpote families on the [[Krmpote-Migration-Lic-1605|first Lič migration]].
- **1702** — vojvoda [[Marko-Butorac-b????-Lika]] in [[Ribnik]] / Lika; antagonist of porkulab [[Milos-Starcevic-b1656-Veliki-Zitnik]] in the [[Ribnicka-Buna-1702|Ribnička buna]].
