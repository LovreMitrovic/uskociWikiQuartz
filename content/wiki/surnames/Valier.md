# Valier

Venetian patrician house. Two bearers in this source: a provveditor and a doge.

## Bearers
- **Pietro Valier** — Provveditor General estraordinario dell'armi from late 1684; presided over the disastrous Sinj campaign April 1685 (docs 41–72) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Silvestro Valier** — doge during early Morean War period; signs ducali on Dalmatian affairs [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].

## In Desnica II (1684–1749) — extended

- **Silvestro Valier (Doge 1694–1700)** — ducale **1696-01-30** making Jovan Sinobad cavalier of San Marco (doc. 283); ducale **1696-06-27** making Zaviša Janković cavalier of San Marco (doc. 288); 1697 ducal raised Zaviša's pay from 30 to 50 ducats/month (docs. 303, 306); 1698-05-18 dukal awarded soldier's pay to wounded priest Lazar Lazarić (doc. 310) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
