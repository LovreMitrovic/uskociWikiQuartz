# Tintić

Single attestation: **fra Petar (Piero) Tintić** — *capellano da Petropoglie*. 1683-12-09 envoy of harambaša Matija Nakić and the conti of Petropolje (1000 households) to provveditore Donà, asking either war declaration or guidance how to be protected from Venetian-side raids (doc. 349).
