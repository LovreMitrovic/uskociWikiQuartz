# Mocenigo

Venetian patrician house. Bearers in this source served as provveditor general and doge.

## Bearers
- [[Domenico-Mocenigo-b????-Venice|Domenico Mocenigo]] — Provveditor General estraordinario dell'armi in Dalmazia et Albania, arrived May 1684; main correspondent docs 1–43; eventual scapegoat for Sinj defeat April 1685 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- [[Alvise-Mocenigo-II-b????-Venice|Alvise Mocenigo II]] — doge from 1700; signs ducali on Dalmatian affairs through volume's later docs [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].

## In Desnica II (1684–1749) — extended

- **Mocenigo (the predecessor provveditor)** — **1684-07-15 terminacija** granted Ilija Mitrović 15 ducats/month (doc. 248) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Aluise (Alvise) Mocenigo** — provveditor generale of Dalmatia **late 1696–1702**, succeeding Daniel Dolfin; ordered Resep Budisalić's release Jan 1697 (docs. 300, 301); later Doge; dukal **1705-08-20** granted hereditary count title to Ilija, Nicolò, Stojan Mitrović-Janković (doc. 360); dukal of 22 March 1702 (doc. 342) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Cà Mocenigo** — Venetian residence in Venice; 1704 lodging of Franciscan Pavlo Vušković where Mattio Žabetić was seen during the Bukovica revolt aftermath (doc. 357) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
