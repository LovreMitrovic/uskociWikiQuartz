# Mandić

Sinj-area Muslim aga family. Two generations attested in this source.

## Bearers
- **Hasan-beg / Šain-aga Mandić** — one of the *principali di Sign* (notables of Sinj); his son Ivisa was a slave of the Radoš consortium, ransom 2000 reali + 4 horses (800 paid). Letters between father and son survive in docs 110, 111, 112 (Bosnian Cyrillic in Vol. II). The Marušić letter (doc 110) addresses him as the captor of vojvoda Ivan Marušić (docs 65, 68, 110, 111) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Ivisa / Hasan-beg Mandić (the younger)** — son of Šain-aga; captive of the Radoš consortium (docs 65, 68); his letters from prison are in docs 111, 112 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
