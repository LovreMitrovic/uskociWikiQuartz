# Vlastelica

Single-cluster attestation: **Marko Vlastelica** — 1684-02-22 named among the post-uskakanje capi at Vrana (doc. 377). 1684-02-28 the same name appears in the Knin captain Mustaj-beg Firdusović's letter to *Morlacchi sudditi turcheschi* who *were forced* to defect (doc. 380): *"Marco Vulastelizza et a Martin Despot et a Rubel et a Bili Vuch et a Blaich et a Piero e Duijam Zalich"* — i.e. these are the named leaders the Knin Turks knew by name.
