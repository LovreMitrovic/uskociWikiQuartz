# Černičić (Cernicich)

Ottoman beg family of Knin/Krka/Lika.

## Bearers in Desnica II
- **Mehmed-beg Černičić** — sangiacco of Krka, Lika and Krbava (late 17th c.) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc. 224 context).
- **Mahmut-beg Černičić** — son of Mehmed-beg; disdar of Knin [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].

## Notes
- Distinct from the [[Cernizza|Crnica]] (Cernizza) of Tinj — the Ottoman *Cernicich* sangiacco line is a different family despite spelling overlap.
