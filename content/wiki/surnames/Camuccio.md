# Camuccio

## Bearers
- **ingegner Camuccio** — Venetian military engineer; designed siege works at Sinj April 1685 with 12 scaling ladders (docs 60, 61); supervised the 1686 Sinj-Salona campaign and continued through the Knin conquest 1688 (doc 144) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
