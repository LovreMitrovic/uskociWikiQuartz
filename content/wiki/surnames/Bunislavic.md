# Bunislavić

Medieval vlach katun-bearing surname in eastern Hum, 15th c. Attested 1406 in [[Vlatko-Bunislavic-b1406]]'s katun (Hrabak 1981:188) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]]. Bun-stem onomastic family with [[Buncic]], [[Bunovic]], Bunuševci.
