# Dobrić

## Bearers
- **harambaša Jovan Dobrić** — 1648-07-17: *"Uskoci iz Dobropoljaca naseljeni u Ljubču od 60 ljudi izgubili dvojicu"* at Ribnik (doc. 39).
- **harambassà Ivan Dobrievich** — 1663-05-08 leads the guard with men of [[Parcic|Parčić]], Žemunik, Bokanjac and Malpaga at the Otres pass (doc. 130).
- **Milja Dobrić** — 1684-03-01 named *capitano* of [[Radovin]] by Donà (doc. 381).
