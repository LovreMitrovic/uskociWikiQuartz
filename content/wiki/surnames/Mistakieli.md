# Mistakieli

Single attestation: **dottor Nicolò Mistakieli** — 1656-06-15 medic in Zadar, sued Smoljan Smiljanić for treatment fees (43 reali) for repeatedly tending the wounds of Smoljan's uncles Ilija and Filip Smiljanić (doc. 103). Probably Greek-of-Latin-rite Zadar surname.
