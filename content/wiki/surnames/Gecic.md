# Gečić

## Bearers
- **knez Đurđe (Zorzi) Gečić** + **knez Ilija Knežević** — 1683-10-25 negotiated the Turks' withdrawal from Vrana (doc. 323).
- **Žorži (Zorzi) Gečić, harambaša** — 1684-02-22 capo of one of the Vrana settlement families (doc. 377). 1683-12-26 also in pop Lazar Dragović's roster (doc. 358).
- **Zuanne Giechich** — 1684-02-22 capo, listed alongside Vukadin Zepina, Petar Derghich, Rade Mirčetić, Pavao Lahis, Marko Klarić, Sava, Marko Vlastelica, Ivica Blasević, Goreta (doc. 377).
