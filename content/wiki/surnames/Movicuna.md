# Močivuna

Morlach lineage that *uskočio* from Ottoman village **Jakorševci** "molti anni" before 1669 (Priuli's terminacija doc. 167) and settled in **Bokanjac** above Zadar (Spianata). Multi-generational pravoslavni line — christened at Sv. Ilija Zadar.

## Bearers
- **Vuk (Vučko) Močivuna** — d. 1666-05-01 at Zečevo (doc. 151, glagolska zabeleška from Filipjakov: "poginu dobri junak Vučko Močivuna" — in a battle in which 73 Kotarane were killed). Pay 4 ducati/month assigned 1653-10-22 by Dolfin (doc. 76 — "di statura bassa, giovane sbarbato d' anni vinti due in circa" → b. ~1631) for beheading a Turk and presenting the head to the provveditore. Earlier 1650-09-20 same pay, transferred 1666-05-21 to brother Vučen (doc. 155).
- **Vučen Močivuna** — brother of Vuk; 4 ducati/month inherited 1666-05-21. Distinct from a *Vučin Močivuna* who held two Turkish slaves (Vuk Cusat and Vido Pagin) on the Spianata 1665 (doc. 146).
- **Vučko Močivuna** — likely the same as Vuk; harambaša 1662-04-12 (doc. 125) — Korner forbids him assembling raids without written permission.
- **Milisav (Milissa, Milosav) Močivuna** — *uskočio* "many years before" 1669 from Jakorševci (doc. 167); harambaša; pay 4 ducati + peso di biscotto/month from 1669-10-10 by Priuli. Wife **Kata** baptised slave-girl Maria 1677-01-07 at Sv. Ilija (doc. 231). Son **Matija (Matio)** baptised 1663-07-12 stile vecchio (doc. 134); compare *Sapissa Giancovichia* and comare *Anizza Murlaca*. With Smoljan Đevrsković and Luka Kožulović 1671-06-21 represented the *pravoslavni Morlaci* against the Trećoredci re Sv. Ivan church in Spianata (doc. 193). Bokanjac capitan 1684-03-01 (doc. 381).
- **Todor Močivuna** — d. in Cetina (the 1666 conflict); widow Anica relicta (doc. 164, 1668-12-23). Children: son **Dragoslav** + 3 daughters in *pupillar età*. Anica granted 1 peso di biscotto/month for support of children (doc. 164).
- **Anica (Anizza) udovica Toda Močivune** — 1668; possibly the same as the comare *Anizza Murlaca* at the 1663 baptism of Mate (doc. 134).
- **Dragoslav Močivuna** — son of Todor (1668).
