# Vlasi Uskoplja

Vlach katun from bosanska nahija Uskoplje (1516: 24 dzemats, 248 houses), of older Hercegovinian origin (Nikšić, Banjani). 4 dzemats migrated c. 1528 to area around Plavno, Knin, Kosovo, and the springs of Cetina; significant numbers later joined the uskok migration through Bihać to Žumberak under vojvoda [[Vuk-Popovic-b????]] [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].
