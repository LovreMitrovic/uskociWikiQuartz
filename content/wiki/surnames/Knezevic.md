# Knežević

## Bearers
- **Vranje Knežević** — 1648-07-17: *"Uskoci iz [[Parcic|Parčića]] naseljeni ispod Poljica, od sedamdeset ljudi izgubili osam"* at Ribnik (doc. 39).
- **Mirko Knežević** — 1663-05-08 sudac of [[Posedarje]] (doc. 131).
- **Radosav Knežević** — 1673-05-01 *piezzo* (guarantor) for Vuk Cusat (doc. 204).
- **Martino Knežević** — 1673-05-01 same source.
- **Ilija Knežević** (knez) — 1683-10-25 capo de Morlacchi sudditi turcheschi who negotiated with the retreating Vrana Turks for 15 cechini, 2 barette, 2 manzi escort fee (doc. 323).
- **Đurađ (Zorzi) Knežević dalla Cula** — 1683 (probably same as Đurađ Đekić of doc. 358).

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)

- **Stefan Knežević (Steffano Cnesevich)** of Castel Venier (Vinjerac) — 1694-04-18 ordered to return goods stolen by Filippo, former servant of Orsola Troić (doc. 273) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Tadia Knežević** — captain of Castel Venier (Vinjerac); witness in Sfakioto's 1700 Grimani-commission protocols (doc. 321) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
