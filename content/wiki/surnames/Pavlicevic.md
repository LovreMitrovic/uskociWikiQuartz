# Pavličević

Bunjevac surname; among the 16 reconstructed families of the [[Krmpote-Migration-Lic-1627|second (1627) Krmpote migration]] (Pavličević 1989) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]] (medium confidence). Notably the **historian Dragutin Pavličević** (1989 study of Krmpote migrations) shares the surname.
