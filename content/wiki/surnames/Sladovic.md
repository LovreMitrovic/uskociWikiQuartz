# Sladović

Catholic Vlach (Bunjevac) lineage — third of the three core Bunjevac katuns named in the 1477 defter (with [[Krmpotic]] and [[Vojnic]]). Least documented of the three.

## 1477 attestation
Hercegovina defter, **nahija Mostar**: džemat of [[Milosav-sin-Sladica-b????-Mostar]], 19 houses. Unusually for the 1477 popis, the džemat is organized **by villages** (Podci = [[Podaca]] in Makarsko primorje; Igre = [[Igrane]] on Biokovo; [[Kozica]] in Vrgoračka krajina) rather than by transhumant pastures. This suggests either "vlahizirano" sedentary peasants given Vlach status, or a fully territorialized Vlach katun [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

## 17th c. — westward migration
Early 17th c.: present across [[Bukovica]] and [[Podgorje]] under vojvoda [[Gvozden-Sladovic-b????-Mostar]]; group known as **"Gvozdenovi ljudi"**. **1605**: 20 families joined the Krmpote migration to [[Lic]] — surnames Balinović, Brošković, Ilnić, Karanović, Lovrić, Martinović, Mihovilović, Mikulić, Sladović, Veljanić, Vilenica.

After 1605 the group's fate intertwined with the Krmpote branch. Some Sladovići joined the [[Senjska-Kapetanija]] garrison; a Senj-line branch later settled in [[Licko-Lesce]] (Otočac). The historian **[[Manojlo-Sladovic-b????-Lika]]** (1856 *Pověsti biskupijah Senjske i Modruške*) probably descends from this branch [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].
