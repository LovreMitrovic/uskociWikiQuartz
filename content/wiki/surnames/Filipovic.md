# Filipović

[[Franjo-Filipovic-b????-Zagreb]], canon of Zagreb, converted to Islam in 1578 — cited by Šarić alongside other 16th-c. cases of noble or clerical defection across the Tromeđa [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. By the mid-17th c. the Filipovići appear as a senior Bosnian beg-family; whether they descend from the 1578 Zagreb canon is conjectural but not implausible.

## Bearers
- **[[Franjo-Filipovic-b????-Zagreb]]** — Zagreb canon, Muslim convert (1578).
- **Jusuf alaj-beg Filipović** — Bosnian alaj-beg; 1652-12 captured by Šibenik Morlachi at Duare (docs. 68, 69 [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]); ransomed 1659 in Danilo via *baratto* for the Venetian patrician **Daniele Morosini** (doc. 264 footnote — establishing the Filipović–Morosini friendship). Later beheaded by Stojan Janković: per Stojan's 1670 supplica "Recep aga Filipovich" cut down in Kotari (docs. 168, 170).
- **Reep-aga Filipović** — alternative spelling of the same beheaded individual (Stojan's 1668 attestato, doc. 168).
- **Mehmed-beg Zain Filipović** — 1680-05-07 writes to provveditor Korner from Glamoč asking help to find his grandson, son of Jusuf (doc. 264). Father of Jusuf.
- **Mehmed-beg Filipović** — 1682-03 writes from Glamoč about a man captured on the Sukošan road (doc. 281).
- **Filipovich kuće (kod Cliuna)** — 1684 Bashas of Bosnia lodge there before the campaign against the Morlachi (docs. 372, 384).
- **Ferhat-paša Filipović** — 1648 paša prior to the post-Klis-fall attack on Kotari (doc. 19).

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
- **alaj-beg Filipović of Glamoč** — 1684 his palaces and ten surrounding villages are burned by Janković and Smiljanić in the August 1684 Glamoč raid (docs 27, 28) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **alaj-beg Filipović of Bosnia** — September 1686 newly named *alajbeg of Bosna* (with title *capigi*); arrived at Livno together with the new Zvornik pasha Bastić to repair the houses and towers burned in the first Livno raid; Janković and Smiljanić then carried out the second Livno raid burning everything within 50 miles (doc 141, 7 September 1686) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
