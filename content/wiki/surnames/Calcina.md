# Kalčin (Calcina)

## Bearers
- **kavalier dottore Grgur (Gregorio) Calcina** — 1680-06-07 sopraintendent of the Liga at Visočani (doc. 270). 1682-11-06 sopraintendent at Zaravecchia Liga (doc. 295, 297).
- **Nicolo Calcina** — 1682-11-06 also nominated (doc. 296).

## Note
- See also existing surname [[Kalcina|Kalčina]] entry for medieval-modern continuity background.

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)

- **Pietro Calčina (Pietro Calcina / Petar Kalčina)** — appointed **governor of pertinenza 2 (Posedarje)** by Cornaro in the 1689 Liga reform (doc. 224); reported defeat of Popržen to capitano Papafava (doc. 225). 1698 commission member (doc. 312). 1701 captain; claimed to have been invested 1675/1680 in *cancelleria illirica*, opposed Lomazzi's appointment (doc. 327). Previously served as cavalier in Levant under generals. [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **dottor cavalier Gregorio Calčina (Grgur Kalčina)** — late by Feb 1694; cancelliere of Illyrian and Serbian letters in Zadar; succeeded by Lomazzi (doc. 272). Father of Pietro per doc. 327 ("il quondam signor kavalier Calcina"). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
