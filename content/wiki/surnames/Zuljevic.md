# Žuljević

## Bearers
- **don Pavao (Pava) Žuljević** — *redovnik* (priest) from the Poljica province; volunteered to enter slavery at Sinj as a hostage substitute for vojvoda Ivan Marušić in the cell of Šain-aga Mandić, while Marušić travelled to discuss his ransom with the *generale* Valier (doc 110, undated, Marušić's Bosnian-Cyrillic letter to Stipan Garković) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
