# Milohanić (Milokanić)

Catholic Vlach kindred with branches on both sides of the Habsburg-Ottoman border [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Likely linked to medieval Milohanić village near Korlat (Banadego). [[Nikola-Milokanic-b1521]] — Istria-born former Muslim ("Osman"), testified 1591 about ~50-year cycle Istria → Ottoman → Istria with 184 kin. [[Grgur-Milohanovic-b????-Nova-Vas]] founded Miljacki (Zemunik Gornji) 1561, retreated 1570 with 40 families to Žbandalj near Poreč.
