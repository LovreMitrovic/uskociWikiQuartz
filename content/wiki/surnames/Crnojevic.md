# Crnojević

## Bearers in Desnica II
- **Patriarch Arsenije III Crnojević (Arsenio Zarnoevich)** — Serbian Patriarch; sheltered in Habsburg lands; in 1693 appointed Teodosije Mitrović his exarch and vicegerent (docs. 262, 266); letter issued 1693-01-10 (old style) at Lepavina monastery [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
