# Radmilović

Bukovica surname attested in [[Zegar|Žegar]].

## Bearers attested
- **[[Mihajlo-Radmilovic-b????-Zegar|Mihajlo Radmilović]]** — Morlach who escaped from Žegar to Venetian territory; goods stolen in transit by villagers of Razanci ([[Marko-Matak-b????-Razanci|Marko Matak]]) and Radovin ([[Ivan-Brkljaca-b????-Radovin|Ivan Brkljača]] and [[Giacomo-Bistric-b????-Radovin|Giacomo Bistrić]]); ordered restored by Mocenigo (doc 31, 1684). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]

## Notes
- Per Desnica's 1951 editorial footnote (to doc 115), the Radmilović surname is **still present in [[Zegar|Žegar]]** today (1951). Together with Zelić, Vukčević, and Nanić, the families "came in 1648 with the Jankovićs to Budim near Posedarje and during the Morean War many returned to Lika; Budim is today (1951) ruins".
