# Failed Sinj Attempt under Marcello (October 1684)

Provveditore **[[Alvise-Marcello-b????-Venice|Alvise Marcello]]**, supported by **[[Antun-Bortulacic-b????-Zadar|Antun Bortulačić]]**, mounted a brief siege of [[Sinj]] in October 1684 but withdrew without result (doc 42, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

The attempt foreshadowed the much larger Venetian disaster at [[Sinj-Battle-1685|Sinj in April 1685]] and the eventual conquest in [[Sinj-Conquest-1686|September 1686]].
