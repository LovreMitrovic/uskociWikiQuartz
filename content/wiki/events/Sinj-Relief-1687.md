# Relief of Sinj from Atlagić's Siege (April 1687)

**[[Girolamo-Cornaro-b????-Venice|Cornaro]]** and **[[Antonio-Zen-b????-Venice|Antonio Zen]]** marched to relieve [[Sinj]] in April 1687 from a siege mounted by **[[Mehmed-Atlagic-b????-Knin|Mehmed Atlagić]]** as the new Bosnian pasha; Atlagić withdrew across the [[Cetina]] (doc 169, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

The relief preserved the Venetian gain from [[Sinj-Conquest-1686|September 1686]] and freed the army to launch the summer raids that ended in the death of [[Stojan-Jankovic-b1635-Zegar|Stojan Janković]] at Duvno (doc 169, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
