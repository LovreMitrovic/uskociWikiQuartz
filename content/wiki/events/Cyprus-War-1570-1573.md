# Cyprus War (1570–1573)

Ottoman capture of [[Zemunik]], Učitelja Vas, and [[Tinj]] in northern Dalmatia. Triggered new Vlach defections to Venetian Dalmatia and Istria, though land shortage at Zadar limited reception [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. After the war, [[Ferhat-beg-Sokolovic-b????]] uvakufio Tinj for the Zemunik vakuf and brought western-Hercegovinian Vlach colonists ([[Vojnic]], later "Bunjevci"). Period sees the final replacement of "Vlasi Istre" identity by new Vlach demographic streams from southeastern Dinarides.
