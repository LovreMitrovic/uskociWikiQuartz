# Lika Migration under Janković (May 1685)

**[[Stojan-Jankovic-b1635-Zegar|Stojan Janković]]** brought 70 [[Lika]] families — about 900 souls — onto Venetian territory under the authority of provveditore **[[Polo-Michiel-b????-Venice|Polo Michiel]]** (doc 70, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

The transfer continued the trickle of Lika refugees that swelled into the Imperial-Venetian competition for settlers behind the [[Lika-Settlement-Census-1685|1685 settlement census]] (doc 70, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
