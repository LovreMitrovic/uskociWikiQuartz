# Bjelaj and Vakuf Raid (early September 1685)

**[[Stojan-Jankovic-b1635-Zegar|Stojan Janković]]** with **[[Tartaglia-officer-b????|Tartaglia]]**, Geliseo and Razzetini destroyed the Bjelaj fortress and its Vakuf in early September 1685 and brought back about 500 Orthodox musketeers from Srb to Venetian territory (docs 97, 98, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

The raid was a precursor to the larger [[Bjelaj-Migration-1692|1692 Bjelaj migration]] under conti Đurić, Pirić and Zorić (docs 97, 98, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
