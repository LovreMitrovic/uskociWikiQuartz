# Glamoč–Grahovo Campaign (August 1684)

Some 5,000 Morlachs under **[[Stojan-Jankovic-b1635-Zegar|Janković]]**, **[[Smoljan-Smiljanic-b????-Bukovica|Smiljanić]]** and **[[Antun-Bortulacic-b????-Zadar|Bortulačić]]** raided [[Glamocko-Polje|Glamoč]], burning the palaces of alajbeg **Filipović** and killing 50 Turks (docs 27, 30, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

On the return march they ambushed two Bosnian-Hercegovinian pashas (one identified as Mazut-paša) at Grahovo: 150 Turks were killed including 30 court agas and two "Bugliuch bassé"; one pasha was wounded; Sinovčić cut down the pasha's kjehaja (docs 27, 30, 32, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]). Smiljanić was lightly wounded in the hand; no Morlach deaths reported (docs 27, 30, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
