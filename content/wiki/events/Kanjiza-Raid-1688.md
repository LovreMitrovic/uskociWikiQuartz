# Kotarci Raid on Kanjiža (April 1688)

About 300 Kotarci under **Kalinić** rode out from [[Korlat]] in April 1688 against Kanjiža and took 50 captives, 4,000 small animals and 500 large (doc 202, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

On the return the raiders were intercepted by 100 cavalry under **Dukajtar** and lost 15–20 dead (doc 202, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
