# Venetian Conquest of Koroni (1685)

Venetian forces took Koroni (Coron) in the Morea in 1685, an early signal victory of the Morean War (doc 100, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

The success in the Morea was the strategic counterpart to the Dalmatian campaigns of [[Stojan-Jankovic-b1635-Zegar|Stojan Janković]] and the four Kotari capi the same season (doc 100, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
