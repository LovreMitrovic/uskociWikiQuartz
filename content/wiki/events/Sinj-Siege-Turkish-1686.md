# Ottoman Siege of Sinj (April 1686)

A Turkish force besieged Venetian-held [[Sinj]] in April 1686; the garrison held off the assault, and **Bose Milković** mounted an ambush against the besiegers (doc 133, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

Sinj remained Venetian and was secured outright in the [[Sinj-Conquest-1686|conquest of September 1686]] under [[Girolamo-Cornaro-b????-Venice|Cornaro]] (doc 133, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
