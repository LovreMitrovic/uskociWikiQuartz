# Ilija's Raid on Bilaj (June 1687)

**[[Ilija-Mitrovic-b????-Zegar|Ilija Mitrović]]** led 800 men, including 400 cavalry, in a raid on the Bilaj tower in June 1687; the captain of Bilaj lost his brother and son in the engagement (doc 170, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

The raid paralleled his brother [[Zavisa-Jankovic-b????-Zegar|Zaviša]]'s [[Zavisa-Rebac-Raid-1687|Rebac raid]] and prefigured the disastrous family expedition to Duvno two months later in which their elder brother [[Stojan-Jankovic-b1635-Zegar|Stojan]] would be killed (doc 170, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
