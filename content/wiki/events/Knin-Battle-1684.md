# Battle of Knin (2 June 1684)

Roughly 2,000 Kotari Morlachs under **[[Stojan-Jankovic-b1635-Zegar|Stojan Janković]]**, **[[Smoljan-Smiljanic-b????-Bukovica|Smoljan Smiljanić]]** and **[[Antun-Bortulacic-b????-Zadar|Antun Bortulačić]]** fell on the [[Knin]] garrison; about 120 Turks were killed and ~90 horses captured (doc 9, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

Janković was lightly wounded. The action was the first joint sortie of the four Kotari capi after Vrana's defection and signalled an offensive Morlach posture in the opening months of the Morean War (doc 9, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
