# Pazin Peasant Revolt (1570)

Peasant uprising in Pazin County involving ~2,000 subjects, triggered by new wine and feudal taxes imposed across Kranjska, Kras, Vipava, and Pazin [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Pazin captain [[Gaspar-Mauric-b????]] reported (4 Sept 1571) that many "Ćići" emigrated back to Ottoman territory ("in die Thurkey"), where authorities resettled them — likely in former Vlasi Istre nahijas in the [[Sandzak-Klis]].
