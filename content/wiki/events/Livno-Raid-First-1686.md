# First Livno Raid (mid-July 1686)

About 5,000 infantry and 1,500 cavalry under the four Kotari serdars — **[[Stjepan-Posedarski-b????|Posedarski]]**, **[[Antun-Bortulacic-b????-Zadar|Bortulačić]]**, **[[Stojan-Jankovic-b1635-Zegar|Janković]]** and **[[Smoljan-Smiljanic-b????-Bukovica|Smiljanić]]** — struck the Livno field in mid-July 1686 (doc 136, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

Some 400+ Turks were killed and roughly 1,800 houses burned across the polje (doc 136, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]). A second, smaller Livno raid followed two months later — see [[Livno-Raid-Second-1686]].
