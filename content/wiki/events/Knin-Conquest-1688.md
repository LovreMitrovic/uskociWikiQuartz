# Conquest of Knin (29 August – 11 September 1688)

Provveditor Generale **[[Girolamo-Cornaro-b????-Venice|Girolamo Cornaro]]**'s 15-day siege of **[[Knin]]**: assault on the city 2 September; siege of the inner fortresses; surrender on 11 September by **Mehmed pasha [[Mehmed-Atlagic-b????-Knin|Atlagić]]** "to discretion" (docs 213–220, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

Atlagić, his son **Mehmed-beg Atlagić**, and his nephew **Alibeg Atlagić** ("la più brava spada") were all captured.

The conquest secured the Krka–Cetina interior and the entire frontage from Knin to Vrlika. **[[Vrlika]]** capitulated without a shot to colonnello **Fenzi** in early September 1688 (doc 216); **Bože Cerniza** was placed as garrison commander. The conquest of Knin and Vrlika together complete the Venetian recovery of the Cetina–Krka basin begun with [[Sinj-Conquest-1686|Sinj 1686]].

## Wider context (August 1688)
- Imperial victory at Brod / crossing of the Sava (early Aug 1688) — Bosnian pasha defeated
- Imperial siege of Gradiška, ~12,000 Imperial troops near Belgrade (doc 211)
- The combined Holy League pressure made Bosnian relief of Knin impossible
