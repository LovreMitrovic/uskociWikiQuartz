# Mikiel's Knin Reconnaissance (February 1685)

A 600-strong Venetian force under provveditore Mikiel, with **[[Stojan-Jankovic-b1635-Zegar|Stojan Janković]]** and **[[Smoljan-Smiljanic-b????-Bukovica|Smoljan Smiljanić]]**, burned outlying houses around [[Knin]] while only 8 Turkish horses were watering at the river; 4 Turks were captured (doc 51, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

The reconnaissance demonstrated that [[Knin]] was vulnerable but a full assault on it would not come until [[Knin-Conquest-1688|1688]] (doc 51, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
