# Varcar Vakuf Raid (September 1687)

About 4,000 Morlachs penetrated six days deep into Bosnia toward Varcar Vakuf in September 1687, causing great destruction; **[[Zavisa-Jankovic-b????-Zegar|Zaviša Janković]]** was wounded in the action (doc 179, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

The raid was the family's first major action after the [[Stojan-Jankovic-Death-Duvno-1687|death of Stojan at Duvno]] in late August (doc 179, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
