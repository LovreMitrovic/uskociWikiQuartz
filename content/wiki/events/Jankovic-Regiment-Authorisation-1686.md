# Authorisation of Stojan Janković's oltramarino regiment (7 Nov 1686)

By **dukal of 7 November 1686** (doc 148, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]), Doge **[[Marc-Antonio-Giustinian-b????-Venice|Marc'Antonio Giustinian]]** authorised a 500-strong **oltramarino regiment** under the command of **kavalier [[Stojan-Jankovic-b1635-Zegar|Stojan Janković]]**, with his older son **[[Nikola-Jankovic-b????-Zadar|Nikola]]** designated as colonnello, and younger son **[[Konstantin-Jankovic-b????-Zadar|Konstantin]]** designated as head of an attached cavalry company.

By 21 November 1686 Janković had assembled 300 men toward the regiment in Zadar (doc 158). Captains of the regiment included [[Nikola-Blagojevic-b????-Sibenik|Nikola Blagojević]] (Šibenik), [[Jovan-Bozic-b????-Zadar|Jovan Božić]] (Zadar), and others recommended by vice-commissario [[Antonio-Molin-b????-Venice|Antonio Molin]] (doc 158).

The regiment marked a major institutional elevation of the Janković family from harambaše/sardari of Morlach contingents to commanders of a regular paid Venetian unit.

## Notes
- Stojan was killed at [[Duvno]] in August 1687 (doc 174); Nikola died in the Levant ~1687/early 1688; only Konstantin survived among the named sons.
