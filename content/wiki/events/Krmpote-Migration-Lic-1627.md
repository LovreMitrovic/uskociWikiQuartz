# Second Krmpote migration to Lič (1627)

Second wave of Krmpote families from Ottoman territory onto Habsburg [[Lic]] [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]]. No surviving register; family list reconstructed by Pavličević 1989 by comparison with the 1605 settler population (any 1712 Lič Bunjevac surname not in the 1605 list).

## Reconstructed family list (16 families)
[[Blazevic]], [[Deranja]], [[Filipovic]], [[Jovanovic]], [[Krpan]], [[Pavelic]], [[Pavlicevic]], [[Pericic]], [[Prpic]], [[Radosevic]], [[Starcevic]], [[Sojat]], [[Tomic]], Tomičić, [[Tomljanovic]], [[Vukelic]].

## Confidence
**Medium** — reconstruction, not register-direct. The list is methodologically sound but no 1627 document survives.
