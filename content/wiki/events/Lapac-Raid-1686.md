# Lapac Raid (early January 1686)

**[[Stojan-Jankovic-b1635-Zegar|Stojan Janković]]** led 1,250 Morlachs into the Lapac region in early January 1686, burning the **Pašić palazzo** at Boričevac and capturing **Idris-aga of [[Bihac|Bihać]]** (doc 118, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

The raid extended the Morlach offensive far into Bosnian Krajina territory in mid-winter and was followed by larger campaigns deeper into Bosnia later that year (doc 118, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
