# Vlach Return Migration to Klis Sanjak (1528–1530)

Under the Ottoman *istimâlet* policy of [[Gazi-Husrev-beg]] and [[Murat-beg-Gajdic-b????-Sibenik]], several hundred Vlach families returned from Habsburg Istria and Kras to their old habitats in Podgorje, Bukovica, and Ravni Kotari [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Recorded in the Ottoman mufassal defter 1528–1530 (TR-BOA-TTD No. 157) as **Eflâkân-i vilayet-i Istra** ("Vlasi zemlje Istre") — 640 households across 9 nahijas (Ostrovica, Zečevo, Podgorje, Zrmanja, Bukovica, Karin, Obrovac, Benković, Kličevac), in 16 dzemats. Peak settlement before subsequent decline.
