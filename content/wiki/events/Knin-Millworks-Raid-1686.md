# Knin Millworks Raid (June 1686)

**[[Stojan-Jankovic-b1635-Zegar|Stojan Janković]]** led a raid that destroyed 25–29 mills along the Krka above [[Knin]] together with a defensive tower in June 1686 (doc 135, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

Crippling the Knin mills targeted the garrison's grain supply and prepared the ground for the eventual [[Knin-Conquest-1688|conquest of Knin]] two summers later (doc 135, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
