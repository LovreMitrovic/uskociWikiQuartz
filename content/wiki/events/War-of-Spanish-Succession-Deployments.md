# War of the Spanish Succession — Venetian Morlach Deployments to Italy (1701–1714)

During the War of the Spanish Succession (1701–1714) Venice redeployed Dalmatian Morlach formations to Italy, including units led by **[[Zavisa-Jankovic-b????-Zegar|Zaviša Janković]]** and **[[Lazaro-Smiljanic-b????-Bukovica|Lazar Smiljanić]]** (doc text, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

The Italian deployments stripped the Kotari frontier of seasoned commanders just as the Habsburg-Venetian rivalry over Lika and the [[Bukovica-Revolt-1704|1704 Bukovica revolt]] heated up (doc text, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
