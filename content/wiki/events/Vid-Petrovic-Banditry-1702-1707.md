# Vid Petrović's Banditry on Mt. Prolog (1702–1707)

**[[Vid-Petrovic-b????-Kosovo|Vid Petrović]]** of Kosovo (near Sinj) operated as a haiduk on Mt. Prolog from 1702 onward, raiding both Ottoman and Venetian subjects (doc text, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

He was killed in 1707 at Bruvno by the agent **Canagetti**; the son of conte **Obradović of Bruvno** was killed alongside him (doc text, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
