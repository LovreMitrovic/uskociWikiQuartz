# Skoplje (Uskoplje) Orthodox Migration (November 1687)

In November 1687 the Orthodox priest **Đurađ** led 26 families together with 50 armed men out of the **Durakbegović tower** in Uskoplje (Hercegovina) onto Venetian territory (doc 183, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

The settlers founded the Orthodox enclaves at Dicmo, Biletić and Broćanac on the Venetian-Cetina frontier — the source's footnote explicitly traces these communities to the 1687 transfer (doc 183, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]). Companion event to the Catholic [[Rama-Franciscan-Migration-1687|Rama migration]] of October 1687.
