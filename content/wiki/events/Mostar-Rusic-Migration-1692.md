# Mostar / Rašić Migration to Makarska (1692)

**Ivan Rašić** led roughly 200 souls (about 25 families) out of the [[Mostar]] area onto Venetian territory in 1692; they settled at Zavojane, Stigli and Hadži Lukovo in the Makarska hinterland (doc text, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

Companion to the [[Bjelaj-Migration-1692|Bjelaj migration]] of the same year and to the larger [[Brochno-Goranci-Migration-1693-94|Brochno–Blato–Goranci transfer]] of 1693–1694 (doc text, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
