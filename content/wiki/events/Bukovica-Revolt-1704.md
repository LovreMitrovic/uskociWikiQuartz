# Bukovica Revolt (1702–1704)

A revolt in the Bukovica part of the Zadar contado against tax-collection abuse, decima reform, and troop quartering, suppressed in summer 1704 by Provveditor Generale **[[Marin-Zane-b????-Venice|Marin Zane]]**.

## Origins (1702)
Per pop **[[Petar-Jagodic-Kuridza-b1666-Bukovica|Petar Jagodić-Kuridža]]**'s long deposition (doc 389, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]):
- Colonel **[[Antonio-Canagetti-b????|Antonio Canagetti]]** (dragoons) demanded 300 zecchini from the parish of San Pietro in Bokovica via mandate of provveditor **Zane**.
- The commune denied the debt; Jagodić drafted a four-point petition for the commune asking instead:
  1. Voiding of the 300-zecchini claim
  2. Restitution of 100 manzi previously usurped by colonel **[[Posedarski|Posedarski]]**
  3. Reform of decima collection (decimario refusing in-cash payment, requiring instead a 60-dragoon entourage at communal cost)
  4. Permission to pay decima in wheat at communal expense delivery
- Provveditor Zane assented in part; ordered Posedarski to pay the 100 manzi back and Canagetti to drop the 300 zecchini claim.

## Escalation
Posedarski, summoned, lured 30 capi to **Zerno** on pretence of payment, then refused to pay and threatened to hang a dozen of them. The commune resolved to send three capi to Venice via the dominante.

## Suppression (1704)
- **Banishment** with capital sentence of three commune capi and pop Jagodić.
- Capi fled to imperial Lika.
- **[[Mattio-Zabetic-b????-Zaravecchia|Mattio Žabetić]]** of Zaravecchia arrested in Venice 1704.
- Pop Jagodić arrested under successor provveditor **[[Vincenzo-Vendramin-b????-Venice|Vincenzo Vendramin]]**, taken to Klin (Cliuno?), then Venetian *Pozzi* prison.
- The three banished capi liberated 1715 in War of Spanish Succession amnesty.

## Resolution
- 1746-03-29 — Pop Jagodić, blind, deaf, and crippled by ~40 years' imprisonment, freed by State Inquisitors and entrusted to the Greek **[[Petar-Spiro-b????-Venice|Petar Spiro]]** in Venice (doc 390).
- 1749-04-09 — Jagodić dies, age 80, entered in matica umrlih of Sv. Ilija (Greek-rite) parish, Zadar (doc 392).

## Co-leaders documented
- **[[Petar-Jagodic-Kuridza-b1666-Bukovica|pop Petar Jagodić-Kuridža]]** — parish priest of Sv. Petar, Bokovica.
- **[[Ilija-Nanic-b????-Zegar|Ilija Nanić]]** of Žegar.
- **[[Mattio-Zabetic-b????-Zaravecchia|Mattio Žabetić]]** of Zaravecchia (whose brother was earlier quartered for the Bortulačić murder of 1692).

## Notes
- The revolt was partial breakdown of the [[Kotari-Pertinenze-Reform-1689|Pertinenze framework]] of 1689 — the colonel Posedarski's misuse of his pertinenza authority, combined with the dragoon decimal-collection regime, generated the grievance.
- The 40-year imprisonment of Jagodić without trial is one of the more striking documents in the volume — Desnica reproduces both his 1746 deposition and the State Inquisitors' release order in full.
