# Ottoman Conquest of Croatia (1522–1527)

Decisive Ottoman push into central Croatia, ~12,000 km² conquered. Old Croatian *župe* and noble communities, "banska zemlja" (Banadego), and Krbava knežstvo destroyed; Knin and Krbava-Modruš dioceses reduced to titular sees [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Conquered area organized as **Vilajet Hrvati** under sandžak Bosna, with Gazi Husrev-beg as sanjakbeg and Murat-beg Gajdić as subaša/vojvoda. Triggered the first major Vlach refugee migration northwest into Istria and Kras.
