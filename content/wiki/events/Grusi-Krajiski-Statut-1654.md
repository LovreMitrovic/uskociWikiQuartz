# Krajiški statut iz Grusi (5 August 1654)

The earliest surviving Kotarska "*liga*" (krajiški) statute — passed at a Krajina-wide assembly at **Grusi** on 5 August 1654, attended by the *gospodin serdar sa svim vojvodami i suci*. Italian translation by Đorđe Đerolim Đordani (cancelliere della lettera illirica e serviana) appended to the Bosnian-Cyrillic original.

## 9 capitoli
1. *od potoči*: when called to chase the Turks, missing the muster = 50 libre fine.
2. *od ubojstva*: accidental killing → 300 groša to the *liga* + cost of *potroški*.
3. *od lupeža* (theft): horse / ox / sheep / hen / sheaf / other goods → 1 head returned to owner + 6 heads to *liga*.
4. *prodaja, krađa, jatak*: full *liga* punishes traitors and accomplices.
5. Disobedience to one's *sudac* or *kapetan* = 25 libre.
6. Drawing a weapon at the *sbor* / in army / in town = 10 groša for sword/musket.
7. *od neprijatelja plivo zaiao*: failure to chase a fleeing enemy = 7-fold restitution.
8. Trampled meadow — same penalty as wheat.
9. Failure to come to a *lumbarda* (cannon-warning) = 5 *tolari* to the liga.

Signed: *"Ovo pisa sva Kraina s licenciom gosposkom. Gospodin serdar sa svim voivodami i suci učiniše, i svi ovomuh kuntenti biše."* + *"Ovo učini sva Kraina gorjna i dojna do Pakoštana."*
