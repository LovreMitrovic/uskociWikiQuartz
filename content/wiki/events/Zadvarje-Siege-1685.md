# Bosnian Pasha's Siege of Zadvarje (June 1685)

The **Bosnian pasha** advanced from the [[Cetina]] valley and besieged the Venetian fortress of Zadvarje (Duare) above the Cetina canyon in June 1685; the garrison held until relief arrived (docs 74–78, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

The siege came as part of the broad Ottoman counter-offensive of spring–summer 1685 in lower Dalmatia, alongside the [[Kastela-Attack-1685|Kaštela attack]] and the [[Sinj-Battle-1685|Sinj defeat]] (docs 74–78, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
