# Capitulation of Vrlika (early September 1688)

The disdar of Vrlika surrendered the fortress to **Fenzi** without firing a shot in early September 1688 (doc 216, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

**Bože Cerniza** placed the new garrison there. The bloodless surrender was a direct consequence of Atlagić's collapsing position during the simultaneous [[Knin-Conquest-1688|siege of Knin]] (doc 216, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
