# Vlach Migration from Croatia to Istria and Kras (1518–1523)

Several waves of medieval Croatian Vlachs fled northwest ahead of advancing Ottomans, peaking 1518–1523 [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Settled hill regions of northern Istria, Kras, and Notranjska. Among them was [[Tomas-Ruzic-b????-Sibenik]] (Vodice on Kras, attested 1524) and the [[Poropat]] kindred from Buzetski Kras. These would form the demographic core of the later "Vlasi Istre" who returned to Ottoman territory 1528–1530.
