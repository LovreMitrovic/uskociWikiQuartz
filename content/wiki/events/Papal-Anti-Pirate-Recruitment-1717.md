# Papal Anti-Pirate Recruitment of Morlachs (1717)

Cardinal **Paulucci**, Secretary of State of Pope Clement XI, requested 300 Morlachs from Venice in 1717 to serve aboard papal galliots in the anti-piracy campaign in the Adriatic and Mediterranean (doc text, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

The request is among the earliest documented uses of Kotari Morlachs in extra-Venetian service and presages the Morlach companies that fought in 18th-century Italian theatres (doc text, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
