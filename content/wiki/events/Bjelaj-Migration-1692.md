# Bjelaj Migration (March 1692)

5,000 souls migrated from the **[[Bjelaj]]** region (Bosnian frontier near Lika) into Venetian Dalmatia under three serdari — **conti Đurić, Pirić, and Zorić** — and were settled at:

- **[[Plavno]]** (the Bosnian-frontier village near Knin)
- **[[Zrmanja]]**
- **[[Padene]]**
- **[[Mokro-Polje]]**
- **[[Oton]]**

(Desnica II, chunk 3.)

## Notes
- Compared to the 1685 wave (3,051 families / 10,290 fighters under multiple serdari), this 1692 migration is concentrated in Bukovica's eastern edge and the upper Krka watershed.
- Provveditor **[[Alessandro-Molin-b????-Venice|Alessandro Molin]]** organised the resettlement.
- The serdari Đurić, Pirić, and Zorić each enter the wiki as new lineages with this migration; the Đurić brothers Paolo and Vuk are documented (Paolo's pension transferred to Vuk after his death).
