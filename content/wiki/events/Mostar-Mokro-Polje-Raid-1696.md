# Selman Pasha's Raid on Mokro Polje (~1696)

While Provveditore Generale **[[Daniel-Dolfin-b????-Venice|Daniel Dolfin]]** was at Ulcinj in 1696, **Selman pasha of Mostar** struck [[Mokro-Polje]] but was repulsed by **[[Jovan-Sinobad-b????-Posedarje|Jovan Sinobad]]** (doc text, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

About 100 Turks were killed and 100 enslaved; Sinobad's nephew was killed in the action (doc text, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
