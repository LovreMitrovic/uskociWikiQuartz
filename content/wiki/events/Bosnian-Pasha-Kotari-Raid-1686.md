# Bosnian Pasha's Raid on Kotari (12–19 November 1686)

About 16,000 Turks under five pashas, supported by 6-pounder cannon, descended from [[Knin]] onto the Kotari plain between 12 and 19 November 1686 (docs 149–157, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

The raiders burned the straw houses of Bribir, [[Ostrovica]], Velim and [[Kozulova-Polje|Kožulovo polje]]; [[Vrana]] and Perušić defended themselves and held (docs 149–157, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]). It was the largest Ottoman counter-strike on the Kotari during the Morean War, retaliation for the Venetian conquest of [[Sinj-Conquest-1686|Sinj]] two months earlier.
