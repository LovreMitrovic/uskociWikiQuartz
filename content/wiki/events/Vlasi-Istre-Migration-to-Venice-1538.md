# Migration of Vlasi Istre to Venetian Territory (1538)

During the [[Venetian-Ottoman-War-1537-1540]], at the urging of providur [[Alvise-Badoer-b????-Venice]], approximately **5,000 Vlachs** crossed from Ottoman to Venetian territory in the Zadar hinterland [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Led in part by [[Zakman-Gledjevac-b????-Karin]], [[Petar-Najcinovic-b????-Dobruca-Vas]], and the [[Milovanic]] and [[Poropat]] families. Most were transported by ship via Zadar, Pontadura (Vir), and Ljubač to Istria; ~2,000 families settled there, declining to ~1,700 by 1554 due to harsh ecology and partial return. Captain [[Nikola-Jurisic-b????]] also drew off "Ćići" with 40,000 head of livestock back to northern Istria/Kras.
