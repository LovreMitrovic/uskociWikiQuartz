# Robbery of the Bishop of Zagreb (May 1688)

In May 1688 six Kotari Morlachs and two men from [[Vinjerac]] ambushed the **Bishop of Zagreb** as he returned from [[Rijeka]]; they took his money and three horses and killed one member of his retinue (doc 203, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

The episode prompted Habsburg-Venetian diplomatic friction at a time when the two allies were also competing for Lika settlers (doc 203, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
