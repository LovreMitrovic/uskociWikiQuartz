# Rama Franciscan Migration (October 1687)

In October 1687 fifteen friars together with 500+ armed family members fled the **San Petar monastery in Rama** (central Bosnia) onto Venetian territory; they first settled at [[Sinj]] / Dicmo and were eventually placed at the Sustipan abbey near Split (docs 181, 184, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

The migration is a foundational moment for the Catholic-Vlach population transferred from Hercegovina/central Bosnia to the Sinj area; **frate Andrea**, "intrinseco e famigliare" of [[Mehmed-Atlagic-b????-Knin|Mehmed Atlagić]], later figured in the Venetian operations against him (docs 181, 184, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]). Compare the contemporaneous [[Skoplje-Migration-1687|Orthodox migration from Skoplje]] (Uskoplje).
