# Biograd

Coastal town of northern Dalmatia. Home of the *Biograd kapetani* in Venetian service from the late 16th c. — e.g. [[Juraj-Matkovic-b????-Biograd]] (1581) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].

## Notes
Full name [[Biograd-na-Moru]]; the Venetian records call it Zaravecchia (see [[Zaravecchia]] / [[Stari-Grad-Zaravecchia]]).
