# Kaštela

Coastal villages between [[Trogir]] and [[Split]]. 7 April 1685: 3000 Turkish cavalry under Ahmed-paša of Hercegovina attacked Kaštela; defeated by 3000 castellani + Morlachs; Mustafa Jasko captured (docs 56, 62) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Kaštelani also raided Mostar district 1687.
