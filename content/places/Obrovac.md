# Obrovac

Ottoman fortress and *varoš* on the Zrmanja. Site of the Vlach *nahija* Obrovac (6 villages + varoš, 20 houses) in 1528–1530, headed by primićur [[Pavel-Rudelja-b????-Obrovac]] [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Ottoman fleet built here under [[Murat-beg-Gajdic-b????-Sibenik]] (1530, Velebit timber). Captured by Venetians June 1538, sparking [[Vlasi-Istre-Migration-to-Venice-1538]].

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
In Mitrović sub-region (docs 11, 15, 19, 51) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Both Veliki and Mali Obrovac (Obrovaz superiore/inferiore) referenced; salt scale; Casanova governor; Vlasto governor 1693. Civic complaints; families Anić, Buljat, Marinović, Miljanić, Klanac etc. settled 1696 (continued in modern Kruševo per source footnote). Janko Mitrović as Obrovac harambaša (doc 288). Mills leased to Zaviša Janković + Ilija Mitrović. Captaincy held by Zaviša; later assigned to Filip Mitrović (1 April 1688). Tomadelli signed for the illiterate Zaviša from Obrovac. Salt scale of [[Karlobag]] opened 1696 to compete with Obrovac.

## Cross-reference
Distinct from [[Obrovac-na-Cetini]] (the Cetina-valley tower burned April 1685, doc 61). The "I due Obrovazzi" of doc 19 may refer to Veliki + Mali Obrovac on the Zrmanja.
