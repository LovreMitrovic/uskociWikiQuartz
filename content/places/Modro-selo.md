# Modro (Modrino selo)

Casale (rural settlement) in [[Bukovica]] near the parish church of Sv. Petar.

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
The casale and surrounding lands were originally held by a Turk known only as **"Modre"** (eponym of the place). After 1692 the lands passed to the [[Sinobad]] family. In 1697, **Radoš [[Kresovic|Kresović]]** and his brother **Sava Kresović** took possession of the casale and refused to return it to Sinobad — a recorded land dispute (doc 302) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
