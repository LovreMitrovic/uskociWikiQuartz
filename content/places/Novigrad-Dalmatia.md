# Novigrad (Dalmacija)

Coastal castello on the Novigradsko more (docs 8, 15, 19, 51) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Novigrad Pertinenza HQ from 1689 Cornaro Liga, including [[Karin]], two Obrovci, Kruševo, [[Zegar|Žegar]], [[Ervenik]], [[Zelengrad]], Drinjci, Otavac, Popovići, Lussichi (Lišani?) and "all of Bukovica." Capitanio Papafava. Vukadin Mitrović died defending Novigrad 1653.
