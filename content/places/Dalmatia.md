# Dalmatia (Dalmacija)

Adriatic coastal region under Venetian rule in the Uskok period; theatre of the Ottoman–Venetian frontier wars and of settlement of Vlach/Morlach uskoks. Numerous Venetian-service families are attested here [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]] [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
