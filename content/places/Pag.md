# Pag

Adriatic island; recipient of part of the 1647 [[Krmpote-Migration-Vinjerac-1647|Krmpote exodus]] under harambaša [[Harambasa-Njegovan-b????-Medvidja]]. Some Pag-settled families later switched to Habsburg subjecthood [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
100 men recruited Oct 1684 by Mocenigo (doc 37) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. 1592 raid by Senj uskoks. Salt traders ("Paghesani") with intricate negotiations. Don Vincenzo Mersio's origin (doc 341).
