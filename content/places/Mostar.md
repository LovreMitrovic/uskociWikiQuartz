# Mostar

Hercegovinian fortress and *nahija* seat. 1477 *istimâlet* contracts here brought Vlachs led by [[Vladimir-vojvoda-b????-Hercegovina]] back to Mostar/Hum nahija under sandžakbeg [[Hamza-beg-b????]] and vojvoda [[Sinan-beg-b????-Mostar]] [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. [[Mehmed-beg-b????-Poljica]] (sandžakbeg of Hercegovina, early 16th c.) was a native of [[Poljica]].

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
District descriptions in fra Mijo Radonić's Hercegovina relazione May 1684 (doc 7) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. District includes Rakitno; raided by Kaštelani / Splićani 1687. Rašić family origin (Rusić village, Mostar district); Selman pasha of Hercegovina threatened Knin. Ilija Janković distinguished himself "sotto Mostar" in Venetian campaign (doc 375).
