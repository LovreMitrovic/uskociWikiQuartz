# Krmpote (Bukovica)

Village in [[Bukovica]] near [[Zelengrad]]; distinct from [[Krmpote-Senj]] [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 301). 15 houses settled by Mede Milcović 1693; later harassed [[Parcic|Parčić]]. Mede Milicovich capo.

## Cross-reference
Three Krmpotes documented in this wiki: (a) Krmpote near Senj (existing), (b) [[Krmpote-Senj]], (c) this Krmpote-Bukovica near Zelengrad.
