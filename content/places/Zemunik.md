# Zemunik

Town in Zadar contado. Lost to Ottomans in [[Cyprus-War-1570-1573]]; subsequently uvakufio for [[Ferhat-beg-Sokolovic-b????]] vakuf (Zemunik mosque + mekteb) and Hrvace [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Selo Miljacki (Zemunik Gornji) was founded 1561 by returnee [[Grgur-Milohanovic-b????-Nova-Vas]].

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
Pertinenza HQ from 1689 Cornaro Liga (with [[Gruhe]], [[Malpaga]], [[Sukosan|S. Cassan / S. Bartolomeo]], [[Bibinje]], [[Crno|Cerno]], Kristagna, [[Bokanjac]], [[Kozino]], Gunpuri, Porto Schiavina) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (docs 51, 56). Hassan Durakbegović killed there earlier. Assembly point for cavalry. Captain Marlatić (Bibinje, doc 316).
