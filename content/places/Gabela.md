# Gabela

Ottoman fortress at [[Neretva]] mouth (Hercegovina relazione, doc 7) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Zeno discussed possible diversion (docs 172, 175). Conquered with Zaviša Janković's help (1000 men) — Zaviša's 1696 attestation.
