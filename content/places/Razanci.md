# Ražanac

Village in Ventura's sub-region (docs 19, 31); under Posedarje pertinenza in 1689 Cornaro Liga [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Luka Njegovan lived above Ražanac in Podgorje (doc 186) — Krmpote→Vinjerac→Ražanac trajectory.
