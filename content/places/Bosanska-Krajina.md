# Bosanska krajina

NW-Bosnian frontier with Habsburg Croatia. Šarić distinguishes **two zapadnobosanski Bunjevac groups** [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]]:
- **"Stariji"** — around Livno, Kupres, [[Glamoc]] (continuous from 16th c.).
- **"Noviji"** — Bosanski Petrovac, Ključ, Sanski Most. These are actually *lički* Bunjevci who settled after the 1878 Austro-Hungarian occupation: predominantly **Krnjeuša, Kolunić** (Bosanski Petrovac), **Velagići, Ramići** (Ključ), **Kljevci** (Sanski Most). Many of these settlements were destroyed/displaced in WWII and the 1992–95 war.

The "stariji" component witnesses Catholic Vlach continuity in the prekoplaninska imigracijska zona of [[Sandzak-Klis]]; the 1743 vizitacija in Gornje [[Glamocko-Polje]] registers Catholic "Matheus Bunievza" as one such trace.
