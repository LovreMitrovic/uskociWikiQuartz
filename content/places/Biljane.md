# Biljane

Village under [[Nadin]] pertinenza in the 1689 Cornaro Liga (with Sopot, [[Benkovac]], Perušić near Benkovac, Kožulovac, [[Korlat]], [[Kula-Atlagica]], Cosulovo Polje, [[Ostrovica]]) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Zaviša Janković's holdings (Bjeline = Biljane).

## Cross-reference
The 1756 Grimani survey enumerates the two Biljane separately: [[Gornje-Biljane|Gornje Biljane]] (*Bigliane Superiore*) and [[Donje-Biljane|Donje Biljane]] (*Bigliane Inferior*), each with its own roster [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. This page remains the collective entry for the pair.
