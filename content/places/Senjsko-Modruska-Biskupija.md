# Senjsko-modruška biskupija

Catholic diocese covering the **Senj and Modruš sees** united in administration. Around 1700 covered four [[Senjska-Kapetanija|kapetanije]] of the Karlovački generalat (Senj, Otočac, Ogulin, Tounj) plus Lika and Krbava.

**The only early-modern ecclesiastical institution to introduce "Bunjevac" into official record** [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]]. Sequence:
- **1702** — biskup [[Martin-Brajkovic-b????-Senj]] uses dvojni naziv *catholici Valachi alias Bunievczi*.
- Subsequent biskupi continue: **Benedikt Bedeković, Adam Ratkaj, Nikola Pohmajević, Ivan Anton Benzoni, Juraj Vuk Čolić** — within years the *vlaški* part dropped, leaving only *Bunievczi*.
- **1733** — vojni svećenik [[Andrija-Josip-Mandal-b????-Lika]] no longer calls Bunjevci Vlachs but says they are *similia* in dress, customs, and life.

Šarić's reading: senjska Crkva took the name from popular tradition because *vlaški* had become a synonym for Orthodox/Schismatic in adjacent dioceses (Zagreb, Ljubljana). It served as **differentia specifica** to mark Catholic Bunjevci off from Orthodox vlach krajišnici.
