# Piva

Highland region and river in present-day northwestern Montenegro, in the Ottoman Sanjak of Herzegovina during the period covered here. Noted as the homeland of the hajduk lineage of Bajo Pivljanin (the toponym "Pivljanin" = "man from Piva"). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]

## Notes
Referent of the alias `[[Plav|Piva]]` used on [[Pivljanin-Nikolic]]; distinct from the town of [[Plav]] in the Sanjak of Scutari.

## People associated with this place
- [[Pivljanin-Nikolic]] — surname line originating from Piva
