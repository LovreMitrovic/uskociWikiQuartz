# Sandžak Mohač (Baranja)

Ottoman administrative unit covering today's [[Baranja]] and northern Slavonia after 1543. Source for two key Bunjevac defter attestations [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]]:
- **1554** defter, selo [[Marok]]: **[[Marton-Bunavac-b????-Marok]]** ("Márton Bunavácz"). Earliest possible written form of "Bunjevac".
- **1561** defter, selo [[Izsep]]: same person as "Martin Bunivac" (Velics 1890).
- Same registers also list raja designated *"Hirvat"* in selo Sukit (mađ. Szőkéd) and selo Ata (mađ. Áta) — Ottoman regional Croat designation for migrants from [[Vilajet-Hrvati]].
