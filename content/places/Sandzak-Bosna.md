# Sandzak Bosna

Ottoman *sandžak* covering Bosnia. Sanjakbeg [[Gazi-Husrev-beg]] (active 1521–1541) administered the early conquests in Croatia via [[Vilajet-Hrvati]] [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Site of the 1528–1530 mufassal defter (TR-BOA-TTD No. 157) and 1530 sumarni defter that first record the **Vlasi Istre**.
