# Buna (river and place)

Short (~8 km) right-bank tributary of the [[Neretva]] in W. [[Hercegovina]], rising at a karst spring beneath the old fortress of [[Blagaj]]. Place "Buna" stands on the river of the same name.

## Significance for genealogy
Center of the **Buna-river origin legend** of the Bunjevci, first written down by Alberto Fortis (1778, Lika) and fra Marijan Lanosović (1794, Bačka). Šarić argues the legend is a 17th-c. **Bosnian-Franciscan construct** — using the Buna and [[Blagaj]] (medieval seat of Sandalj Hranić and Stjepan Vukčić Kosača) to symbolically anchor Bosnian Franciscan jurisdictional claims over Bunjevac populations against rival Dalmatian dioceses.

The **1477 Hercegovina defter** for nahija Blagaj (which contained Buna) shows a different picture: among 36 vlach džemats, none can be identified as Bunjevac. The dominant figure is **vojvoda Petar Hrabren** (Donji Vlasi, manastirski ktitor), suggesting a predominantly Orthodox vlach population. The Neretva already in the 15th c. divided ikavski (W) from ijekavski (E) štokavski; the consistently-ikavski Bunjevci therefore cannot have originated east of the river [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

The closest core Bunjevac winter pastures (Krmpote zimišta [[Buhovo]], [[Borajna]]) lay 22 km west by air; selo [[Vojnici-Ljubuski]] 35 km west.
