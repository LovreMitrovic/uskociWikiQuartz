# Pag

Adriatic island and town (Venetian *Pago*). Francesco [[Grasso]], an inhabitant of Pag, reported to colonnello Posedarski about cannons delivered to Karlobag in 1696 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
