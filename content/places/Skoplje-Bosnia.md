# Skopje (Bosnia, near Klivuno)

Bosnian Skoplje, near [[Livno]] [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 181). 25 Knin Turks ambushed between Skopje and Klivuno Oct 1687. Distinct from Macedonian Skopje and from Hercegovinian [[Uskoplje-Hercegovina|Uskoplje]]. Seat of Jusuf aga Derviševčić in 1700 (doc 321). Burned by Zaviša Janković (Vakup superior + 300 houses; 1696 attestation).
