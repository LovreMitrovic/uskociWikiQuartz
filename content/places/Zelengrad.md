# Zelengrad

Pre-Ottoman fortress in Zadar district. [[Petar-Milovanic-b????-Medvidja]] is attested 1518 in a Zadar deed as *catonarius districtus Zelengradi* — the earliest dated record for the Milovanić Vlach lineage [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
Under Novigrad pertinenza in 1689 Cornaro Liga [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Bukovica villages [[Krmpote-Bukovica]] and [[Parcic|Parčić]] near here.
