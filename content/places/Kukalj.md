# Kukalj

Locality on the Venetian side of the Zadar hinterland ([[Novigradski-Distrikt|novigradski distrikt]]) where the [[Frletic]] Vlach kindred settled in *plaćenička* military service after the 1538 defection [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].
