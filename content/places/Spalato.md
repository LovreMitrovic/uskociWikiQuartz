# Split

Principal Venetian city and port in central Dalmatia (Italian *Spalato*). Cesare Casanova of the [[Casanova]] family spent years here before his 1690 appointment as governor of Obrovci [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
