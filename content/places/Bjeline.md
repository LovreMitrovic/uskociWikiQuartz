# Bjeline

Village east of [[Benkovac]]. 1528: mezra "Stare Biline" with 2 non-Muslim houses, where [[Culina-sin-Milica-b????-Bjeline]] (knez, *timar* in [[Lika]]) resided [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. 1540: home of two "popovski sinovi" Nikola and Josif (Jusuf) — possible glagoljaški priest's family. Old Catholic church Sv. Ivan (today orthodox Sv. Petka).

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
Bjeline / Bigliane (under [[Nadin]] pertinenza 1689) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Zaviša Janković's holdings — 20 (later 40) ploughing oxen (docs 329, 330, 377). Note: [[Bilisani]] (Belissane / Nabelissane near Obrovac) is a distinct village despite similar name.
