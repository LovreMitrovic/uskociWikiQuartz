# Sinj

Cetina-valley fortress (docs 33, 38–42, 60–65) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. **Sinj attempt under Marcello (Oct 1684)** — failed siege; led by Alvise Marcello + Bortulačić (doc 42). **Sinj Battle 7 April 1685** — major Venetian disaster: Morlach panic, Italian infantry rout; Stojan Janković fought bravely, almost alone among capi; many officers killed/captured (Tanusi, Marušić, Macedonia, Palese, Rados-brother, Lombardo missing); Alberti captured by Bosnian Pasha. Sinj defenders: ~70 sejmena + 200 mestani = ~270. **Sinj Relief (April 1687)** — Cornaro and Zeno relieved Sinj from Atlagić's siege; Atlagić withdrew across [[Cetina]]. Conquest finalized; Sinobad among first up the breccia. Site of bishopric.
