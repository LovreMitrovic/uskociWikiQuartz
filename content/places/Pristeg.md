# Pristeg

Village near [[Benkovac]]. Old Catholic church Sv. Frane. Within Uželice belonged to "Donji i Gornji Čerani s Ožalicom" (1528: 17 non-Muslim houses) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
Under [[Nadin]] pertinenza in 1689 Cornaro Liga; also listed under [[Vrana]] pertinenza [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
