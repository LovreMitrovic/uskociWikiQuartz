# Podgorje

The Velebit foothills along the Adriatic. In 1528–1530 a Vlach *nahija* with 147 houses across 6 villages and the *varoš* of [[Obrovac]] — the most populous Vlasi Istre nahija, headed by [[Mate-Rojic-b????-Podgorje]] (80 houses) and [[Mikula-Rojnovic-b????-Podgorje]] (57 houses) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. By 1540 entirely depopulated; old villages listed as "puste mezre". Major *yaylak* (summer pasture) for transhumant Vlachs into the late 16th c.
