# Muškovci

Village near [[Obrovac]] — territorialized katun of the medieval Mušković Vlachs (catunar Dien Mušković, 1433 Lika royal-Vlach charter) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. 1544: 25 families from here fled Lika summer pasture into Habsburg [[Prilisce]].
