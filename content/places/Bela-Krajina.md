# Bela Krajina

Habsburg region in southern Kranjska. Reception area for Vlach uskok refugees from the 1530s onward, including settlement at Marindol kod [[Crnomelj]] (1551 register names [[Stanoje-Prijic-b????-Marindol]] as *desetnik*) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].
