# Ribnik

Lika locality. Two historical claims to fame:

## 1648 — Battle of Ribnik
Site of the catastrophic Kotarska defeat **Battle of Ribnik (16/17 July 1648)** — turning point of the early-Candian-War Morlach campaign. The Kotarska force of ~3000 (under don Stjepan Sorić, Petar Smiljanić, Janko Mitrović and a host of harambaše) attempted to take Ribnik on intelligence supposedly favourable; ambushed at the narrow exit-pass; **pop Sorić captured wounded and beheaded by the Turks**; **Petar Smiljanić killed**; their entire senior cadre fell.

Doc. 39 (1648-07-17) lists losses harambaša-by-harambaša with *village of origin → settlement village* — the single richest origin/settlement document in [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]. See [[Battle-of-Ribnik-1648]].

## 1702 — Ribnička buna
Lika locality giving its name to the **[[Ribnicka-Buna-1702|Ribnička buna]]** of 1702 — the Bunjevac uprising sparked by the conflict between porkulab [[Milos-Starcevic-b1656-Veliki-Zitnik]] and vojvoda [[Marko-Butorac-b????-Lika]]. 1712 popis: Rukavina, Franić, Jurjević, Rogić, Sekulić, Vranešić, Župan family presence [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].
