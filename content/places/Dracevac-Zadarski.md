# Dračevac Zadarski

Village of the Zadar mainland district (Venetian *Malpaga*), southeast of Zadar. Distinct from [[Dracevac|Dračevac kod Jasenica]] — the Frletić village near Jasenice with which the 1610 census was initially conflated.
