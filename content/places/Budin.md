# Budin

Place where Sinobad-Vukčević-Mitrović Morlachs lived (doc 19, 30) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Under Islam pertinenza in 1689 Cornaro Liga. Some families left Budin because of Sinobad-Mitrović feud 1684; Polo Michiel asked to mediate.
