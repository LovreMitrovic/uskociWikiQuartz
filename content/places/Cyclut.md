# Čitluk (Hercegovina, near Mostar)

Ottoman fortress/town in Hercegovina near [[Mostar]]; defended in Morean War (first and second defense) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 358). Sinobad and Zaviša fought there. Distinct from [[Citluk-Licki]] (in Lika).
