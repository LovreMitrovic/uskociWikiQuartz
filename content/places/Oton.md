# Oton

Village in the Knin hinterland. In 1652 Constadin Stojanović "of Oton" ([[Stojanovic]]) appears as an Ottoman-subject galley slave [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]; in 1692 Paolo [[Djuric|Đurić]] settled his people in Zrmanja, Plavno, Pađene, Mokro Polje and Oton [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
