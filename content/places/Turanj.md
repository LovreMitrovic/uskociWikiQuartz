# Turanj

A coastal village on the Zadar mainland, known in Venetian records as *Toretta de Marco*, *Torrete* / *Torreta* / *Toretta*. In Bortulačić's sub-region (doc 19) and under [[Biograd-na-Moru|Zaravecchia]] pertinenza in the 1689 Cornaro Liga [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].

## 1610 → 1756: what changed [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]] [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]

|  | 1610 | 1756 |
|---|---|---|
| Households | 44 | 43 |
| Souls | 276 | 329 |
| Mean household | 6.3 | 7.7 |
| Distinct surnames | 33 | 31 |
| Surnames in both | — | 5 |

Turanj is the outlier: it barely moves. 44 households in 1610, 43 in 1756; 276 souls to 329. Yet only 5 of 33 surnames carry over. A village of stable size whose population was almost entirely exchanged — consistent with the Morean-War depopulation and subsequent resettlement of the coastal strip. [[Milatovic|Milatović]], the largest 1610 group at 6 households, is gone completely.

**In both censuses (5):** [[Morovic|Morović]] 1→3, [[Bausevic|Baušević]] 1→2, [[Bicic|Bičić]] 1→1, [[Budanovic|Budanović]] 1→1, [[Sodic|Šodić]] 1→1.

**In 1610, gone by 1756 (28):** [[Baba]], [[Barbarosa]], [[Berzisic|Beržišić]], [[Boljic|Boljić]], [[Bosko]], [[Boskovic|Bošković]], [[Debanic|Debanić]], [[Defratar]], [[Dranjic|Dranjić]], [[Frakasov]], [[Greko]], [[Koskic|Koškić]], [[Maletic|Maletić]], [[Maruncic|Marunčić]], [[Matulj]], [[Mihaljevic|Mihaljev]], [[Milatovic|Milatović]], [[Micic|Mičić]], [[Misulic|Mišulić]], [[Ozrinovic|Ozrinović]], [[Palikucic|Palikučić]], [[Radobelja]], [[Ruzic|Ružić]], [[Staljcic|Staljčić]], [[Stankovic|Stanković]], [[Toljancic|Toljančić]], [[Zecic|Zečić]], [[Zojic|Zojić]].

### How much of this turnover is real?
Some of it is certainly an artifact. The two censuses were written by different scribes 146 years apart, and their Venetian spellings were normalized into Croatian by two separate ingests using different judgement — so one family can enter the wiki under two hubs and read as a disappearance plus an arrival. The pairs listed above are the visible cases; there are probably more that no longer look similar enough to spot. Treat the continuity figures as a **lower bound**.

The rest is real, and has a cause: the Morean War (1684–1699) and the Venetian resettlement that followed it moved large numbers of people into the Zadar contado from Ottoman territory. Both effects push in the same direction, which is why the raw overlap is so low.
