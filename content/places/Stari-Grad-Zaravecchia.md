# Stari Grad (kraj Biograda / Zara Vecchia)

Locality near Biograd na Moru / Zara Vecchia, distinct from [[Starigrad]] (Starigrad-Paklenica on the Velebit coast). Listed in the Mitrović sub-region with "I due Obrovazzi — Stari Grado — Budin" (doc 19) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].

## Cross-reference
The Mitrović jurisdictional list places "Stari Grado" alongside both Obrovacs and Budin, suggesting the southern (Biograd-area) toponym, not Starigrad-Paklenica.
