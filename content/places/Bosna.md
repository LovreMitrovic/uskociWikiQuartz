# Bosna (Bosnia)

Ottoman frontier province bordering Venetian Dalmatia; home region of Ottoman raiders such as the *četobaša* Karalija, active from [[Cliuno|Livno]] [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
