# Karin

Ottoman fortress and Vlach *nahija* (53 houses, 9 villages) in 1528–1530, headed by vojvoda [[Zakman-Gledjevac-b????-Karin]] [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Held a Franciscan monastery (Bezgrešnoga začeća BDM) tending Catholic Vlachs under Ottoman rule until c. 1538; reopened 1550 by [[Mato-iz-Kresevo-b????]] from Visovac. Karin nahija retained territorial integrity through 1540 register (29 houses).

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
Hajra/Kosa Lakić's birthplace (doc 56) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Land dispute with [[Novigrad-Dalmatia|Novigrad]] (doc 15). Defended itself against pasha April 1688 (doc 198). Mills leased; under [[Novigrad-Dalmatia|Novigrad]] pertinenza in 1689 Cornaro Liga; mentioned in Obrovac confine 1696. Component of pertinenza.
