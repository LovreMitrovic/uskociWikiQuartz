# Sv. Šime (Zadar)

Latin-rite church in [[Zadar]]. Burial place of Antonia, wife of Stojan Janković (d. 29 January 1687, age c. 30) (doc 166) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Lent its name to the S. Simeone contrada in Zadar.
