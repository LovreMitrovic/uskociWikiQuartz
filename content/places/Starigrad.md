# Starigrad

Coastal village on the Velebit coast that received Krmpote families during the [[Krmpote-Migration-Vinjerac-1647|1647 migration]] under harambaša [[Harambasa-Njegovan-b????-Medvidja]] [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
Velebit-coast Starigrad (Starigrad-Paklenica) listed under Posedarje pertinenza alongside Rasanze, Castel Venier ([[Vinjerac]]), Radovin, Ljuba (Cornaro Liga 1689, Dolfin Liga 1696) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Sibuljina/Tribanj 2 miles distant; encroachment dispute with Karlobag people; in Tribanj resettlement 1702/1704 some 25 families moved. 1649 Diet of Bratislava (Posonia) art. 32 restored Jablanac and Starigrad to Frankopans.
