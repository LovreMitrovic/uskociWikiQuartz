# Pozzi (Venetian state prison)

The lowest tier of the Venetian state prison in the Doge's Palace complex — the *pozzi* ("wells") were ground-floor cells, the harshest detention in the Republic. Distinct from (and lower than) the *Piombi* (lead-roofed upper cells) and the **Camerotti** (mid-level, often used for political prisoners awaiting transfer).

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
- **[[Petar-Jagodic-Kuridza-b1666-Bukovica|pop Petar Jagodić-Kuridža]]** — moved from the Camerotti to the Pozzi after several years of confinement; held there for the bulk of his ~40-year imprisonment following the [[Bukovica-Revolt-1704|1704 Bukovica revolt]]; released 29 March 1746 at age 80, blind and deaf, by order of the State Inquisitors (doc 389) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].

## Notes
- Jagodić's own deposition (doc 389) describes the conditions: "10 mesi" in the Pozzi initially, then transfer to "altre carceri ma sempre oscure" — "always dark," with progressive loss of sight and hearing over four decades.
