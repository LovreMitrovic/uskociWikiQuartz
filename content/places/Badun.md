# Bađun

Locality in Ravni Kotari; one of the sub-region seats (Novigrad–Karin–Bađun–Pridraga–Klošter) under the four "new" sub-leaders Mocenigo appointed in 1684 (doc. 19) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
