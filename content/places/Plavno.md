# Plavno

Field/village near [[Knin]]. Site of "torre di Durach Begovich" (Durakbegović tower); Žepina lost an arm here 1684; Plavanjci offered to defect June 1684 (docs 2, 13) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. After 1688/92 conquest assigned to Bjelajci alongside [[Zrmanja]], Pađene, [[Mokro-Polje]], Oton; tower of Muhamedbeg Durakbegović given to Pirić; Janko Vrsalj built fence sheltering 50+ families 1694.

## Cross-reference / open question
May be the same place as [[Plavna]] — historiographical question open. Saric (2024) records the older "Plavna Istrija" nahija as resettlement of [[Vlasi-Istre]] 1540; Desnica's "Plavno" 1684–1694 sits in the same broad Knin region. Treated here as distinct entry per source-name usage.
