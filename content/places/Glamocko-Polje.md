# Glamočko polje

SW Bosnian polje. 1524 Habsburg border reports describe a "dangerous settlement of 6,000 vlach houses" in [[Glamoc]] and Unac — origin point for raiding into Habsburg Croatia and Kranjska (Vasić 1963) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

In 1743 a biskupska vizitacija of Gornji Glamoč registered a Catholic named "Matheus Bunievza" — late Bunjevac trace in the prekoplaninska imigracijska zona [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]] (Erdeljanović 1930).

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
**Glamoč–Grahovo Campaign (Aug 1684)**: 5000 Morlachs under Janković, Smiljanić, Bortulačić raided Glamoč; alajbeg Filipović's palaces burned, 50 Turks killed [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]; on return, ambushed two Bosnian-Hercegovinian pashas at [[Grahovo]]. Burgo of Glamoč burned by Zaviša after Cuprez (1696 attestation). Site of Sinobad's death 1715 (doc 372): Andrija/Jovan Sinobad killed there. Filipović came from here.
