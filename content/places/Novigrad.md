# Novigrad (Dalmatia)

Coastal fortress town on the Novigrad Sea in the Zadar hinterland. In the Krmpote migration tradition (Civran's relacija), the *Carapotani* relocated to the area "around Novigrad" before eventually moving on to [[Lic]] [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].
