# Poljica

Autonomous principality (Poljičko kneštvo) under nominal Venetian then Ottoman authority. 1475 statute regulated relations with Ottoman *martolosi*; 1506 [[Ivanis-Nenadic-b????-Poljica]] and others fled to Ottoman territory; 1508 attempted defection; 1514 nominal submission to Sultan; 1540 final Ottoman incorporation as vlach nahija under Sandzak Hercegovina [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Birthplace of sandžakbeg [[Mehmed-beg-b????-Poljica]].

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
Listed Sept 1687 alongside [[Makarska]] and [[Omis|Almissa]] for Morlach captain assemblies (doc 177) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
