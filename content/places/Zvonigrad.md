# Zvonigrad

Fortress on the [[Zrmanja]] in Zadar contado [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (docs 304, 315, 316, 318, 321). Mehmed aga as disdar pre-conquest. Devastated by Venetian arms; Jovan Sinobad governor 1690; Filip Sinobad 1691; Mihail Sinobad 1694 (d. 1697).
