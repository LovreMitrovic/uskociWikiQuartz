# Senjska Kapetanija

Habsburg captaincy seated at [[Senj]], on the Adriatic coast adjacent to the [[Tromedja]]. Base of the Senj uskoks. Captain in 1560: [[Ivan-Lenkovic-b????]] [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Hosted [[Vlasi-Istre]] defectors among the uskoks, including [[Andrija-Frletic-b????-Dracevac]] and Vuk Frletić.
