# Kruševo (kod Obrovca)

Village in nahija [[Obrovac]] (Donja, Gornja Kruševa, with subhamlets Hotišina = Otišina, Ribnice). 1528: ~15 non-Muslim houses [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. 1554 Šibenik record: [[Vukoslav-Carapotic-b????-Krusevo]] (likely [[Krmpotic]]) and [[Mate-Milkovic-b????-Krusevo]] as Ottoman-side debtors. Old Catholic churches Sv. Jure and Sv. Kuzma i Damjan (in Ribnica).

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
Under Novigrad pertinenza in 1689 Cornaro Liga [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Mattio Miljanić captain 1704. Inhabitants Đurđević (now Jurjević), Anić, Karamarko, Miljanić, Klanac, Buljat, Marinović still trace into modern Kruševo (per source footnote on 1696 Obrovac inhabitants).
