# Crete

Mediterranean island; the principal theatre and casus belli of the [[Candian-War-1645-1669]], fought between Venice and the Ottoman Empire over Venetian *Candia*. The war's Dalmatian front drove the Morlach/Bunjevac migrations central to this wiki. [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
