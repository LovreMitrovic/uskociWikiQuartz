# Hercegnovi (Castel Novo)

Bay-of-Kotor stronghold under Venetian siege 1687–88; captured during the Morean War (doc 175) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Spingaroli participated in the conquest. Vodmac vineyard at Podhumnica region (formerly Resul Aga Babović's) granted to Bishop Simeon Prešević 1697 (doc 307).
