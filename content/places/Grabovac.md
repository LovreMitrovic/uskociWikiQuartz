# Grabovac

Orthodox monastery in [[Baranja]], founded **1585** by monks from manastir **Dragović** on the [[Cetina]] in [[Sandzak-Klis]]. Reinforced **1619** by Orthodox Vlachs from Klis-area accompanying further Cetina-region monastics [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

Documents the parallel Orthodox migration stream into Podunavlje alongside the Bunjevac (Catholic) wave (see [[Bunjevac-Migration-to-Backa-c1606]]).
