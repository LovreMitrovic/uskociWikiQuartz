# Bruška

Village NE of [[Benkovac]]. 1528: 7 non-Muslim houses. 1540: 6 non-Muslim houses — the **only** village remaining in vlach nahija [[Bukovica]] after the 1538 mass defection [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].
