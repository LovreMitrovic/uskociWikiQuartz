# Mokro Polje

Village near [[Knin]]. Concentration of Orthodox Vlach population by 1636 [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Settled by Vlachs Bjelajaca from Bosnia 1690.

## Per Desnica
- 1670-08-12 — Barbaro stations a 25-soldier guard there under capt. Marko Grubišić of Novigrad (doc. 174).
- 1683-12-16 — Kotarci-Šibenčani raid Pađen sub-village; some return through Mokro Polje (doc. 353).
- 1684-01-11 — **Vukadin Bjedov da Mokropoglie** (10 houses) settled at Obrovac after the Vrana uprising (doc. 364).
- 1684-02-28 — 600 minute + 150 grosse animali plundered "christiani sotto Grazaz" by 130 Obrovac Morlachi joined with Novigrađani (doc. 379).

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
Cadi-grande of Knin oversaw Mokropoglie [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 315). Assigned to Bjelajci 1692 (along with [[Plavno]], [[Zrmanja]], Pađene, Oton). Đuro Popović harambaša 1693. Selman pasha raid ~1696 — Sinobad repulsed; killed 100 Turks, 100 enslaved; nephew killed.
