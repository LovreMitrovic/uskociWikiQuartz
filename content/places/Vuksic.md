# Vukšić

Village in [[Kozulova-Polje]] vakuf. Old Catholic churches Sv. Mihovil and Sv. Kata; the village is mentioned in connection with the Sv. Mihovil fair whose revenues went to the vakuf [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
"Ussich" — castle 4 miles south of Vrana (doc 51 footnote 2 identifies as Vukšić) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Under [[Ostrovica]] pertinenza in 1689 Cornaro Liga.
