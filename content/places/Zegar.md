# Žegar (na Zrmanji)

Village in nahija [[Zrmanja]] (Bukovica). 1528: 7 non-Muslim houses [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Later concentration of Orthodox Vlach population (1636 Forza report).

## Source-village of major uskakanja (per Desnica)
- The **Mitrović clan** (Janko, Giovanni, Stefan, Pavle, Andrija) — five brothers — *uskočili* with ~70 households / ~150 fighters into Venetian Ravni Kotari c. 1647 (Stojan's 1670 supplica, doc. 170). Janko becomes the first *serdar de Morlacchi* (1656).
- **Vukadin Mitrović** also from Žegar (doc. 51, 1650 — d. defending Novigrad 1653).
- **Žegarani Morlaci** (200 pješaka) — settled below [[Nin]] after Ribnik 1648 (doc. 39, harambaša Mileta Vukčević).
- **70 Žegarski porodice** — brought by Sorić's 1648-06 raid on Udbina, settled at Islam and Žemunik (doc. 32).

## 1683 retaliations
- 1683-12-07 Senjski raid + 400 Kotarci led by Bajo Pivljanin and Ilija Mitrović plundered Morlachi di Segar — 700 cavalli/manzi, 1 Senjanin and 5 Žegarci killed (docs. 345, 348) — the raid attacked the very village from which the Mitrovići had originated 36 years earlier.
