# Kosinj

Lika locality. 1691 grant location for the [[Rukavina]] family [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]]. 1712 popis: Donji Kosinj parish (Otočac regiment) had **Vukelić** family presence; **Bunjevčevići u Kršu kod Kosinja** are documented as Orthodox c. 1700.
