# Ervenik

Village in nahija [[Zrmanja]]. 1528: 5 non-Muslim houses (recorded as "Irvnenik"). 1540: residence of pop Radoje (one of two priests recorded among Vlasi Istre that year) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Old Catholic churches Sv. Mihovil and Sv. Nikola.

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
Under Novigrad pertinenza (Hrvenik) in 1689 Cornaro Liga [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (docs 329, 330, 377). Settled with Bjelajci 1692. Zaviša Janković's holdings; Novac Vujanić captain 1704.
