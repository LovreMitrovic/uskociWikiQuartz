# Hreljin

Zrinski estate in the Croatian coastal hinterland that received the 1605 [[Krmpote-Migration-Lic-1605|first Krmpote migration]] settling at [[Lic]]. In 1681 a Dvorska komora inquiry recorded testimony from two Krmpote starešine concerning earlier obligations to the [[Zrinski]] family for [[Lic]], [[Krmpote-Senj|Krmpote]], and [[Ledenice]] residents — Marcus Perpich and Stephanus Duich, both registered as **"Rascianus"** (a Habsburg administrative attribution, not a self-identification with Serbian ethnicity in the modern sense) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].
