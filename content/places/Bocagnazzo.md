# Bocagnazzo (Bokanjac)

Venetian/Italian name for the village of Bokanjac near Zadar. In 1672 promised as a settlement site to harambaša Šimun Milković for 25 families (doc. 200) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
