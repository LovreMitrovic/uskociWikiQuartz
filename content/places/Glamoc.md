# Glamoč

Town and district in western Bosnia. 1524 Habsburg border reports described a "dangerous settlement of 6,000 vlach houses" at Glamoč and Unac, an origin point for raiding into Habsburg Croatia (Vasić 1963) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]]. Alajbeg Filipović's palaces here were burned in the Glamoč–Grahovo campaign of August 1684 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
