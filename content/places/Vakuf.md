# Kulen Vakuf

Ottoman town captured during the Morean War; Saviša wounded there [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 344, 358). Vakup superior + 300 houses burned by Zaviša at [[Skoplje-Bosnia|Skopje]] area; Resep Bairamović from Vakup. See also [[Varcar-Vakuf]] (Mrkonjić-Grad).
