# Zagreb

Habsburg Croatian center [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Bishop of Zagreb robbery May 1688: 6 Kotarci + 2 from Vinjerac ambushed Bishop returning from Rijeka, took money + 3 horses, killed 1 of his retinue (doc 203). Karlović died at nearby [[Medvedgrad]] 1531.
