# Split (Spalato)

Major Venetian Adriatic port; base for many operations (docs 24, 35, 38, 40, 42, 54, 55, 57, 60–66) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Cosmi archbishop. Sustipan abbey (Sant Steffano) outside Split assigned as temporary refuge to [[Rama]] Franciscans 1687. [[Solin]] / Salona nearby.
