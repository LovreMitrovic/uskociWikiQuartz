# Brgud (kod Benkovca)

Village in [[Bukovica]]. 1528–1530: 15 non-Muslim houses; home of [[Samil-Jakovic-b????-Brgud]] (recorded in 1530 sumarni after conversion to Islam, absent from earlier mufassal) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. 1540 register: 16 non-Muslim houses. Old Catholic church on site of today's orthodox Sv. Lazar.
