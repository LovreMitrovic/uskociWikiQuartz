# Vučjak

Mountain pass on the **Lika–Dalmatia border**, on the route between **Krupa** and [[Gracac|Gračac]] (per Desnica's footnote on doc. 86). Site of the death of [[Ilija-Smiljanic-b????-Udbina|Ilija Smiljanić]] (8 September 1654, return from raid on Ondič near Udbina, ambushed by Turks waiting in the boscage).
