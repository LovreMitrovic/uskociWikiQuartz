# Kras

Karst plateau in the Habsburg-Venetian borderlands above the Adriatic. Major refuge for Croatian Vlachs fleeing Ottoman conquests c. 1518–1523 ([[Vlach-Migration-to-Istria-1518-1523]]). Source area of [[Vlasi-Istre]] return migration 1528. Includes settlements [[Vodice-na-Krasu]] (where [[Tomas-Ruzic-b????-Sibenik]] was attested 1524), [[Buzet]], Rašpor [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].
