# Bunjane (Đakovica)

Village near Đakovica in Metohija; 1330 **Dečanska povelja** records 62 houses of agricultural (Serbian) population [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]]. The toponym belongs to the medieval Bun-stem onomastic family of place- and clan-names (with [[Tudoricevci]] / Bunovići, Bunuševci of [[Vranjsko-Krajiste]], Bunčići of [[Buncici]]).
