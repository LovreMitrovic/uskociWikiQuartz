# Kranjska (Carniola)

Habsburg duchy. Affected by 1570 peasant unrest (wine tax) extending to [[Pazinska-Grofovija]]; site of [[Pazin-Peasant-Revolt-1570]]. Includes [[Bela-Krajina]] (south) and [[Notranjska]] (inner) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].
