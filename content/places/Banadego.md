# Banadego (Banska Zemlja)

Older Venetian and Croatian designation for the lands of the Croatian Ban — pre-Ottoman northern Dalmatia and adjacent regions [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Preserved as a place-name in 16th-c. Venetian sources ("territorio turchesco" formerly Banadego) describing former Croatian holdings now under Ottoman rule.
