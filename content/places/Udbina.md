# Udbina

Lika–Krbava centre, capital of the *kapetanija Udbina*. Critical for Kotarska genealogy: **the original home of the Smiljanić clan** (per Desnica's footnote on doc. 20: "Smiljanići poreklom iz Udbine, a ne kako se priča i veruje, iz Smiljana u Lici"). 

The 1647 attestation reading "Pietro Smiglianich da Vavina" is a copyist's error for *Undina = Udbina*.

## Surnames originating in Udbina
- **Smiljanić** — Petar (d. 1648), Ilija (d. 1654), Filip (d. 1656), Smoljan, Lazaro.

## Other Desnica attestations
- 1648-06-21 — Sorić, Smiljanić, Mitrović raid Udbina, burning Mazin valley + Rudopolje sub-villages (doc. 32).
- 1648-08-19 — *capitan d' Udvina* a senior Turkish military rank (alai-bei) of Udbina; one such captured at Otres (doc. 34); subsequently killed by an embittered harambaša whose brother had been killed (doc. 34).
- 1654 (Sept) — Stojan Janković and Ilija Smiljanić raid the village **Ondič near Udbina** (doc. 86) — Ilija is killed on retreat at Vučjak.
