# Suhovare

Locality in the Zadar hinterland (Sovare / Suhovare). In 1680 the hajduk Bajo Nikolić-Pivljanin leased a meadow (*prado*) here from Ottoman owners (doc. 265) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
