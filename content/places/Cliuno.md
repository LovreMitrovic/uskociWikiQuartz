# Cliuno (Livno)

Venetian/Latin name for Livno in western Bosnia. Site of Janković's and Smiljanić's raids of 1686; after the second Livno raid (doc. 141) the Ottoman relief force under a new Zvornik pasha and the alajbeg Filipović was routed [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
