# Sv. Ilija (Zadar)

Greek-rite (Orthodox) church in [[Zadar]]. Burial place of Magdalena Janković (d. 13 May 1684), daughter of Stojan Janković (doc 3) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Center of the Croatian/Albanian (Vlach/Serb) cavalry confraternity disputed in Valier's ruling 12 Feb 1685 (doc 47). Significant institutional marker of Janković/Mitrović family Orthodox identity. The text says "sepolta nella chiesa di sant' Elia" while the source archive heading is "Parohijski arh. crkve Sv. Stošije" — the Greek-rite chapel was administratively under [[Sv-Stosija-Zadar|St. Anastasia's]] jurisdiction but physically Sv. Ilija.
