# Sukošan

Coastal village south of [[Zadar]] in Smiljanić sub-region (docs 19, 51) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Under Zemunik pertinenza in 1689 Cornaro Liga (S. Bartolomeo).
