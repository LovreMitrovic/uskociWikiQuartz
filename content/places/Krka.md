# Krka (river)

River; "cadi grande" of [[Knin]] oversaw the area (doc 315) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Krka monastery of San Michiel Arcangelo (Sv. Arhangel) granted to Greek-rite monks 1690; bounded by Lačani, Migliaska (three mills), Karslac, Atlum Greb, Biela Vlaha, Giama Colubinca, Skradin road. Šuplja Crkva (Supglacriqua) — ruined church near Krka, on road to Knin (doc 51).
