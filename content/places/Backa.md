# Bačka

Pannonian region between the Danube and Tisa; one of the four main Bunjevac branches ("podunavski / ugarski") according to Šarić's framework [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

## Bunjevac settlement
- **c. 1530s** — earliest recorded Ottoman deportations of buntovni Vlachs from [[Vilajet-Hrvati]].
- **1606+** — main wave (see [[Bunjevac-Migration-to-Backa-c1606]]).
- **1622** — first explicit "**parochia Bunievczi**" in the Kalocsa archdiocese, requested by don [[Simun-Matkovic-b????]].
- **1633** — barski nadbiskup reports "parochia di Backa" with 400 zaselaka, "lingua illirica."

## Identity formation
In Bačka the Bunjevac name acquired a *legitimating* rather than depreciative meaning, in contrast to Dalmatia. By 19th c. it became central to the cultural revival under [[Ivan-Antunovic-b1815-Backa]] and [[Boza-Sarcevic-b????-Subotica]] in [[Subotica]] [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

Settlement names of clearly Dinaric origin appearing along the Krivaja near Subotica by mid-17th c.: **Kupuszina, Stanišić, Zobnatica**.
