# Uskoplje (Hercegovina)

Area whence prete Zorzi led 26 families + 50 armed men to Venetian land Nov 1687 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 183). Explains Orthodox enclaves at [[Dicmo]], Biletić, Broćanac in Cetina region. Distinct from Macedonian Skopje and Bosnian [[Skoplje-Bosnia|Skoplje]]. Linked to existing [[Vlasi-Uskoplja]] surname.
