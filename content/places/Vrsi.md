# Vrsi

Village near Nin in the Zadar district. Settlement site of Petar Miljanić's [[Cuculovaci|Čučulovci]] group (70 → 6 households) recorded in the aftermath of the 1648 Battle of Ribnik [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]].
