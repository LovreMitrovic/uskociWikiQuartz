# Podaca

Village in Makarsko primorje. 1477 Hercegovina defter: one of the three constituent villages of the **Sladović džemat** (Podci = Podaca), alongside [[Igrane]] and [[Kozica]] [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]]. Šarić argues this anomalous "by-villages" organization (rather than transhumant pastures) suggests either a vlahizirano peasant population given Vlach status, or a fully territorialized vlach katun.
