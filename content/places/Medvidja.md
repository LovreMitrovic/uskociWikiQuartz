# Medviđa

Village in [[Bukovica]]. 1528–1530: 6 non-Muslim houses, dzemat of [[Petar-Milovanic-b????-Medvidja]] (also home of his brother [[Tomas-Milovanic-b????-Medvidja]]) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. 1540 register: 5 non-Muslim + 1 Muslim houses. Later "Krmpotsko Selo" hamlet (today Staro Selo) added by Bunjevac [[Krmpotic]] settlers; old church Sv. Ivan Krstitelj "Usik".
