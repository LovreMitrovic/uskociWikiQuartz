# Kula Atlagića

Village near [[Benkovac]]. 1528: "Obatija" (= Opatija) with 11 non-Muslim houses; old church Sv. Nikola at "Tihlić". 1574 vakuf register: paired with [[Korlat]] as "Korlatović i Pehlić" (6 Christian houses) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Residence of [[Fran-Prejic-b????-Opatija]] (1528).

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
Under [[Nadin]] pertinenza in 1689 Cornaro Liga (with [[Biljane]], Sopot, [[Benkovac]], Perušić, [[Kozulovac]], [[Korlat]], [[Kozulova-Polje|Cossulovo Polje]], [[Ostrovica]]) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Atlagić family lands referenced near attacked village.
