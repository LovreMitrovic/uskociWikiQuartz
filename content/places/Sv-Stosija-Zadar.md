# Sv. Stošija (Zadar)

Cathedral of [[Zadar]] (St. Anastasia). The Greek-rite [[Sv-Ilija-Zadar|Sv. Ilija]] chapel was administratively under Sv. Stošija's parish archive ("Parohijski arh. crkve Sv. Stošije"), even though Magdalena Janković's 1684 burial was physically at Sv. Ilija (doc 3) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
