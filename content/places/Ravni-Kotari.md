# Ravni Kotari

Plain region in the [[Zadar]] hinterland, fertile and contested between Ottoman and Venetian powers in the 16th c. Includes [[Benkovac]], [[Nadin]], [[Polaca]], [[Miranje]] and surroundings. Major settlement zone of the [[Vlasi-Istre]] returning 1528 [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].
