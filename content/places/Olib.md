# Olib

Island near [[Zadar]] [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 336). Half-owned by conte Califfi family, half by Soppe Fortezza heirs; sold to Zaviša Janković 1701 for 8000 ducats (perpetual half-half between Zaviša's male line and his deceased brother Ilija's male line).
