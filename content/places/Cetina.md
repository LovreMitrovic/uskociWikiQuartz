# Cetina

River and surrounding region. Forms the southeastern boundary of [[Vilajet-Hrvati]]. Site of the 1516 Ottoman capture of utvrda Nutjak, after which 9 vlach džemata defected from Poljica to Ottoman territory in Radobilja [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Cetinski Morlaci recorded by Domenico Negri (c. 1500) as speakers of "iskvarenim latinskim jezikom" — "Romani".

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
Cetina campaign March–April 1685 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (docs 28, 33, 42, 55, 59–61). Cetina Morlak migration March 1685: Ilija Peraica brought 600 armed men + families + 30,000 small animals + 10,000 cattle to [[Trogir]] territory. Atlagić retreated across [[Cetina]] April 1687; pasha at sorgente di Cettina April 1688. [[Cetinski-Most]] (Cetinski most / ponte di Cettina) — bridge mentioned docs 56, 59, 60. Vukovićev most / "ponte Vucovich" — docs 59, 61. [[Otok-Cetina|Otok]] — island in Cetina where Turks camped before Sinj battle. [[Dragovic|Dragović]] — peninsula on Cetina with Greek-rite monastery, restored by Bušović 1694.
