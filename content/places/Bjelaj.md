# Bjelaj (Bilaj)

Main castle in [[Lika]]; in Turkish times provided 100 cavalry + 200 foot [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Ilija Janković's June 1687 raid (800 men, 400 cavalry; killed Bilaj captain's brother and son, doc 170). Conquered by Venetians c. 1692; population of ~5000 led to Venetian devotion 1692; tower of disdar stormed by Juka Vukmirović with bombs. 5000 souls migration with conti Đurić, Pirić, Zorić, settled [[Plavno]], [[Zrmanja]], Pađene, [[Mokro-Polje]], Oton (1692-03-21). Sunaje village near Bilaj — Milovan Babić (doc 215). Christians from Bilaj area used Zarvovizza/Zrvnica mountains to ambush Turks Dec 1687.
