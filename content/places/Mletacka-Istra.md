# Mletačka Istra (Venetian Istria)

Coastal and southern Istria under Venetian rule. Designated *Scudo della Dominante* ("shield of the Dominante") for its strategic value protecting Venice. Major reception zone for Vlach refugees 1538 — ~2,000 families settled, declining to ~1,700 by 1554 [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Settlements include [[Premantura]], [[Marcana]], [[Zbandalj]], [[Sikici]], plus older villages absorbing newcomers.
