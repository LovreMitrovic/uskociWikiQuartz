# Modrino selo

Village in [[Bukovica]] near St. Peter's church (doc 302) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Sinobad family ancestors lavorano under Turks here. House of Turco Modre granted to Sinobad 1692; later disputed.
