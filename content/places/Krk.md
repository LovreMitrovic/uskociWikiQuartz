# Krka (Sandžak)

Ottoman sandžak on the Dalmatian frontier (also Sandžak of Cetina). Its sandžak-begs from before 1671 to 1684 were of the [[Durakbegovic]] beg family, whose *zijamet* was at [[Vrana]] [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]].

## Notes
The bare link `[[Krk]]` used on some pages refers to this Krka sandžak, not the Adriatic island of Krk. See also [[Sandzak-Krka]] and [[Krka]].
