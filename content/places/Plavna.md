# Plavna / Plavno

Region near [[Knin]]. Settled c. 1528 by [[Vlasi-Uskoplja]]; depopulated, then resettled 1540 with returning [[Vlasi-Istre]] under primićur [[Hlapac-sin-Radovana-b????-Plavna]] and vojvoda [[Dragisa-sin-Dragoja-b????-Plavna]] (forming new "Plavna Istrija" nahija) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Later receives Vlachs Bjelajaca from Bosnia 1690.
