# Parčić

Village in [[Bukovica]] near [[Zelengrad]] [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Settled by Mede Milcović with 15 houses 1693; later disputed with [[Krmpote-Bukovica]]. Cvitan Cvitignanić captain 1704 (doc 352).
