# Sanjak of Scutari (Skadarski sandžak)

Ottoman administrative district centred on Skadar (Shkodër), including the Montenegrin coastal region near Bar; recorded as the origin of Orthodox Vlach families (e.g. from [[Mrkojevici]]) who migrated toward Istria in the 1620s. [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]]
