# Vranjsko krajište

Eastern frontier krajište of the medieval Serbian state, in the upper Južna Morava drainage (Vranje, Inogošta, Preševo, Dubočnica, Znepolje). Disintegrated after the Battle of Rovine (1395). Home to the **Vlasi Bunuševci** in the 14th c. — one of the medieval Bun-stem Vlach groups [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]] (Blagojević 1989).

The Bunuševci performed border-guard service to a *krajiški vlastelin*. Toponymic remnant: village **[[Bunusa]]** south of Leskovac, formed by territorialization of their katun.
