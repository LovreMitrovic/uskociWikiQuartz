# Pakoštane

Village in Bortulačić's sub-region (doc 19) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Under Zaravecchia pertinenza in 1689 Cornaro Liga (with Krčina, Toretta, [[Sv-Filip-i-Jakov|Sv. Filip i Jakov]]). Commune that with Biograđani plotted Bortulačić's murder (1692-01-16, with Vrana commune). Pardoned 1694. Petar Banković from there 1701 (doc 332).
