# Pakoštane

Village in Bortulačić's sub-region (doc 19) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Under Zaravecchia pertinenza in 1689 Cornaro Liga (with Krčina, Toretta, [[Sv-Filip-i-Jakov|Sv. Filip i Jakov]]). Commune that with Biograđani plotted Bortulačić's murder (1692-01-16, with Vrana commune). Pardoned 1694. Petar Banković from there 1701 (doc 332).

| Distinct surnames | 14 | 34 |
| Surnames in both | — | 2 |

Pakoštane triples: 19 households to 63, 93 souls to 445. Only [[Punos|Punoš]] and [[Skilic|Škilić]] appear in both, and 32 of the 34 surnames present in 1756 are new to the village. Of the four villages this is the clearest case of wholesale resettlement — the 1610 Pakoštane was a small remnant, and the 1756 village is essentially a new foundation on the same site.

**In both censuses (2):** [[Punos|Punoš]] 1→4, [[Skilic|Škilić]] 1→2.

**In 1610, gone by 1756 (12):** [[Ajazovic|Ajazović]], [[Baskovic|Bašković]], [[Dikovic|Diković]], [[Dlancic|Dlančić]], [[Ljubovic|Ljubović]], [[Perinovic|Perinović]], [[Pilizaric|Piližarić]], [[Procic|Pročić]], [[Punosevic|Punošević]], [[Svitaric|Svitarić]], [[Veziljevic|Veziljević]], [[Zojic|Zojić]].

**New in 1756 (32):** [[Bungur]], [[Juresic|Jurešić]], [[Rasin|Rašin]], [[Sarin|Šarin]], [[Gasparovic|Gašparović]], [[Cudina|Ćudina]], [[Baricic|Baričić]], [[Dalako]], [[Kurtovic|Kurtović]], [[Lokic|Lokić]], [[Mazanovic|Mažanović]], [[Pancic|Pančić]], [[Placar|Plaćar]], [[Vukov]], [[Bakja]], [[Boskovic|Bošković]], [[Costiera]], [[Crpetic|Crpetić]], [[Draskovic|Drašković]], [[Grujicic|Grujičić]], [[Kacija]], [[Lasman|Lašman]], [[Miocevic|Miočević]], [[Miosevic|Miošević]], [[Mircetic|Mirčetić]], [[Nikolin]], [[Socic|Sočić]], [[Sosin|Sošin]], [[Vesiljevic|Vesiljević]], [[Vujasin|Vujašin]], [[Vulinovic|Vulinović]], [[Vuskovic|Vušković]].

### Likely the same family, split by spelling

- 1610 [[Veziljevic|Veziljević]] (1, *Vesigliouich*) ~ 1756 [[Vesiljevic|Vesiljević]] (1, *Vesiglievich*) — Near-identical Venetian spellings normalized differently by the two ingests.
- 1610 [[Pilizaric|Piližarić]] (1, *Pilixarich*) ~ 1756 [[Pelizaric|Pelizarić]] (3 in Biograd, 1 in Vrana, *Pelizarich*) — Also present as [[Pilizaric|Pilizarić]] (4 households) in 1610 Biograd — three spellings of what is probably one Biograd-area family.

These are flagged, **not merged** — merging Person or Surname pages needs a decision.

*(Two classes of split have since been reconciled and no longer show up as turnover here: hub files that carried two canonical spellings — Sodić/Šodić, Vicinović/Vičinović, Drasković/Drašković, Kuzinović/Kužinović, Barbarosa/Barbarossa — and the former **Smoljić** hub, merged into [[Smolic|Smolić]] on 2026-09-08 as one family.)*

### How much of this turnover is real?
Some of it is certainly an artifact. The two censuses were written by different scribes 146 years apart, and their Venetian spellings were normalized into Croatian by two separate ingests using different judgement — so one family can enter the wiki under two hubs and read as a disappearance plus an arrival. The pairs listed above are the visible cases; there are probably more that no longer look similar enough to spot. Treat the continuity figures as a **lower bound**.

The rest is real, and has a cause: the Morean War (1684–1699) and the Venetian resettlement that followed it moved large numbers of people into the Zadar contado from Ottoman territory. Both effects push in the same direction, which is why the raw overlap is so low.
