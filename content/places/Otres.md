# Otres

Bukovica village. Sites of:

- **1648-05-11** — Janko Mitrović and other harambaše assemble at Otres after the Unac raid (doc. 28).
- **1662-02-13** — *Otreška pogibija*: Morlachi defeated by superior Turkish force after vainly pursuing a 100-Lika raid; about 200 lost (docs. 119, 120). Ražanac parish *godovna knjiga* lists the named dead from Ražanac including Šime and Ivan Matak, Grubiša and Ivan Skulić, Jure Valičić, Šime Ugarković, **Nikola Bunjevac**, Juriša Dundović, Ivo Radobilić, Garga and Maraš Jović, Vučen Vlaković, Vid Lilić, Mate Sikirić, Bože Garkinić — and from Novigradska parohijska knjiga: D. Vičko Vlatković and others.
- **1683 (uskakanje)** — One of the muster-points of the Vrana uprising (doc. 353).

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
Bukovica village; Morlachs failed to muster here April 1688 (doc 198) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
