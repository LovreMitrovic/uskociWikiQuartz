# Subotica

Northern [[Backa]] city. Major Bunjevac population center from the 17th-c. migrations onward.

In 1687 [[Vojnic|Vojnići]] are listed among Subotica graničari; the **subotički Vojnići** were ennobled by the Hungarian crown in **1741** (Sekulić 1986). Per Sekulić, they trace origin to Dalmatia — Šarić raises but does not resolve whether they descend from the Tinj/[[Zupanovic|Županović]] Vojnići of [[Ravni-Kotari]] [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

## 19th-c. revival nucleus
- **1868** — [[Boza-Sarcevic-b????-Subotica]] launches the *Bunjevački kalendar*.
- **1870** — [[Ivan-Antunovic-b1815-Backa]] launches *Bunjevačke i šokačke novine* (Kalocsa).

This Subotica intellectual circle reaffirmed *bunjevaštvo* as a distinct cultural identity in response to Hungarian magyarization. Šarić frames this as a movement *opposite* to the Lika trajectory (where Bunjevac identity was being absorbed into Croatian) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].
