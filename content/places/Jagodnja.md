# Jagodnja

Village in [[Ravni-Kotari]] paired with [[Miranje]] as the dzemat of [[Tomas-Ruzic-b????-Sibenik]] (1528) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Old Catholic church (now site of orthodox Sv. Simeon).

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
Listed in Bortulačić's sub-region (doc 19) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Two Jagodne (Giagodne) under [[Vrana]] pertinenza in 1689 Cornaro Liga.
