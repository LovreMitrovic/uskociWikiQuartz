# Črnomelj

Town in [[Bela-Krajina|Bela Krajina]], southern Kranjska. Nearby Marindol was a settlement of Vlach uskok refugees from 1551 (register names Stanoje Prijić as *desetnik*) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].
