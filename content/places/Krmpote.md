# Krmpote (Bukovica)

Ancestral Vlach/Bunjevac district in the [[Bukovica]]/Obrovac area (later selo [[Medvidja]]), from which the *Krmpoćani* ([[Krmpotic]]) migrated to Habsburg [[Lic]] in 1605 and 1627 [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]]. Knez Nikola Tadijić led uskoks "iz Krmpote" at the Battle of Ribnik 1648 [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]].
