# Klis

Croatian fortress above [[Split]], last major Croatian stronghold south of [[Velebit]] until taken by Ottomans in 1537. Gives name to the [[Sandzak-Klis]]; first sanjakbeg [[Murat-beg-Gajdic-b????-Sibenik]] [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. [[Tomas-Ruzic-b????-Sibenik]] served here as a soldier before his 1518 flight to Istria.

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
Fortress; sub-region for various ops [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (docs 38, 42, 51, 60, 61). Cornaro's camp April 1687.
