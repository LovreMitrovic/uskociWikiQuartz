# Ćićarija

Karst highland region between [[Habsburska-Istra]] and [[Mletacka-Istra]], home to the bilingual Slavic-Romance "Ćići" Vlachs. Last preserved area of the Istro-Romanian / "vlaški" idiom, today only in [[Zejane]] and a handful of villages along Čepić polje [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].
