# Vrlika

Cetina-area fortress (doc 7) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Surrendered Sep 1688 to Fenzi without a shot; commands "fertile vastissime campagne" (docs 214, 216, 220). Bože Cerniza placed garrison there. 1697 Crnice brothers from there. 1700 governator Franćeski. 1729 Stojisavljević family origin (Vučen Stojisavljević married Marija Mitrović).
