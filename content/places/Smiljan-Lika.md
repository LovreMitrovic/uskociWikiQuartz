# Smiljan

Village in [[Lika]]. By legend the ancestral home of the [[Smiljanic|Smiljanić]] family, but Desnica's footnote (doc. 20) shows the Smiljanići actually originated in [[Udbina]], not Smiljan [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]].
