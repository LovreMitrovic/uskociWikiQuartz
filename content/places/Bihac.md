# Bihać

Habsburg-era frontier town and captaincy seat in NW Bosnia. Way station for "uskok" Vlach migrations from Ottoman territory toward [[Zumberak]] in the 1530s, including the group of vojvoda [[Vuk-Popovic-b????]] [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
By Desnica II era (Ottoman): Turkish frontier fort; 600 cavalry, 800 foot under Turks [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Zaviša Janković at Biač near [[Ripac]] (1696 attestation).
