# Morlakija

Cartographic-imagological region of the early modern eastern Adriatic hinterland, broadly overlapping with the [[Tromedja]] and Velebit Podgorje. Used by Venetian observers (e.g. [[Vincenzo-Forza-b????]] 1636) to designate the Vlach-populated mountainous backlands [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. "Bukovica je jedna od najvećih planina Morlakije" — Forza.
