# Zvornik

Ottoman town and seat of the sandžak of Zvornik in Bosnia, on the Drina. [[Bastic-pasha-b????-Zvornik|Bastić]] was named pasha of Zvornik in 1686 and sent to reinforce Livno [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
