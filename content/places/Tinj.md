# Tinj

Town. Conquered by Ottomans in [[Cyprus-War-1570-1573]]; uvakufio 1574 by [[Ferhat-beg-Sokolovic-b????]] as the *čiftluk Tinj* (six villages including Lišane, Sikovo, Raštane, Gorica, Prkos) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. After 1585 settled with western-Hercegovinian Vojnić Vlachs ("Bunjevci"). 1636 Catholic župa under don [[Stipan-Soric-b????-Gorica]]. Region also known as "Županovići" after [[Zupanovic]] starešina family.

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
"Castello di Thin" (docs 19, 51) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Under [[Vrana]] pertinenza in 1689 Cornaro Liga.
