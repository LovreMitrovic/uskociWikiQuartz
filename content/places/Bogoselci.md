# Bogoselci

Village near Zadar; its inhabitants, with those of [[Bibinje]], Kolarin, Brkljača and [[Sukosan|Sukošan]], were posted at the Margitina Voda pass-guard under harambaša Ivan Dević (1663, doc. 130) [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]].
