# Venice

Capital of the Republic of Venice (Venezia; Croatian *Mleci*), the sovereign power over Venetian Dalmatia and the seat of its patrician families and Senate. Numerous Venetian provveditori, rectors, and patricians documented in the Kotari uskok corpus originate here [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]] [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
