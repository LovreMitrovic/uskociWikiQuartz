# Vojnići (Ljubuški)

Eponymous origin village of the **[[Vojnic|Vlasi Vojnići]]**, NW of Ljubuški in W. [[Hercegovina]] — the result of territorialization of the original 1477 katun (džemat of [[Vukasin-sin-Vojihne-b????-Mostar]]). Located ~35 km west of [[Buna]] [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

Earlier attestation: **1408** charter of Bosnian king Stjepan Ostoja mentions Vlasi Vojihnići, subjects of the W. Hum noble line Radivojević-Vlatković (Dinić 1940).
