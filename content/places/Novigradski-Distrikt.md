# Novigradski distrikt

Venetian administrative district around Podnovje/Novigrad in the Zadar hinterland where Vlach families (e.g. the [[Najcinovic|Najčinovići]] and [[Frletic|Frletići]]) settled after the 1538 defection [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].
