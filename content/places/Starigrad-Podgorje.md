# Starigrad

Settlement on the Velebit Podgorje coast (with Jablanac nearby). A knežija held by the [[Catalinic|Katalinić]] family; its knez Žorži Katalinić was killed in 1678 defending Morlach livestock against a Lika Turkish raid, and a 50-man Starigrad company was established in 1696 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].

## People associated with this place
- [[Sokolic]], [[Catalinic]] families — dues collection and knežija
