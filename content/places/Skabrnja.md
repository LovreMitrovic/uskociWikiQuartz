# Škabrnja

Village in Ravni kotari southeast of [[Zadar]], between [[Zemunik]] and [[Prkos]]. Parish church of **sv. Luka** (the 1686–1719 census opens with the župnik sv. Luke) [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]. The census period begins immediately after the Morean-War reconquest of Ravni kotari, so this roster reads as the (re)settled village's early household base.
