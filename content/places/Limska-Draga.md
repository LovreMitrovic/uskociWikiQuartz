# Limska draga

Karst valley in central Istria, in the area of medieval Dvigrad. **1622** — selo Jural in Limska draga received 14 Orthodox Crnogorac families from [[Mrkojevici]] under pop Jovan, and (briefly) 12 Catholic families under fra Franjo Poruba [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]]. The Catholic families separated and complained to rašporski kapetan Contarini.
