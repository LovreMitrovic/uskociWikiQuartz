# Nova Vas (kraj Poreča)

Istrian village near [[Porec]]. Origin of [[Grgur-Milohanovic-b????-Nova-Vas]] who in 1561 founded Miljacki at [[Zemunik]] Gornji and led the 1570 retreat to Žbandalj. One of the surviving "istrorumunjski" speech communities into the modern era [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].
