# Čučulovci

Village of origin of Petar Miljanić's band; recorded (as resettled in [[Vrsi]], 70 → 6 fighters) in the muster around the Battle of Ribnik, 1648 [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]].

## Notes
Variant spelling of [[Cucullovaci]].
