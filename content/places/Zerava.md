# Žerava

Village in the Zadar hinterland. In 1648 Radivoj Šušić's [[Ugarci]] group (60 → 3 households) settled here in the aftermath of the Battle of Ribnik [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]].

## People associated with this place
- [[Susic]], [[Rorcic]] families
