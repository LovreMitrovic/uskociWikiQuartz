# Gračac

Town in southern Lika. Selo Kijani near Gračac holds a Cyrillic stećak inscription of Ivan Balenović [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]; recorded as an origin of the Miljanić family [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].
