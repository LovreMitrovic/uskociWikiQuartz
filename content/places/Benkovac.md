# Benkovac

Town in [[Ravni-Kotari]]. Vlach *nahija* Benković (30 houses, 4 villages) in 1528–1530 led by primićur [[Petar-sin-Jancica-b????-Ostrovica]]; included Hotešteva, Obatija (Kula Atlagića), [[Polaca]], Korlatović, Mirani-Jagodnja [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Nahija dissolved by 1540 (villages absorbed into Husrev-beg vakuf [[Kozulova-Polje]]).

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
Under [[Nadin]] pertinenza in 1689 Cornaro Liga [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (docs 329, 330, 377). Zaviša Janković's holdings nearby (Bjeline / [[Biljane]], [[Korlat]], Kožulovo polje, Erunik / [[Ervenik]], Kolarine, Perušić, Lisičić).
