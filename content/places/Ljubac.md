# Ljubač

Fortress and locality on the coast between [[Pag]] and Zadar. Uskoks from Jesenice in the Velebit Podgorje were settled between Pag and the fortress of Ljubač (1648) [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]; the [[Dobric|Dobrići]] from Dobropoljci were settled in Ljubač in 1648 [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]. Ljubač was also a transit point in the 1538 Vlach transport to Istria [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].
