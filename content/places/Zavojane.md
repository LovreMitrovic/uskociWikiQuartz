# Zavojane

Village in [[Mostar]]/Zaupa province, Catholic, with church of Madonna; given to Rašić 1692 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (200 souls / 25 families settled at Zavojane, Stigli, Hadži Lukovo).
