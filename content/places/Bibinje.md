# Bibinje

Coastal village south of [[Zadar]] in Smiljanić sub-region (doc 19) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Under Zemunik pertinenza in 1689 Cornaro Liga. Captain Marlatić there (doc 316).
