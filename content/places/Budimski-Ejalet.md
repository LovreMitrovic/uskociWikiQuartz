# Budimski ejalet

Largest Ottoman administrative province of Hungary (Buda eyalet), 1541–1686. Comprised [[Sandzak-Mohac]] (Baranja), [[Backa]], and other northern districts.

Receiver of two demographic waves relevant to Bunjevac history [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]]:
1. **1530s+** — Ottoman deportations of "buntovni vlaški element" from [[Vilajet-Hrvati]].
2. **1606+** — main Bunjevac wave from [[Sandzak-Klis]] / [[Sandzak-Krka]] after the [[Drnis-Rebellion-1608|Drniš revolt]] and other Klis-area unrest, settling the bajska/somborska/subotička nahija depopulated after the 1598 Pálffy migrations.
