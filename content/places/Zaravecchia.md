# Biograd na Moru (Zaravecchia)

Venetian commune on the coast south of [[Zadar]] [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Pertinenza HQ from 1689 Cornaro Liga (with Krčina, Toretta, [[Sv-Filip-i-Jakov|Sv. Filip i Jakov]], [[Pakostane|Pakoštane]]). Mattio Žabetić captain; site of 1704 unrest. Commune that with [[Pakostane|Pakoštane]] plotted Šimun Bortulačić's murder 1692; pardoned 1694. Distinct from [[Stari-Grad-Zaravecchia|Stari Grad]] also called Zara Vecchia in some texts.
