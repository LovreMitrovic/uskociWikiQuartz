# Zrmanja

River and surrounding region between [[Velebit]] and [[Bukovica]]. Ottoman *nahija* under the Vlasi Istre (131 houses, 22 villages, 1528–1530), led by primićuri [[Jakov-Poropat-b????-Zacadje]] and [[Slade-Livoja-b????-Zrmanja]] [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Maintained territorial integrity into 1550 register (as "Zrmanja Istrija"). Includes settlements [[Krupa]], [[Zegar]], [[Ervenik]], [[Bilisani]].
