# Golubić (kod Obrovca)

Village in [[Bukovica]]. 1540: 12 non-Muslim houses in nahija Karin; residence of pop Stipan area (with [[Dragomirani]]). Home of Rajko, son of pop, recorded same year [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].
