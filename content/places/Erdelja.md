# Erdelja (Prikanci)

Vlach settlement on the Bosiljevsko vlastelinstvo of the Frankapan. Founded c. 1540s by a smaller Vlach group in *libertini* service to grof Frankapan [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Catholic / pokatoličeni Vlachs.
