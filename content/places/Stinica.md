# Stinica

Port near [[Karlobag]], 5 miles from Lukovo, with church of Madonna; settled in 1696 by Bukovčani who fled to [[Sv-Juraj-Senj|Sv. Juraj]] near Senj during the Candian war [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
