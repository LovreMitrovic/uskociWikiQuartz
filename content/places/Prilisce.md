# Prilišće

Village on the ribničko vlastelinstvo of the Zrinski. 1544: settled by 25 Vlach Mušković families from Obrovac who fled summer pasture in [[Lika]] to Habsburg territory; granted hereditary *libertini* status by [[Nikola-Zrinski-b????]] (deed 25 June 1544) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Catholic, čakavski (ikavski) speakers; likely from the [[Vlasi-Istre]] population.
