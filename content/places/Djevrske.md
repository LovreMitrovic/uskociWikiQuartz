# Đevrske

Village in the Bukovica; origin of uskoci who resettled near [[Petrcane|Petrčane]]. Harambaša Smoljan Miković led men "iz Đevrsaka" at Ribnik, 1648 (doc. 39) [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]].
