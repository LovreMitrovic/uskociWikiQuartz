# Pazarište

Lika village settled in 1690 by Bunjevac families during the [[Lika-Bunjevac-Colonization-1690|recolonization of Lika–Krbava]]. Core Bunjevac nucleus alongside [[Smiljan]] and [[Lovinac]].

Founding settler: **porkulab [[Milos-Starcevic-b1656-Veliki-Zitnik]]**, holding 168 štrikova zemlje per the 1712 popis (Kaser 2003) across Pazarište, [[Vaganac]], and **[[Veliki-Zitnik]]** [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]]. 1712 župnik: **[[Juraj-Smolcic-b????-Pazariste]]**.

The Starčević lineage based at [[Veliki-Zitnik]] (a sub-locality of Pazarište) produced four generations later **[[Sime-Starcevic-b1784-Lika]]** and **[[Ante-Starcevic-b1823-Veliki-Zitnik]]**.
