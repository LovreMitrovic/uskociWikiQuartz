# Biograd na Moru

Coastal town of northern Dalmatia south of [[Zadar]], called **Zaravecchia** (Zara Vecchia) in Venetian records. Venetian commune and pertinenza HQ from the 1689 Cornaro Liga (with Krčina, Toretta, [[Sv-Filip-i-Jakov|Sv. Filip i Jakov]], [[Pakostane|Pakoštane]]); Mattio Žabetić captain; site of 1704 unrest [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Home of the *Biograd kapetani* in Venetian service from the late 16th c. — e.g. [[Juraj-Matkovic-b????-Biograd]] (1581) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Among the places where the [[Spingaroli]] were attested. With [[Pakostane|Pakoštane]] plotted Šimun Bortulačić's murder 1692; pardoned 1694.

## Notes
Venetian **Zaravecchia** / **Zara Vecchia** = modern Biograd na Moru (merged from former separate pages Biograd, Zaravecchia). Distinct from [[Stari-Grad-Zaravecchia|Stari Grad]] (a separate locality near Biograd, in the Mitrović sub-region) and from [[Starigrad]] (Starigrad-Paklenica).
