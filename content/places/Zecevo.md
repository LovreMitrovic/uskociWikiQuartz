# Zečevo

Two distinct historical claims:

## 16th c. — Vlach nahija
Ottoman Vlach *nahija* (50 houses, 11 villages) in 1528–1530, headed by primićur [[Mikula-Blazic-b????-Zecevo]] [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Dissolved by 1540, lands absorbed into [[Kozulova-Polje]] vakuf and [[Ostrovica]] nahija.

## 17th c. — Battle ground (Desnica)
Site of two pivotal battles in the Candian War:

### Battle of Zečevo I (1 August 1648)
Vuk Mandušić killed defending the small castle of Zečevo against an Ottoman raid that had pillaged Zaravecchia. **Vuk taken alive after killing four Turks himself, kept alive a few hours, beheaded; Ilija Smiljanić helped bury his torso.** 50 Turks dead, ~20 of *naših* including Vuk, harambaše Ščandić, kapetan Pero Aričić, harambaša Ilija Cverljević, harambaša Zuanne Kraljević, both Galiotović brothers, one captured. Documented by ~5 forensic depositions (docs. 42–46).

### Battle of Zečevo II (1 May 1666)
Filipjakov *glagolska zabeleška* (doc. 151): *"Taj isti dan poginuše Kotarani na Zečevu i tote poginu ljudi niko od 73 čovika među kojima poginu dobri junak Vučko Močivuna. Iz samoga Filipjakova poginu ljudi 16. Pisa ja Nikola Kužinović budući žalan i vele žalostan za našom lipom braćom bog im dušu pomilova."* — 73 Kotarani killed including Vuk Močivuna; o. Filip Kučić (sepolto al duomo) also fell — Cornaro's report doc. 153.

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
Castle with advance guards near [[Skradin]] (doc 51) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Defensive position in [[Bukovica]] May 1688. Under [[Ostrovica]] pertinenza in 1689 Cornaro Liga.
