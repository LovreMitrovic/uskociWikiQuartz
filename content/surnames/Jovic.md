# Jović

## Bearers
- **Garga (Grga) Jović** — killed 1662-02-13 at Otres (doc. 119).
- **Maraš Jović** — same.
- **Ivesko Jović da Razanze, kapetan** — 1678 in Radoš's report on Sv. Juraj uskoks; named alongside Dragosav Capelletto Budin as one of the four arbitri (doc. 239). 1684-03-01 named *capitano* of Ražanac (doc. 381).
- **kapitan Gioca** (1684) — possibly = Ivesko Jović as Italian *Gioca* = Jovica diminutive (doc. 385).

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)

- **Iveško Jović (Ivesco Giouich)** — captain of Ražanac; **age 80 in 1699** (b. ~1619); testified in Sfakioto's Grimani-commission protocols about Venetian invasion of Lika before Imperials (doc. 315) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
