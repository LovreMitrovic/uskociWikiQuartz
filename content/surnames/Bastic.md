# Bastić

## Bearers
- [[Bastic-pasha-b????-Zvornik|Bastić]] — newly appointed pasha of Zvornik; September 1686 went together with the new Bosnian alajbeg Filipović (and 600 Turks) to Livno to repair the houses and towers burned in the first Livno raid; that operation triggered the second Livno raid by Janković and Smiljanić (doc 141, 7 Sept 1686) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
