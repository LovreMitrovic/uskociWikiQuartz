# Pierantonii

Zadar patrician family.

## Bearers
- **Antonio Maria Pierantonii** — husband of Caterina Grizogono; abandoned his wife (Sept 1686 had left her c. 1.5 years earlier); his property was the subject of doc 143 (14 Sept 1686), in which Caterina Grizogono ceded to Stojan Janković her perpetual right to redeem a house in the parish of San Stefano / San Simeone (Zadar), originally pledged to Michiel Angelo Gioachini (doc 143) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
