# Grimani

Venetian patrician house.

## Bearers

- **Francesco Grimani** — provveditor in campo at the 1688 Knin conquest; Cornaro's nephew (lines 10162, 10275, 10291, 10307, 10388, 10414, 10461) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **commissario Grimani** — Venetian commissar with whom *fiscale* officials and the *kavalier* Grimani served at the borders (lines 14875, 14911, 15063) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
