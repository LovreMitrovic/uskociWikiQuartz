# Kalinić

Single attestation: Šaramprov "of Kalinika" (Šaramprov di Calinich) is named in connection with Nikanor Stanković of Velika 1698 (doc. 308) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] — the surname appears as a place-modifier rather than as an active bearer.
