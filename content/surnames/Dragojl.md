Single attestation: **knez Dragojl iz Miljevaca** — 1648-10-08 *"sismatico, basso, barba grisa folta, pello nero"* — 4 ducati/month assigned by Foscolo (doc. 47). *Sismatico* = "schismatic" = Orthodox.
