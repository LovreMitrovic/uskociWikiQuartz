# Lakić

## Bearers
- **Matija (Mattio) Lakić** — Morlak of Karin; father of Hajra/Kosa (doc 56) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- [[Hajra-Lakic-b????-Karin|Hajra (Kosa) Lakić]] — daughter of Matija; captured August 1684 by Gračac Turks while threshing millet; sold at Knin to sandžak-beg Mehmed-beg Durakbegović who Islamised her ("Hajra") and made her favourite; recaptured March 1685 in a Klis–Trogir Morlak ambush (doc 56) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
