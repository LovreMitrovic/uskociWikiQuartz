# Sinčević

*Spelling note (2026-09-08, unresolved): the earlier pages read *Sinčević*, the 1756 pages *Šinčević* (from Venetian *Sincevich*). Both are attested and the source does not settle it. Person pages under this hub are therefore inconsistent — **Sinčević** vs **Šinčević**. Left as-is pending a decision rather than picked arbitrarily.*


## Bearers
- **Grubiša Sinčević** (father) and **Žorži Sinčević** (son) — 1684-01-24 hold 4 gold rings, 1 silver ring, 9 silver buttons, gold collana with 8 *perusini* and 250 pearls — Krivelari pawn jewellery later raided (doc. 366).
- **Gregorio Cusinovich detto Sian** (1684-01-24, doc. 366) — *susach* worth 5 reali — possibly the same family.
