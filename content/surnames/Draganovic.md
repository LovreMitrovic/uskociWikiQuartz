# Draganović

One of the 24 Krmpote (Vlasi Krmpote) core families in the First Krmpote migration to [[Lic|Lič]] (1605), from the [[Medvidja|Medviđa]]/[[Bukovica]] area (Pavličević 1989) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].
