# Sorić

Catholic Vlach surname, Zadar-hinterland origin. Šarić lists it among Zadar-hinterland anthroponyms with medieval-modern continuity [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. The line straddles the 1636 župnik through to the Vrana uprising 1683.

## Bearers
- **don Stjepan (Stipan) Sorić** — 1636 župnik in Gorica/Tinj parish; 1647 *governator de Morlacchi*; killed at Ribnik 1648-07-17. See [[Stipan-Soric-b????-Gorica]] for full bio.
- **Mate Sorić** — brother of don Stjepan; succeeded him 1648-07-31 as Goričanski capo; received the gold chain originally meant for Stjepan.
- **Gregorio Sorić** — 1683 implied as relative pursuing recovery of pop Stjepan's preserved hand from Margarita Ivančević (doc. 311).
- **pop Stevan Sorić of Tepšinci** — 1684-03-23 with knez of Tepšinci and Parčići (Lika), pleads to provveditore Donà for asylum and bread for the would-be migrant Lika families; wants to be settled at Obrovac (donji + gornji), per his Cyrillic letter (doc. 385). NOT obviously the same family but the *pop Stevan* / *pop Stjepan* coincidence in the same diocese is suggestive.

## Notes
- The same surname appears among the Šibenik *pravoslavni* Morlaci — *Steffano Sare* harambašša at Sv. Zulijan (doc. 84, 1654) — but *Sare* may be distinct from *Sorić*, hard to pin down.
