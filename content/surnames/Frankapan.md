# Frankapan (Frankopan)

Croatian-Hungarian magnatial house. [[Krsto-I-Frankapan-b????]] held estates in Istria, Kras, and Notranjska; lost them spring 1527 after siding with Ivan Zápolya against Ferdinand Habsburg. The dispossession of his Vlach subjects was a possible trigger for the 1528 return migration to Ottoman territory [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Bosiljevsko vlastelinstvo (later Frankapan) hosted a smaller Vlach *libertini* settlement at [[Erdelja]].

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)

The Frankapan estates of Jablanac and Starigrad on the Velebit Podgorje feature in Giacinto Peri's 1695 report on Lika/Krbava/Turkish Croatia [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc. 281):
- **Toni Francesco Sokolić** — still alive at time of Peri's report 1695; collected dues at Starigrad and Jablanac during last Frankopans' time (doc. 281).
- **1649 Diet of Bratislava (Posonia) art. 32** — restored Jablanac and Starigrad to Frankapans; lost again to Senjani after death of last Frankopan (doc. 281).
- 1699 Imperial-Venetian boundary commission referenced Obrovac as "antico feudo dei conti Frangipani" (per Imperial side claim) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
