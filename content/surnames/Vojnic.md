# Vojnić (Vojihnić)

Catholic Vlach (Bunjevac) lineage from western Hercegovina — one of the three core Bunjevac katuns of the 1477 defter (with [[Krmpotic]] and [[Sladovic]]).

## Medieval and early-Ottoman attestations
- **1408** — first mention as **Vlasi Vojihnići** in a charter of Bosnian king Stjepan Ostoja; subjects of the W. Hum noble line Radivojević-Vlatković [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].
- **1477** — Hercegovina defter, **nahija Mostar**: džemat of [[Vukasin-sin-Vojihne-b????-Mostar]], 10 houses + 3 unmarried. Wintered/summered with vojvoda Vladimir's katun across W. Hercegovinian planinas and župas.

## Klis sandjak phase
Colonized into the conquered Croatian frontier in the early 16th c. The village **Vojnić near Trilj** likely a transit toponym. By Hrabak's evidence, also at Dobropoljci in [[Bukovica]]. After the [[Cyprus-War-1570-1573]], [[Ferhat-beg-Sokolovic-b????]] resettled them on his new **Tinj vakuf** (1573–1574); the area thereafter known as **Vojnići** or **Županovići**, after starešine [[Milos-Zupanovic-b????-Tinj]] and [[Filip-Zupanovic-b????-Tinj]]. 1550: dzemat of knez [[Mihovil-Vojihnic-b????-Sinj]] in nahija Sinj [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].

## 17th c. — split between three powers
- **1605** — 15 Vojnić families joined the Krmpote migration to [[Lic]]: Božić, Galešić, Jurjević, Matijević, Pavličić, Petković, Stojčević, Vojnić, Vojnović.
- **1619** — starešine Miloš Županović and [[Mate-Vuksic-b????-Tinj]] negotiated with the senjski kapetan to bring ~1500 Vojnići to Habsburg territory; migration did not happen.
- **1636** — Forza vizitacija reports **župa Županovići**: 6 villages (Gorica, Raštane, Prkos, Bubnjane, Lišane Tinjske, Brkljačina Polača), 1550 souls; župnik [[Stipan-Soric-b????-Gorica]].
- **1647** (Candian War) — most Vojnići crossed to Venetian territory. Knez [[Filip-Zupanovic-b????-Tinj]] led part of them to **[[Valtura]]** near Pula.

## Possible Bačka connection
Per Sekulić 1986, the **Vojnić** family of [[Subotica]] (ennobled 1741, listed among Subotica graničari 1687) traces origin to Dalmatia — Šarić raises but doesn't resolve whether they descend from the Tinj/Županović Vojnići [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].
