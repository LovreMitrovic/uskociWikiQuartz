# Milovanić

Vlach kindred. [[Petar-Milovanic-b????-Medvidja]] attested 1518 as *catonarius districtus Zelengradi* — earliest dated record. By 1528 head of Bukovica dzemat in Medviđa [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Defected to Venice 1538; entered Venetian military service. 1570 captain Slade Milovanić commanded Croatian cavalry on Zadar territory.
