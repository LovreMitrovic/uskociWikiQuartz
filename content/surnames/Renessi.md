# Renessi (Reneši)

## Bearers
- **kapetan Bošičio (Bossichio) Renesi** — 1647 commands the company in which Petar Smiljanić was first enrolled (doc. 20).
- **Žorži (Zorzi) Renessi** — 1677-02-17 governator of Zaravecchia and 4 littoral villages (doc. 232).
- **Ana of alfier Reneši** — 1677-10-01 comare at the baptism of Nikola Nikolić, son of Mitar Nikolić-Pivljanin and Janja Janković (doc. 236).
- **Francesco Renessi** — 1682-11-11 testimonio at Stojan Janković's signing for Marko Pozder's horse (doc. 298).
