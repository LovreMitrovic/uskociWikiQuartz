# Karlović

Croatian noble lineage, knezovi krbavski. [[Ivan-Karlovic-b????-Krbava]] held estates in Krbava and Lika; sometimes paid Ottoman *harač* [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. 1514: 3,000 of his subjects taken in Ottoman raid; same year hundreds of his peasant and Vlach families fled to Ottoman territory. Mills on Zrmanja later went to the Husrev-beg vakuf. 1582 Rab rector references "Karlo Karlović" as former owner of Bukovica villages.
