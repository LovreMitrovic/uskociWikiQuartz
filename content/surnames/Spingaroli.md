# Spingaroli

Zadar noble; long-serving Venetian governator.

## Bearers in Desnica II
- **Francesco Spingaroli** — appointed **governor of pertinenza 9 (Zaravecchia)** by Cornaro in 1689 Liga reform (doc. 224); reappointed governor of Biograd, Pakoštane and Vrana by Dolfin March 1694 (doc. 275); helped at sieges of Castelnuovo and Knin (doc. 286). Age 32 in 1699 (b. ~1667), testified in Sfakioto's protocols (doc. 315); supplied 25 sessane and 8 horses for Grimani 1699 (doc. 314); in 1704 one of three serdars sent to negotiate with rebellious Bukovica Morlacchi (with Gvozden Radasović and Smiljanić, doc. 352); 1726 governor of Zaravecchia for Lower Kotari (doc. 377) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
