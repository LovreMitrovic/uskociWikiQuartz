# Žabetić

Family of Zaravecchia (Biograd na Moru) connected by faction-violence to two of the central late-17th-c. Kotari incidents.

## Bearers in Desnica II
- **[[Mattio-Zabetic-b????-Zaravecchia|Mattio Žabetić]]** of Zaravecchia — co-leader of the [[Bukovica-Revolt-1704|1704 Bukovica revolt]] alongside pop [[Petar-Jagodic-Kuridza-b1666-Bukovica|Petar Jagodić-Kuridža]] and [[Ilija-Nanic-b????-Zegar|Ilija Nanić]]; arrested in Venice 1704. [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **Brother of Mattio Žabetić** (unnamed) — quartered for participation in the 1692 [[Bortulacic-Murder-Vrana-1692|Bortulačić murder at Vrana]]. [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]

## Notes
- The two events bracket Žabetić family involvement in faction violence against Venetian-recognised authority over an ~12-year span: brother executed for the Vrana murder of 1692, then Mattio himself ringleader of the 1704 Bukovica revolt.
- Connected family: [[Tubic|Tubić]] (Tomaso Tubić, banished and pardoned for the same 1692 cluster).
