# Dević

Surname listed by Šarić among Zadar-hinterland anthroponyms with medieval-modern continuity [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].

## Bearers (Desnica)
- **Milinko Dević** — harambaša of [[Miranje]] 1646-03-26 (doc. 2 [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]) — co-petitioner with pop Petar Popović and Petar Grudarović for *uskakanje* of 20 families.
- **Ivan Dević** — harambaša 1663-05-08, posted with villagers of Bogoselci, Bibinje, Kolarin, Brkljača, Sukošan at the **Margitina Voda** pass-guard (doc. 130).
- **Stevan Deović** — 1664 Zadar burgher (doc. 140); kreditor of Vuk Močivuna; held a Turk slave Oman Miličević for debt-recovery.
