# Rukavina

Major Bunjevac lineage of [[Lika]] and [[Podgorje]]. The family preserves an oral genealogy tracing its origin to the **Vladimirovići** of Hum — a noble line said to have been driven out of Raška by the Nemanjići. Šarić treats this as a "raška reminiscencija" reflecting how Bunjevac elite memory absorbed early-Hercegovinian "raško razdoblje" rather than a literal medieval pedigree (the legend was nourished by 18th-c. baroque Slavism and Kačić Miošić's pjesmarica) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

## Documented presence
- **Pre-Candian War** — Rukavine were starešine of catholic Morlachs in [[Jasenica]] in [[Morlakija]] (per Forza 1636 supplementary information cited by Šarić) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].
- **1691** — granted estates by Habsburg authorities in **[[Sv-Mihovil]], [[Sv-Rok]], [[Vratnik]], [[Ricice]], [[Kosinj]]** as part of the Bunjevac influx into recovered Lika (Fras 1835).
- **1712** Lika–Krbava popis — Rukavina present in the parishes of Budak, Ribnik, Udbina (Lika regiment); Perušić, Kompolje (Otočac regiment); Drežnik-grad, Vaganac (Ogulin regiment).

Andrija Kačić Miošić's 1756 pjesmarica names Rukavine among "vitezovi hrvatski" alongside [[Starcevic]], [[Balenovic]] and [[Dosen]] — illustrating the surname's military prominence by mid-18th c. [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]]
