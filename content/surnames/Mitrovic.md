# Mitrović (later Janković-Mitrović)

The clan that would become the legendary *kotarska* lineage **Janković-Mitrović**. Per Stojan Janković's 1670 supplica (doc. 170, [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]), five brothers — **Janko, Giovanni, Stefan, Pavle and Andrija** — abandoned their patria of **Žegar** at the start of the Candian War (~1646–47) and brought ~70 households / ~150 fighting men into Venetian Ravni Kotari, settling at Posedarje.

The change of family-name from **Mitrović** (the patronymic from grandfather *q. Dimitri*) to **Janković** (from *Janko*, the eldest brother) happens in Venetian usage during Janko's own lifetime; the official pay-rolls and documents continue to call them *Mitrovich* into the 1680s.

## Bearers attested in this source
- [[Janko-Mitrovic-b1605-Zegar|Janko Mitrović]] q. Dimitri — Žegar → Posedarje; harambaša → serdar; d. 1659-02-28 in Zadar.
- [[Vukadin-Mitrovic-b????-Zegar|Vukadin Mitrović]] (probably brother or cousin of Janko) — Žegar; harambaša; d. 1653 defending [[Novigrad-Dalmatia|Novigrad]].
- **Jakov Mitrović**, son of Vukadin — slave at one point; pay assigned to him 1653 (doc. 74) and again 1681-05-08 by ducal of Doge A. Contarini for unpaid biscotto arrears (doc. 274).
- **Giacomo Mitrović da Islam** — captured by Stojan Janković (doc. 187, 1671 plundering of Greek merchants in Posedarje); separately Giacomo Mitrović bandit of Posedarje pardoned 1673 (doc. 204).
- [[Stojan-Jankovic-b1635-Zegar|Stojan Janković]] (= Stojan Mitrović) — son of Janko; the famous kavalier.
- [[Zavisa-Jankovic-b????-Zegar|Zaviša/Sabba Janković]] — son of Janko; m. Maria Lase 1676-08-17.
- [[Ilija-Mitrovic-b????-Zegar|Ilija Mitrović]] (the bandit) — son of Janko; banished 1680; instigator of the Vrana uprising 1683–84.
- **Konstantin Janković** — son of Stojan; capitan of 50 fanti from 1679-02-09.
- **Zorzi-Alvise Janković** b. 1674-04-23, son of Stojan and Vinka.
- **Anastazija Janković** b. 1675-02-26, daughter of Stojan and Vinka.
- **Josip Janković** b. 1677-09-30, son of Stojan and Antonia Reci.
- **Nikola Janković** — son of Stojan, in cavalry from 1675.
- **Janja Janković** (b. ~1655 d. 1680) — daughter of Janko; m. [[Dmitar-Nikolic-b????-Plav|Dimitri Nikolić-Pivljanin]].
- **Mitar/Dimitri Mitrović** "da Grazaz" — slave of [[Stojan-Jankovic-b1635-Zegar|Stojan]] in his Zadar house, escaped 1680 (doc. 266, 267).
- **Alil son of Mitar Mitrović da Grazaz** — captured slave (10 May 1680, doc. 266).

## Notes
- The kin-link **Mitrović ↔ Pivljanin** (through Janja's marriage) is essential context for the 1683–84 Vrana uprising: Bajo Nikolić-Pivljanin and his brother Mitar shared this family.
- The **Sinobad case** (April 1684, docs. 391, 396) accuses Ilija Mitrović of murdering Perica Sinobad (Jovan's father) and burning his houses in Budin.

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)

The Mitrović/Janković lineage is the central thread of [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] from 1684 through 1731, with extensive documentation of the second and third generations.

### Second generation (children of Janko)
- **Stojan Janković** — repeatedly referenced as "quondam kavalier Gianco" by 1689 onwards; the 1689 Cornaro attestation for his brother Zaviša notes Stojan's earlier service [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc. 225).
- **Zaviša/Sabba Janković (Saviša Mitrović detto Janco)** — central figure of the chunk; Cornaro's 1689 attestation documents his deeds at Sinj, Zadar, Vrcerev Vakup (where wounded), and Knin [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc. 225). 1692-08-20 partnership contract with Bortolo and Zuanne **Gambiraži** of Obrovac for 1000 ducats trading capital, brokered by Lomazzi (doc. 254). 1692-03-17 surety bond of 2000 ducats with Oratio Lantana for behavior of brother Ilija (doc. 246). Pay raised from 30 to 50 ducats/month by Doge Silvestro Valier dukal of 1697 (docs. 303, 306). 1696-06-27 made cavalier of San Marco by Doge Valier (doc. 288). 1701-06-05 took oath at relics of St. Šimun in Zadar challenged by conte Frane Posedarski (doc. 330). 1701-11-26 bought half of Olib island from conte Giacomo Califfi q. Francesco for 8000 ducats (doc. 336). 1702 led troops to Italy in War of Spanish Succession; died early 1702 leaving widow + 3 creatures including son Ilija. Pay raised to 50 ducats. Could not write Italian — Tomadelli signed for him (doc. 279); signed in Cyrillic ("Ja Zaviša Mitrović potrugjem", doc. 270). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **Ilija Mitrović (the rebellious)** — central villain 1690–1693; refused to meet provveditor Molin 1690 (doc. 228); fled Obrovac on Molin's arrival; killed Bože Milković's brother and other Morlachs (doc. 243); reconciled with Milković and Sinobad parties by Dolfin's mediation 1692 (doc. 247); 15 ducats/month restored by terminacija of 1 April 1692 (doc. 248); accused of murdering Andrea Tomišić in Gorica 1692 (doc. 268); died of natural causes ("rilasciata natura") late 1693 (doc. 269); per docs. 305, 379 he was the third brother of Stojan and Zaviša, lived in Zadar with wife and children. [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]

### Third generation (children of Stojan, Zaviša, Ilija)
- **Konstantin Janković** — son of Stojan; capitano of Croat cavalry from 1686 (ducal 7 Nov 1686, no public expense); died **1692-02-27 aged ~12**; buried in Greek church of Sant'Elia by curato don Andrea Caldana. Death recorded by Caldana (doc. 244). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **Nikola Janković** — son of Stojan; raised 500 oltramarini per ducal 7 Nov 1686 (doc. 249).
- **Mijat (Michiel) Janković** — son of Ilija; died **1692-08-23**; buried at Sant'Elia (doc. 255).
- **Nicolò Mitrović** — son of Ilija; appointed capitano of Croat cavalry company (formerly Konstantin's) on **1692-04-01**, with tenente training troops until of age (doc. 249); ducal of 21 Sept 1702 confirmed life tenure (doc. 369); died 1709, replaced by brother Stojan (younger).
- **Stojan Mitrović (younger)** — son of Ilija (the rebellious), nephew of original kavalier Stojan; made captain of new 50-man company guarding Keglević fort 1701 (doc. 325); given Šimun Jere's cavalry company by Carlo Pisani in 1712/13 (doc. 371); 1709 inherited brother Nicolò's cavalry company.
- **Marija Mitrović** — daughter of Ilija the rebellious; married **Vučen Stojisavljević** of Vrlika; mother of Petar Mitrović-Stojisavljević raised in the Mitrović household (doc. 379).
- **Ilija Janković (younger)** — son of Zaviša; minor at father's death 1702; reserved 50 ducats then 20; 1701 captain of Italy-bound company; 1705-08-20 granted hereditary count title with Nicolò and Stojan (doc. 360); 1717 made full serdar of Upper Kotari (40 ducats/month); died ~1731. Possibly party to alleged duel with priest Marko Mesić at Lukovo (doc. 364, footnote). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]

### Fourth generation
- **Kirijak Dedić-Mitrović (Janković)** — sister's son of deceased serdar Ilija Janković (the younger); alfiere; appointed serdar of Upper Kotari **1731-11-01** by Vendramin (docs. 381–384); minor at appointment, deputy assigned. [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]

### Other Mitrović bearers
- **Filippo Mitrović** — capitano of 56-foot company in Obrovac, dismissed by Molin Jan 1690 (doc. 227). Distinct from Filip Smiljanić. [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **Rade Mitrović** — Obrovac inhabitant 1696 (doc. 284). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **Petar Mitrović** (= Petar Stojisavljević) — assumed Mitrović cognomen having been raised in the Mitrović house; appointed serdar of Knin Krajina **1726-02-23** by Niccolò Erizzo (docs. 376, 379); removed by ducal of 1 Dec 1729 in favor of Giacomo Sinobad (doc. 380). See [[Stojisavljevic]]. [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]

### 1705 hereditary count title
Doge Alvise Mocenigo dukal of **1705-08-20** granted hereditary *conte* title to Ilija (younger), Nicolò, and Stojan Janković-Mitrović in recognition of family service [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc. 360).

### Notes
- Doc. 288 ducal confounds "carambassà Gianco Mitrouich capo principale de Morlachi di Obrouazzo" with "sardar Stojan Mitrovich detto Gianco" — these are two distinct persons (Janko = founder; Stojan = Janko's son called Gianco).
- The 1670 Janković investitura on the house of Jusuf-aga Tunić at Islam continued to be referenced as a Mitrović holding in 1700 with 400 gognali (docs. 329, 330).
