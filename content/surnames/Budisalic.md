# Budisalić

Brothers from upper Obrovac, one Muslim galley-slave and one converted Christian settler.

## Bearers in Desnica II
- **Resep (Redžep) Budisalić** — Turk from upper Obrovac; on Venetian galley; **released 1697-01-29** by Doge Aluise Mocenigo (per dukal docs. 300, 301), to also convert; replaced by Resep Bajramović of Vakup [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Nicolò Budisalić** — formerly Turk from upper Obrovac; **converted Christian**, lives in **[[Bilisane|Bilišane]]**; brother of Resep (doc. 300) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].

## Notes
- The surname Budisalić (variant Budisaglić) is also attested in 1605 Lič settler list of Krmpote families ([[Krmpotic]]).
