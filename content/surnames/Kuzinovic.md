# Kužinović

*Spelling note: `canonical` tidied 2026-09-08 from "Kužinović (Kuzinović)" to **Kužinović**; the undotted *Kuzinović* is kept in `variants` via the source spellings. The 1610 village entries use three forms — *Cuzenouich*, *Chuxinouich*, *Chuxinoucih* — treated as one family.*

## Bearers
- **Nikola Kužinović iz Filipjakova** — 1666-05-01 author of the *glagolska zabeleška* on the back of the Kašićev Ritual recording the death of Vučko Močivuna and 73 Kotarane at Zečevo (doc. 151). Single most poignant Croatian-language source-text in the volume.
- **Gregorio Cusinovich detto Sian** — 1684-01-24 in the Krivelari pawn-jewellery list (doc. 366); pos. same family.
