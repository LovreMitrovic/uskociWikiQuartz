# Prejić (Prijić)

Distinguished Vlach lineage — only common surname (versus single-name) recorded in 1528–30 register among Vlasi Istre, sign of high social standing [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. [[Petar-Prejic-b????]] held the largest non-Muslim *timar* in vilajet Hrvati 1530. Some converts to Islam (Ferhad, Hasan, Bogdan); some uskoks in Bela krajina ([[Stanoje-Prijic-b????-Marindol]] 1551).
