# Poropat (Poropatić)

Old Vlach kindred attested in Zadar sources from late 14th c. as part of Butko Brančić's katun [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Surname derives from the Vlach anthroponym **poropat** ("nomad / beskrevetnik"). Returned from Buzetski Kras to Zrmanja c. 1528. Some defected to Venetian territory 1538 (Novigrad distrikt); a long-term concentration remained on the Buzet Venetian side (Trstenik, Dane, Lanišće). Mentioned in [[Ljubac]] in 1610 census.
