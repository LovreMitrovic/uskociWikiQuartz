# Šarčević

Bačka variant of [[Saric|Šarić]]. Foremost member: **[[Boza-Sarcevic-b????-Subotica]]** (Ambrozije Šarčević) — launched the *Bunjevački kalendar* in [[Subotica]] in 1868, foundational publication of the 19th-c. Bunjevac narodni preporod alongside [[Ivan-Antunovic-b1815-Backa]]'s 1870 *Bunjevačke i šokačke novine* in Kaloča [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].
