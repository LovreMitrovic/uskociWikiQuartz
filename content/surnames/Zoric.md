# Zorić

Bjelaj-area capi family of the 1692 mass migration.

## Bearers in Desnica II
- **Todoro Zorić (Todor Zorich)** — capo of Bjelaj province with Đurić and Pirić; led ~5000 souls to Venetian devotion 1692; settled in Plavno area [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (docs. 251, 252).
