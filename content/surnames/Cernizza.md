# Crnica

## Bearers
- **col. Vuk Crnica** — colonnello, served from start of Candian War. 1655-02-25 ducal of Doge Molino orders his arrest with Žorži Posedarski, Petar Šope, Vasilij Slaven for fleeing the Knin battle (doc. 92, 93). 1671-02-01 — investitured by Barbaro with 400 *kanapa* in Tinj/Bubnjane/Lišani plus the ruined tower of Ferhat-pašić (doc. 182). **Died 1671-04-29** per Sv. Stošija matica umrlih (Desnica's footnote on doc. 182). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **kavalier Marko Crnica** — son of Vuk; co-investitured with father (doc. 182). 1682-11-06 elected sopraintendent of the Liga from Nin (doc. 295, 297). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **kapetan Žorži (Zorzi) Crnica** — 1678-06-29 patron of a galiotta posted at Zaravecchia (doc. 249). 1684 — surnamed *capitan Vuco Cernizza* (different person? — same as Vuk?) is at Sukošan dispatching the galiotta to *Morlacca* (doc. 241).

## Notes
- The 1671 investitura is *aspirational*: Desnica's footnote notes that *"Sve su Barbarove investiture ostale mrtvo slovo, jer je kopnena Dalmacija kod razgraničenja od 1671 g. opet pripala Turcima. Ipak su neke od ovih infeudacija posle konačnog izgona Turaka bile oživele."*
- Vuk's tower (the *kula begova Ferhat-pašića*) survived in **Tinj** until ~1934, when local villagers blew it up with mines (Desnica's footnote on doc. 182).

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)

- **Bože (Boze) Cernizza** — at garrison of **[[Vrlika]]** 1688; captain (referenced 1688). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **Two Crnice (Cernizza) brothers** — rebels from Vrlika; abandoned Vrlika garrison and went over to Ottomans 1697; later recalled and **died in Šibenik prison** (doc. 305) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
