# Karalija

## Bearers
- [[Karalija-b????|Karalija]] — *famoso* Ottoman *četobaša* (raid commander); on the return of Janković and Smiljanić from the second Livno raid in early September 1686 his band was intercepted in the Ottoman frontier; Karalija and two of his principal companions were beheaded (doc 141, 7 Sept 1686, Stojan and Smoljan's deposition) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
