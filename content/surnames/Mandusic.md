# Mandušić

Šibenik-zaleđe lineage of harambaše. **Vuk (Vučen) Mandušić** — the *Vuk Mandušić* of the *kotarski* epic — was childless; the lineage continues through his sister's son **Tadija Vrančić**, after whom the family-name shifts to *Mandušić-Vrančić* (cf. doc. 60, 1652-04-21).

## Bearers
- [[Vuk-Mandusic-b????-Sibenik|Vuk (Vučen) Mandušić]] — d. 1648-08-01 at Zečevo; harambaša / kapo de Morlacchi di Šibenik.
- [[Tadija-Vrancic-b????-Sibenik|Tadija Vrančić]] (a/k/a Tadija Mandušić-Vrančić) — sister's son of Vuk; capitan of his uncle's company from 1648-08-03; later named *Mandušić alias Vrančić* (doc. 60).
- **Žorži Mandušić-Vrančić** — Tadija's father; received 4 ducati + peso di biscotto/month 1652 (doc. 60, sharing pay with son Tadija).
