# Jagodić-Kuridža

Bukovica family with a parish-priest line; the surname appears in many spellings across the Italian-script documents.

## Bearers in Desnica II
- **[[Petar-Jagodic-Kuridza-b1666-Bukovica|pop Petar Jagodić-Kuridža]]** (c. 1666 – 9 April 1749) — Orthodox paroh of Sv. Petar in Bukovica; principal instigator of the [[Bukovica-Revolt-1704|1704 Bukovica revolt]]; condemned to death in absentia, fled to Lika; later imprisoned ~40 years in the Pozzi (Venetian state prison); released 29 March 1746 at age 80, blind and deaf; died at [[Zadar]] (matica umrlih, Sv. Ilija parish, 9 April 1749). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **Petar Jagodić** (lay brother of pop Petar) — d. ~1733. [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **pop Mihailo (Mijat) Jagodić** — paroh of Bilišane; cousin (germano) of pop Petar. [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **Magdalena (Manda) Jagodić** — daughter of pop Petar; traveled to Zadar 1737 to claim father's property (doc 387). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]

## Notes
- The compound surname *Jagodić-Kuridža* combines two designators (kin name + nickname/topographic). Pop Petar's Italian-script renderings all coalesce on a Jagodić root; *Kuridža* drops in Italian renderings. Latin-script *Giadovich* is the most distorted form.
