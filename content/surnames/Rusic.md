# Rušić (Rusić)

Hercegovinian Catholic family from Mostar district.

## Bearers in Desnica II
- **Ivan Rašić/Rušić (Giovanni Rassich)** — from Rusić village (near Mostar); Catholic; led 200 souls / 25 families from Mostar district to Makarska area; settled Zavojane, Stigli, Hadži Lukovo on lands of late Salin-aga Đikić; **appointed harambaša Oct 1692** [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc. 258).
- **Šimun Rusić (Simon Rusich) dalla Torretta (Tornik)** — named arbitrator by Lisičić/Podgrađe side in 1702 dispute with Brgud (doc. 348) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
