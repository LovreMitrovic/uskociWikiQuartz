# Rkać

## Bearers
- [[Rade-Rkac-b????-Lika|Rade Rkać]] — companion of Jovan Sudarović of Parčići, Lika; in the original Italian text named *Rado Grego* — Desnica's footnote (n. 1, doc 114) explains: "U originalu 'Grego', prezrivi naziv za pravoslavne, koji odgovara nazivu *rkać*"; appeared with Sudarović before Valier in Split on 23 November 1685 and proposed Venetian-side settlement in Žegar (doc 114) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].

## Notes
- The "surname" here may be a confessional epithet rather than a true family name; *rkać* is not a name a person used of themselves, so this entry preserves the archival usage rather than asserting a continuing lineage.
