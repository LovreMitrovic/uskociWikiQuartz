# Sfakioto

Greek family of Cretan (Sfakià) origin in Venetian service.

## Bearers in Desnica II
- **Sfakioto** — lawyer/advocate working under Provveditor Generale Grimani during the **1699–1701 [[Border-Demarcation-Grimani-1699-1701|border demarcation commission]]** following the Treaty of Karlowitz; drafted the *cinque processi* establishing Venetian rights at Knin, Sinj, and Zvonigrad.
