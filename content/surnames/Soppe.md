# Šope (Soppe)

Zadar patrician family with multiple branches active over the period.

## Bearers
- **Petar (Pero) Šope (Soppe)** — *governator, capitano equitum* of Zadar; 1655-02-25 banished by ducal of Doge Molino for cowardice at the Knin battle (doc. 92, 93).
- **knez Julio (Giulio) Soppe** — *capitano delle ville littorali*. Active across 1683–84: 1683-10-23 leads the company that at Bokanjac–Boccagnazzo turned back the looters; burned the houses of 4 ringleaders (doc. 322). 1683-12-09 with col. Posedarski sent to investigate (doc. 348). 1684-03-01 governator of Zaravecchia (doc. 381). One of the four 1684 *direttori* (doc. 395).
- **Giovanni Battista Soppe Fortezza, kavalier** — 1668-12-09 leads the Bribir ambush as *direttore* (doc. 163, 165). 1679 (undated) sent by Valier to Žemunik to confer with Hasan-beg Durakbegović and the krčki kadi (doc. 261).
- **Biagio (Biasio) Sope dottore** — 1682-11-06 nominated for the Liga oversight (doc. 296).
- **Simon Sope** — 1682-11-06 same source.

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
- **conte Giulio (Julio) Soppe** — *consigliere* in Zadar, witness to the 14 September 1686 Stojan-Janković purchase contract (doc 143); also serving as one of the four 1684 *direttori* (doc 19) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **kavalier Giovan Battista Soppe Fortezza** — appears across the early Morean War correspondence (docs 15, 19, 24, 32) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Biagio Soppe, dottor** — consigliere of corte di Zara, executor of Marija Smiljanić's 1693 testament (doc. 267) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Giovanni Battista Soppe Fortezza, magnifico signor governator** — nobile zaratino consigliere 1701; **co-owner of half of Olib island** (the other half sold by conte Giacomo Califfi to Zaviša Mitrović 1701-11-26, doc. 336) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
