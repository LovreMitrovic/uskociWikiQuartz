# Modre

The "Turco Modre" gave his name to **Modrino selo** village near St. Peter's church in Bukovica, his casale assigned to Jovan Sinobad 1692 (doc. 259) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Described as *congiunto* (relative) of Sinobad — implying the family had Christian and Muslim branches separated by religion. The casale dispute with Radoš and Sava [[Kresovic|Kresović]] continued into 1697.

## Notes
- Likely a personal name/byname rather than a heritable surname; preserved in the toponym Modrino selo.
