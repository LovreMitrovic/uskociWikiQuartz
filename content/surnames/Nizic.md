# Nižić

Single attestation: **don Šimun (Simon) Nižić** — 1647-04-01 sent by Foscolo to the Velebit Podgorje to persuade Morlachi to *uskočiti* (doc. 3). The accompanying note mentions *"li 4 moscettoni che gia feci pretar al quondam Nisich"* (doc. 7) — a relative previously deceased.
