# Nuncović

## Bearers
- **Nuncović** — Venetian *confidente* (informant); July 1685 wrote letters from Ottoman territory to *governator* Marinović of Forte Opus reporting the Bosnian Pasha's preparations against Duare (Zadvarje) (doc 82, line 3903) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
