# Popović

Surname appearing across the Tromeđa as both clergy and lay knez.

## Bearers
- **pop Petar Popović** (paroh of [[Miranje]] and surrounding Ottoman villages) — 1646-03-26 with harambaše Petar Grudarović and Milinko Dević offers Foscolo to bring 20 Morlach families across; pay 4 ducati/month (doc. 2). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **knez Radivoj Popović** (1683) — capo of village **Dejanović** (Petropolje, between Skradin and Drniš); listed by pop Lazar Dragović in 1500-family Zagorje *uskakanje* petition (doc. 358). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)

- **Đuro Popović detto Opačić (Jura Popovich Opacich)** — harambaša of [[Mokro-Polje|Mokro Polje]]; received 15 stara of millet from Dolfin to distribute as seed; obliged to return same amount; signed 1693-02-23 in Zadar (doc. 265) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
