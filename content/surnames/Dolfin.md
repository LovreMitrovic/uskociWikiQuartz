# Dolfin

Venetian patrician.

## Bearers in Desnica II
- **Daniel Dolfin** — provveditor generale di Dalmazia from Feb 1692, succeeding Alessandro Molin; "kavalier provveditor"; **reconciled the Mitrovići and Milkovići 1692** (doc. 247); ratified many decisions through 1696 (docs. 243 onwards). Gave 1697 relazione to Senate (doc. 305) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Daniel Dolfin (the 4th, "quarto")** — provveditor general of Dalmazia **1737–1738**; handled pop Petar Jagodić-Kuridža's correspondence (docs. 386, 387, 388) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Alvise Dolfin** — Venetian consigliere 1705 (doc. 358) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Nicolo Dolfin** — Venetian senator 1727 (doc. 378) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
