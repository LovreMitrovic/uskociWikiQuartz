# Gambiroža

Surname listed by Šarić among Zadar-hinterland anthroponyms with medieval-modern continuity [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)

- **Zuanne (Ivan) Gambirasi (Gambiroža)** — fratello of Bortolo; partner with Zaviša Mitrović in Obrovac trading company **1692-08-20**; Zaviša contributed 1000 ducats capital, recorded by notary Lomazzi (doc. 254) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Bortolo (Bartol) Gambirasi (Gambiroža)** — fratello of Zuanne; co-partner with Zaviša 1692 (doc. 254) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
