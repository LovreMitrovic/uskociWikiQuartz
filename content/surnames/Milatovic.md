# Milatović

Single attestation: **Martin Milatović** — 1679-05-16, in Valier's prison in Zadar accused of selling Milovan Garković (son of Radojica) to Turks (doc. 247).
- *nipote* (nephew): also called Martin (Milatović).

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)

- **Martin Milatović** — accused raider in 1693-11-04 raid on Gorica (doc. 268) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Possibly the same man as the 1679 attestation.
