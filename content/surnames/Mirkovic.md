# Mirković

## Bearers
- **Matija Mirković da Kožulovo polje** — 1653-01-16 co-signs proxy (doc. 70).
- **harambaša Matija Mirković** — 1663-05-08 leader at the Nadin pass with men of Kosulovac, Bukovica-Bukničani, Frletići and Diklo (doc. 130).
- **kapetan Matija Mirković** — 1664-05-21 named *serdar* by Contarini in place of the killed Marko Milković (doc. 137). One of Contarini's two pre-1664 serdari (the other being Kiakić).
- **Rade Mirčetić** — 1683-12-26 with pop Lazar Dragović in the 1500-family Zagorje plea (doc. 358); same as Frane Mirčetić — see [[Mircetic]].
- **Mirko Knežević** — 1663-05-08 sudac of Posedarje (doc. 131); kin link to Mirković unclear.
- **Pavissa Ivančević and Frane Mirčetić** — 1684-01-04 are described together as the Zagorje uskakanje leaders (docs. 362, 363).

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)

- **Aurelio Mirković (Aurelio Mircovich)** — difensor of Pago; signer of Pago salt contract 1694 (doc. 279) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
