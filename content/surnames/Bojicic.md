# Bojičić

Single attestation: **Alija Bojičić, commandant di Duare ([[Zadvarje]])** — *"famoso Turco"*; killed April–May 1663 by harambaša Mihail Miličević rečeni Kovač of Makarska (doc. 132). Per Šilobadovićeva kronika the date was 1663-04-26; per Vinjalić 1663-05-26.
