# Šiljanović (Siglanović)

Istrian Vlach starešina [[Rade-Siljanovic-b????]] led peasants to former Vrana district as *zakup* on Venetian land in 1549 [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Possible but unconfirmed continuity with the medieval kraljevski Vlach katun **Šiglanovići** of Knin županija (14th c.).
