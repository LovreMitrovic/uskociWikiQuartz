# Detrico (Detrešić)

Zadar patrician house. The provveditori entrust them with confidential paciatorial work on the Krajina.

## Bearers
- **Gregorio (Grgur) Detrico** — 1671-02-26 (Split) Barbaro entrusts him with all *cavallaria pagata* and direction of the Kotarci, with secret instructions (doc. 191). 1671-07 leads 60 cavallaria to receive the Bosnian basha for the razgraničenje. 1675-01-17 stands as compare at the baptism of Stojan Janković's daughter (doc. 207-related). 1678-06-29 in Šibenik instructed by Valier on the post-Žemunik reconciliation, with secret authority to commission private assassinations of named perpetrators (doc. 249).
