# Šarić

Surname documented in vlach/Bunjevac milieu of the Tromeđa from the 17th c. onward. The major **kotarski uskočka** branch is Šibenik-based pravoslavna, centered on serdar **Cvijan/Cvitko Šarić** (active 1647–1668+).

## Bearers in this wiki
- **[[Cvijan-Saric-b????-Sibenik]]** — serdar šibenskih pravoslavnih Morlaka 1647–1668+; recorded 1660 by Evlija Çelebi as "hrvatski razbojnik" [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]]. Wife **Dessa**. See his page for the multi-generational genealogy.
- **Iuan/Ivan Šarić** — son of Cvijan & Dessa; baptised 1654-07-02 Sv. Zulijan Šibenik (docs. 81, 83 [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]).
- **Žorži (Zorzi) Šarić** — *harambassa* in 1654 Sv. Zulijan parish protest list (doc. 84).
- **Zuanne/Jovan Šarić** — son of Cvijan; family pay transferred to him 1662-03-14 by Korner (doc. 123); back-pay arrears settled by ducal of Doge Alvise Contarini 1682-06-03 (doc. 285).
- **Đeno Sarić** (1842) — early recorder of the Buna-river origin legend, who wrote that the "stara postojbina Bunjevaca" was on the Buna [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]] (no separate page).
- **[[Marko-Saric-b????-Zagreb]]** — modern historian, author of [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]] and [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].

The 1868-launched Subotica *Bunjevački kalendar* of **[[Boza-Sarcevic-b????-Subotica]]** is the same surname in its **Šarčević** variant — bačka spelling of Šarić [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].
