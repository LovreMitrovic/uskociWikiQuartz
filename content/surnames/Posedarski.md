# Posedarski (Benja-Posedarski)

Croatian noble kindred holding [[Posedarje]] on Novigrad more, on the original Tromeđa frontier. Continuous from the late-medieval period into the Mletačka era, ennobled and integrated into the Venetian *colonnellato della nazione*. The Posedarski "zlatna knjiga" and family archive are now held by knez Anton Benja-Posedarski in Zadar — extensively cited by Desnica.

## Pre-1646 bearers (per Šarić)
- [[Stjepan-Posedarski-b????]] — chaplain who spoke before Pope Leo X (1519); possibly the same as the Stjepan converted to Islam.
- **Frane Posedarski** (early 16th c.) — knez, converted to Islam.

## 1646–1684 bearers (per Desnica)
- **Frano (Francesco) Posedarski** the **Elder** (d. 1671-01-06 in Zadar, age ~70 — doc. 185): governator → colonnello of *cernide*. Lost two nepoti at the 1647 Diklo / Bokanjac ambush (doc. 13). Buried Sv. Domenico Zadar.
- **Žorži (Zorzi) Posedarski** — son of Frane the Elder; named *capo di tutti li Morlacchi* (general superintendent of all Venetian Morlachi) by ducal of 9 Dec 1664 (doc. 143); pay 500 ducati/year. Died 1679-07-21 at Posedarje, taken to Zadar and buried at Sv. Domenico (doc. 259). His widow appears in the 1680s correspondence.
- **Frano (Francesco) Posedarski** the **Younger** — *nipote* of the elder Frane (and son of Žorži per doc. 232 — his father is Žorži the colonnello). Governator of Nin from 1672-03-20 (doc. 202); after Žorži's death named *capo de Morlacchi e sopraintendente delle cernide* by Valier 1679-08-01 (doc. 260) — but the Senate did not approve, so Stojan Janković got the post 1681 (doc. 275 footnote). One of the four 1684 *direttori* over the Morlachi (doc. 395).
- **Pietro Posedarski** — *capo* / *capitan* of cavalry (compagnia di cavalli) of Zadar; assigned to investigate the Greek-merchant murder 1671-01-14 (docs. 187, 188).
- **Paolo Posedarski** — *capitan* of a Zadar *compagnia paesana de fanti*, posted at Ervenik 1670-08-12 (doc. 174).
- **Antonio Posedarski** — *capitan* of cavalry, posted at Zara Vecchia (doc. 322, 1683).
- **Juraj/Žorži Posedarski** — *capitano d' cavalli* on the Knin campaign 1655; banished by ducal of Doge Francesco Molino for cowardice (fled the field with Battista Benzon) — doc. 92, 93.
- **Anton Benja-Posedarski** — Zadar nobleman who in the 1930s held the family archive; not in the historical narrative but the source-fond's custodian.

## Notes
- The family-archive, **Posedarski Zbornik**, is one of Desnica's principal sources: it preserves Foscolo's correspondence with Frane the Elder (the *governator Possedaria* of doc. 5 et seq., 1647 onward) and other otherwise-untranscribed material.
- The dispatch in doc. 105 (1656-08-10) shows Žorži Posedarski lobbying to be named *colonnello sopra di Morlacchi*, a move Janko Mitrović and the Morlach harambaše explicitly resisted on the grounds that they would not accept a non-patrician commander.

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)

The Posedarski continue as the senior Zadar-noble Morlach commanders into the 18th c.

### New context for Frane (Francesco) the Younger
- 1689 (Cornaro Liga reform, doc. 224): family service noted.
- 1691-02-08 named **colonnello sopraintendente of Kotari** by Molin (doc. 235); both his father conte Francesco (the elder) and uncle Žorži were predecessor joint colonnelli.
- 1692 helped Dolfin reconcile the Mitrović and Milković parties (doc. 247).
- 1696 one of Liga giudici under Dolfin (doc. 286); visited Starigrad, Dračevac, Jasenice, Obrovac for Doge Valier and reported on cannon-deliveries to Karlobag (doc. 290).
- 1701 leased decima at Kotari (per chunk 4); 1701-06-05 challenged Zaviša Janković to oath at relics of St. Šimun (doc. 330).
- 1701-09-10 sent on diplomatic mission to Bosnian Pasha with extensive gift list to Halil-pasha (doc. 333).
- 1702 served Lisičić–Brgud arbitration. [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]

### New bearer
- **conte Pietro Posedarski** — nobile zaratino, **age 43 in 1699** (b. ~1656); witness re Morlacca possession in Sfakioto's Grimani-commission protocols (doc. 315). Probable brother or cousin of Frane the Younger. [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]

### Family note
- Per Molin's 1691 doc. 235: the new colonnello Francesco is "figlio e nipote respective de medemi" — son of conte Francesco the Elder and nephew of his fratello conte Žorži. Both elder brothers were joint colonnelli. [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
