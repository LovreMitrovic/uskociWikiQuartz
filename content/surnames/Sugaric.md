# Šugarić

Surname listed by Šarić among Zadar-hinterland anthroponyms with medieval-modern continuity [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Šugarje in Podgorje is the relocation site for [[Rudelic]] families from Radovin in 1683.
