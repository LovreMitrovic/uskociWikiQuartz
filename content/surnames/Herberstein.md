# Herberstein

Habsburg-Imperial military house.

## Bearers
- **conte di Herberstein (general di Carlistot)** — Habsburg general at Karlovac; in July 1685 corresponded with Venetian provveditor Pietro Valier (lines 4458, 4715, doc 85) about a coordinated raid into Krbava with Croatian troops while Valier engaged the Pasha at the Hercegovina border [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
