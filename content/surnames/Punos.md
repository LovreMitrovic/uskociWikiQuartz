# Punoš

## Bearers
- **Pietro Punoš** — 1648-05-07 brought Mehmed Jelovac of Babodol to provveditore Foscolo on the galley of Lorenco Donà as ransom for his two children (doc. 27).
- **Žorži (Zorzi) Punoš** — 1652-09-20 master of Turkish slave **Tale Burić of Vakup**; Foscarini grants Burić 20-day leave to gather his ransom (doc. 63). 1653-01-16 co-signs the Smiljanić proxy (doc. 70).
