# Marinović

## Bearers
- **governator Marinović** — *governator del Forte Opus* (Opuzen); July 1685 receives confidential letters from the *confidente* Nuncović reporting on the Bosnian Pasha's intentions against Duare/Zadvarje (doc 82, line 3903) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Ivan Marinović** — early 18th-c. resident of [[Krusevo|Kruševo]], a village bordering the Klanac/Buljat district; mentioned in doc cited at line 13783 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Ivan Marinović (kavalier)** — together with kavalier Andrija Butković and Petar Kalčina entrusted with arbitrating a dispute (line 14836) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
