# Rudelić (Rudelja)

Pre-Ottoman Vlachs of the wider Zadar hinterland [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Toponyms Rudele (Kistanje) and Rodaljica reflect their old katun. By 1528 a small dzemat under [[Pavel-Rudelja-b????-Obrovac]] in nahija Obrovac. Branches migrated to Rovinjsko Selo in Istria (village judges) and Radovin in Zadar district; from Radovin in 1683 to [[Karlobag]] and Šugarje, with some to Smiljan in [[Lika]].
