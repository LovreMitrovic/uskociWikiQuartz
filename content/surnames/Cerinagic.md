# Ćerinagić (Kerimagić)

Hercegovinian beg family.

## Bearers in Desnica II
- **Aga Mustibegh Ćerinagić (Mustajbeg)** — Turkish aga of Trebinje; lands and ruined tower in **Padglivie/Trebinje** taken; in 1696 the 12 fields and ruined tower were granted to **Demetrio (Dmitar) Pivljanin**, brother of late Bajo Pivljanin [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc. 287).
