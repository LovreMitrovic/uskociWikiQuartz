# Garković

Single attestation: **Radoica (Radojica) Garković, kućni starješina iz Budina** — 1679-05-16. His son **Milovan Garković** sold to Turks by Martin Milatović. Provveditore Valier protects Radojica from retribution by the Capelletto/Vukčević clan (doc. 247).

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)

The surname appears in the 1689 Cornaro Liga reform context for Ražanac (doc. 224) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
