# Selaković

## Bearers
- **Iviša Selaković da Scardona, habitante a Visovaz** — 1647-12-17 in Šibenik signs (with X) the Petropolje uskakanje contract (doc. 18 [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]).
- **Petronije Selaković, kaluđer iz [[Kistanje]]** ("Krčki kaluđer") — 1647-05-18 already at work for Foscolo on Morlach defection (doc. 11 footnote); 1648-04-15 Foscolo grants him 4 ducati/month, *"Calogero Petronia. Barba e mustacchi castagni, occhi uarii, pel oscuro. Segno sopra la tempia sinistra"* (doc. 23). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
