# Bardi

## Bearers
- **alfier Antonio Bardi** — Zadar-based confidant of Stojan Janković; Stojan's letter from Bribir (doc 121, undated, Jan 1686) is addressed to him as "carissimo amico" — Bardi was to relay news to commissario Marin Michiel; Bardi later witnessed the 14 September 1686 Stojan-Janković purchase contract for the San Simeone house (doc 143) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
