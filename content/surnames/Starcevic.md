# Starčević

Bunjevac lineage that became one of the most consequential families in 19th-c. Croatian political history. The surname appears in the 1627 (second) Krmpote migration to [[Lic]] [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]]; settled in **Veliki Žitnik / Pazarište** after the 1690 colonization of [[Lika]].

## Lika progenitor and the Bunjevačka buna
**Porkulab [[Milos-Starcevic-b1656-Veliki-Zitnik]]** (1656–?) settled at [[Pazariste]] in 1690. Per the 1712 popis (Kaser 2003), he held 168 štrikova (parcels) in Pazarište, Vaganac, and Žitnik, with one son Jakov (age 20) and five other family members.

His conflict with vojvoda [[Marko-Butorac-b????-Lika]] in 1702 sparked the **[[Ribnicka-Buna-1702]]** (also "Bunjevačka buna").

## Lika regiment officers (mid-18th c.)
Miloš's sons **[[Jakov-Starcevic-b????-Pazariste]]** and **[[Matija-Starcevic-b????-Pazariste]]** became kapetani in the newly formed Lika regiment in 1746. Andrija Kačić Miošić, in *Razgovor ugodni naroda slovinskoga* (1756), celebrated Jakov among the "vitezovi hrvatski" — early evidence that Bunjevac kin in Lika were absorbing a Hrvatski (regimental/zemaljski) identity [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

## The grammarian and the founder of HSP
- Jakov's grandson **[[Filip-Starcevic-b????-Pazariste]]** — village knez at end of 18th c.
- Filip's son **[[Sime-Starcevic-b1784-Lika]]** (1784–1859) — priest and grammarian, promoter of "zemaljski" / "pučki" hrvatstvo.
- Šime's nephew **[[Ante-Starcevic-b1823-Veliki-Zitnik]]** (1823–1896) — founder of modern Croatian etnonacionalni identity (Hrvatska stranka prava).

The **Klan Starčevića** continued in the political work of David and Mile Starčević. Šarić uses the lineage as the paradigmatic case of how Lika Bunjevci absorbed a modern Croatian national identity within a single century [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

## Note on confessional drift
The 1712 popis lists **Starčević as a surname shared between Bunjevci (Catholic) and Orthodox Vlasi** in Lika–Krbava — one of 37 such overlapping surnames documenting common Dinaric origin [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].
