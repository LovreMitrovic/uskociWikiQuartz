# Pašić

Bihać-area Muslim aga family; possessed a fortified palace and *recinto* near Boričevac.

## Bearers
- [[Ibrahim-Pasic-b????-Ripac|Ibrahim Pašić]] — *uglednik* of Ripač; January 1686 his palace and *recinto* in the villages around Boričevac were burned by Stojan Janković in a 12-day raid into Lapac; many Turks and Christian Morlachs had taken refuge in the palace and surrendered, the Turks setting fire to it as a sign of withdrawal (doc 118, 14 Jan 1686 deposition of Stojan) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Mehmed-beg Pašić** — nephew of Ibrahim; Udbina spahija; April 1687 captured by Zaviša Janković's *četa* together with the dizdar of Udbina and Ara Kumalić while supervising the ploughing of his lands (doc 133, 12 April 1687) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
