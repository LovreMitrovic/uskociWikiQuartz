# Matković

Family present in two distinct early-modern milieux:
1. **Biograd kapetani** (Venetian service) from late 16th c. — see [[Juraj-Matkovic-b????-Biograd]] (1581) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].
2. **Bosanska franjevačka provincija missionaries** in Podunavlje:
   - **fra [[Anto-Matkovic-b????-Temisvar]]** (1582) — opens school for "Dalmatince" in Temišvar diocese.
   - **don [[Simun-Matkovic-b????]]** (1622) — secular priest who issues the **first explicit "parochia Bunievczi"** request to Congregatio Propaganda Fide for the Kalocsa archdiocese [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].
