# Čačić

## Bearers
- **kapetan Ivan (Jovan) Čačić** of castel Venier — 1663-02 attested by Contarini for beheading **Murat-aga Salihagić, disdar of Perušić** during a counter-pursuit (doc. 127). 1664-05-21 promoted to *serdar* by Contarini, replacing the killed Petar Gargurica (doc. 137).
