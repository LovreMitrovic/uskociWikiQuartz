# Lepur

Surname listed by Šarić among Zadar-hinterland anthroponyms with medieval-modern continuity [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)

- **Gregorio (Grgur) Lepur** — pre-war landholder in Rusichia Draga ([[Lisicic|Lisičić]]); paid duty to Turks; one of 20 families who lived in Lisičić and moved to Perušić during Morean War (doc. 331) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Grgur Lepur (harambaša)** — appears in 1684 as one of the Vrana harambaše signing the petitions to Mocenigo (docs 35, 36) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
