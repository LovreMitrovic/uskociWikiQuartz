# Zelić

Bukovica surname attested in [[Zegar|Žegar]].

## Notes
- Per Desnica's 1951 editorial footnote (to doc 115), the Zelić surname is **still present in [[Zegar|Žegar]]** today (1951). Together with Radmilović, Vukčević, and Nanić, the families "came in 1648 with the Jankovićs to Budim near Posedarje and during the Morean War many returned to Lika; Budim is today (1951) ruins". [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]

## In Desnica II (1684–1749) — bearers

- **Nikola Zelić (Nicola Zelich) da Žegar** — brother-in-law (cognato) of late kavalier Zaviša Mitrović; appointed **vice-serdar of Upper Kotari 1731-12-29** by Sebastian Vendramin to assist the young serdar Kirijak Dedić-Janković; called "zio" (uncle) of Kirijak (doc. 384) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
