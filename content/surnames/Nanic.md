# Nanić

Bukovica surname attested in [[Zegar|Žegar]].

## Bearers attested
- **[[Ilija-Nanic-b????-Zegar|Ilija Nanić]]** of Žegar — co-leader of the [[Bukovica-Revolt-1704|1704 Bukovica revolt]] alongside pop Petar Jagodić-Kuridža and Mattio Žabetić.

## Notes
- Per Desnica's 1951 editorial footnote, the Nanić surname is **still present in [[Zegar|Žegar]]** today (1951). Together with Zelić, Radmilović, and Vukčević, the families "came in 1648 with the Jankovićs to Budim near Posedarje and during the Morean War many returned to Lika; Budim is today (1951) ruins" (footnote to doc 115). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]

## In Desnica II (1684–1749) — extended

- **Ilija Nanić "da Xegar/Segar"** — captain of Islam and Kašić 1698 (doc. 309); 1702 named arbitrator by Brgud villagers in Lisičić-Brgud dispute (doc. 348); **one of three ringleaders of the 1704 Bukovica revolt** alongside pop Petar Jagodić-Kuridža and Mattio Žabetić (docs. 352, 356, 357); banished, condemned to death in absentia, fled to Lika (Imperial side) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
