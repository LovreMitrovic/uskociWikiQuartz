# Nikolić-Pivljanin

Hajduk family from **Piva** (Plav region), resident in Zadar from end of the Candian War to start of the Morean War (Desnica's footnote on doc. 211). Brothers' names: **Bajo, Mitar/Dmitar, Petar.**

The family's link to the [[Mitrovic|Mitrović-Janković]] of Žegar is established by **Mitar's marriage to Janja Janković** (sister of Stojan), 1675-01-17 at Sv. Ilija Zadar (doc. 210). Their son **Nikola Nikolić** was baptised 1677-10-01 (doc. 236).

## Bearers
- **Bajo Nikolić-Pivljanin** — the celebrated *hajduk*; in this corpus 1675–1684 lives in Zadar as paid cavalryman of capt. Soliman's company. 1675-04-26 offers to take in the Turkish slave Omer Mustafa (doc. 138 — wait, that's a different transaction). 1680-05-03 leasing the *prado* in **Sovare** at Suhovare from Turks (doc. 265). 1682-06-02 Korner orders him not to abuse his brother Petar (doc. 284). Joins his nephew/brother-in-law Ilija Mitrović in the Vrana uprising 1683; pardoned by Donà 1683-12-08 (doc. 347). Reassigned to Kotor 1684-04-12 with brother Mitar (doc. 393).
- **Mitar (Dmitar/Dimitri) Nikolić-Pivljanin** — Bajo's brother. Married **Janja Janković** 1675-01-17 (doc. 210). Son **Nikola Nikolić** b. 1677-10-01 at Sv. Ilija (doc. 236). Goes AWOL Jan 1684; pardoned 1684-02-19 (doc. 376). Per Desnica's footnote on doc. 376: "Mitar Nikolić, brat Baja Pivljanina, boravio je, kao i ovaj, u Zadru, kao plaćeni vojnik mletačke konjice" — and joined Ilija Mitrović's rebellion (Mitar was married to Ilija's sister, that is, was Stojan and Ilija's *zet*).
- **Petar Nikolić** — 1682-06-02 Korner forbids Bajo to abuse him (doc. 284).
- **Nikola Nikolić** — son of Mitar and Janja, b. 1677-10-01.
- **Simeon Nikolić-Pivljanin** — son of Bajo and his wife **Manda**, baptismal record (doc. 226 head — birth attestation in the Sv. Ilija registers).

## Notes
- The 1685 attestation by Bajo "havendo già 7 anni in [[Zadar]]" implicit places him in Zadar from c. 1671. (Desnica's footnote on doc. 211 puts the Pivljanini in Zadar from "od svršetka kandijskog pa do početka morejskog rata" — i.e. 1669–1684.)

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
- **Bajo Pivljanin** — receives Venetian cavalry pay alongside Janković Jan 1685 (doc 44); a separate "fatto del harambassa Bajo" mentioned March 1685 (doc 57) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Demetrio (Dmitar) Nikolić-Pivljanin** — 1696 granted **12 fields and ruined tower of Mustajbeg Ćerinagić in Padglivie/Trebinje** as benemerito brother of late Bajo Pivljanin (doc. 287). The source's text "figliolo" is corrected by Desnica's footnote to "fratello" — i.e., he is Bajo's brother, not son [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
