# Kapuano

Single attestation: **Mihovil (Michel) Kapuano iz Šibenika** — 1668-10-06 verbally abused fra Cirilo Gabrieli and was throwing garbage at the Sv. Zulijan Šibenik church; ordered by Priuli to desist on pain of 500 ducati (doc. 162).
