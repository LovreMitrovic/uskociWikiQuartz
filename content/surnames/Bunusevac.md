# Bunuševac (Bunuševci)

Medieval vlach katun-name in the upper Južna Morava drainage — eastern frontier of the medieval Serbian state. Attested in 14th c. as **Vlasi Bunuševci** of the [[Vranjsko-Krajiste]] (Blagojević 1989:33–36) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

Performed čuvar-granice (border-guard) service to a *krajiški vlastelin*. Toponymic remnant: village **[[Bunusa]]** south of Leskovac (territorialization of the katun).

Possible link to selo **Punoševci/Punoševići** in Hum 1402 (Hrabak 1997:145), and to selo **Ponoševac/Punashec** near Đakovica (Gashi 1982:53) — though these are speculative.

Bun-stem onomastic family with [[Buncic]], [[Bunovic]], [[Bunislavic]].
