# Đurić

Bjelaj-area capi family that led the 1692 mass migration of ~5000 souls into Venetian state.

## Bearers in Desnica II
- **Conte Paolo Đurić (Paolo Giurich)** — capo of Bjelaj province; led ~5000 souls to Venetian devotion; awarded **10 ducats + biscuit/month June 1692**; settled in Zrmanja, Plavno, Pađene, Mokro Polje, Oton; killed in clash with Turks; pension transferred to brother Vuk **1693-08-11** [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (docs. 251, 252).
- **Conte Vuk Đurić (Vuk Giurich)** — brother of Paolo; received Paolo's 10 ducats/month pension after his death 1693 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (footnote of doc. 251).
