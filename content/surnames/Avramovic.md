# Avramović

## Bearers
- **Dragić Avramović** — Morlak *suddito* newcomer; petitioner February 1685 for help for the widow and children of his brother Dragoja, who killed Čejvan-aga Lopušić of Knin and was himself killed; awarded 1 *peso* of biscuit/month (doc 49) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Dragoja Avramović** — brother of Dragić; killed Čejvan-aga Lopušić of Knin then died in the same skirmish (doc 49) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
