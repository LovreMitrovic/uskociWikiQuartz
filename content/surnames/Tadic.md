# Tadić

## Bearers
- **knez Nikola Tadijić** — 1648-07-17 *uskočki* leader: *"Uskoci iz [[Krmpote]] naseljeni ispod Poljica, od 150 pješaka izgubili 10"* at the Battle of Ribnik (doc. 39 [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]).
- **Nicolo Tadich da Carpote** — 1653-01-16 same individual; co-signs the proxy authorising Ilija Smiljanić to plead the Morlachi case in Venice (doc. 70).
- **Tadić** (capitan) of Starigrad — owner of *Mlin del conte Tadijch* in the perimeter of the 1679 land allocation to the Sv. Juraj uskoks (doc. 258).
