# Sudarović

## Bearers
- [[Jovan-Sudarovic-b????-Lika|Jovan (Zuanne) Sudarović]] of Parčići — Lika Morlach harambaša with c. 150–300 men under arms, settled in Karlobag and upper Obrovac on the Habsburg side. The Imperial commissar (a *Karlović*) tried to retain him with the offer of life-tenure as captain of Karlobag plus privileges, but Sudarović preferred Venice; on 23 November 1685 (doc 114) appeared with his companion Rade Rkać before Pietro Valier in Split and proposed to settle in the abandoned **Žegar** castle with a paid 100-man company plus food provisions until harvest, bringing a small cannon captured from the Udbina Turks. Awarded 4 cecchini for travel and sent back to Janković and the cavalry provveditor [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
