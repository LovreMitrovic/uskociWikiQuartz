# Marković

## Bearers
- **Miloš Marković** — 1648-07-17: *"Uskoci iz Brguda naseljeni kod Nina od 55 ljudi izgubili pet"* at Ribnik (doc. 39 [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]).
- **harambaša Jovan Marković** — 1655-10-26 named *governator* of the kula at Malpaga (Dračevac) by Zen, with monthly biscotto, for repulsing the Turkish invasion (doc. 95).
- **kapetan Ivan Marković (Marcovinovich)** — 1683-12-07 with serdar Vučen Stefanović, Marko Lugović and other *confinanti* (Šibenik krajišnici) writes to fellow harambaše to muster at Drniš (doc. 346).
- **kapetan Nikoleto Marković** — 1683-02-18 takes the spying mission to Sarajevo (doc. 305) after Nikolet Mikieli was disqualified.
