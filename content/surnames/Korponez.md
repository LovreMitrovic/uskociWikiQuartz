# Korponež

## Bearers
- **kapetan Nikola (Nicolo) Korponež** — captain of a galeotta in the service of provveditor estraordinario Zen at Cattaro; on 6 March 1686 deposed (doc 131) that he had brought sopraintendente Perini and kavalier Cittadella under the Pastrovići towers together with collonello Radoš's galeotta; the besieged Pastrovići recognized his name from the tower; the Turks then asked him in Albanian about Janković's whereabouts; his bluff that Janković was approaching with 30,000 men caused the Turks to lift the siege [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
