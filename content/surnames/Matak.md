# Matak

Two attestations from Ražanačka godovna knjiga (doc. 119), 1662-02-13 — killed at Otres:
- **kapetan Šime Matak**
- **Ivan Matak, sin Šimin**

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
- **Marko Matak of Razanze** — villager of Ražanac; in 1684 took a *gecerma* (woollen vest) of crimson-violet from the goods of Mihajlo Radmilović of Žegar; ordered to make restitution by Mocenigo (doc 31) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
