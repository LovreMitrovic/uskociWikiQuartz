# Pirić

Bjelaj-area capi family of the 1692 mass migration.

## Bearers in Desnica II
- **Nicolo Pirić (Nicolo Pirich)** — capo of Bjelaj province with Đurić and Zorić; led ~5000 souls to Venetian devotion 1692; settled in **Plavno**, given the **tower of Muhamedbeg Durakbegović in Plavno** [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (docs. 251, 252, 253).
