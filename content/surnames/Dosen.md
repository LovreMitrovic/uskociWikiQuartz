# Došen

Lika/Bunjevac surname of military prominence; named alongside the Rukavine, [[Starcevic|Starčević]] and [[Balenovic|Balenović]] among "vitezovi hrvatski" in Andrija Kačić Miošić's 1756 pjesmarica [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].
