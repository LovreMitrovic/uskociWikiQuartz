# Najčinović (Jančić)

Vlach kindred attested in Benković nahija from 1528. Surname forms appear interchangeably ("Petar Jančić" in 1528–30 register = "Petar Najčinović" in later sources) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. After 1538 defection: kapetan in Podnovje (novigradski distrikt); some moved to Venetian Istria (Dvigrad, Labinština).
