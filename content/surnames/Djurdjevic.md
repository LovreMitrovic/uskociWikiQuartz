# Đurđević (Jurjević)

Footnote in Desnica explicitly: "Gjurgjević (sada Jurjević)" — the family is the same line as the modern Jurjević of Obrovac/Kruševo [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].

## Bearers in Desnica II
- **Vido (Vid) Đurđević** — alfier (ensign) under serdar Zaviša Mitrović; resident of Obrovac; witness to doc. 265 (1693); subject to surety bond by Zaviša 1694-01-13 (docs. 265, 270, 284). 1702 commanded Janković company at Obrovac after Zaviša's death; volunteered to lead cavalry company to Italy; **declared harambasa of Obrovac March 1702** (doc. 351); 1704 his animals seized at Obrovac during Bukovica revolt (doc. 352); 1703 called to Split to raise paesana company [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Martin Đurđević (Jurjević)** — Obrovac inhabitant 1696 (doc. 284) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
