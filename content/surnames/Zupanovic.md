# Županović (Župan)

Vojnić starešina family — gives name to the "Županovići" subregion around [[Tinj]] [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. The Catholic-Vlach population of this župa was pastorally tended by don Stjepan Sorić in 1636 [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

## Bearers (Desnica)
- **harambaša Bose (Bože) Županović** — 1647-08-31 capitan in Foscolo's command (doc. 16); 1652-09-24 holds the Turk Rustem-beg as his slave purchased from Janko Mitrović after the Grahovo battle (doc. 64); 1653-01-16 governator Bose Zuppanovich co-signs the proxy authorising Smiljanić to plead in Venice (doc. 70). ## In Desnica II (1684–1749)

- **Tomica Župan (Tomiza Zupan)** — Obrovac inhabitant 1696 (doc. 284) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
