# Šutić

Single attestation: **fra Grga (Gregorio) Šutić** — Bosnian Franciscan; 1683-11 reports to Donà his secret meeting with the rebel knez Ilija da Radassinovzi, conte Marko Klarić of Stankovci, harambaša Martin Đagnimić and harambaša Idero (Ždero) at the village Uselize sopra Vrana — pivotal document for the Vrana uprising (doc. 333).

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)
- **fra Grgur Šutić** — continues to act as Bosnian Franciscan *cappellano* / mediator; appears in the 1684 Vrana petitions to Mocenigo and in the post-rising negotiations (docs 34, 36) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
