# Jakić

## Bearers in Desnica II
- **Filippo (Filip) Jakić (Jachich)** — Gorica resident; testified to stolen items in 1693-11-04 raid; **recognized speech as from Žegar** (doc. 268) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Also led mission with Stankovic to expose the Žegar raid [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc. 268).
