# Babahmetović

## Bearers
- **Zelel-aga Babahmetović** — former Venetian state slave, exchanged for *conte Sabbini* during the Candian War; his daughter (a widow returning to him) travelled in Mehmed-beg Durakbegović's escort intercepted March 1685 (doc 56) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **figliola di Zelel-aga Babahmetović** — unnamed widowed daughter of Zelel-aga, returning to him in the same March 1685 escort (doc 56) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
