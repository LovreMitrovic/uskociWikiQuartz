# Gazi Husrev-beg

Bosnian Ottoman beylerbeyi line — best known by laqab. Founder of the [[Kozulova-Polje]] vakuf (1531). His Bosnia *hasovi* in 1530 covered 10 cities, 158 villages, 64 čiftluks (annual revenue 643,979 akči) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].
