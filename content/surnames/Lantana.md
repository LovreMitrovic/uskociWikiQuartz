# Lantana

Zadar patrician family producing notaries and *fiscali* through the period.

## Bearers
- **Francesco Lantana** — *bilježnik* (notary); his archival series *Spisi bilježnika Franceska Lantana* is cited at line 4136 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Oratio (Horatio) Lantana** — Zadar plemić; stood as *piezzo* (guarantor) for 2000 *ducati correnti* in an *officio* operation (lines 11938, 11952, 11991) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **dottor Lantana** — fiscal officer (deceased before 1700), succeeded in office by *Lomazzi* (line 12424) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **scoglio del Lantana** — small island c. 3 miles from Zadar where outgoing provveditor Lorenzo Donà lay ill in May 1684 (doc 1) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].

## In Desnica II (1684–1749) — extended

- **Oratio Lantana** quondam Marc' Antonio — Zadar mercante; **pieggio (guarantor) for Zaviša Mitrović for 2000 ducats 1692-03-17**; held money/items as security; released Dec 1696 (docs. 246, 297) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Marc' Antonio Lantana** — late father of Oratio (doc. 246) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
