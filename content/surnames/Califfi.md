# Califfi

Italian/Venetian noble family resident in [[Zadar]]; held half-interest in the island of [[Olib]].

## Bearers in Desnica II
- **Conte Francesco Califfi** — deceased by 1701; father of Giacomo. [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **[[Giacomo-Califfi-b????-Zadar|Conte Giacomo Califfi]]** (son of conte Francesco) — sold half-island of [[Olib]] to [[Mitrovic|Mitrović]] family in **1701** for 8000 ducats with peculiar dynastic conditions: perpetual half-half ownership between Saviša Janković's male line and his deceased brother Ilija's male line. See [[Olib-Sale-1701]]. [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
