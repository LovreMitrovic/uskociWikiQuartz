# Oluić

## Bearers
- [[Vucic-Oluic-b????-Bilisani|Vučić Oluić]] — Morlach newcomer settled at Bilišane above Obrovac; in late January 1686 reported to Stojan Janković that the Croatian *ban* had crossed into Bosnia four days earlier with great damage; Oluić's *partita* had been at Sinogora (= Siva Gora / Sitna Gora?) between Lika and Krbava and had seen the smoke (doc 123, 28 Jan 1686 deposition of Stojan) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
