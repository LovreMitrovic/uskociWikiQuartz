# Frletić

Vlach kindred whose ancestors appear as "sons of Firultić" in 1528–30 (Tornik, Podgorje) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Settled on Venetian side (Kukalj, Trščani, novigradski distrikt) after 1538 in *plaćenička* military service; later in Ljubač and Žman (1608). Some moved to Venetian Istra (around Poreč). Late 16th c.: among Senj uskoks ([[Andrija-Frletic-b????-Dracevac]], Vuk); some who returned to Ottoman side converted to Islam ([[Hasan-aga-Frletic-b????-Dracevac]]).
