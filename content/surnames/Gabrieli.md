# Gabrieli

Single attestation: **fra Cirilo (Cirillo) Gabrielli** — Greek-rite Orthodox monk, parohiski svešenik of Sv. Zulijan Šibenik 1654–1668. Conducts baptisms (Iuan Šarić 1654, Lukas Zubičević 1665, Đurđe Busović 1663). Defended in 1654 by 37 Šibenik Morlach harambaše against Jovan Vuković's plot to oust him (doc. 84). 1668-10-06 his parish brawls with Mihovil Kapuano spill into provveditor Priuli's office (doc. 162).
