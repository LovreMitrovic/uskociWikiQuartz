# Stojanović

## Bearers
- **Constadin (Kostadin) Stojanović of Oton** — 1652-10-05 Turkish-subject slave on the galley of Antonio Barbaro; offered as ransom for Bartul Banenović of Pavičići (Ilija Smiljanić's *germano*) (doc. 66).
- **Mihovil Stojanović** — 1684-03-01 named *capitano* of [[Pakostane|Pakoštani]] (doc. 381).

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)

- **Nikola Stojanović (Nicolo Stoianovich)** — courier; named in 1734 as messenger to forward money to imprisoned pop Petar Jagodić-Kuridža in Venetian Pozzi/Camerotti prisons (doc. 385) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
