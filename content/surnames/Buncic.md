# Bunčić

Medieval vlach katun-name in eastern Hum (Hercegovina), 15th c. Concretely:
- **1419** — katun of [[Vukota-Buncic-b1419]] (Hrabak 1981:188).
- **15th c. stećak** — selo [[Buncici]] (Radmilović-Dubrava near Bileća), tomb of [[Bunac-Rusovic-b????-Buncici]]; the bearer probably a starešina of the Vlasi Banjani aglomeracija (Bešlagić 1971:390).

Onomastic evidence for the **Bun-stem family** of vlach personal/clan names from which the etnik [[Bunjevac]] ultimately descends (along with [[Bunovic]], Bunuševci, [[Bunislavic]]) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].
