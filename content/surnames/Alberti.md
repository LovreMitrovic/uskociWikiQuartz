# Alberti

## Bearers
- **Ivan (Zuanne) Alberti** — Zadar plemić; *guvernadur* (governor) and capitano paired with kapetan Frane Ventura in 1684 directives (line 981); Cornaro's 1689 *pertinenze* reform later names *governator Zuanne Alberti* over Novegradi–Charin–Bađun–Pridraga (line 1045) — same individual, *abile Zaratino, d'anni 60* (line 14918) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- [[Zuanne-Alberti-b????-Split|Zuanne Alberti]] of Split — Splitski plemić, captured by the Turks beneath Sinj at the April 1685 defeat; held by the bassa di Bosna; his letters survive (lines 3359, 3373, 3438). He is "preso per schiavo proprio dal bassa di Bosna" — distinct from the Zadar Alberti [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **agente Gerolimo Alberti, interprete** — Venetian agent and interpreter (line 4282) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **sopraintendente Alberti** — at the 1688 Knin conquest commanded the *natione oltramarina* with Zadar borghesani (line 10494) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
