# Goreta

## Bearers
- [[Stipan-Goreta-b????-Sibenik|Stipan Goretta]] — capo of the Šibenik-Skradin territory Morlachs; arrived at Bribir during the November 1686 Bosnian Pasha incursion together with Palikuća and Žinović; their men panicked and fled the *recinto*, forcing Stojan Janković to abandon the post (doc 155) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
