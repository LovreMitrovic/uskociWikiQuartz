# Komanija

## Bearers
- **Mehrem Komanija** — Turk *di consideratione* (notable); captured by Jovan Sinobad in his settlement work at Poličnik and Nadin; cited in the 15 March 1686 *terminacija* by Pietro Valier awarding Sinobad 2 ducats/month for life (doc 132) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
