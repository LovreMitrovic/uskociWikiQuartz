# Prešević

## Bearers in Desnica II
- **monsignor Simon Prešević (Simeon Prenčević / Simon Prancovich)** — Orthodox bishop of Belgrade; received vineyard in **Vodmac/Valmaz** village (Podhumnica, near Hercegnovi) previously owned by Turk Resul Aga Babović, May 1697 (doc. 307). Granted 15 fields in Trebinje + 15 more in 1696 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc. 295).
- **Ivan Prešević** — brother/relative of bishop Simeon; granted 15 fields in Trebinje + 15 more 1696 (doc. 295) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Avakum Prešević** — *sugino* (cousin) of Ivan; nephew of bishop Simeon (doc. 295) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Vojin Prešević** — *sugino* of Ivan; nephew of bishop Simeon (doc. 295) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].

## Notes
- Multiple spellings in source: Presevich, Prancovich, Prenčević. Canonical form here adopted as Prešević.
