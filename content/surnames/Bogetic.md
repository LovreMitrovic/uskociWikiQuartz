# Bogetić

Family that gives its name to the village **Bogetić** in the Petropolje cluster.

## Bearers
- **Lorenzo (Lovre) Jadrešić rečeni Bogetić, dalla villa Bogetić** — 1647-12-17 signed (with X) the Petropolje uskakanje contract (doc. 18); doc. 18 lists him both as Jadrešić and as the eponym of the village.
- **Stefano Boghetich** — 1652-10-09 carambassà of Šibenik Morlachi (doc. 67), petitions for the release of Ibraim Bibilica of Lapac.
- **Simon Boghetich** — 1653-01-04 capt. — pay 4 ducati/month (doc. 69).
