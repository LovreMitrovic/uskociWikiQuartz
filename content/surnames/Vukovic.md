# Vuković

Single attestation: **Jovan (Zuanne) Vuković rečeni Obrad** — 1654 plotted to remove paroh fra Cirilo Gabrieli of Sv. Zulijan Šibenik and replace him with a *young kaluđer*. Resisted by 37 Šibenik *pravoslavnih* Morlach harambaše (doc. 84).
