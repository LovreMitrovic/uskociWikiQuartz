# Sinovčić

## Bearers
- **Tomo Sinovčić** — of Novigrad (Dalmatia); rewarded by Mocenigo on 5 September 1684 for killing 5 Turks at Grahovo plus the *kjehaja* (lieutenant) of Mazut-paša (doc 32 footnote) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Tomaso Sinovčić** — from Novigrad; complained-against by Obrovac inhabitants for taking lands across the river 1690 (doc. 230) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Likely the same man as 1684.
