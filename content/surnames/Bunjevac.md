# Bunjevac

The **etnik-turned-surname** at the heart of the wiki's namesake population. Šarić's argument: Bunjevac as etnik **forms in Catholic Vlach milieu of the Tromeđa around 1550** and acquires its surname function precisely when bearers move into non-Bunjevac (typically Orthodox vlach or Hungarian-administered) environments, where neighbors mark them by the etnik [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

## Etymology
**Bunj-** stem (vlach anthroponym Bun/Bunj < Latin Bonifacius via Balkan-Romance vocalism o>u; later n>nj jotacija post-14th c.) + **-evac/-evci** patronymic suffix. Parallel to medieval vlach groups [[Buncic]], [[Bunovic]] (Tudoričevci/Dečani), Bunuševci (Vranje), [[Bunislavic]] (1406 east Hum).

## Confessional pattern
Surname attaches in **Orthodox vlach milieu** for Catholic migrants (most attestations Lika Vojna krajina), and reciprocally in Catholic milieu for Orthodox Vlachs. Examples: Krš near Kosinj (still Orthodox Bunjevčević today); Prozor near Otočac (Catholic Bunjevčević, possibly converts).
