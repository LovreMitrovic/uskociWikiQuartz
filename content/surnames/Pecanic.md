# Pečanić

*Spelling note (2026-09-08, unresolved): the 1610-era pages read *Pečanić*, the 1756 pages *Pećanić* (from Venetian *Pechianich*, where `chi` normally gives ć). Both are attested Croatian surnames and the source does not settle it. Person pages under this hub are therefore inconsistent — **Pečanić** vs **Pećanić**. Left as-is pending a decision rather than picked arbitrarily.*
