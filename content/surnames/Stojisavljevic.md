# Stojisavljević

[[Vrlika]]-area family connected by marriage to the [[Mitrovic|Mitrović]] line.

## Bearers in Desnica II
- **[[Novko-Stojisavljevic-b????-Vrlika|Novko Stojisavljević]]** — patriarch of the line. [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **[[Vucen-Stojisavljevic-b????-Vrlika|Vučen Stojisavljević]]** (son of Novko) — married **[[Marija-Mitrovic-b????-Zegar|Marija Mitrović]]** (of the Žegar Janković-Mitrović line); adopted compound name Mitrović-Stojisavljević. [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **[[Petar-Mitrovic-Stojisavljevic-b????-Vrlika|Petar Mitrović-Stojisavljević]]** — descendant carrying the compound surname. [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]

## Notes
- The marriage tied the Vrlika line to the Janković-Mitrović clan; compound surname preserves this kin-link in matica records.
