# Harlić

Major Dračevac re-settlement family. Doc. 199 (1671-12-17 — Morozini's resettlement of Dračevac/Komorce, drawing 43 souls):

- **Jellia Charlich con figlio** — 2 souls.
- **Zorzi Harlich e figlio** — 2 souls.
- **Marco Harlich** — 1 soul.
- **Antonio Harlich col figlio** — 2 souls.
- **Matteo Harlich figlio e nipote** — 3 souls.

So 5 male Harlić household-heads in the 1671 Dračevac roster — single-village onomastic cluster.
