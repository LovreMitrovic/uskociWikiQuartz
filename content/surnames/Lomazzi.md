# Lomazzi (Lomaca)

Zadar notary family.

## Bearers in Desnica II
- **Nicolo Lomazzi (Nikola Lomaca)** — Zadar notary; **appointed Illyrian interpreter 1692-10-22** by Doge Morosini to replace dr Lantana; **1694-02-01 appointed cancelliere of Illyrian and Serbian letters in Zadar** confirmed 8 March by Dolfin (docs. 254, 257, 272); recorded Zaviša-Gambiraži contract Aug 1692 (doc. 254); defended his post against Pietro Calčina's claim May 1701 (doc. 327); found a Cyrillic Serbian poem on back of a notarial document [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
