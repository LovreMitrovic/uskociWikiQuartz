# Sokolović

Bosnian Ottoman beylik family. [[Ferhat-beg-Sokolovic-b????]] served as kliški sandžakbeg, settled ~200 Istrian Vlach families in Knin area 1568, and uvakufio [[Tinj]] (1574) for the Zemunik vakuf with Vojnić Bunjevac colonists [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].
