# Smiljanić — *poreklom iz Udbine*

One of the senior *kotarski uskočki* lineages, with multi-generational prominence. **Originally from Udbina, not Smiljan** — Desnica's correction is critical and overrides the legend.

## Genealogy
- **Petar Smiljanić** (founder; d. 1648-07-17 at Ribnik): had two armed sons — **Ilija** and **Filip**.
- **Ilija Smiljanić** (b. ~1622, d. 1654-09-08 at Vučjak): serdar; childless? — widow Kata remarried Milivoj Zubičević 1655.
- **Filip Smiljanić** (b. ~1633, d. Jan 1656 in a Šibenik brawl): kapetan; killed before producing heirs.
- **Smoljan Smiljanić** (a/k/a Smoglianovich, also *Mihalevich*): nephew (*nipote*) of Ilija and Filip; serdar from 1664; heir of the family pay; uterine half-brother of Šimun and Marko **Brajinović** (their mother Anka had a first marriage to Frane Brajinović of Spianata-behind-Sv. Marko).
  - **Lazaro Smiljanić** (in tenera età 1679): son of Smoljan; capitan of a 50-man company.

- **Kata** — Turkish-born wife of Ilija, remarried Milivoj Zubičević (Šibenik). Their son **Lukas Zubičević** was b. 1665-10-15.

## Notes
- The Smiljanići of [[Udbina]] led the **Bukovčani** *uskakanje* — Petar (1648), then his sons Ilija and Filip in succession.
- The legend of "Smiljanić Ilija" / "Filip Smiljanić" / "Vuk Smiljanić" in epic poetry goes back to this family but is layered over historical fact.

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)

Smiljanić family continues prominently through Morean War and into 18th c.; widow Marija (née Bolić) and son Lazaro provide the testamentary archive.

### New bearers and contexts
- **Marko Smiljanić** — capitano; brother of Smoljan; awarded 10 ducats/month **1690-05-21** by Doge Morosini for service in the Morean campaigns (Calamata, Navarino, Modon, Napoli di Romania, Negroponte, Malvasia) (doc. 233). Died **1693-02-23 aged ~46** (b. ~1647) in Zadar; given last unction by don Andrea Caldana; buried San Domenico (doc. 264). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **Marija Smiljanić (née Bolić)** — widow of Smoljan Smiljanić; testament in Zadar 1693-10-05; died between then and 1694-04-13 when will opened; buried San Domenico; mother of Lazaro and Margarita; sister-in-law of Marko (doc. 267). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **Lazzaro (Lazaro) Smiljanić** — son of Smoljan and Marija; heir per Marija's will; wrote his own will **1696-07-28** leaving estate to wife Maria (Bolić) and unborn child, alternately to slaves and Dominicans; commended at siege of Ulcinj 1696 (docs. 267, 289, 299). 1700 supplicated to be made serdar — claimed he was last surviving member of Smiljanić family (doc. 320, with footnote noting supplica's exaggerations: Filip actually killed in Šibenik brawl 1656, not by Turks; Smoljan's death at Sinj not corroborated). Eventually appointed **serdar of Lower Kotari** (succeeding Bože Milković after his ~1700 death); led troops to Italy 1702 (replaced provisionally by Antonio Vujanović); died ~early 1709 (docs. 320, 322, 323, 338, 352, 367, 368, 377). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **Maria Bolić-Smiljanić** — wife of Lazzaro Smiljanić; pregnant when he wrote will 1696 (doc. 289). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **Margarita Smiglianich** — daughter of Smoljan and Marija; living 1693 (doc. 267); married a "Salentich governor" who received green velvet jacket bequest. [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **Marko Smiljanić (the younger)** — minor son of Lazar Smiljanić; reserved as serdar of Lower Kotari **1709-02-20** by Vendramin; held captaincy of paesana company in terraferma (docs. 367, 368, 377). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **Aloisi (nephew of Marija Smiljanić)** — bequeathed silver buttons (doc. 267).
- **Giovanni / Zuanne (other nephew of Marija)** — bequeathed silk sash (doc. 267).
- **Smiljanić (governor)** — one of three serdars sent to negotiate with rebellious Bukovica Morlachi 1704 alongside Gvozden Radasović and Spingaroli (doc. 352) — likely Lazar.

### Notes
- Per supplica footnote in doc. 320: Filip's death was in a Šibenik brawl with Venetian soldiers (drunk), not killed by Turks as Lazaro claimed. Smoljan's claimed death at conquest of Sinj is also doubted by editor.
- Marija's testament debtors include don Gregorio Begna (4 zecchini); recipients include Caterina Pizzocara, Vincenza Pastrović, padre Scutari (provinciale Dominican), Ellena daughter of dottor Cassio (diamond ring), her *compare* quartier mastro Luca Sbizza.
- Notary: Andreas de Toris; consigliere/giudice: Biagio Soppe; commissario: Casseretti; conte di Zara: Giovanni Mirelli (doc. 267).
