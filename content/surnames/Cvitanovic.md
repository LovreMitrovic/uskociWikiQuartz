# Cvitanović

## Bearers
- [[Vukon-Cvitanovic-b????-Sibenik|Vuconie (Vukon) Cvitanović]] — Šibenik-territory rider; in November 1686 Stojan Janković dispatched him with karambassà Nakić from Bribir to alert the Zadar contado of the Bosnian Pasha's irruption (doc 155) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Matteo Cvitanović** — captain in Split; in 1700 in horse-trade litigation with tenente colonnello Frano Franćeski (Franceschi), governor of Vrlika, who had fallen from a stallion riding *alka* on the Split quay during carnival; horse died, valued at 40 reali (doc 324) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
