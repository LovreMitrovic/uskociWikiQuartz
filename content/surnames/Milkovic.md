# Milković

Bunjevac starešina family from [[Medvidja|Medviđa]] [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]] (Šarić identifies the Milkovići as a Bunjevac starešina family).

## Bearers
- **Mate Milković of Kruševo near Obrovac** — 1554, debtor of a Šibenik butcher (Šarić citation).
- **harambaša Ilija Milković** — second-in-command in Mitrović's Žegar circle. 1648-07-29 deposes about Sorić's Udbina raid (doc. 32). 1654-07-01 promoted to *cavalleria leggera* by Dolfin for blowing up Zvonigrad (doc. 80). 1656-03-04 assigned to guard the Krka passes (doc. 101). 1658-06-28 named alongside Janko in Bernardo's *concerto* with Petar Zrinski (doc. 106). 1659-02-11 wounded with Janko at Cetina (doc. 112). 1659-03-01 — **died** in Zadar; buried Sv. Domenico (docs. 114, 115).
- **Marco Milković** — 1661-03-01 named (with Petar Grgurica) capi de Morlacchi by Andrea Korner (doc. 118). 1663-11-21 — **died** *bravamente combattendo* (doc. 135). 1664-05-21 succeeded by Matia Mirković.
- **Šimun Milković** — 1668-09-21 supplica to Doge: of his 12 *uskočio* family men, 10 perished, he and brother Mihail are slaves; supplicates ransom (doc. 160).
- **Mihail Milković** — Šimun's brother, slave in Turkey 1668.
- **harambaša Šimun Milković** — 1672-02-05 originally promised Bokanjac as settlement site for 25 families of *Ivosevci* + 11 of *Butoraz* (doc. 200). The Parčići dispute the assignment.
- **kapetan Šimun Milković** — 1671-12-17 settles in Dračevac with brother and son (doc. 199; "fratello et nipote — no. 3").

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)

- **Bože Milković (Boze Milcovich)** — **serdar of Lower Kotari** by 1691; previously already governor; brother killed by Ilija Janković's followers 1692; led 2,000 men to Malpaga; reconciled with Zaviša Mitrović 1692 by Dolfin's mediation; partook in Liga 1696; led mission of Filip Jakić & Stankovic to expose Žegar raid 1693 (docs. 224, 236, 243, 247, 268, 286). 1699 supplied 25 sessane and 8 horses for Grimani; testified in Sfakioto's protocols (docs. 314, 316, 319). Bedridden ill Dec 1699 (doc. 323); died ~1700, succeeded by Lazar Smiljanić as serdar of Lower Kotari [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Bože Milković's "barba" (uncle)** — anonymous; destroyed Zvonigrad in past war ("fu ravinato nella guerra passata da mio barba") per Bože's 1699 testimony (doc. 316) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **harambaša Mede Milcović (Mede Milicovich)** — see [[Krmpotic|Krmpotić]] entry; settled Bukovica Krmpote and Parčić, granted Medvidja fields 1693 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (docs. 263, 301).
