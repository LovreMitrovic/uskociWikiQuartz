# Tomičić

## Bearers
- **harambaša Martin Tomičić** — 1648-07-17: *"Uskoci iz Drvenika naseljeni kod Nina od 100 ljudi izgubili petoricu"* at Ribnik (doc. 39 [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]).

## Notes
- The same name **Tomicich** is one of three sub-villages of [[Rudopolje]] in Lika that don Sorić burned in 1648-06-18 (with **Vodena Glava** and **Pancino selo** — doc. 32). Whether Martin Tomičić's family stem from this Lika locality is plausible but not directly attested.
