# Gargurica (Grgurica)

## Bearers
- **Petar Gargurica (Pietro Gargurizza)** — 1661-03-01 named (with Marko Milković) capo de Morlacchi by Andrea Korner, 10 ducati/month; serdar over castel Venier, Ražanac, Posedarje, Novigrad area (doc. 118). 1663-05-08 leads guards at Dramotich pass with men of Krmpote, Čučulovci, Rodaljice, Novigrad (doc. 130). Killed valorously combatting Turks 1663-11-21 (doc. 135).
- **Jovo (Giuco) Garguricić** — 1684-03-01 named *capitano* of [[Vinjerac|Castel Venier]] (doc. 381).
