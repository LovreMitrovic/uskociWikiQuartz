# Miljanić

Two distinct geographic Miljanić-clusters appear: **Gračac** (the senior 1647 cluster) and **Čučulovci → Vrsi** (the post-1648 settlement).

## Bearers
- **Žure (Giure) Miljanić** — 1647-08-16 led the Grazac raid as guide (doc. 15 — Petar Smiljanić's deposition). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **Vido Miljanić** — brother of Žure; came from Grazac after the raid as Turkish negotiator for ransoms (doc. 15).
- **harambaša Petar Miljanić** — 1648-07-17: *"Uskoci iz Čučulovaca naseljeni u Vrsima od 70 pješaka poginulo šest"* at Ribnik (doc. 39).
- **Mattio Miglianich da Cuculovaz** — 1653-01-16 co-signs the proxy authorising Smiljanić to plead in Venice (doc. 70).

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)

Multiple Miljanić bearers in 1696 Obrovac inhabitant list (doc. 284) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]:
- **Giadrijem Miljanić (Jadrijem Miglianich)** — Obrovac 1696.
- **Mattio Miljanić (Mate Miljanić)** — Obrovac 1696. (Possibly same as 1653 da Cuculovaz.)
- **Stipan Miljanić** — Obrovac 1696.
- **Bože (Boso) Miljanić** — Obrovac 1696.
- **Nikola Miljanić** — Obrovac 1696.
- **Mattio Miljanić (Mattio Miglianich)** — captain of Krušev (Crussevo); **1704 rebel leader** in Bukovica revolt (doc. 352) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].

The Miljanić Obrovac group is one of those families noted by Desnica's footnote as **still living in Kruševo, the village adjacent to Obrovac** (continuity to modern times) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
