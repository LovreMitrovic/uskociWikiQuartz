# Radoš

Trogir/Klis-area officers; a slaveholding consortium owning Mandići of Sinj.

## Bearers
- **Ivan (Zuanne) Radoš** — *conte cavalier*; head of the consortium that owned Hasan-beg Mandić as slave (docs 5, 33, 60, 65, 68) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **collonello Radoš** — brother of Ivan; killed at the Sinj defeat April 1685 (doc 60) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **collonello Rados** (later) — galeotta-commander operating with Korponež off Pastrovići March 1686 (doc 131) — possibly the same family [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
