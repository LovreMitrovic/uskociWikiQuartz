# Balen (Balenović / Balinović)

Bunjevac surname; founder family of the Krmpote katun. Šarić classifies the root as **arbanaški (Albanian)** stratum within the 1712 Bunjevac onomastic stock [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

## Attestations
- **mid-16th c.** — [[Ivan-Balenovic-b????-Kijani]] sin Mihovilov, buried in selo Kijani near [[Gracac]] (Cyrillic stećak inscription).
- **1605** — Balinović (with original katun-aglomeracija) listed among the 24 Krmpote families joining the [[Krmpote-Migration-Lic-1605|first Lič migration]]; also listed among the Sladović "Gvozdenovi ljudi" 1605 group of 20 (the surname appears in both lists).
- **1756** — Kačić Miošić in *Razgovor ugodni* names **Balenovići** among "vitezovi hrvatski" alongside [[Starcevic]], [[Rukavina]], Došen, Milinković [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].
