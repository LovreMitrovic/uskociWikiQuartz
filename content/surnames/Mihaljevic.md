# Mihaljević / Miljević / Miličević

Drniški origin family of harambaše. Spelt **Mihaglievich** in Italian.

## Bearers
- **Matija (Mattio) Mihaljević da Drniš, habitante nel borgo della Marina (Šibenik)** — 1648-08-01 deposes about the Battle of Zečevo and Vuk Mandušić's death (doc. 43 [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]).
- **Mihail Miličević rečeni Kovač** — 1663-06-22 named *paga intera di soldato* in Makarska, "intervenuto fra gli altri nel fatto suddetto" of killing the famous Turk **Alija Bojičić of Zadvarje** (doc. 132). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **Smoljan Smiglianich (alias Mihalevich)** — see [[Smoljan-Smiljanic-b????-Bukovica|Smoljan Smiljanić]] — 1656 lawsuit names him "Smoglian Smiglianich o Mihalevich" (doc. 103). His paternal patronymic was Mihaljević; the more famous Smiljanić surname comes from his maternal kin.
