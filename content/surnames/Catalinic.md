# Katalinić

## Bearers
- **Žorži (Zorzi) Katalinić** — knez of Starigrad; killed 1678 defending Morlach animals against a Lika Turkish raid (doc. 253).
- **Frane (Francesco) Katalinić** — 1678-11-10 son of Žorži; named knez of Starigrad to succeed his father by Valier (doc. 253).

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)

- **Frane (Francesco) Cattalinich (Vid Katalinić)** — capitano of [[Starigrad-Podgorje|Starigrad]] (under Velebit/Morlacca); reported on Karlobag developments to Molin Jan 1690 (doc. 228); appointed captain of new 50-man Starigrad company by Dolfin Nov 1696 (docs. 290, 291, 294); 1696 testified with Šimun Vukosalkić about Stinica being inhabited by Bukovčani who fled to Sv. Juraj near Senj during Candian War (doc. 291) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
