# Tomljanović

Bunjevac surname; among the 16 reconstructed families of the [[Krmpote-Migration-Lic-1627|second (1627) Krmpote migration to Lič]] (Pavličević 1989) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]] (medium confidence). 1712 popis: present in Lika regiment parishes (e.g. Kompolje); also Tomljenović variant in Zavalje (Ogulin regiment).
