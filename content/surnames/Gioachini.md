# Gioachini

## Bearers
- **Michiel Angelo Gioachini** — Zadar resident; on 24 July 1684 acquired (with right of redemption) the Pierantonii–Grizogono house in the parish of San Stefano / San Simeone, Zadar, for 260 ducats; on 14 September 1686 received the 260 ducats from Stojan Janković and surrendered the house (doc 143) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
