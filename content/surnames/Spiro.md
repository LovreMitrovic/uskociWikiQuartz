# Spiro

Greek family resident in Venice.

## Bearers in Desnica II
- **Petar (Piero) Spiro Greco** — Greek who maintained pop [[Petar-Jagodic-Kuridza-b1666-Bukovica|Petar Jagodić-Kuridža]] in Venetian custody for ~40 years. On 29 March 1746, after Jagodić's release by the State Inquisitors, Petar Spiro was entrusted with him: ordered "non lasciarlo sortire dal circondario de Greci" (not to leave the Greek quarter) and to send him home at the first opportunity (doc 390).
