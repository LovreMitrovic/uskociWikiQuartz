# Mušković

Old Vlach kindred — territorialized katun at [[Muskovci]] near Obrovac (catunar Dien Mušković, 1433 Lika kraljevski Vlasi charter) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. 1544: 25 families fled Lika summer pasture across to Habsburg, settled at [[Prilisce]] under [[Nikola-Zrinski-b????]] as hereditary *libertini*. Catholic, čakavski-ikavski.
