# Galiotović

## Bearers
- **Bose Zuppanovich** + **Luca Glumaz Giurliich** + **Zorzi Galossich** + **Ivan Ivancevich da Poglie** — 1647-08-31 enumerated by pop Sorić as the harambaše who failed to march (doc. 16). NB: *Galossich* may be a variant of *Galiotović* but uncertain.
- **dvojica braće Galiotović** — 1648-08-01 *due fratelli Galliotovich, uno de' quali pero é fatto priggione* at the Battle of Zečevo I (doc. 43, Mihaljević's deposition).
- **Grgur (Gregorio) Galiotović da Voinich, alfier del capitano Luca Glumaz** — 1653-10-24 *"di statura comune, barba rasa, mostacchi pel castagno, d' anni 30 in circa"* — beheaded the kninski aga **Asan Cuna** under Knin and presented the head to Dolfin; awarded peso di biscotto/month for life of war (doc. 77).
