# Durakbegović

Bosnian beg family that held the Vrana *zijamet* and the Sandžak of Krka (Cetina) on the Dalmatian frontier from before 1671 to the Vrana uprising 1683.

## Bearers
- **Durak-beg (Durac bei) Durakbegović** — sandžak-beg of Krka. Active 1671–1684. The post-1671 razgraničenje granted Vrana back to "li figlioli di Durag begh antichi padroni di quella" (doc. 196, 1671-07-20). His wife (la moglie del sangiacco) lived at Vrana then Plavno (doc. 316, 367). His son **Allilbeg Duracbegovich** was beheaded by Stojan Janković per Stojan's 1670 supplica (doc. 170).
- **Muhamed-beg (Mohamed-beg) Durakbegović** — sandžak-beg of Krka. Letters in Bosnian Cyrillic 1675-08-09, 1675-08-12, 1675-09-x, 1682-01-02, 1684-02-22 (docs. 212, 213, 216, 277, 377). Father (1682) is a confidant of the Posedarski; mediates between Knin Turks and Venice. By 1684 ex-sandžak (*passato sangiacco*), called by the Bosnian basha to dissuade the Morlachi from defection (doc. 377).
- **Mehmed-beg Durakbegović** — also referred to (in 1684 he is alive and resident; 1684 has wife and son who flee to Plavno then Blagaj — doc. 367).
- **Hasan-beg Durakbegović** — brother of the sandžak; 1670 the *casa di Assan Begh* in Vrana investitura'd to the Vasallo brothers (doc. 178). 1679 with Krčki kadi at Žemunik, conferring with Soppe (doc. 261). Implicated in the August 1682 Žemunik affair where Hassan begh led an unauthorized force against the Morlachi cultivating the Žemunik fields (doc. 300, retrospective).
- **Durakbegovica** (anonymous wife of the sandžak) — 1683-09-29 levaling Vrana, retreats to Plavno (doc. 316). 1684-01-28 with son retreats further to Blagaj in Hercegovina (doc. 367).
- **Allilbeg Duracbegovich** — son; beheaded by Stojan Janković.

## Notes
- Mocenigo (after the source's end-date) made Mehmed-beg Durakbegović sandžak of a place "verso Canisa" — survives the Vrana uprising (doc. 350).

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)

- **Muhamed-beg Durakbegović** — Turkish bey of Plavno; his **tower (cula) at Plavno was assigned to Nicolò Pirić** in 1692 when 5000 souls of the Bjelaj region settled with conti Đurić, Pirić and Zorić (docs. 251, 252, 253) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
