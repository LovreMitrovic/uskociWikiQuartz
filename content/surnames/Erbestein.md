# Herberstein (Erbestein)

Habsburg general.

## Bearers in Desnica II
- **General Herberstein/Erbestein** — Habsburg general of Karlstadt (Karlovac), county of Senj; was provisioning a large ship with cannons to bring Barletta salt to Karlobag and supply Lika/Krbava forts in 1690; **died before executing his plans** [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (docs. 228, 281). Came to Lika after Venetians ~1689 to consolidate Imperial gains (doc. 316).
