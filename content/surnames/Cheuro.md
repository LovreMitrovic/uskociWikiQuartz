# Cheuro (Ćuro / Ker)

Single attestation: **Tommaso Cheuro con tre figli e nipote** — 5 souls in the 1671-12-17 Dračevac roster (doc. 199). Croatian transliteration uncertain — possibly *Ćuro*, *Ker*, *Hor*. Cf. *Petar Chieur* in another doc.
