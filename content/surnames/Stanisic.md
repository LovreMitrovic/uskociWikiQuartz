# Stanišić

Surname listed by Šarić among Zadar-hinterland anthroponyms with medieval-modern continuity [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)

- **Zorzi (Đurađ) Stanković (Stancovich)** — Gorica resident; goods stolen in 1693-11-04 raid; recognized Žegar dialect of raiders (doc. 268) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Surname spelled Stanković in the source.
- See also separate [[Stojanovic|Stojanović]] entry.

Note: a Nikanor Stanković of Velika appears at Šaramprov of Kalinika 1698, protected from Bishop Busović's harassment (doc. 308) — surname Stanković, possibly distinct from Stanišić [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
