# Vlatković (Jurjević-Vlatković)

Catholic Bosnian-Hum noble lineage holding lands in late-medieval western Hercegovina. [[Augustin-Vlatkovic-b????]] (fra) entered Ottoman service c. 1482 with peasant and Vlach subjects, governing vojvodaluk Gornje Primorje, Gorska župa and Ljubuški (later nahije Fragustin, Primorje, Ljubuški) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].

## Bearers in this wiki
- [[Augustin-Vlatkovic-b????]] — fra, vojvoda in Ottoman service from c. 1482.
- **D. Vičko Vlatković** — Novigrad parohijski paroh, killed 1662-02-13 at Otres (Novigradska godovna knjiga, doc. 119 [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]).
