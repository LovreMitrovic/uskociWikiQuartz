# Iljanović

## Bearers
- **conte Matimir Matija Nicolaus Illynova** — exiled Imperial-service noble in Vienna; in his Latin/Italian letter of 30 July 1686 (doc 139) wrote to Stojan Janković claiming descent from the *signori de Illyanova*, hereditary counts of Gabela and dukes of *Culmia* (Hum/Herzegovina), asserting hereditary rights over the lands "anco sotto Zarra," and asking Stojan to spare those peasants from looting because he intended to "ripatriar" to Dalmatia after the war. The Venetian State Inquisitors flagged this as a Ragusan intrigue (docs 147, 154); treat as **low-confidence** historical claim [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].

## Notes
- Source-page summary: "the Iljanović letter (doc 139, 30 July 1686) purporting to derive Stojan Janković from Hercegovinian dukes was flagged by State Inquisitors as a Ragusan intrigue" [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
