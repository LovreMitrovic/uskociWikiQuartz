# Vukelić

Bunjevac surname; among the 16 reconstructed families of the [[Krmpote-Migration-Lic-1627|second (1627) Krmpote migration]] (Pavličević 1989) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]]. 1712 popis: present in Donji [[Kosinj]] (Otočac regiment) and Kuterevo parishes [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].
