# Vukčević

## Bearers
- **Mileta Vukčević, harambaša** — 1648-07-08 4 ducati/month assigned by Foscolo, *"comune, pello e barba castagna quadra, occhi uarii, segnato nel ciglio sinistro"* (doc. 33). 1648-07-17: *"Žegarani uskočeni pod Nin od 200 pješaka izgubili deset"* at Ribnik (doc. 39).
- **Pavle (Paval) Vukčević** — 1663-05-08 harambaša guarding the **Brusevo** pass with men of [[Zegar|Žegar]], [[Ervenik]] and [[Posedarje]] (doc. 130). 1666-05-06 elected vice-harambaša of Posedarje by unanimous vote during Stojan Janković's captivity (doc. 152).
- **Nikola Vukčević** — 1673-05-01 *piezzo* for Vuko Kovač (doc. 204). 1684-04-07 with Jovan Sinobad and others, victim of Ilija Mitrović's plundering (doc. 391).
- **Vuk Vukčević** — 1679-05-16 protected by mandate from his son's kidnappers (doc. 247): "ne per se ne per interposte persone ardiscano offender, molestar, o inquietar..." Vule (a son of Dragosav Capelletto Budin) and Bogoie his nephew are warned off Radojica Garković's house — Vukčević / Garković feud spillover.
- **Vukadin Vukčević** — 1679 son of Dragosav (doc. 247).
- **Bogoje Vukčević** — 1679 nephew of Dragosav (doc. 247).
- **Guozden Vuccevich** — 1684-04-07 also among the Sinobad case complainants (doc. 391).
- **Simon Vuccevich** — 1684-04-07 ditto (doc. 391).

## Notes
- The Vukčevići of Budin are documented in 1684 in feud (krvarina) with the [[Mitrovic|Mitrovići]]; Polo Michiel asked to mediate (doc 15, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
- Per Desnica's 1951 editorial footnote (to doc 115), the Vukčević surname is **still present in [[Zegar|Žegar]]** today (1951). Together with Zelić, Radmilović, and Nanić, the families "came in 1648 with the Jankovićs to Budim near Posedarje and during the Morean War many returned to Lika; Budim is today (1951) ruins".
