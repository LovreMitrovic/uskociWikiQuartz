# Grudarović

Single attestation: **harambaša Petar Grudarović** of [[Miranje]] in 1646-03-26 (doc. 2 [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]) — co-petitioner with pop Petar Popović and Milinko Dević for *uskakanje* with 20 Morlach families and 50 fighting men.
