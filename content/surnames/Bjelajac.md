# Bjelajac (Vlasi Bjelajaca)

Last Vlach colonization wave from Bosnia, 1690 — settled Plavno, Strmica, Pađene, Mokro Polje, Zrmanja, Popina, and produced much of the modern microtoponymy of the region [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].
