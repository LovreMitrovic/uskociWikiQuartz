# Bolić

Married twice into the [[Smiljanic|Smiljanić]] line.

## Bearers in Desnica II
- **Marija Bolić** (the elder) — wife of Smoljan Smiljanić; widow by 1690; testament 1693-10-05; died before 1694-04-13; mother of Lazaro and Margarita Smiljanić (doc. 267) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **Maria Bolić-Smiljanić** (the younger) — wife of Lazaro Smiljanić; pregnant when he wrote will 1696-07-28 (doc. 289) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].

## Notes
- Two Marijas, two generations: Lazaro's mother and Lazaro's wife — possible cousin marriage or coincidence.
