# Peraica

## Bearers
- [[Ilija-Peraica-b????-Cetina|Ilija Peraica]] — *famoso* harambaša/sardar; in March 1685 led 600 armed men plus families with 30,000 small animals and 10,000 cattle from the Cetina region to Trogir territory; awarded 10 ducats/month and made sardar for c. 1600 souls (docs 59, 60, 61, 66) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **karambassà Peraizza** — same person; on 8 September 1685 his *partita* of c. 175 people (men and women) was attacked and his band defeated by Turkish *fuste* off the coast (doc 112, undated; date from internal "ottavo giorno del mese sudetto") [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- [[Frane-Peraica-b????-Zegar|Frane Peraica]] — see person page; mentioned later in the volume [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
