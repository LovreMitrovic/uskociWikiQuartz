# Jurišić

[[Nikola-Jurisic-b????]] held the Podgrad and Postojna estates from 1528 (taken from [[Krsto-I-Frankapan-b????]]). In 1538 he persuaded a group of "istarskih Ćića" with ~40,000 head of livestock to leave Zrmanja for northern Istria/Kras under Habsburg protection — referring to them as "Alt Römer" [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].
