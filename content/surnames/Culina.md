# Čulina (Čulinović)

Vlach lineage attested on the original Tromeđa in 16th–17th c. Possibly continuous with the 1528-attested [[Culina-sin-Milica-b????-Bjeline]] (knez, *timar* in Lika), though Šarić leaves the link uncertain [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Mid 16th c.: living at Pridraga (novigradski distrikt) and Ljubač (1608: 2 houses); some moved to Ledenice in [[Senjska-Kapetanija]].
