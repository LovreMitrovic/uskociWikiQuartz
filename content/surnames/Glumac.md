# Glumac

## Bearers
- **Luka Glumac (Đurlić) — Luca Glumaz Giurliich** — 1647-08-31 capitan in pop Sorić's force (doc. 16); 1653-01-16 *Luca Glumaz da Voinich* co-signs proxy (doc. 70). 1648-06-21 capitan Tochich and Glumac with don Sorić's brother (Mate) command 300 in the Mazin valley raid (doc. 32). 1653-10-24 still active as captain — his alfier was Grgur Galiotović (doc. 77).
- **Ilia Glumaz** — 1663-05-08 harambaša at Roški Slap pass with men of Vrana, Tin, Pakoštani, Zaravecchia, Sv. Filip-Jakov, Torrette (doc. 130).
