# Molin

Venetian patrician.

## Bearers in Desnica II
- **Alessandro Molin (Aleksandar Molino)** — provveditor generale in Dalmatia **1689–1692**; established Obrovac salt scale; appointed Casanova governor of Obrovac; divided Kotari into upper/lower; appointed serdars Zaviša Mitrović (Upper) and Bože Milković (Lower); appointed Sinobad governor of Knin; etc. (docs. 226–242, 257) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Succeeded by Daniel Dolfin Feb 1692.
