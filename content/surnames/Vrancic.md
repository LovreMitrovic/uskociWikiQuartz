# Vrančić

Šibenik-zaleđe lineage; merged with [[Mandusic|Mandušić]] after Vuk Mandušić's death in 1648 left no closer male heir than his sister's son Tadija Vrančić. Style becomes **Mandušić alias Vrančić**.
