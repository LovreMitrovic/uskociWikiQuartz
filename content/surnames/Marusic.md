# Marušić

## Bearers
- **Marušić** (officer) — killed at the Sinj defeat April 1685 (doc 60) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **vojvoda Ivan Marušić** — captured by Šain-aga Mandić of Sinj; held at Sinj; in his Bosnian-Cyrillic letter (doc 110, undated) to Stipan Garković asks the kraina capi to capture a Sinj Turk for his exchange [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **figliolo del Marussich** — son of vojvoda Ivan Marušić; Turkish slave at Zadar; brought letters between captives Sept 1686 (doc 113) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
