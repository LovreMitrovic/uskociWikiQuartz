# Ružić

Vlach kindred originating from Šibenik area, members of the medieval "kraljevski Vlasi" (royal Vlachs) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Migrated to Vodice on Kras 1518; returned to nahija Kličevac (Miranje, Jagodnja) under [[Tomas-Ruzic-b????-Sibenik]] 1528. After 1538/40 returned to Šibenik area; 1540 morlački katun "Ružić", with katunar [[Jure-Heracic-b????]] in 1553.
