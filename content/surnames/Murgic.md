# Murgić

Lika surname (not necessarily Bunjevac itself). Foremost member: **[[Ivan-Murgic-b????-Perusic]]** († 1881) — career officer in the Vojna krajina, whose posthumous *Vienac* article (1882) is the **last izvorna svjedočanstva** of self-identifying Bunjevci in Lika–Primorje [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

Erdeljanović 1930 mistakenly assumed Murgić was Bunjevac (Šarić corrects this).
