# Bortulačić

Zadar-patrician (gradski) line, never on payroll, served as *venturiere* (volunteer/civil servant). Active throughout the 1666–1684 emergencies of the Kotari–Bukovica frontier.

## Bearers
- **Frane (Francesco) Bortulačić q. Šimun** — 1666-08-26 receives Korner's instructions on managing the chain-of-fires *fuochi/fumi* signal system from Šibenik to Zadar (docs. 156, 157). Mentioned as *q. Simon* in 1678. Held position by 1678 (doc. 242, on the Liga oversight).
- **Šimun (Simon) Bortolazzi q. Girolamo** — kavalier from a different *Bortolazzi* sub-branch; at Sv. Ilija 1676-08-17 stands as *kompare* at Zaviša/Sabba Janković's wedding (doc. 229). Active in 1683–84 as one of the four *direttori* over the Morlachi (doc. 395). 1684-04-09 (doc. 392) named *capo de Morlacchi essistenti alla Vrana* with 20 ducati/month, on the express request of those very Morlachi who *"nominarono precisamente D. Simon Bortolazzi."*
- **Jerolim (Girolamo) Bortolazzi** — 1678 nominated to oversee the Liga (doc. 242).

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)

The murder of Šimun Bortulačić (already noted above as *capo de Morlacchi alla Vrana*) is documented at length in [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].

- **Šimun Bortulačić (Bortolazzi)** colonnello, governor of Vrana — murdered **1692-01-16** in Vrana by Biograđani and Pakoštanci with arquebus, body covered with stones; aged ~31; buried in Sv. Stošija, Zadar (docs. 224, 242, 243, 247, 269, 271, 275). Primary instigator of the murder: **Tomaso Antičić** of Zaravecchia/Pakoštane, who escaped wounded from arrest in Zadar (doc. 269). Two main perpetrators captured and condemned 1693–94; **Mattio Žabetić's brother** was *squartato* (quartered) for participation (doc. 357); **Tomaso Tubić** of Zaravecchia was banished then pardoned (doc. 357).
