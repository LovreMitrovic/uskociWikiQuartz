# Vlasto

Greek (or Greek-rite) clergy line.

## Bearers
- **pop don Grigorije (Gregorio) Vlasto** — 1671-06-21 capelan and parohijski sveštenik of *pravoslavnih Morlaka* of the Spianata (Sv. Ivan church). 1671-06-25 disputes with the Trećoredci of Sv. Ivan over the *colusorne* contracts they had signed with the deceased kaluđer Mihail Zarcanin (docs. 193, 194). 1672-11-28 baptizes Bojana Novaković at Sv. Ilija Zadar (doc. 203).

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)

- **Dimo (Dimitrije) Vlastò** — Greek; appointed **governor of both Obrovci** 1693-01-10, replacing late Cesare Casanova; salary 14 ducats; oversaw salt customs (doc. 260) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
