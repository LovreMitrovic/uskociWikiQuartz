# Balenović

Croatian surname; Andrija Kačić Miošić's 1756 *pjesmarica* names the Balenovići among the "vitezovi hrvatski" alongside Rukavina, Starčević and Došen [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]]. Likely related to the [[Balen]] / Balinović line.
