# Benja (Begna)

Zadar patrician family later merged with the Posedarski into the **Benja-Posedarski** style. The family-archive holds the *Posedarski Zbornik*, one of Desnica's principal source-fonds.

## Bearers
- **conte Lodoviko Benja** — 1670-09-14 investitured by Barbaro with the **Benkovac** castle and 400 *gognali* (doc. 175).
- **conte Giulio Begna** — 1682-11-06 nominated for the Liga oversight (doc. 296).
- **kavalier-corazze Begna** — 1648-07-14 sent with Civaleli into the territory to console the post-Ribnik Morlachi (doc. 37).
- **knez Anton Benja-Posedarski** — Zadar nobleman who in the 1930s held the family archive (custodian of the *Posedarski Zbornik*) — referenced extensively in Desnica's *fontes* but not as a historical actor in the corpus.

## In Desnica II (1684–1749)

- **Giulio Begna** — appointed **governor of pertinenza 5 (Nadin)** by Cornaro in the 1689-03-11 Liga reform (doc. 224) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **don Gregorio Begna** — late by 1693; recorded as debtor to Marija Smiljanić for 4 zecchini in her testament (doc. 267) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
