# Gleđevac

Vlach katun originating from Humska zemlja (Hercegovina), attested in Zadar hinterland from 1391 as subjects of the krbavski princes [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. By 1496 territorialized in Bukovica village (kod Karina). Returned from Istria to Karin region 1528. Defected en masse to Venice 1538 (Podnovje, novigradski distrikt). Name "Žakman" (variant of Šikman) is a typical Vlach -man-suffix anthroponym.
