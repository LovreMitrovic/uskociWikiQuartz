# Rošić

## Bearers
- **Vukašin Rošić (Rosić)** — harambaša from Čitluk Lički; in February 1685 led 20 Morlak families from Čitluk near Novi (Lika) to the Starigrad/Velebit area; petitioned for the Perušić castle (doc 45) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
