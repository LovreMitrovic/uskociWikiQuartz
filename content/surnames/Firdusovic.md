# Firdusović

## Bearers
- **Mustaj-beg Firdusović** — *capitano di Chnin*. 1662-02-18 attested as kapetan kninski (doc. 121). 1684-02-end coauthor with Mehmed-aga (kninski disdar) of letter to the rebel knezovi promising amnesty if they return to Ottoman submission (doc. 380).
