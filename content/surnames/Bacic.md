# Bačić

Surname recorded in Šarić's continuity-list of Zadar-hinterland anthroponyms with possible medieval roots. Some bearers absorbed into the "bunjevačka" Catholic Vlach population near [[Benkovac]] and [[Obrovac]] [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].

## Bearers (Desnica 1683)
- **harambaša Vid Bačić** — 1683-11-09 with alfier Petar Radić, *capo* of the Boraja-Suhi Dolac-Labin-Prapatnica-Bristivica-Ljubitovica-Vrsno villages; presents petition to Donà for *uskakanje* of 1000 souls / 400 fighters (doc. 332). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
