# Grizogono

Zadar patrician family.

## Bearers
- **Caterina (Cattarina) Grizogono** — daughter of *quondam* Gregorio Grizogono *quondam* Biasio; wife of Antonio Maria Pierantonii (who abandoned her c. 1685); 14 September 1686 ceded to Stojan Janković her perpetual right to redeem a house in San Stefano / San Simeone parish, Zadar, originally pledged to Michiel Angelo Gioachini for 260 ducats; received 410 lire on account from Stojan, with the rest to follow on appraisal (doc 143) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
