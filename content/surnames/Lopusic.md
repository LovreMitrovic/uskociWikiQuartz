# Lopušić

## Bearers
- **Čejvan-aga Lopušić** — Knin frontier Turk, characterised by Valier as "*de' principali e forse il più infesto tra tutti i confinanti*" (one of the chief and most dangerous of the frontier); killed by Dragoja Avramović in early 1685 (doc 49) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
