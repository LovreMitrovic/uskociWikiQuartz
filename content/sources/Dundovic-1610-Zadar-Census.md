# Popis stanovništva kopnenog dijela zadarskog okružja iz 1610. godine

## Summary
A 1610 Venetian population census (popis) of the **mainland** (kopneni) part of the Zadar district, transcribed and published by Zdenko Dundović (2019) from the original in the Biblioteca del Museo Correr, Venice (Codice Cicogna, ms. 3112, fasc. 2). **Prilog 1** lists, village by village, the **head of each household** by name and surname, with a household breakdown (starci / muškarci / žene / dječaci / djevojčice / ukupno — old men / men / women / boys / girls / total). 18 mainland villages of the Zadar contado are covered.

Only heads of household are named; all other household members appear as counts. There is therefore no kinship data (no spouse, child, or parent names) in the source.

## Reliability and biases
- **Primary** in content (a contemporary official enumeration), delivered here as a modern scholarly **transcription** — high reliability for the fact of each household head's name, village, and household size in 1610.
- Names are recorded in **Venetian chancery orthography** (e.g. *Gliubouich* = Ljubović, *Dlancich* = Dlančić). Croatized forms in this wiki are editorial normalizations; where uncertain, the Person page carries `confidence: low` and the original spelling is preserved in `given_name_variants` / `surname_variants` and body prose.
- Dundović compares this 1610 list with Roman Jelić's edition of the 1608 census and corrects some of Jelić's surname readings — a later ingest of the Jelić material should be reconciled against this one.
- The census covers only the mainland (kopneni) district, not the islands.

## Key facts extracted
- Villages: 18 (Pakoštane, Biograd, Sv. Filip i Jakov, Turanj, Sukošan, Bibinje, Dračevac Zadarski, Ražanac, Posedarje, Ljubač, Gruhe, Bokanjac, Puntamika, Diklo, Bartulac, Petrčane, Kožino, Punta Ljupča).
