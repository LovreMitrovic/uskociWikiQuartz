# Boško Desnica, *Istorija kotarskih uskoka 1684–1749, sveska II* (1951)

## Citation
Desnica, Boško. *Istorija kotarskih uskoka 1684—1749. Sveska II.* Srpska akademija nauka, Zbornik za istoriju, jezik i književnost srpskog naroda, treće odeljenje, knjiga XIII (*Historia Uscoccorum Cotarensium 1684—1749, Fasciculus II*). Beograd: Izdavačko preduzeće Narodne Republike Srbije, 1951. Redigovao Svetozar Brkić.

Companion to [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]] (1646–1684); together the two volumes form the canonical primary-source base for ravnokotarska genealogy 1646–1749.

## Period covered
**6 May 1684 — 9 April 1749** (65 years). Document density is heaviest in 1684–1700 (Morean War years); the long 1700–1749 tail covers administrative aftermath, the 1704 Bukovica revolt, the War of Spanish Succession redeployments, and final Venetian-Habsburg border arrangements.

## Form, language, transmission
- **Form**: 392 numbered documents transcribed in the same fidelity policy as Sveska I — original orthography preserved.
- **Languages**: Italian (overwhelming majority), Latin (dukali), Croatian/Serbian (a few letters from Morlach harambaše and Bosnian agas, both ćirilica and latinica), one **Bosnian-Cyrillic letter from vojvoda Ivan Marušić** (in captivity, doc 110).
- **Document types**: dispatches of the *provveditore generale* to the Senate (Mocenigo, Valier, Cornaro, Molin, Dolfin, Mocenigo II, Vendramin, Riva, Zane, Diedo); *terminacije*; *patenti* and *dukali*; *ducali* of doges Marc'Antonio Giustinian, Silvestro Valier, Alvise Mocenigo II; reports of inquisitori di stato; parish registers (Sv. Ilija and Sv. Šime in Zadar); inquisitions/preslušavanja; letters from Atlagić, Filipović, Mandić, Pašić; family attestations from the Janković-Mitrović archive.

## Archival fonds drawn upon
1. **Mletački državni arhiv** (Venice State Archive): *Senato — Provveditor general dell' armi (in Dalmazia)*, filze 112/521 onwards through ~155, the spine of the volume; also *Inquisitori di Stato*, busta 47 and busta supplicahe.
2. **Zadarski stari arhiv** (Zadar State Archive): *Spisi* of provveditori Mocenigo, Valier, Cornaro, Molin, Dolfin (multiple), Mocenigo II, Vendramin, Riva, Zane, Diedo, Erizzo.
3. **Family archives**: 
   - **Janković-Mitrović archive** ("Trassunto delle benemerenze della famiglia Mitrovich") in the possession of B. Desnica, Obrovac.
   - **Posedarski Zbornik** in the *Arhiv kneza Antona Benje-Posedarskog u Zadru*.
   - **Smiljanić zbornik** ("Summario di ducali...", 1786) in the *Bihać* society in Split.
4. **Parish/church archives**: Sv. Ilija (Greek-rite) Zadar; Sv. Šime Zadar; matica umrlih of pravoslavna crkva Sv. Ilije in Zadar (used for Janković-family deaths and for pop Petar Jagodić's death notice 9 April 1749).

## Reliability and biases
- **Primary**: original Venetian administrative correspondence; near-real-time dispatches.
- **Strengths**:
  - The single densest source for the **Janković-Mitrović family** in its prime: Stojan's regiment authorisation (7 Nov 1686), his death at Duvno (Aug 1687), the deaths of his wife Antonia (Jan 1687) and four of his children, his sons Nikola and Konstantin, the long aftermath through brother Zaviša and nephew Filip Mitrović.
  - Comprehensive lists of new Morlach migrations: 152 families/2,300 souls from Lika (1684); 70 families/900 souls (May 1685); 316 families/2,561 souls (June 1685); aggregate 3,051 families / 10,290 fighters (1685 census, doc 107); 5,000 souls from Bjelaj region (March 1692, doc with serdari Đurić, Pirić, Zorić); 5,000 souls from Brochno-Blato-Goranci (1693–94, settled at Zadvarje and Vrgorac); 26 families with Orthodox priest Đurađ from Skoplje/Vakup (Nov 1687); 15 Franciscan friars + 500 family members from Rama (Oct 1687, settled Sustipan).
  - The **Kotari Pertinenze reform of 11 March 1689** (doc 224) — Cornaro's ordinance dividing the Zadar contado into 9 administrative units, with named governors, judges, descrizione rules, militia rolls.
  - The **Knin conquest of 29 August – 11 September 1688** (docs 213–220) in unprecedented detail.
  - Hundreds of single-mention surnames with place attestation, for example: Žepina (Plavno, doc 2), Brkljača/Bistrić/Boljač (Radovin/Vinjerac, doc 31), Avramović (doc 49), Vojkorsić (Čitluk, doc 54), Lakić (Karin, doc 56), Sinovčić (Novigrad, doc 32), Kosić (Starigrad, doc 92), Sudarović (Parčići in Lika → Žegar, doc 114), Tepšić, Gagić (Biljane, doc 124), Cona, Cvitanović, Tintor, Goreta, Palikuća, Žinović (Šibenik-Skradin capi, doc 155), Salomonić, Iljanović, etc.
- **Biases**:
  - Venetian-administrative perspective dominates; Morlach voices appear in mediated petitions and depositions.
  - Numerical claims (Turkish casualties, captured animals) are partisan; Desnica flags inflations in footnotes.
  - The **Iljanović letter (doc 139, 30 July 1686)** purporting to derive Stojan Janković from Hercegovinian dukes was flagged by State Inquisitors as a Ragusan intrigue (docs 147, 154); record but treat as low-confidence.
  - The **Lapac complaint (doc 126, 1 Feb 1686)** anonymous accusation against Stojan Janković and Šimun Bortulačić of extortion and unlawful killings — record but no resolution in source.
  - Desnica's footnotes are interpretive secondary commentary; treat document bodies as primary.
- **Reliability**: **high** for names/dates/places/kin-links; **medium-high** for numerical claims; **high** for cross-references to family archives no longer privately held.

## Key facts extracted (summary)
- Persons added or substantially expanded: ~400 (the full Janković-Mitrović family in its 1684–1749 prime, the four Kotari capi, all named Venetian provveditori and inquisitori, named Ottoman beys/agas at Knin/Sinj/Livno/Bihać, every harambaša named in deposition or migration roster, every named bearer in the 1689 pertinenze reform).
- Surnames added or expanded: ~220, including all single-mention bearers with place attestation (per user's directive: capture every surname).
- Places added or updated: ~80 — the wider geography extends north into Lika–Krbava (Lapac, Boričevac, Ripač, Bjelaj, Vakuf, Srb, Komić), east into Bosanska Krajina (Livno, Glamoč, Grahovo, Banja Luka, Kupres), into Hercegovina (Mostar, Gabela, Stolac, Počitelj, Trebinje, Nevesinje, Gacko, Vrgorac), south to Kotor / Pastrovići / Budva / Cetinje, plus a tail of new pertinenze unit names (Pridraga, Polešnik, Bađun, Klošter, etc.).
- Events documented: Sinj battle (Apr 1685), Bjelaj raid (Sep 1685), Cetina migration of Peraica's people (Mar 1685), Zadvarje siege relief (June 1685), Vrana attempt (Oct 1684), 1685 Lika census, 1686 Sinj conquest, Stojan Janković's death at Duvno (Aug 1687), Rama Franciscan migration (Oct 1687), Skoplje migration (Nov 1687), Pasha's April 1688 Kotari raid, Knin conquest (Aug–Sep 1688), Vrlika capitulation (Sep 1688), Kotari Pertinenze reform (11 Mar 1689), Šimun Bortulačić's murder at Vrana (16 Jan 1692), Bjelaj migration (March 1692), Brochno-Goranci migration (1693–94), Mitrović–Sinobad / Mitrović–Milković reconciliations (1692), Ilija Mitrović's death (1693), 1704 Bukovica revolt under pop Petar Jagodić-Kuridža.
