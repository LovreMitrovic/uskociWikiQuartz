# Ražov/Milković — Škabrnja monografija (popis 1686–1719)

## Summary
A census table, **"Selo Škabrnja — Popis domaćinstva i broj domaće stoke od 1686. do 1719."**, printed on p. 37 of the monograph *Škabrnja – gaj, selo, župa, općina* by Slavko Ražov and Ante Milković. It lists **39 heads of household** in [[Skabrnja|Škabrnja]] with a demographic breakdown per household (ljudi za oružje / dječaci / žene / djevojčice / ukupno — men-at-arms / boys / women / girls / total) and livestock counts (konji / goveda / telad / krave / ovce — horses / cattle / calves / cows / sheep). Row 1 is the parish priest (župnik sv. Luke) with no household data. Only heads are named; all other household members appear as counts — no kinship data except **patronymics** ("pok. X" = son of the late X) and three **detto** bynames (Itković, Bugarin, Bauer).

Scope note: only the census table was ingested from this page. A testament fragment printed above the table on the same page is out of scope for this source page.

## Citation
Slavko Ražov i Ante Milković, *Škabrnja – gaj, selo, župa, općina*, str. 37. Publication year and publisher not yet recorded (photographed excerpt only).

## Reliability and biases
- **Primary in content** (an early-modern enumeration of households), delivered as a **transcription in a local-history monograph** — the weakest link is that the table's own archival citation is **not visible on the photographed page** (a testament quoted on the same page cites *Spisi Zadarskih kapetana 1794.–1795., knjiga 4., str. 103*, but that citation closes the testament, not the table). Reliability `medium` until the archival source is identified.
- The period "od 1686. do 1719." is **ambiguous** — one enumeration or a compilation across that range is not stated. Persons are therefore dated `fl. 1686–1719`.
- Names are printed in **modern Croatian orthography**, i.e. already editorially normalized by the authors; original record spellings are not shown.
- **Arithmetic verification passed** (done at ingest): each row satisfies UKUPNO = ljudi za oružje + dječaci + žene + djevojčice + 1 (the head himself), and all ten column sums match the printed totals row (24, 34, 45, 45, 186, 113, 128, 84, 143, 1555).

## Key facts extracted
- Households: 39 (incl. the priest's row without data); souls: **186** (UKUPNO includes the head).
- Demographics: 24 men-at-arms, 34 boys, 45 women, 45 girls (+ 38 enumerated heads).
- Livestock totals: 113 horses, 128 cattle, 84 calves, 143 cows, 1,555 sheep.
- Village hierarchy visible: župnik sv. Luke (Raspović), kapetan (Vujavić), sudac (Lokinović).
