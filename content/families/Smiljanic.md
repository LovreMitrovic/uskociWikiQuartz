# Smiljanić household

**The Smiljanići were originally from [[Udbina]], not Smiljan in Lika.** This is per Desnica's footnote on doc. 20 of [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]] — a definitive correction overriding family-tradition.

## Genealogy
- [[Petar-Smiljanic-b????-Udbina|Petar Smiljanić]] (founder; d. 1648-07-17 at Ribnik).
  - [[Ilija-Smiljanic-b????-Udbina|Ilija Smiljanić]] (d. 1654-09-08 at Vučjak).
    - Wife **Kata** (b. Turkish, baptised Christian; remarried Milivoj Zubičević 1655).
  - [[Filip-Smiljanic-b????-Udbina|Filip Smiljanić]] (d. Jan 1656 in Šibenik).
- (Side branch / nephew of Ilija and Filip):
  - [[Smoljan-Smiljanic-b????-Bukovica|Smoljan Smiljanić]] (a/k/a Smoglianovich; also patronymically *Mihalevich*) — uterine half-brother of Šimun and Marko Brajinović (sons of his mother Anka by her first husband q. Frane Brajinović).
    - **Lazaro Smiljanić** (in tenera età 1679) — son.
- (Cousin): **Bartul Banenović da Pavičići** = *Germano del carambassà Elia Smiglianich* (doc. 66).

## Religion
Records do not specify confession; their family arrangements (Kata's Catholic re-baptism on marriage to Ilija, the Sv. Stošija burial of Filip and his alfier) and the Brajinović Spianata-Sv. Marko residence suggest **rito latino**.

## 1671 inheritance restoration
The family-house in **Spianata behind Sv. Marko** was Anka's; on Smoljan's superintendence the silver-bridled saddlery, brocaded vests, gold ornaments, kilims, and weapons were restored to half-brothers Šimun and Marko Brajinović in 1671 (doc. 184 — original document in *Arhiv B. Desnice u Obrovcu*).
