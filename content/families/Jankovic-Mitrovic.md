# Janković-Mitrović household

Five-brother *uskakanje* from Ottoman [[Zegar|Žegar]] c. 1647 of which the eldest was [[Janko-Mitrovic-b1605-Zegar|Janko Mitrović]] q. Dimitri. Brought ~70 households / ~150 fighting men into Venetian Ravni Kotari.

## Generation 1 (Mitrović)
- **q. Dimitri Mitrović** (father; not himself in the corpus).
- **Janko Mitrović** (eldest; serdar; d. 1659 in Zadar).
- **Giovanni** (Stojan's supplica doc. 170).
- **Stefan** (ditto).
- **Pavle** (ditto).
- **Andrija** (ditto).
- **Vukadin Mitrović** (probable cousin; d. defending Novigrad 1653).

## Generation 2 (Janković, sons of Janko)
- [[Stojan-Jankovic-b1635-Zegar|Stojan Janković]] (b. ~1635, d. 1687) — kavalier 1670, capo de Morlacchi 1681.
- [[Zavisa-Jankovic-b????-Zegar|Zaviša/Sabba Janković]] — m. Maria Lase 1676.
- [[Ilija-Mitrovic-b????-Zegar|Ilija Mitrović]] — bandit; principal *fomentatore* of the Vrana uprising 1683.
- [[Janja-Jankovic-b1655-Zadar|Janja Janković]] (b. ~1655, d. 1680) — m. Dmitar Nikolić-Pivljanin 1675.

## Sons of Vukadin
- **Jakov Mitrović** — slave; pay assigned 1653 then 1681.
- **Giacomo Mitrović da Islam** — 1671 in the Greek-merchant murder case.

## Generation 3 (sons of Stojan)
With first wife **Vinka/Vuka** (d. by 1676):
- **Zorzi-Alvise Janković** b. 1674-04-23 (compare: Battista Sopino).
- **Anastazija Janković** b. 1675.
- **Nikola Janković** — in cavalry.

With second wife **[[Antonia-Reci-b????-Zadar|Antonia Reci]]** (m. 1676-08-16; Greek of Latin rite of Zadar; daughter of donna Nicolotta and q. Apostolo Rezzi):
- **Josip Janković** b. 1677-09-30.
- **Konstantin Janković** — capitan of 50 fanti from 1679-02-09.

## Marriages, in / out
- Janja Janković ↔ Mitar Nikolić-Pivljanin (1675) — anchors the Pivljanin line in Zadar.
- Wife Vinka — kuma at Bojana Novaković's baptism (1672).
- Stojan Janković ↔ Antonia Reci (1676) — marriage into Zadar Greek-of-Latin-rite milieu.

## Religion
**Pravoslavni rito greco** — Sv. Ilija parohijska crkva u Zadru.

## Archive
**Arhiv porodice Desnica u Obrovcu** — original *terminacije* on parchment for Janko, Stojan, Vukadin, Jakov; the *Trassunto delle benemerenze della famiglia Mitrovich*; the 1655 Smiljanić-Zubičević *dote* document; and other family papers used by Boško Desnica.
