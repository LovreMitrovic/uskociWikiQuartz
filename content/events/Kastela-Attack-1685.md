# Ottoman Attack on Kaštela (early April 1685)

About 3,000 cavalry under **Ahmed-paša of Hercegovina** struck Kaštela near Split in early April 1685, but were repulsed by some 3,000 castellani and Morlach defenders (doc 62, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

Mustafa Jasko was captured during the engagement; the attack came simultaneously with the Venetian disaster at [[Sinj-Battle-1685|Sinj]] and was the southern prong of a coordinated Ottoman counter-offensive in spring 1685 (doc 62, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
