# Bunjevac colonization of Lika–Krbava (1690+)

Major influx of Bunjevac families from coastal Senjska Krajina ([[Krmpote-Senj]], [[Krivi-Put]], [[Ledenice]], [[Senjska-Draga]], [[Sv-Jakov]]) and from Venetian territory ([[Vinjerac]], [[Starigrad]], [[Pag]]) into recovered Lika–Krbava after the Habsburg expulsion of the Ottomans.

Settlement nuclei: **[[Pazariste]], [[Smiljan]], [[Lovinac]]** [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

## Notable settlers
- **Porkulab [[Milos-Starcevic-b1656-Veliki-Zitnik]]** settles [[Pazariste]] in 1690; per the 1712 popis (Kaser 2003) holds 168 štrikova in Pazarište / Vaganac / [[Veliki-Zitnik]] with one son Jakov (age ~20) and 5 other family members.
- **Rukavine** receive estates in [[Sv-Mihovil]], [[Sv-Rok]], [[Vratnik]], [[Ricice]], [[Kosinj]] in 1691 (per Fras 1835).

## 1712 popis (Kaser 2003)
First systematic register: **~158 Bunjevac surnames** counted across 23 Lika/Otočac/Ogulin parishes. **37 surnames are shared between Catholic Bunjevci and Orthodox Vlasi** — common Dinaric origin documented [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]] (see footnote 58 of the article).

Bunjevac priests at major Bunjevac parishes in 1712:
- **Lovinac** — [[Damjan-Zdunic-b????-Lovinac]] (the *pop Zduna* of folk tradition)
- **Novo** — [[Petar-Butkovic-b????-Novo]]
- **Smiljan** — [[Simun-Zdunic-b????-Smiljan]]
- **Pazarište** — [[Juraj-Smolcic-b????-Pazariste]]

The katoličko svećenstvo was led by **[[Marko-Mesic-b????-Lika]]** during the popis.

This colonization seeds the "ličko-primorska" Bunjevac branch that culminates a century later in the [[Starcevic|Starčević]] political dynasty.
