# Senate-Approved Attempt to Poison Atlagić (22 January 1687)

On 22 January 1687 the **Inquisitori di Stato** sent **[[Girolamo-Cornaro-b????-Venice|Girolamo Cornaro]]** poison and instructions for an attempt on the life of **[[Mehmed-Atlagic-b????-Knin|Mehmed Atlagić]]**, freshly appointed Bosnian pasha (docs 165, 167, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

The plot was either abandoned or failed; a footnote to doc 185 hints that the chaplain duties of frate Andrea (Rama) the next year may have masked a second attempt (docs 165, 167, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
