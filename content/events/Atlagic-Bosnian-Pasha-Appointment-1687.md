# Atlagić's Appointment as Bosnian Pasha (early 1687)

In early 1687 **[[Mehmed-Atlagic-b????-Knin|Mehmed Atlagić]]** was exempted from a 50,000-real tribute owed to the Grand Vizier in exchange for a promise to return Morlach refugees to the sultan and lead the campaign against Venice as Bosnian pasha (docs 163, 170, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

The appointment placed the senior Atlagić of [[Knin]] at the head of Ottoman operations in Bosnia until his capture at the [[Knin-Conquest-1688|fall of Knin]] in September 1688 (docs 163, 170, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
