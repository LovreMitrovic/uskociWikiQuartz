# Mitrović–Sinobad Reconciliation (March 1692)

In March 1692 Provveditore Generale **[[Daniel-Dolfin-b????-Venice|Daniel Dolfin]]** mediated a reconciliation between the Mitrović and Milković parties; an oath was sworn in church and the long-running feud (engami implacable) between the **Mitrović** house and the **Sinobad** house was also formally resolved (doc text, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

To keep the families apart **[[Jovan-Sinobad-b????-Posedarje|Jovan Sinobad]]** moved his household from [[Posedarje]] to [[Knin]] (doc text, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]). The Mitrović–Sinobad krvarina had been documented as far back as 1684 among the Budin Morlachs.
