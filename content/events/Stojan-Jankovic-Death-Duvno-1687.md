# Death of Stojan Janković at Duvno (23 August 1687)

Per Provveditor Generale **[[Girolamo-Cornaro-b????-Venice|Girolamo Cornaro]]**'s dispatch (doc 174, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]), kavalier **[[Stojan-Jankovic-b1635-Zegar|Stojan Janković]]** was killed at **[[Duvno]]** between 23 and 26 August 1687: struck down by musket and sword while only 8–10 of his companions followed him into the assault — the larger Morlach body refused to advance.

The death ended the most decorated Kotari military career of the Morean War period and marked an inflection point in Venetian Morlach command: his older son **[[Nikola-Jankovic-b????-Zadar|Nikola]]** died in the Levant within months, leaving only **[[Konstantin-Jankovic-b????-Zadar|Konstantin]]** of the named heirs (awarded 10 ducats/month March 1688). The lateral branch through brother **[[Zavisa-Jankovic-b????-Zegar|Zaviša]]** continued the family's military leadership into the 1690s.

## Notes
- Provveditor Generale della Cavalleria **[[Antonio-Zen-b????-Venice|Antonio Zen]]** was present in the same campaign; the Duvno operation was part of the wider Cetina–Hercegovina pressure following the conquest of [[Sinj]] in 1686.
- The refusal of the Morlach body to follow Stojan into the final charge is, in Cornaro's reading, the visible end of the period in which Morlach loyalty ran through one charismatic leader.
