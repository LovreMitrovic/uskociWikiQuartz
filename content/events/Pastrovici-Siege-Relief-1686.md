# Relief of Paštrovići Towers (March 1686)

A Venetian galeotta under **Corponese** broke the Ottoman blockade of the Paštrovići towers in March 1686 and resupplied the besieged garrisons (doc 131, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

The action stabilised the Venetian outposts on the Montenegrin coast after the [[Crna-Gora-Defection-1685|Montenegrin defection of May 1685]] (doc 131, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
