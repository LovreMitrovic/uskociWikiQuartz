# Ribnička buna (1702)

Bunjevac uprising in [[Lika]], also called the **Bunjevačka buna**, sparked by the conflict between **porkulab [[Milos-Starcevic-b1656-Veliki-Zitnik]]** of [[Pazariste]] and **vojvoda [[Marko-Butorac-b????-Lika]]** [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

This is the first major Bunjevac collective political action in recovered Lika, marking them as a *"narod koji se mnogo bunio i mnogo selio"* in Šarić's framing. The Starčević lineage that emerges from this episode produces, four generations later, **[[Ante-Starcevic-b1823-Veliki-Zitnik]]** (1823–1896), founder of modern Croatian etnonacionalni identity.

The episode is also the moment when **biskup [[Martin-Brajkovic-b????-Senj]]** of [[Senjsko-Modruska-Biskupija]] first uses the term **"catholici Valachi alias Bunievczi"** in official record (1702) — the first ecclesiastical adoption of the Bunjevac ethnonym.
