# Zaseda na bribirskom potoku (9 December 1668)

Set-piece ambush by 200 horse + 200 foot of Kotarci (under Stojan Janković, Smoljan Smiljanić and **Giovanni Battista Soppe Fortezza**) of a 120-strong elite Knin Turkish raiding party led by **Ibraim aga Kovačević**. After provveditor Priuli's intelligence, Soppe led the main column from Nadin to the Bribir torrent and trapped the returning Turks at the river crossing.

Turkish casualties: ~104 killed (only 15 Turks escaped on horseback). Venetian-side: 1 dead. 66 saddled cavalry captured. Senate awarded Soppe + Smiljanić + Mitrović gold rings (doc. 165, 1669-01-02).

The Bribir potok had previously been the site of Vuk Močivuna's death (1666), and the Morlachi explicitly framed this 1668 victory as vendetta for that earlier defeat.
