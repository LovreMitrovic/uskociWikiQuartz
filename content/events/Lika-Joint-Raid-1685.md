# Joint Lika Raid by the Four Capi (22 July 1685)

The four capi — **[[Stjepan-Posedarski-b????|Posedarski]]**, **[[Antun-Bortulacic-b????-Zadar|Bortulačić]]**, **[[Stojan-Jankovic-b1635-Zegar|Janković]]** and **[[Smoljan-Smiljanic-b????-Bukovica|Smiljanić]]** — raided across [[Lika]] hitting Rebac, Ribnik, Medak, Parčići, Tepsincich, Pavlocich and Komić on 22 July 1685 (docs 86, 87, 89, 90, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

About 300 Morlach houses were resettled onto Venetian territory as a result of the raid (docs 86, 87, 89, 90, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
