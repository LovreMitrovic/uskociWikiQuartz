# First Krmpote migration to Lič (1605)

First major migration of the [[Krmpotic|Vlasi Krmpote]] from Ottoman territory in the [[Bukovica]]/Obrovac area (selo [[Medvidja]], formerly "Krmpote") onto Habsburg territory, settling **[[Lic]]** on the Zrinski estate of [[Hreljin]] [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

## Family list (Pavličević 1989)
24 surnames in the 1605 group, mostly Krmpote core but including some [[Vojnic]] and [[Sladovic]] families:

**Krmpote (24 families):**
Balinović ([[Balen]]), [[Barulovic]], [[Bozic]], [[Budisalic]], Butorčić ([[Butorac]]), [[Cvitic]], [[Draganovic]], [[Hromic]], [[Kovac]], [[Krivalica]], [[Krmpotic]], [[Krnjac]], [[Malovridnjak]], [[Matijevic]], [[Markovic]], [[Mihovilovic]], [[Mikulic]], [[Miletic]], [[Oprokovic]], [[Pecanic]], [[Petrovic]], Skorupović ([[Skorup]]), [[Simunovic]], [[Vukoslavic]].

**Vojnići (15 families):**

**Sladovići — "Gvozdenovi ljudi" (20 families) under [[Gvozden-Sladovic-b????-Mostar]]:**
Balinović, [[Broskovic]], [[Ilnic]], [[Karanovic]], [[Lovric]], [[Martinovic]], Mihovilović, Mikulić, [[Sladovic]], [[Veljanic]], [[Vilenica]].

## Confessional note
Some Orthodox families joined the migration; absent of Orthodox infrastructure in Lič area, they fully Catholicized within a century. By ~1700 the Krmpoćani were a homogeneous Catholic community [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].
