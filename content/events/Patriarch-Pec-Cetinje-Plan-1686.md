# Patriarch of Peć's Cetinje Relocation Plan (December 1686)

In December 1686 the Patriarch of Peć considered relocating his seat to Cetinje and sending 100 cavalry to **[[Stojan-Jankovic-b1635-Zegar|Stojan Janković]]** in [[Ravni-Kotari|Kotari]] (doc 160, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

The plan attests the Serbian patriarchal hierarchy's interest in coordinating with Venice's Morlach commanders during the Morean War, well before the [[Patriarch-Crnojevic-Teodosije-1693|1693 appointment of Teodosije by Arsenije III Crnojević]] (doc 160, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
