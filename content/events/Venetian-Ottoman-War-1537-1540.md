# Venetian-Ottoman War (1537–1540)

Ottoman capture of [[Klis]] fortress (1537) and creation of the [[Sandzak-Klis]] under [[Murat-beg-Gajdic-b????-Sibenik]]. Venetian siege and capture of [[Obrovac]] in June 1538 sparked mass defection of [[Vlasi-Istre]] to Venetian territory ([[Vlasi-Istre-Migration-to-Venice-1538]]) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Ended with peace 2 October 1540, restoring some Vlach privileges on the Ottoman side and triggering partial return migration.
