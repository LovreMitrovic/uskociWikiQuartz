# Grimani Border Demarcation — Sfakioto's Five Processi (1699–1701)

Following the [[Karlovacki-Mir-1699|Treaty of Karlowitz]], the commission under provveditore generale **Grimani** (1699–1701) ran **Sfakioto's five processi** — extensive testimony-gathering on rights and jurisdiction at [[Knin]], [[Sinj]] and Zvonigrad — to fix the new Venetian–Ottoman boundary (doc text, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

The processi are the principal Venetian dossier on the post-Karlowitz "Acquisto Nuovo" lands and remained authoritative through the [[Bukovica-Revolt-1704|1704 Bukovica revolt]] and beyond (doc text, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
