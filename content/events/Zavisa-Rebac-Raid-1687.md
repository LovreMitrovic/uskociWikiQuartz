# Zaviša's Raid on Rebac (June 1687)

**[[Zavisa-Jankovic-b????-Zegar|Zaviša Janković]]** led 500 men in a raid on Rebac in June 1687 that took 20 horses, 200 cattle and 8 captives (doc 170, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

The action ran in parallel with his brother [[Ilija-Mitrovic-b????-Zegar|Ilija]]'s [[Ilija-Bilaj-Raid-1687|raid on Bilaj]] the same month (doc 170, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
