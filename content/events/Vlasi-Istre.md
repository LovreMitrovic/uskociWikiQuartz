# Vlasi Istre (Ottoman Vlachs of Istria)

The "Osmanski Vlasi Istre" — an Ottoman Vlach population of the original Tromeđa (borderland) area, subject of Marko Šarić's study, c. 1500–1585 [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Their migrations to and from Venetian and Habsburg territory (notably [[Vlasi-Istre-Migration-to-Venice-1538]]) were shaped by Ottoman *istimâlet* policy under sanjakbeg [[Murat-beg-Gajdic-b????-Sibenik]] and by the wars of the period.
