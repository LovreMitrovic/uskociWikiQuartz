# Drniš revolt (1608)

Klis-area unrest around [[Drnis|Drniš]], led by [[Mehmed-Terzibasic-b????-Drnis|Mehmed Terzibašić]]. Its fallout contributed to the Bunjevac/Vlach migration wave out of the Klis sandžak toward Bačka after 1606 [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].
