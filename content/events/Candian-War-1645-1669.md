# Candian War (1645–1669)

Ottoman–Venetian war centred on Crete (Candia). In the Dalmatian theatre it triggered mass Morlach/Bunjevac migrations from Ottoman into Venetian territory, including the 1647 Krmpote exodus to [[Vinjerac]] and Starigrad [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]], and the Foscolo-era reception operations directed by the [[Posedarski]] governatori [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]].
