# Death of Jovan Sinobad at Glamoč (1715)

**[[Jovan-Sinobad-b????-Posedarje|Jovan Sinobad]]**, kavalier and sopraintendente of Knin, was killed in an Ottoman engagement at [[Glamocko-Polje|Glamoč]] in 1715 during the opening campaigns of the Second Morean War (doc 313, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

His death triggered a recall of veterans to active service and reshuffled the senior Kotari command for the rest of the war. He was succeeded eventually by his son **Andrea Sinobad** as serdar of Knin (doc 313, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
