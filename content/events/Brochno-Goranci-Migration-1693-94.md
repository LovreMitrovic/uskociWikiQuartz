# Brochno–Blato–Goranci Migration (1693–1694)

About 5,000 souls from the **Brochno–Blato–Goranci** villages (Hercegovina) crossed onto Venetian territory in 1693–1694 under five serdari and 22 harambasse, settling around Zadvarje and [[Mletacka-Dalmacija|Vrgorac]] (doc text, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

The migration is among the largest single transfers documented for the post-1690 phase of the Morean War in lower Dalmatia, alongside the [[Bjelaj-Migration-1692|1692 Bjelaj migration]] and the [[Mostar-Rusic-Migration-1692|Mostar/Rašić migration]] of the same year (doc text, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
