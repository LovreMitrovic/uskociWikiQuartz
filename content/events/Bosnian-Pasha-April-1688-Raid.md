# Bosnian Pasha's April 1688 Raid on Kotari

About 5,000 Bosnian cavalry under **[[Mehmed-Atlagic-b????-Knin|Atlagić]]** crossed via Livno and [[Knin]] to Popovići in April 1688 and plundered the Kotari plain from [[Karin|Nadin]] to Novigrad (docs 198, 200, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

The raiders took 23 heads, 4,000–5,000 small animals, 600 horses/cattle and roughly 100 women and children captive; [[Karin]] defended itself successfully (docs 198, 200, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]). The raid was Atlagić's last large-scale offensive before Venice took [[Knin-Conquest-1688|Knin]] that September.
