# Imperial Capture of Nové Zámky / Neuhäusel (1685)

The fortress of **Neuhäusel (Nové Zámky)** in Upper Hungary was taken by Imperial assault in 1685, eliminating one of the principal Ottoman strongholds in the kingdom (doc 95, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

The fall of Neuhäusel and the [[Esztergom-Battle-1685|Esztergom victory]] together marked the decisive turn of the Hungarian campaign in 1685 (doc 95, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
