# Vlach status abolition (c. 1530)

After the Battle of Mohács (1526), Ottoman fiscal pressure for the Hungarian campaigns led to the **abolition of special Vlach status** and equalization of Vlachs with ordinary *raja*. The political project failed financially and provoked **mass revolts and the first major Vlach migrations** out of Ottoman frontier districts [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

## Effects on Bunjevac formation
- **Spring 1530+** — large-scale "uskakanje" (defection) from gornje Pounje, Unac, Glamoč, Grahovo, Knin, and Cetina into [[Kranjska]], Hrvatska, and [[Zumberak]]; smaller Vlach streams to [[Kras]] in Istria.
- **1533** — Vlach uprising in the western part of [[Sandzak-Hercegovina]].
- **1530s+** — Ottoman authorities deported "buntovni vlaški element" from [[Vilajet-Hrvati]] to the [[Podunavlje]]. A 1564–65 ferman documents one such group permitted to return to its old homeland after ~30 years.

This is the structural backdrop to all of the major Bunjevac migration events that follow ([[Krmpote-Migration-Lic-1605]], [[Bunjevac-Migration-to-Backa-c1606]], etc.).
