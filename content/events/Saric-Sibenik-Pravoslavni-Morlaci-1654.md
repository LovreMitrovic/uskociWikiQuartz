# Šibenik Orthodox Morlach petition (1654)

On 1654-07-26 *Cuitho Sarich harambassa* ([[Cvijan-Saric-b????-Sibenik|Cvijan Šarić]]) headed a 37-name list of the [[Sibenik|Šibenik]] Orthodox (pravoslavni) Morlachi — mostly *harambaše* — who collectively defended their parish priest fra Cirilo Gabrieli against Jovan Vuković Obrad (doc. 84) [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]].
