# Petition of Four Morlach Capi to Senate (28 July 1684)

The four Kotari capi — **[[Stjepan-Posedarski-b????|Posedarski]]**, **[[Antun-Bortulacic-b????-Zadar|Bortulačić]]**, **[[Stojan-Jankovic-b1635-Zegar|Janković]]** and **[[Smoljan-Smiljanic-b????-Bukovica|Smiljanić]]** — petitioned the Venetian Senate against Provveditore Generale **[[Domenico-Mocenigo-b????-Venice|Mocenigo]]**'s appointment of four "new" sub-leaders (Alberti, Soppe, Ventura, Soppe Fortezza), arguing the move violated their established jurisdictions (doc 22, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

The Senate ruled in the capi's favour, confirming their primacy over the Morlach population and rolling back Mocenigo's appointments (doc 23, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
