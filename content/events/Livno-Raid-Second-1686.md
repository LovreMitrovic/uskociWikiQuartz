# Second Livno Raid (early September 1686)

**[[Stojan-Jankovic-b1635-Zegar|Janković]]** and **[[Smoljan-Smiljanic-b????-Bukovica|Smiljanić]]** led a follow-up raid on the Livno field in early September 1686 in which 150+ Turks were killed (docs 141, 142, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

On the return march **Karalija** and two captains were killed by the pursuit, marking a costly Morlach loss to set against the Livno gains (docs 141, 142, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
