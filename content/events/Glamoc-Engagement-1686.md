# Glamoč Cavalry Engagement (early summer 1686)

A Kotari sortie cut down roughly 200 Turkish cavalry on the [[Glamocko-Polje|Glamoč]] field in early summer 1686 (doc 138, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

The clash kept the Glamoč–Livno corridor open for the larger raids of July and September that year (doc 138, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
