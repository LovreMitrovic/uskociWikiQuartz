# Olib Sale — Half-Island to Mitrović (1701)

In 1701 conte **[[Giacomo-Califfi-b????-Zadar|Giacomo Califfi]]** transferred half of the island of **Olib** (near Pag) to the **Mitrović** family for 8,000 ducats, with peculiar dynastic conditions: the half-share was to remain perpetually divided between the male line of **[[Zavisa-Jankovic-b????-Zegar|Zaviša Janković]]** and the male line of his deceased brother **[[Ilija-Mitrovic-b????-Zegar|Ilija]]** (doc text, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

The arrangement is one of the few documented Mitrović land acquisitions outside the Zadar mainland and codified the joint Zaviša/Ilija inheritance pattern that would shape the family's 18th-century landholdings (doc text, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
