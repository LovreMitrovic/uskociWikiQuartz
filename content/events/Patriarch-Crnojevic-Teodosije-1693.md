# Patriarch Arsenije III Crnojević Appoints Teodosije Exarch (21 January 1693)

On 21 January 1693 (10 January Old Style) **Patriarch Arsenije III Crnojević** of Peć — by then a refugee in Habsburg lands — issued letters at the **Lepavina monastery** appointing **Teodosije** as his exarch over the Serbian Orthodox flock in Venetian Dalmatia (doc 262, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

The appointment formalised the Serbian church's jurisdiction over the [[Ravni-Kotari|Kotari]] migrant population during the Morean War and complements the [[Patriarch-Pec-Cetinje-Plan-1686|earlier 1686 Cetinje relocation plan]] (doc 262, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
