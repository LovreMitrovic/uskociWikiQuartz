# Battle of Sinj (7 April 1685)

A Venetian-led assault on Ottoman **[[Sinj]]** that ended in a major Venetian disaster: panic among the Morlachs, rout of the Italian infantry, multiple senior officers killed or captured. The defeat scapegoated [[Domenico-Mocenigo-b????-Venice|provveditor Mocenigo]] and elevated [[Pietro-Valier-b????-Venice|Pietro Valier]] (who himself would be replaced by Cornaro the following year).

(docs 33, 38, 39, 40, 42, 60–65, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]])

## Killed or missing
- Major **[[Tanusi-b????-Venice|Tanusi]]** (Oltremarini commander)
- Conte **[[Conte-Marianovic-b????|Marianović]]** (capitano)
- Officer **Marušić**
- Officer **Macedonia**
- Capo **[[Palese-b????-Zadar|Palese]]** of Zadar (spiked the cannon and died defending it)
- Colonnello / governator **Rados** (brother of [[Zuanne-Rados-b????-Zadar|Ivan Radoš]])
- Engineer **[[Camucio-engineer-b????|Camuccio]]** (wounded)
- Vice ragionato of Valier with 300 cecchini cash
- Unnamed Capuchin friar carrying banner of Virgin and *brandistocco* (boar-spear), presumed enslaved
- **[[Ivan-Alberti-b????-Split|Ivan Alberti]]** (governator) — captured by Bosnian Pasha, taken as personal slave, ransom letter from Klis

## Survived with distinction
- **[[Stojan-Jankovic-b1635-Zegar|Stojan Janković]]** — fought through almost alone among the Morlach capi
- **[[Marin-Michiel-b????-Venice|Marin Michiel]]** — wounded under his horse; saved by [[Nakic|karambaša Nakić]]'s offered horse
- **[[Marchese-Carlotti-b????|Marchese Carlotti]]** with Valier's son — last to leave the field

## Wider context
- Bosnian Pasha advanced from [[Maidan]] across the Cetina with cavalry and infantry
- **Ahmed-paša of Hercegovina** simultaneously attacked [[Kastela|Kaštela]] with 3,000 cavalry but was repulsed
- The Sinj defeat prevented further Cetina-region operations for the rest of 1685; Venetian initiative shifted to Lika ([[Lika-Raid-1685]]) and Kotari migrations (ultimately the [[Lika-Settlement-Census-1685]] roster)
