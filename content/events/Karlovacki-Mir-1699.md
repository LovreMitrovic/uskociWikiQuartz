# Karlovački mir (1699)

Treaty ending the Great Turkish War; recovered [[Lika]] and [[Krbava]] for the Habsburgs.

The Habsburg boundary commission was led by **count [[Luigi-Marsigli-b????]]**. In commission documents, Marsigli refers to the Bunjevci of the Primorska Krajina as **"Meerkroaten"** (Sea-Croats) — his term for the predominantly Bunjevac population of the [[Senjska-Kapetanija]] coastline at the turn of the 18th c. (Kaser 2003) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].
