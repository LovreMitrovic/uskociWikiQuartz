# Montenegrin Tribes' Defection to Skadar Sandžak (May 1685)

Parts of the Montenegrin tribes joined the **Sandžak-beg of Skadar** against Venice in May 1685, reversing the cooperative posture that had begun the Morean War (doc 72, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

The defection complicated Venetian operations on the southern Adriatic flank for the rest of the war (doc 72, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
