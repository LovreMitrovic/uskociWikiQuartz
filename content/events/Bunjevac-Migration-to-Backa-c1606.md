# Bunjevac migrations to Bačka (c. 1554 – c. 1622)

Ottoman-administered transfers of "buntovni vlaški element" from [[Sandzak-Klis]] / [[Sandzak-Krka]] into the depopulated [[Budimski-Ejalet|Budim eyalet]] (Bačka, Baranja). Documented in three layers [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]]:

## 1. Earliest layer (c. 1530–1582)
- **1530s** — Ottoman deportations of rebellious Vlachs from [[Vilajet-Hrvati]] to Podunavlje (cited in 1564–65 ferman).
- **1554** — Mohač defter, selo Márok (Baranja): rajetin **[[Marton-Bunavac-b????-Marok]]** ("Márton Bunavácz"). Earliest possible written form of "Bunjevac" [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]] (caveats: possibly a transcription error for *Dunavac*; possibly an etnik attached to a non-Bunjevac).
- **1561** — same person registered in nearby selo Izsép as "Martin Bunivac".
- **1581** — knez **[[Vranes-Ivanovic-b????-Kaloca]]** with a knežina near Kaloča.
- **1582** — Bosnian Franciscan **[[Anto-Matkovic-b????-Temisvar|Anto-Matkovic-b????]]** opens a school for "Dalmatince" in the Temišvar diocese.
- 1554 + 1561 deftere also register raja designated *"Hirvat"* (regional Croat from former vilajet Hrvati) in selo Sukit and selo Ata (Šarošac 1991).

## 2. Main wave (1606–1620) — after the Long War
After 1598, ~15,000 Serbs from the bajska/somborska/subotička nahija migrated north to the Ostrogon area at Pálffy's invitation. Ottoman authorities backfilled with Bunjevac groups from [[Sandzak-Klis]] [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]]:
- **1608** — Šibenik knez reports to Venetian senate that **2,000 morlačkih kuća** were transferred across the Danube.
- **1608+** — fallout of the [[Drnis-Rebellion-1608|Drniš revolt]] under [[Mehmed-Terzibasic-b????-Drnis]].
- **1619** — further Klis-area Vlach migrations including Orthodox Vlachs going with Dragović monastery monks to [[Grabovac]] in Baranja.
- **1622** — first explicit "**parochia Bunievczi**" attestation: don **[[Simun-Matkovic-b????]]** addresses a request to the Congregatio Propaganda Fide for the Bunjevac parish in the Kalocsa archdiocese.
- **1626** — Athanasio Georgiceo records: "raja for 25 years has fled Klis sandžak; population dropped from 30,000 houses to 20,000."

## 3. Toponymic and linguistic evidence
- Mid-17th-c. settlement names along the Krivaja near Subotica show clearly Dinaric origin: **Kupuszina, Stanišić, Zobnatica**.
- Dragović monastery near Cetina founded the orthodox monastery [[Grabovac]] in Baranja in 1585.

## Motivation
Šarić frames these as ambivalent: nominally Ottoman *kaznene deportacije*, but for the Bunjevci themselves often a welcome escape from the **"tatarska dinastija"** of Klis sandžak-begs ([[Mustafa-beg-Klis-b????]] and his son [[Mehmed-beg-Klis-b????]], both Tatar-origin) and the Memibegović dynasty of [[Sandzak-Krka]].
