# Cetina Morlach Migration under Peraica (March 1685)

**[[Ilija-Peraica-b????-Cetina|Ilija Peraica]]** led roughly 600 armed men with their families plus 30,000 small animals and 10,000 cattle from [[Cetina]] into Trogir territory under Venetian protection (docs 59–66, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

The migration shifted the Cetina Morlach population from Ottoman to Venetian allegiance and supplied a new manpower pool for the campaigns in lower Dalmatia later that year (docs 59–66, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
