# Battle of Esztergom (1685)

The Imperial army under **Charles V, Duke of Lorraine** defeated the Ottomans at Esztergom in 1685; some 2,000 Turks were killed and 25 cannon captured (doc 95, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

The Imperial Hungarian victories of 1685 ran in parallel with the Venetian Dalmatian offensive — the Holy League's two-front assault on the Ottoman Empire after the failed siege of Vienna in 1683 (doc 95, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
