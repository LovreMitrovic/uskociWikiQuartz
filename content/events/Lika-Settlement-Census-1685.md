# Lika Settlement Census (1685)

Aggregate Venetian census of Morlachs newly settled from Ottoman territory into the Venetian Kotari–Bukovica–Cetina–Neretva line over 1684–85, drawn together in doc 107 ([[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

## Totals (per doc 107)
- **3,051 families / 10,290 fighters** total

## Component contingents
- **1,587 families / 5,297 fighters** under [[Stojan-Jankovic-b1635-Zegar|Stojan Janković]] (Lika + Srb)
- **52 families / 171 fighters** under [[Smoljan-Smiljanic-b????-Bukovica|Smoljan Smiljanić]]
- **109 families / 283 fighters** under [[Simun-Bortulacic-b????-Zadar|Šimun Bortulačić]]
- (no separate count) under [[Frane-Posedarski-b????|Frane Posedarski]]
- **140 families / 340 fighters** at [[Vrana]]
- **95 families / 255 fighters** in Primorje
- **550 families / 2,300 fighters** at [[Zadvarje]] + Župa
- **94 families / 120 fighters** at the Neretva (Narenta)
- **24 families / 124 fighters** at [[Forte-Opus|Forte Opus]]
- **400 families / 1,400 fighters** under "Peraica" (Cetina migration of March 1685, [[Frane-Peraica-b????-Zegar|Frane Peraica]] / Ilija Peraica)

## Earlier roll-ups feeding the total
- 1684 (May–Aug): 152 families / 2,300 souls from Lika (doc 8)
- 1685-05: 70 families / 900 souls under Polo Michiel via Janković (doc 70)
- 1685-06: 316 families / 2,561 souls settled between Kožulovo polje and Ostrovica (doc 73, 90)

## Notes
- The 1685 settlement is the single largest Morlach migration into Venetian Dalmatia of the Morean War.
- Settlement was unstable: many of the Šibenik- and Trogir-territory new Morlachs were known to be in correspondence with Livno Turks about returning, and the [[Bosnian-Pasha-Kotari-Raid-1686|November 1686 pasha raid]] tested the new settlements severely.
- Many of the Lika-origin surnames in the Kotari date from this 1684–85 wave; the 1692 Bjelaj migration (5,000 souls under serdari Đurić, Pirić, Zorić) added a second wave.
