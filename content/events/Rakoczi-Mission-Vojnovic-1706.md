# Rákóczi Mission of Baron Vojnović (1706)

In 1706 the Croatian noble **Baron Vojnović**, an envoy of **Ferenc II Rákóczi**'s anti-Habsburg insurrection in Hungary, transited through [[Zadar]] on his way to Venice and France (doc text, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).

Vojnović's transit is a brief but illuminating episode of Croatian diplomatic activity in Venetian Dalmatia during the [[War-of-Spanish-Succession-Deployments|War of the Spanish Succession]] (doc text, [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]).
