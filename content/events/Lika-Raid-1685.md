# Lika raid (1685)

Venetian offensive into [[Lika]] in 1685, to which Venetian initiative shifted after the [[Sinj-Battle-1685|Sinj defeat]] halted Cetina-region operations for the rest of that year; connected with the ensuing Kotari migrations and the [[Lika-Settlement-Census-1685]] roster [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
