# Osvajanje Klisa (1648)

Venetian campaign that conquered Drniš, Klis, and (briefly) Knin from Tekieli Mustafa-paša, who personally led the relief but was defeated at Greben outside Klis. Foscolo's terminacija (doc. 26, 1648-04-20) gives the most detailed published account of pop Sorić's role.

## Sorić's role
- Cut bridges; burned Zvonigrad mills and folli; shifted Bukovački Morlachi by force.
- Helped force the Cikola crossing toward Drniš under Tekieli's defending fire.
- At Drniš seized Tekieli's *general standard* (later sent to Venice in triumph).
- At Klis — held the Greben pass; broke Tekieli's advance at Dugopolje.
- 16-day siege of Klis ended in Venetian capture.
