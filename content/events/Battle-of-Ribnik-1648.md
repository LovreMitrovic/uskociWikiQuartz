# Battle of Ribnik (16–17 July 1648)

**Catastrophic defeat** of the Kotarski uskoci led by don **Stjepan Sorić** and harambaše **Petar Smiljanić** and Janko Mitrović, in their attempt to take the Lika fortress of [[Ribnik]] during the early phase of the Candian War.

## Casualties (per doc. 38, son Ilija Smiljanić's deposition; doc. 39, harambaše roster of 14 *uskočke grupe*)
- **Killed**: don Stjepan Sorić (executed by Turks after capture), Petar Smiljanić, ~10 senior harambaše, ~80 men.
- **Captured**: pop Sorić, then beheaded.
- The 14 *uskočke grupe* roster (doc. 39) lists losses by harambaša with **village of origin → settlement village** — invaluable for migration mapping (see also doc. 70 1653 proxy).

## Aftermath
- 1648-07-31 — Bukovčani re-elect Ilija Smiljanić as their capo; Goričani elect pop Sorić's brother **Mate Sorić**; the gold chain originally for pop Stjepan goes to Mate.
