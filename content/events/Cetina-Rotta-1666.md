# Cetinska rotta (5 April 1666)

Disastrous defeat of the Šibenik-Zadar Morlach raiding force (under serdar **Cvitan Šarić**, with **Janković Stojan** and many Šibenik harambaše) on the Cetina river near Obrovac na Cetini, half a day's march from Klis, when the *čiehaja* of the Bosnian basha caught them with cavalry mid-plunder.

## Casualties
- ~200 Kotarci dead and captured.
- **Stojan Janković captured** — beginning his 14-month slavery in Constantinople.
- Per witnesses (docs. 149, 150) and Cornaro's dispatch (doc. 148): the *vecchio, ardito e pieno d' avidità* serdar Cvitan Šarić had concerted the raid against orders.
- Turkish casualties also heavy: alai-beg **Atlagić** killed; aga **Baraković** killed; *Cinghić* (Čengić) mortally wounded.
