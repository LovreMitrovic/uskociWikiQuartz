# Vranska buna (October 1683 – May 1684)

The mass mobilisation of Ottoman-subject Morlachi who, on news of the Bečki poraz, in concert with Senjski uskoki and Venetian-side Kotarci, threw off Ottoman rule across the Šibenik–Skradin–Vrana–Obrovac corridor. The volume's last 80 documents (#321–#399) trace this crisis as Venetian provveditore Lorenzo Donà tries to *not* declare war while preserving the Morlachi.

## Key documents
- **doc. 321** (1683-10-22) — Posedarski's first alarm: open insubordination, *"viva Christo viva Christo si sono incaminati verso Casich."*
- **docs. 333, 334** — Šutić's report; Radoš's mediation; the Vrana takeover.
- **doc. 358** (1683-12-26) — pop Lazar Dragović in name of 1500 Morlachi families from Kolašac, Parčić, Krmpote, Rodaljica, Kosulovo Polje, Skradin, Kistanje, Perušić, Benkovac, Brkljača Polača, Mor Polača, who occupied **Raslina, Račevica, Dobri Dolac, Dejanović, Putičane, Skonotić, Vrana**.
- **doc. 362** (1684-01-04) — Nakić petition: 1000 Zagora households + 400 Vrana families.
- **doc. 369** (1684-02-08) — Stojan Janković raised to 40 ducati/month.
- **doc. 381** (1684-03-01) — Donà's grand reorganization: 4 *direttori* (Stojan Janković, Smoljan Smiljanić, Frane Posedarski, Šimun Bortulačić) over the Kotari villages, with named capitanì for each village.
- **doc. 383** — strength roster of the kotarski + vranski + littoral villages.
- **doc. 392** (1684-04-09) — Šimun Bortulačić named *capo de Morlacchi* of Vrana.
- **doc. 397** (1684-04-19) — Tepšinci/Parčići uskakanje plea (615 fighters / 5307 souls).

## Social outcome
Per docs. 381, 397 — the volume closes with the Krajina re-territorialised: the Vrana-Obrovac corridor in Venetian hands, Knin still held by Turks under sandžak Durakbegović, and 5300+ Lika souls poised to migrate to Obrovac and Gračac. The Morean War (1684–99) will start the next month, and the *kotarska epopeja* will reach its zenith — but that is the subject of **Sveska II** of Desnica.

## People
Stojan Janković, Ilija Mitrović (the principal *fomentatore*), Smoljan Smiljanić, Frane Posedarski (Younger), Šimun Bortulačić, Bajo Pivljanin, Mitar Pivljanin, Radoš (col. Ivan), Sorić (Stevan, Tepšinci), pop Lazar Dragović, Matija Nakić, Frane Mirčetić, Vid Bačić, Petar Radić, fra Petar Tintić, fra Andrija Rešinčić, fra Grga Šutić.

Turkish side: Durak-beg Durakbegović (who returned to Knin), kapetan Mustaj-beg Firdusović of Knin, alaj-beg Filipović, kliški kajmakam Gazi Mehmed.
