# Murder of Šimun Bortulačić at Vrana (16 January 1692)

**[[Simun-Bortulacic-b????-Zadar|Šimun (Simon) Bortulačić]]** — kavalier, one of the four 1684 capi de Morlacchi, governor of Vrana — killed at **[[Vrana]]** on 16 January 1692 by men from **[[Biograd-na-Moru|Biograd]]** and **[[Pakostane]]**; aged ~31; covered with stones (Desnica II, chunk 3 attestations).

## Investigation and execution
- Two principal perpetrators captured and condemned 1693–94.
- The **brother of [[Mattio-Zabetic-b????-Zaravecchia|Mattio Žabetić]]** of Zaravecchia was quartered for participation.
- **Tomaso Tubić** (related to Žabetić family) was banished, then later pardoned.
- The Žabetić family connection links the 1692 Bortulačić murder to the 1704 [[Bukovica-Revolt-1704|Bukovica revolt]] — Mattio Žabetić, brother of one executed perpetrator, would later be a 1704 ringleader.

## Notes
- The killing fits the pattern of village-faction violence directed at Venetian-recognised Morlach capi who were exercising sopraintendenza over villages outside their original kin-network. Vrana had earlier rejected Bortulačić as their capo in 1684 (docs 34–36).
- Provveditor **[[Daniel-Dolfin-IV-b????-Venice|Daniel Dolfin]]** mediated the broader 1692 settlement of factional feuds in Kotari (Mitrović–Sinobad, Mitrović–Milković reconciliations).
