# Krmpote migration to Vinjerac (1647)

Final mass exodus of Krmpote families from Ottoman territory during the [[Candian-War-1645-1669|Candian War]]. **Harambaša [[Harambasa-Njegovan-b????-Medvidja]]** led 40–50 Krmpote families from selo Krmpote (today [[Medvidja]]) to Venetian territory, settling primarily **[[Vinjerac]]** and **[[Starigrad]]** in the Zadar district; some onward to the island of **[[Pag]]** [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

In the same year, the **[[Vojnic|Vlasi Vojnići]]** crossed entirely to Venetian territory under knez [[Filip-Zupanovic-b????-Tinj]], with part of them resettled at **[[Valtura]]** near Pula in southern Istria.

This is the last large group movement out of the Ottoman Bukovica/Tinj area; by 1647 the Krmpote and Vojnić katuns are functionally split between Venetian and Habsburg territories.
