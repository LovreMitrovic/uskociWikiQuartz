# Luka Letina (fl. 1610, Sukošan)

## Summary
Recorded as *Luca Letina*, head of household in [[Sukosan|Sukošan]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 4 comprised 1 man, 1 woman, 2 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
