# Gioveta Župak (fl. 1756, Korlat)

## Summary
Recorded as *Gioveta Zupach*, head of a household of 26 souls in [[Korlat]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 64 campi 3 quarte 19 tavole, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
