# Nikola Babić (fl. 1756, Donja Jagodnja)

## Summary
Recorded as *Nicola Babich*, head of a household of 4 souls in [[Donja-Jagodnja|Donja Jagodnja]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 8 campi 123 tavole, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
