# Márton Bunavac

Rajetin (peasant taxpayer) recorded in the **1554** Mohač defter for selo [[Marok]] as "Márton Bunavácz" and in the **1561** defter for nearby selo [[Izsep]] as "Martin Bunivac" (Velics 1890:398) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

## Significance: earliest possible "Bunjevac"
**Possibly the earliest written form of the etnik/surname "Bunjevac"** — but Šarić maintains caveats:
1. **Transcription uncertainty.** Ottoman Turkish script renders the prezime ambiguously; could be *Dunavac* (suggested by Kornelija Jurin Starčević).
2. **Etnik logic.** If Bunjevac, the name as etnik would imply the carrier moved from a Bunjevac region — therefore Márton himself was not in his Bunjevac homeland but in a Bačka/Baranja exile.

Other 1554/1561 entries from the same registers list raja designated *"Hirvat"* (Croatian) in selo Sukit and selo Ata — **Ottoman regional Croat designation for migrants from the abolished [[Vilajet-Hrvati]]** (1537). This contemporary toponymic-administrative context strengthens the case that Márton was indeed an early Bunjevac migrant in Podunavlje.

The 1530s+ Ottoman deportations from Vilajet Hrvati to Podunavlje (cited in 1564–65 ferman) provide the migration mechanism. A 1582 [[Anto-Matkovic-b????-Temisvar]] school for "Dalmatince" in the Temišvar diocese, and the 1581 mention of knez [[Vranes-Ivanovic-b????-Kaloca]] in Kaloča area, document the same demographic substrate.
