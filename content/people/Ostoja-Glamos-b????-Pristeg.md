# Ostoja Glamoš (fl. 1756, Pristeg)

## Summary
Recorded as *Ostoia Glamos*, head of a household of 5 souls in [[Pristeg]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 10 campi 109 tavole, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
