# Vuko Milatović (fl. 1610, Sv. Filip i Jakov)

## Summary
Recorded as *Vuco Millatouich*, head of household in [[Sv-Filip-i-Jakov|Sv. Filip i Jakov]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 5 comprised 2 men, 3 women. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
