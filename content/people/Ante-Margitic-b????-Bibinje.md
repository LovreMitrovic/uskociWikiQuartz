# Ante Margitić (fl. 1610, Bibinje)

## Summary
Recorded as *Antonio Marghitich*, head of household in [[Bibinje]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 5 comprised 3 men, 2 women. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
