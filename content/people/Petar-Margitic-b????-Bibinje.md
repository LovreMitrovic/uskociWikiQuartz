# Petar Margitić (fl. 1610, Bibinje)

## Summary
Recorded as *Piero Marghitich*, head of household in [[Bibinje]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 5 comprised 1 man, 1 woman, 3 boys. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
