# Vid Velanović (fl. 1610, Kožino)

## Summary
Recorded as *Vido Vellanouich*, head of household in [[Kozino|Kožino]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 7 comprised 2 men, 1 woman, 2 boys and 2 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]

- `birth_place` is **inferred from residence**: the census records the village where the household was enumerated in 1610, not a place of birth, and no birth record is attested. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
