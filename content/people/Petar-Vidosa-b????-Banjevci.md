# Petar Vidoša (fl. 1756, Banjevci)

## Summary
Recorded as *Peter Vidossa*, head of a household of 9 souls in [[Banjevci]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 18 campi, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
