# Petar Krečić (fl. 1610, Sukošan)

## Summary
Recorded as *Piero Chrechich*, head of household in [[Sukosan|Sukošan]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 18 comprised 4 men, 7 women, 4 boys, 3 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
