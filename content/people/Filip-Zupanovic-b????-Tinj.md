# knez Filip Županović (1647)

Successor starešina figure (after [[Milos-Zupanovic-b????-Tinj]]) of the [[Vojnic|Vlasi Vojnići]] of [[Tinj]] / Županovići. In **1647** during the Candian War, led the migration of part of the Vojnići onto Venetian territory, settling **[[Valtura]]** near [[Pula]] [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]] (see [[Krmpote-Migration-Vinjerac-1647]]).
