# Vukašin sin Vojihne (1477)

Head of the [[Vojnic|Vojnić]] katun in the 1477 Hercegovina defter, **nahija Mostar**. 10 houses + 3 unmarried (Aličić 1985:84) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

Wintered/summered jointly with vojvoda [[Vladimir-vojvoda-b????-Hercegovina]]'s katun across W. Hercegovinian planinas and župas. Eponymous founder figure for [[Vojnic]] (Vojnići), and for selo [[Vojnici-Ljubuski]] in W. Hercegovina (~35 km west of [[Buna]]).

Earlier attestation of the lineage: **1408** — Bosnian king Stjepan Ostoja's charter mentions Vlasi Vojihnići, subjects of the Radivojević-Vlatković Hum nobility.
