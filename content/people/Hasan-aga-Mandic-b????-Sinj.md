# Hasan-aga Mandić

Son of [[Sain-aga-Mandic-b????-Sinj|Šain-aga Mandić]] of [[Sinj]]. Captured at the Kaštela in autumn 1685; held in the Zadar captain's prison from 1 Oct 1685 alongside [[Sakija-aga-b????-Sinj|Šakija-aga]].

## Timeline (this source)
- **1685 (spring)** — Slave of the Radoš consortium ([[Zuanne-Rados-b????-Zadar|Ivan Radoš]], [[Matija-Strmic-b????|Matija Strmić]], [[Damjan-Santic-b????|Damjan Šantić]]); ransom set at 2,000 reali plus 4 horses, of which 800 paid (docs 65, 68). Referred to as "Hasan-beg / Ivisa figliuolo del sudetto agà".
- **1685-10-01** — Arrives in Zadar as prisoner, six weeks before doc. 109 (17 Nov 1685).
- **1685-11-17** (doc. 109) — Writes (probably to his father) complaining that captain Marušić had cut off his *biscotto* allowance; gives daily food rates (8 *aspri* + 1.5 lbs biscotto per 30 days under Rados).
- **Undated, autumn 1685** (docs. 111, 112) — Reports (probably to Šain-aga) the news that Janković has gone to Lika with 2,000 men, that Esztergom (Neuhäusel) and Coron have fallen, and that "il karambassà Peraizza" (Ilija Peraica) has been broken on the 8th of September with 175 captured by Turkish corsair *fuste* off the Šibenik coast.
