# Sava Malbaša (fl. 1756, Polača)

## Summary
Recorded as *Sava Malbassa*, head of a household of 6 souls in [[Polaca|Polača]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 2 campi 10 tavole, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
