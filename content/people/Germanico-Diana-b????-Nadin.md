# Germanico Diana (fl. 1756, Nadin)

## Summary
Recorded as *Germanico Diana*, a landholder with no household enumerated in [[Nadin]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 30 campi, held *senza laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
