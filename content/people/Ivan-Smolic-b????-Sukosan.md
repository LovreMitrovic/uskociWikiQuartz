# Ivan Smolić (fl. 1610, Sukošan)

## Summary
Recorded as *Zuane Smoglich*, head of household in [[Sukosan|Sukošan]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 10 comprised 5 men, 4 women, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]

## Notes
- `birth_place` is **inferred from residence**: the census records the village where the household was enumerated in 1610, not a place of birth, and no birth record is attested. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
- Filed under [[Smolic|Smolić]]: the 1610 census spells the name *Smoglich*, which the original ingest normalized to *Smoljić* and kept on a separate hub. Merged into **Smolić** on 2026-09-08 — the same village carries eight Smolić households in the 1756 survey [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]], and this is one family. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
