# Petar Frakasov (fl. 1610, Turanj)

## Summary
Recorded as *Piero Fracassof*, head of household in [[Turanj]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 3 comprised 1 man, 2 women. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
