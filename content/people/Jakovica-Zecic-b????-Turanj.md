# Jakovica Zečić (fl. 1610, Turanj)

## Summary
Recorded as *Giacouiza Zecichia*, head of household in [[Turanj]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. Her household of 4 comprised 1 woman, 2 boys, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]

## Notes
- `birth_place` is **inferred from residence**: the census records the village where the household was enumerated in 1610, not a place of birth, and no birth record is attested. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
Taken to be a woman: the name is a feminine form and the household contains no adult men, and the `-chia` surname ending is a feminine patronymic. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
