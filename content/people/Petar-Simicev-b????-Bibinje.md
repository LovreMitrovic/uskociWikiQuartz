# Petar Simičev (fl. 1610, Bibinje)

## Summary
Recorded as *Piero Simichieu*, head of household in [[Bibinje]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 7 comprised 2 men, 4 women, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
