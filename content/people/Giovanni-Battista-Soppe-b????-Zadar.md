# Giovan Battista Soppe

Governator who, together with serdar Smoljan Smiljanić and Stojan Janković, ambushed and crushed a 120-strong Turkish raiding party at the Bribir torrent on 1668-12-09 (doc 163). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
