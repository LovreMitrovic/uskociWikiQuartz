# Vid Tepša (fl. 1756, Rastević)

## Summary
Recorded as *Vido Tepsa*, head of a household of 18 souls in [[Rastevic|Rastević]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 36 campi, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
