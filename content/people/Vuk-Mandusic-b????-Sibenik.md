# Vuk Mandušić (d. 1 August 1648)

The "Vuk Mandušić" of the *kotarski* epic. Senior **harambaša of the Šibenik Morlachi**. Foscolo's encomium (doc. 46): *"era veramente bravo soldato, discreto e disinteressato capitanio, conditione che non ritrovasi che di rado in queste genti, che per lo più chiamar si possono di rapina; ma quanto li veniva fatto di bottinare, tutto compartiva a soldati, che però lo amavano, lo stimavano l' obedivano altrettanto, quanto era temuto da Turchi."*

## Timeline
- **1648-06-02** — Returns from raid on **Ključ** (300 houses, near Banja Luka): killed >200 Turks, took 46 schiavi; on retreat ambushed at Glamoč, beat off the cavalry (doc. 30). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1648-07-11** — Foscolo proposes to give him the gold chain originally destined for pop Sorić — *"essendo sopra tutti stimato in paese"* (doc. 35). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1648-08-01** — **Killed at Zečevo** combatting an Ottoman raid that had pillaged Zaravecchia. The Battle of Zečevo (doc. 42, Ilija Smiljanić's deposition; doc. 43, Matija Mihaljević of Drniš's deposition): Vuk taken alive after killing four Turks himself, kept alive a few hours, beheaded; Ilija Smiljanić helped bury his torso. The 22-document forensic inquest (docs. 42–46) is the most detailed military narrative in the volume. [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]

## Family
- **Sister's son** [[Tadija-Vrancic-b????-Sibenik|Tadija Vrančić]] = his nearest male kin (no closer relations). Provveditore Foscolo named Tadija *capitan* of his 50-man company on 1648-08-03 (doc. 45). The family's later style is **Mandušić alias Vrančić** (cf. doc. 60, 1652).
- **Father** of harambaša **Žorži Mandušić-Vrančić** = Tadija's father (doc. 60).
