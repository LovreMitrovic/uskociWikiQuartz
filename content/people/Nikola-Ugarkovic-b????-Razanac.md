# Nikola Ugarković (fl. 1610, Ražanac)

## Summary
Recorded as *Nicolò Vgarcouich*, head of household in [[Razanac|Ražanac]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 5 comprised 2 men, 2 women, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
