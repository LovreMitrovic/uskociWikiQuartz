# vojvoda Marko Butorac (1702)

Local Lika vojvoda at [[Ribnik]]; antagonist of porkulab [[Milos-Starcevic-b1656-Veliki-Zitnik]] in the conflict that sparked the [[Ribnicka-Buna-1702|Ribnička / Bunjevačka buna]] of 1702 [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

The surname **Butorac** (older patronymic form **Butorčić**) is documented among Krmpote founder families of the [[Krmpote-Migration-Lic-1605|1605 first Lič migration]].
