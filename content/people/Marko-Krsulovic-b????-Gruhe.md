# Marko Kršulović (fl. 1610, Gruhe)

## Summary
Recorded as *Marco Chersulouich*, head of household in [[Gruhe]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 5 comprised 1 man, 1 woman, 3 boys. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
