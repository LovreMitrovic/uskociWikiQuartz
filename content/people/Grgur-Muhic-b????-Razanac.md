# Grgur Muhić (fl. 1610, Ražanac)

## Summary
Recorded as *Gregorio Muhich*, head of household in [[Razanac|Ražanac]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 10 comprised 2 men, 3 women, 1 boy, 4 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
