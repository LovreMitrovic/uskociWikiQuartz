# Petar Prejić

Knez and largest non-Muslim *timarnik* in the entire [[Vilajet-Hrvati]] in 1530, holding a *timar* worth 3,908 akči (or 4,043 by older *iltizam*) covering numerous mezras and čiftluks across both sides of [[Velebit]] [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Possibly an ex-Bosnian sipahi (Jurin Starčević 2022); not formally listed as a Vlasi Istre dzemat head despite clear connection. Father of [[Bogdan-Prejic-b????]].
