# Šimun Berzaga (fl. 1610, Kožino)

## Summary
Recorded as *Simon Berzagia*, head of household in [[Kozino|Kožino]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 6 comprised 3 men and 3 women. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
