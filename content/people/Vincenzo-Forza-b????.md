# Vincenzo Forza

Vicar of the Zadar archbishop, 1636. Documented the orthodox Vlach population of c. 3,200 souls in Žegar, Krupa, Mokropolje, Plavno, Pađene, Žagrović, Butišnica, Kistanje, and Miranje, attached pastorally to Visovac monastery but oriented to Krka and Krupa orthodox monasteries [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Called Bukovica "jedna od najvećih planina Morlakije".

## Forza on Bunjevac populations
The same 1636 vizitacija reports for Catholic ("rito Latino") "Christiani del rito Latino" — the Bunjevac populations [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]] (Jačov 1986:268–271):

- **župa Županovići** — 6 villages (Gorica, Raštane, Prkos, Bubnjane, Lišane Tinjske, Brkljačina Polača); ~1,550 souls; župnik [[Stipan-Soric-b????-Gorica]].
- **župa Bukovica** — managed by franjevac Ivan Visovački ("fra Zuanne da Vissovaz"); ~1,180 souls in Krmpote, Popovići, Ervenik, Parčić, Žužule.
- selo [[Krmpote-Senj|Krmpote]] (=[[Medvidja]]) ~400 stanovnika alone.
- 51 mjesta + 6 župa total — ~13,700 catholic Ottoman subjects in the Zakrčje/Morlakija area.

Names additional villages in the broader Bunjevac vlach milieu: Bubnjane, Lišane Tinjske, Brkljačina Polača, Kamenjane, Podbrđani, Starošane Gornje i Donje, Veliki Prkos, Kašić, Biljane, Korlat, Bukvić, Kršulović, Kolarina, Bulić, Vukšić, Doljane, Međari, Plastovo, Rupe, Ceranje, Veliki and Mali Pristeg, Dobra voda, Stankovci, Banjevci, Popovići, Krmpote, Parčić, Ervenik.
