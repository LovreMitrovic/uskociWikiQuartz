# Juraj Milatović (fl. 1610, Posedarje)

## Summary
Recorded as *Zorzi Millatouich*, head of household in [[Posedarje]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 6 comprised 1 man, 2 women, 1 boy, and 2 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
