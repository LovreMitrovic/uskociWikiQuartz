# Juraj Vrtačić (fl. 1610, Gruhe)

## Summary
Recorded as *Zorzi Vertacich*, head of household in [[Gruhe]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 2 comprised 1 man, 1 woman. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
