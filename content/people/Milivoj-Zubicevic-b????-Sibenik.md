# Milivoj Zubičević

Harambaša of Šibenik who on 1655-11-01 married Kata, the widow of Ilija Smiljanić (a Turkish-born convert; her *dote* inventoried at 432 ducati 4 lire, doc 97). Their son Lukas was baptised at Sv. Zulijan, Šibenik on 1665-10-15 (doc 144). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
