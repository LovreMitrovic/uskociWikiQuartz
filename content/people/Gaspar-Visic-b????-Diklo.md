# Gašpar Višić (fl. 1610, Diklo)

## Summary
Recorded as *Gasparo Vissich*, head of household in [[Diklo]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 10 comprised 4 men, 4 women, 1 boy, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
