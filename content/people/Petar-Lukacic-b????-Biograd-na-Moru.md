# Petar Lukačić (fl. 1610, Biograd na Moru)

## Summary
Recorded as *Piero Lucacich*, head of household in [[Biograd-na-Moru|Biograd na Moru]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 5 comprised 1 old man, 1 man, 1 woman, 1 boy, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
