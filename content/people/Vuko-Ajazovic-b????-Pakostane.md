# Vuko Ajazović (fl. 1610, Pakoštane)

## Summary
Recorded as *Vuco Agiazouich*, head of household in [[Pakostane|Pakoštane]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 7 comprised 1 man, 1 woman, 2 boys, 3 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
