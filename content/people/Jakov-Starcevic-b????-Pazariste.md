# Jakov Starčević

Son of porkulab [[Milos-Starcevic-b1656-Veliki-Zitnik]]. Listed in 1712 popis at age 20 (born c. 1692).

In **1746** appointed **kapetan in the newly-formed Lika regiment** (alongside his brother [[Matija-Starcevic-b????-Pazariste]]) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

In **1756** [[Andrija-Kacic-Miosic-b????]] in *Razgovor ugodni naroda slovinskoga* celebrates a Jakov Starčević among the **vitezovi hrvatski** ("Ali evo zmaja ognjenoga / po imenu Starčević Jakova!"). Šarić: this is early evidence that Bunjevac kin in Lika were absorbing a Hrvatski (regimental/zemaljski) identity, even when commemorated in epic mode that misplaces them temporally [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

Father (or grandfather, depending on reading) of [[Filip-Starcevic-b????-Pazariste]].
