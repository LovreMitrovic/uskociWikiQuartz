# Toma Bilobradić (fl. 1610, Sukošan)

## Summary
Recorded as *Thomaso Bilobradich*, head of household in [[Sukosan|Sukošan]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 5 comprised 1 man, 3 women, 1 boy. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
