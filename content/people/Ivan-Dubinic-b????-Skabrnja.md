# Ivan Dubinić (fl. 1686–1719, Škabrnja)

## Summary
Head of household in [[Skabrnja|Škabrnja]], recorded as **IVAN DUBINIĆ** in the census of households and livestock 1686–1719 [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]. His household of 3 comprised the head, 1 woman and 1 girl. Livestock: 2 konji (horses), 1 goveda (cattle), 1 krave (cows), 10 ovce (sheep). [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]
