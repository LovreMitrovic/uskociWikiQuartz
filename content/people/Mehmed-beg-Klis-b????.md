# Mehmed-beg, kliški sandžak-beg

**Tatar-origin** kliški sandžak-beg of the early 17th c.; son and successor of [[Mustafa-beg-Klis-b????]]. Together with his father, the antagonist whose extortion drove the major Bunjevac flight to [[Backa]] (Georgiceo 1626) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

Distinct from the Hercegovinian sandžak-beg [[Mehmed-beg-b????-Poljica]] in the wiki (Saric-2024 ingest).
