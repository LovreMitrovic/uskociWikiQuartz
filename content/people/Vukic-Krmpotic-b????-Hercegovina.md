# Vukić Krmpotić (1477)

Head (džematska glava) of the **Krmpote katun** in the 1477 Hercegovina defter, **nahija Humska zemlja**. Defter records 11 houses (poreznih jedinica). Aličić 1985:130–131 [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

Defter notation: "od ranije su to bile njihove baštine pa su ponovo došli pod tim uvjetom" — the katun had returned from refuge under *istimâlet* protection (paralleling vojvoda [[Vladimir-vojvoda-b????-Hercegovina]] in the same defter).

**Pasture range:**
- Winter (zimišta): [[Buhovo]], [[Borajna]], [[Ledinac]], [[Veseli-Vstak]] (W. Hercegovina, ~22 km from Buna).
- Summer (ljetišta): [[Cvrsnica]], [[Vran]], [[Ljubusa]].

Founder figure of the [[Krmpotic|Krmpote/Krmpotić]] lineage that dominates Bunjevac migration history. The katun name persists across the entire Tromeđa: [[Medvidja]] (Krmpote), [[Krmpote-Senj]], etc.
