# fra Anto Matković (1582)

Bosnian Franciscan who in **1582** opened a **school for "Dalmatince"** in the Temišvar diocese (Bušić 2005:51) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

"Dalmatinci" in Hungarian-Catholic terminology = the Bunjevac/štokavski-ikavski population transferred to Podunavlje from the Klis-area Ottoman frontier — same demographic substrate later named *Bunievczi* in the 1622 Kaloča petition of [[Simun-Matkovic-b????]] (likely a relative).
