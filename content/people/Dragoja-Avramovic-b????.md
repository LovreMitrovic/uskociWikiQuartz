# Dragoja Avramović (Dragoglia)

Morlak; brother of Dragić Avramović. Killed in a skirmish with [[Knin]] Turks; killed [[Cejvan-aga-Lopusic-b????-Knin|Čejvan-aga Lopušić]] first, doc 49 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Left widow and children, for whom his brother petitioned successfully for support.
