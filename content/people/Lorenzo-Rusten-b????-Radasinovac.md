# Lorenzo Rusten (fl. 1756, Radašinovac)

## Summary
Recorded as *Lorenzo Rusten*, a landholder with no household enumerated in [[Radasinovac|Radašinovac]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 30 campi 86 quarte, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
