# Pavao Diklić (fl. 1610, Glupavac)

## Summary
Recorded as *Paulo Dichlich*, head of household in [[Glupavac]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 6 comprised 2 men, 1 woman, 1 boy, 2 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
