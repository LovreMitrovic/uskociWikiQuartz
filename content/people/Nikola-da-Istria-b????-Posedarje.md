# Nikola da Istria (fl. 1610, Posedarje)

## Summary
Recorded as *Nicolò da Istria*, head of household in [[Posedarje]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 2 comprised 1 man and 1 woman. The byname *da Istria* denotes origin from Istria rather than a hereditary family surname. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
