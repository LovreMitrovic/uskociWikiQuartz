# Martin Gibalović (fl. 1686–1719, Škabrnja)

## Summary
Head of household in [[Skabrnja|Škabrnja]], recorded as **MARTIN GIBALOVIĆ pok. Marka** in the census of households and livestock 1686–1719 [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]. His household of 4 comprised the head, 1 man-at-arms, 1 woman and 1 girl. Livestock: 2 konji (horses), 1 goveda (cattle), 3 telad (calves), 3 krave (cows), 20 ovce (sheep).
