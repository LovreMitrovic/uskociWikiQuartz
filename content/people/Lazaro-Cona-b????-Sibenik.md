# Lazaro Cona

Morlach figure dispatched on 12 Nov 1686 by Stojan Janković from **Bigliane** (Biljane near Posedarje) to commissario Molin (out at Malpaga) and to the other serdari to ask whether he should remain at Biljane or move; intermediary at the start of the November Bosnian-pasha raid (doc. 155) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].

One of the *capi* of the Šibenik–Skradin Morlachs (with Cvitanović, Tintor, Goreta, Palikuća, Žinović).
