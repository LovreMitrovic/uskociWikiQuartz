# Šime Starčević (1784–1859)

Catholic priest and grammarian. Son of [[Filip-Starcevic-b????-Pazariste]], great-grandson of porkulab [[Milos-Starcevic-b1656-Veliki-Zitnik]] [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

**Promicatelj "zemaljskog" i "pučkog" horvatstva** — a regional/popular Croatian identity, transitional between Bunjevac etnokulturni naslijeđe and the modern etnonacionalni hrvatstvo of his nephew **[[Ante-Starcevic-b1823-Veliki-Zitnik]]**.
