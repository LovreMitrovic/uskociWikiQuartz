# Ivan Baušević (fl. 1610, Turanj)

## Summary
Recorded as *Zuanne Bausseuich*, head of household in [[Turanj]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 10 comprised 2 men, 4 women, 1 boy, 3 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
