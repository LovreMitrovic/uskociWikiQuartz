# Martin Salesa (fl. 1610, Bartulac)

## Summary
Recorded as *Martin Salessa*, head of household in [[Bartulac]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 7 comprised 1 man, 2 women, 2 boys, 2 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
