# Čulina, sin Milića

Knez residing on mezra Stara Bilina (today [[Bjeline]]); held a *timar* in [[Lika]] [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Possible (but unconfirmed) ancestor of the [[Culina]] / Čulinović lineage attested in 16th–17th c. on the original Tromeđa.
