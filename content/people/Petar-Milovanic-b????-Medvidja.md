# Petar Milovanić

Attested 1518 in a Zadar deed as *Petri Milouanich catonarii districtus Zelengradi* — the earliest dated record of the Milovanić Vlach lineage [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Migrated to Habsburg Istria c. 1520, returned by 1528. Knez and head of the Bukovica dzemat in [[Medvidja]] (6 houses). Defected to Venice 1538 with his kindred.

## Timeline
- **1518** — Named in a Zadar deed as *catonarius* (katunar) of the Zelengrad district — the earliest dated record of the lineage. *"Petar Milovanić spominje se još 1518. u jednoj zadarskoj ispravi kao katunar Zelengrada (Petri Milouanich catonarii districtus Zelengradi)."* [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]
- **c. 1520** — Emigrated to Habsburg Istria, returning to the old country by around 1528. *"Oko 1520. iselio se u habsburšku Istru, da bi se već oko 1528. vratio u stari kraj."* [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]
- **1528–1530** — Recorded as *knez Petar, sin Milovana*, head of a *džemat* in nahija Bukovica, living in Medviđi (6 houses); his brother Tomaš, also styled knez, kept a house in the same village. *"knez Petar, sin Milovana spominje se kao starješina džemata u nahiji Bukovica. Živio je u selu Medviđi (Medviža), koje je tada imalo šest kuća… U selu je zapisana i kuća njegova brata Tomaša, sina Milovana, koji je isto nosio titulu kneza."* [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]
- **1538** — The Milovanići defected to Venetian territory and entered military service. *"Milovanići su također 1538. prebjegli na mletački teritorij i ušli u vojnu službu."* [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]
