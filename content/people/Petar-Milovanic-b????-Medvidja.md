# Petar Milovanić

Attested 1518 in a Zadar deed as *Petri Milouanich catonarii districtus Zelengradi* — the earliest dated record of the Milovanić Vlach lineage [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Migrated to Habsburg Istria c. 1520, returned by 1528. Knez and head of the Bukovica dzemat in [[Medvidja]] (6 houses). Defected to Venice 1538 with his kindred.
