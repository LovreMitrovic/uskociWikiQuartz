# Nikola Janković

Eldest surviving son of [[Stojan-Jankovic-b1635-Zegar|Stojan Janković]], from his first marriage (mother Vinka/Vuka). Already enrolled in cavalry from 1675-08-24 by Senate order ([[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]] doc. 215).

## Timeline (this source)
- **1686-11-07** — Ducal of Doge Marc'Antonio Giustinian (doc. 148) authorises Stojan Janković's *oltramarino* regiment of 500 fanti in 10 companies of 50; **Nikola is designated *colonnello* of the colonel's company**, with 40 ducats/month, "adempite le conditioni infrascritte". Original ducal on parchment in *Arhiv B. Desnice* [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
