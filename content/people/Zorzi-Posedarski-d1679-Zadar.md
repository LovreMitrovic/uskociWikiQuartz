# Žorži (Zorzi) Posedarski (d. 1679)

Son of [[Frano-Posedarski-1648-Zadar|Frano Posedarski the Elder]] of the [[Posedarski]] kindred. Named *capo di tutti li Morlacchi* (general superintendent of all Venetian Morlachi) by ducal of 1664-12-09, at 500 ducati/year (doc. 143) [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]. Died 1679-07-21 at [[Posedarje]], taken to [[Zadar]] and buried at Sv. Domenico (doc. 259) [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]].
