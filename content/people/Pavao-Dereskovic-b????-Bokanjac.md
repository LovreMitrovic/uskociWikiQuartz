# Pavao Deresković (fl. 1610, Bokanjac)

## Summary
Recorded as *Paulo Derescouich*, head of household in [[Bokanjac]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 3 comprised 1 man, 2 women. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
