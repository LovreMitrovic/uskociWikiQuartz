# Jure Mitrović (fl. 1686–1719, Škabrnja)

## Summary
Head of household in [[Skabrnja|Škabrnja]], recorded as **JURE MITROVIĆ pok. Ivana detto Bugarin** in the census of households and livestock 1686–1719 [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]. His household of 5 comprised the head, 3 boys and 1 woman. Livestock: 3 konji (horses), 4 goveda (cattle), 3 telad (calves), 5 krave (cows), 30 ovce (sheep). [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]

## Family
- Father: [[Ivan-Mitrovic-b????|Ivan Mitrović]] (deceased by census)

## Notes
- Byname *detto Bugarin*; cf. [[Jure-Bugarin-b????-Skabrnja|Jure Bugarin]] in the same census and the 1610 Bugarin household in Sukošan ([[Bugarin]]). No known connection to the Janković-Mitrović clan of [[Mitrovic|Mitrović]].
