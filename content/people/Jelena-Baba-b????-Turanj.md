# Jelena Baba (fl. 1610, Turanj)

## Summary
Recorded as *Baba Gielena*, head of household in [[Turanj]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. Her household of 3 comprised 2 women, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]

## Notes
"Baba" here is a descriptor (elderly woman / grandmother) rather than a true surname; she is recorded without a family name, and it is used in the surname slot only to form a stable page id. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
