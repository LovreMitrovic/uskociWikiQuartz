# Radan Matić (of Budin)

Budin-based companion (*drug*) of [[Ilija-Mitrovic-b????-Zegar|Ilija Mitrović]], named in the 1684 Sinobad plunder-and-murder case [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]. Named as the killer of [[Pietro-Sinobad-b????-Posedarje|Pietro/Perica Sinobad]] at [[Brgud]] at the start of the Morean War; pursued in a long blood-feud by [[Jovan-Sinobad-b????-Posedarje|Jovan Sinobad]], recorded in a 1701 vendetta document (doc 331) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].

## Notes
- Merged 2026-07-19 with the former `Radan-Matic-b????-Brgud` page (same man; "Brgud" was the murder site, Budin his base). Identity is **inferential** — Desnica I (doc 396) places him in Ilija Mitrović's Budin band in 1684; Desnica II (doc 331) names "Radan Matić" as Perica Sinobad's killer in the 1701 vendetta record; no single document equates the two names directly.
