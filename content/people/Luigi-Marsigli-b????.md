# count Luigi Marsigli (1699)

Italian-Habsburg polymath and military commissioner; led the Habsburg boundary commission negotiating the [[Karlovacki-Mir-1699|Karlovački mir 1699]] with the Ottoman side.

In commission documents he refers to the Bunjevci of the [[Senjska-Kapetanija]] as **"Meerkroaten"** — Sea-Croats (Kaser 2003:21) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]]. The label captures the Habsburg administrative perception: a population still called *catholic Vlach / Rascianus / Walach / Meerkroat* by various official observers, before [[Martin-Brajkovic-b????-Senj]]'s 1702 introduction of *Bunievczi* into ecclesiastical record.
