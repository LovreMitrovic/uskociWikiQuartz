# Juraj Sodić (fl. 1610, Turanj)

## Summary
Recorded as *Zorzi Soddich*, head of household in [[Turanj]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 12 comprised 1 old man, 4 men, 6 women, 1 boy. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
