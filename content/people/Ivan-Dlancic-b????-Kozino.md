# Ivan Dlančić (fl. 1610, Kožino)

## Summary
Recorded as *Zuane Dlanchich*, head of household in [[Kozino|Kožino]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 9 comprised 3 men, 4 women and 2 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
