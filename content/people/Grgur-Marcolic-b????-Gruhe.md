# Grgur Marcolić (fl. 1610, Gruhe)

## Summary
Recorded as *Gregorio Marzolich*, head of household in [[Gruhe]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 5 comprised 1 man, 1 woman, 2 boys, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
