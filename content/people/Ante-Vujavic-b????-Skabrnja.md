# Ante Vujavić (fl. 1686–1719, Škabrnja)

## Summary
Head of household in [[Skabrnja|Škabrnja]], recorded as **Kapetan ANTE VUJAVIĆ pok. Marka** in the census of households and livestock 1686–1719 [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]. His household of 8 comprised the head, 1 man-at-arms, 3 women and 3 girls. Livestock: 10 konji (horses), 10 goveda (cattle), 6 telad (calves), 10 krave (cows), 100 ovce (sheep).
