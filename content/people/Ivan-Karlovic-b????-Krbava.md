# Ivan Karlović

Krbavski knez. 1514: Ottoman raid took 3,000 of his subjects captive; same autumn several hundred peasant and Vlach families fled his estates in Odorjan, Hotuča, Krbava to Ottoman territory in western Bosnia [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Sometimes paid Ottoman *harač*. His chaplain [[Stjepan-Posedarski-b????]] addressed Pope Leo X in his name in 1519. Mills on Zrmanja later went to the Husrev-beg vakuf.
