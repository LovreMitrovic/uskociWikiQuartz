# Jakov Bergamin (fl. 1756, Vrana)

## Summary
Recorded as *Giacomo Bergamin*, a landholder with no household enumerated in [[Vrana]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 6 campi, held *senza laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
