# Muhamed-beg of Udbina

*Spahija* of [[Udbina]], aged ~26 in 1687. Three brothers; nephew of [[Ibrahim-Pasic-b????-Ripac|Ibrahim Pašić of Ripač]]. Captured by Zaviša Janković's *četa* in spring 1686 along with the Udbina disdar [[Ibrahim-aga-b????-Udbina|Ibrahim-aga]] and [[Aga-Kumalic-b????-Udbina|Aga Kumalić]] while supervising the cultivation of his lands.

Provveditor Zen's report (doc. 134, 18 June 1687): *"di poco spirito, non soldato. Considerato però da Turchi, per esser di nascita nobile."* Bought at auction by Zaviša and partners for 650 reali; expected to be ransomed via his uncle [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
