# Chiara Vukmanović (fl. 1610, Biograd na Moru)

## Summary
Recorded as *Chiara Vuchmanouichia*, head of household in [[Biograd-na-Moru|Biograd na Moru]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. Her household of 2 comprised 1 man, 1 woman. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
