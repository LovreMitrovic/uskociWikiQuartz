# Mara Pusić (fl. 1610, Kožino)

## Summary
Recorded as *Mara Pussichia*, head of household in [[Kozino|Kožino]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. Her household of 4 comprised 1 man and 3 women. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
