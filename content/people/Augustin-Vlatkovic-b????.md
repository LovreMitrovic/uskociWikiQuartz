# Fra Augustin Vlatković

Member of Catholic Bosnian-Hum noble house Jurjević-Vlatković. Around 1482 entered Ottoman service with peasant and Vlach subjects, governing the western frontier of [[Sandzak-Hercegovina]] (later nahije Fragustin, Primorje, Ljubuški) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].
