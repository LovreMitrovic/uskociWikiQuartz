# Luka Vuković (fl. 1686–1719, Škabrnja)

## Summary
Head of household in [[Skabrnja|Škabrnja]], recorded as **LUKA VUKOVIĆ pok. Mile** in the census of households and livestock 1686–1719 [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]. His household of 14 comprised the head, 4 men-at-arms, 2 boys, 3 women and 4 girls. Livestock: 10 konji (horses), 10 goveda (cattle), 6 telad (calves), 15 krave (cows), 150 ovce (sheep).
