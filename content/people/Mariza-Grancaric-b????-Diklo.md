# Mariza Grancarić (fl. 1610, Diklo)

## Summary
Recorded as *Mariza Granzarich*, head of household in [[Diklo]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. Her household of 7 comprised 2 men, 2 women, 2 boys, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
