# Nikola Bunjevac (1698)

Catholic refugee from Bosnia, registered with his family in **selo Selna near Garčin** (NE of Slavonski Brod) in the 1698 popis (Zirdum 2001:100) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]] — example of "šokačke" Bosnian katoličke refugee families that adopted the Bunjevac surname through their displacement and integration into otherwise non-Bunjevac milieu.
