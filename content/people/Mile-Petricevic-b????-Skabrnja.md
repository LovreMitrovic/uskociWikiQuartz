# Mile Petričević (fl. 1686–1719, Škabrnja)

## Summary
Head of household in [[Skabrnja|Škabrnja]], recorded as **MILE PETRIČEVIĆ pok. Martina** in the census of households and livestock 1686–1719 [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]. His household of 5 comprised the head, 3 boys and 1 woman. Livestock: 3 konji (horses), 3 goveda (cattle), 5 telad (calves), 6 krave (cows), 30 ovce (sheep). [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]
