# Daniel Dolfin

Venetian patrician, *kavalier provveditor*. **Provveditor generale in Dalmazia from February 1692**, succeeding Alessandro Molin; held the post (with one interval) through 1696, when he was succeeded by Aluise Mocenigo II [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (multiple docs from 243 onward).

## Major acts in this source
- **March 1692** — Reconciled the warring **Mitrović** and **Milković** kuće after the killing of Bože Milković's brother by Ilija Mitrović's followers; mediated the parallel reconciliation between the **Mitrovići and Sinobadi** [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 247).
- **1692-04-01** — Restored Ilija Mitrović's 15-ducat/month *terminacija* (originally granted by Mocenigo 15 July 1684) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 248).
- **1692-04-01** — Appointed **Nicolò Mitrović** (son of Ilija) capitano of the Croat cavalry company formerly held by his cousin Konstantin [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 249).
- **June 1692** — Authorized 10 ducats + biscuit/month to **Conte Paolo Đurić** capo of Bjelaj province for leading ~5,000 souls into Venetian devotion [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 251).
- **1692-10** — Settled **Ivan Rašić**'s 200 souls / 25 families at Zavojane / Stigli / Hadži Lukovo near Makarska [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 258).
- **1693-01-10** — Appointed **Dimo Vlastò** governor of both Obrovacs after Casanova's death [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 260).
- **1693-02-23** — Granted Đuro Popović Opačić of Mokro Polje 15 stara of millet as seed [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 265).
- **1693-11-06** — At Šibenik when Stefano Capelo reported the **Gorica raid** of 4 Nov 1693 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 268).
- **1694-03** — Reappointed Francesco Spingaroli governor of Biograd, Pakoštane and Vrana [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 275).
- **1694** — Authorized the Brochno-Blato-Goranci migration of ~5,000 souls under 5 serdari and 22 harambasse, settling them at Zadvarje and Vrgorac [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 274).
- **1694-08-21** — Appointed **Mihail Sinobad** capitano of Zvonigrad [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 277).
- **1694-12-23** — Appointed **Janko Vrsalj** harambaša at Plavno [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 278).
- **1696-02** — Appointed Mirko Jokić capitano of Obrovac inhabitants; promulgated the Liga statute of 12 Feb 1696 (the second great Liga reform after Cornaro 1689) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (docs 284, 286).
- **1696** — Led the **Ulcinj campaign**; while at Ulcinj, Selman pasha of Hercegovina raided the Knin area (doc 293) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **1696-11** — Appointed Vid Katalinić captain of a new 50-man Starigrad company [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 290–291, 294).
- **December 1696** — Released Oratio Lantana from his 2,000-ducat surety bond for Zaviša Mitrović [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 297).

## Notes
- Two stints as provveditor generale; held the post during the most consequential 5-year stretch of the Morean War on the Dalmatian front.
- Succeeded by **Aluise Mocenigo II** in late 1696.
