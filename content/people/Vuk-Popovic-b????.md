# Vuk Popović, vojvoda

Uskok vojvoda. Led migrations of [[Vlasi-Uskoplja]] from the Knin/Plavno area through [[Bihac]] toward [[Zumberak]] and Bela krajina in the 1530s [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Šarić proposes that "Vuk vojvoda Vlaha Uskoplja" of the 1528 Ottoman defter and the Habsburg-side "vojvoda Vuk Popović" are the same person.
