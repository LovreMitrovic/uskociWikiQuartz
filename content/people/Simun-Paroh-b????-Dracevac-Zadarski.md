# Šimun Paroh (fl. 1610, Dračevac Zadarski)

## Summary
Recorded as *P. Simon Parocho*, head of a one-person household in [[Dracevac-Zadarski|Dračevac Zadarski]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. The designation *parocho* (parish priest) is his office rather than a surname, and the prefix *P.* stands for *Pre* (Father/reverend); his family name is not given in the source. His household of 1 comprised 1 man. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
