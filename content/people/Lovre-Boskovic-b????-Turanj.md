# Lovre Bosković (fl. 1610, Turanj)

## Summary
Recorded as *Lorenzo Boscouich*, head of household in [[Turanj]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 6 comprised 1 man, 1 woman, 4 boys. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
