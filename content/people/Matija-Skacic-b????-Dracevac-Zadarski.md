# Matija Škacić (fl. 1610, Dračevac Zadarski)

## Summary
Recorded as *Matthio Schiacich*, head of household in [[Dracevac-Zadarski|Dračevac Zadarski]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 8 comprised 2 men, 2 women, 2 boys, 2 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
