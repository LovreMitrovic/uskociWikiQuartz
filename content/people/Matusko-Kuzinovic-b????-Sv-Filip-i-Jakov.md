# Matuško Kužinović (fl. 1610, Sv. Filip i Jakov)

## Summary
Recorded as *Matusco Chuxinoucih*, head of household in [[Sv-Filip-i-Jakov|Sv. Filip i Jakov]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 9 comprised 2 men, 3 women, 4 boys. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]

## Notes
- `birth_place` is **inferred from residence**: the census records the village where the household was enumerated in 1610, not a place of birth, and no birth record is attested. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
- Spelled *Chuxinoucih* here; the village has four Kužinović households under three spellings (*Cuzenouich*, *Chuxinouich*, *Chuxinoucih*), treated as one family. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
