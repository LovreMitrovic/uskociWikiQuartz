# Mate Punoš (fl. 1756, Pakoštane)

## Summary
Recorded as *Mate Punos detto Macich*, head of a household of 8 souls in [[Pakostane|Pakoštane]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 1 campi 1 quarte 81 tavole, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]

## Notes
- `birth_place` is **inferred from residence**: the survey records the village where the household was enumerated in 1756, not a place of birth, and no birth record is attested. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
- Carries the byname *detto Macich*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
- A second household head of the same name is recorded in the same village: [[Mate-Punos-b????-Pakostane-2]] (*detto Bugna*) — household of 4. Treated as a distinct person. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
