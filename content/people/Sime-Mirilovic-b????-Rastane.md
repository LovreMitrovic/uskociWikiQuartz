# Šime Mirilović (fl. 1756, Raštane)

## Summary
Recorded as *Sime Mirilovich*, head of a household of 5 souls in [[Rastane|Raštane]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 12 campi 94 tavole, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
