# Ante Mahadić (fl. 1610, Bokanjac)

## Summary
Recorded as *Antonio Mahadich*, head of household in [[Bokanjac]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 4 comprised 1 man, 1 woman, 1 boy, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
