# Petar Sužica (fl. 1610, Ljubač)

## Summary
Recorded as *Piero Suxiça*, head of household in [[Ljubac|Ljubač]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 6 comprised 2 men, 1 woman, 2 boys, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
