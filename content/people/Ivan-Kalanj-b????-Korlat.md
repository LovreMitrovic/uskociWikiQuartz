# Ivan Kalanj (fl. 1756, Korlat)

## Summary
Recorded as *Ivan Calagn*, head of a household of 4 souls in [[Korlat]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 8 campi 1 quarte 29 tavole, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
