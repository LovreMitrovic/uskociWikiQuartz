# Murat-beg Gajdić (Tardić)

Croatian-origin convert; ćehaja of [[Gazi-Husrev-beg]], then subaša/voivod of [[Vilajet-Hrvati]], and from 1537 first sanjakbeg of the new [[Sandzak-Klis]] [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Architect of the *istimâlet* policy bringing [[Vlasi-Istre]] back from Habsburg Istria in 1528–1530 and again in 1540. Born in [[Sibenik]] area; old surname **Tardić** (Juran 2016). 1527: led a 2,000-man raid via [[Lika]] to Metlika and Novigrad on Kras; 1530: ordered Velebit timber-cutting for Obrovac fleet construction.

## Timeline
- **1520s** — As helper (subaša) of [[Gazi-Husrev-beg]], governor of the *vilajet Hrvati*, began organizing the new Ottoman *serhat* and a planned colonization of Vlach groups. *"Bosanski sandžakbeg Gazi Husrev-beg i njegov pomoćnik, subaša Murat-beg Gajdić (po starom Tardić), koji je upravljao vilajetom Hrvati… počinju s planski organiziranom kolonizacijom."* [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]
- **1527-02** — Led an army of 2,000 men in a raid. *"U veljači 1527. Murat-beg Gajdić predvodio je vojsku od 2000 ljudi."* [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]
- **1530-03** — Engaged many of "his men" to cut Velebit timber for fleet construction at Obrovac. *"Murat-beg je u ožujku 1530. angažirao veliki broj „svojih ljudi" i za sječu velebitskih šuma radi izgradnje flote u [Obrovcu]."* [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]
- **1537** — After the fall of Klis and the formation of the *sandžak Klis*, became its first sanjakbeg. *"Njezinim prvim namjesnikom postao je Murat-beg Gajdić, bivši ćehaja bosanskoga sandžakbega Gazi Husrev-bega i subaša (vojvoda) vilajeta Hrvati."* [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]
