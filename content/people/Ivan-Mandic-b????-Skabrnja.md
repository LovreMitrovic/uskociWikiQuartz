# Ivan Mandić (fl. 1686–1719, Škabrnja)

## Summary
Head of household in [[Skabrnja|Škabrnja]], recorded as **IVAN MANDIĆ pok. Petra** in the census of households and livestock 1686–1719 [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]. His household of 6 comprised the head, 2 boys, 1 woman and 2 girls. Livestock: 2 konji (horses), 4 goveda (cattle), 4 telad (calves), 4 krave (cows), 40 ovce (sheep).
