# Pave Lešaić (fl. 1756, Zemunik)

## Summary
Recorded as *Paval Lessaich*, head of a household of 6 souls in [[Zemunik]] (villici) in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 12 campi 48 tavole, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
