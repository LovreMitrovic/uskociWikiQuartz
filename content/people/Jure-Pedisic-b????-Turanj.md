# Jure Pedišić (fl. 1756, Turanj)

## Summary
Recorded as *Giure Pedisich*, head of a household of 8 souls in [[Turanj]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 16 campi 105 tavole, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
