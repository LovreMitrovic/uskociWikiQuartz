# Ivan Mudrinić (fl. 1756, Rastević)

## Summary
Recorded as *Ivan Mudrinich*, head of a household of 11 souls in [[Rastevic|Rastević]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 22 campi 176 tavole, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
