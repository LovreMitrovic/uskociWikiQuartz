# Chiara Vukšić (fl. 1610, Dračevac Zadarski)

## Summary
Recorded as *Chiara Vuchsichia*, female head of household in [[Dracevac-Zadarski|Dračevac Zadarski]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. Her household of 2 comprised 1 woman, 1 boy. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
