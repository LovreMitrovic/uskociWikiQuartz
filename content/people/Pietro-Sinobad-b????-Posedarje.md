# Pietro (Petar / Perica) Sinobad

Father of [[Jovan-Sinobad-b????-Posedarje|Jovan Sinobad]]. **Killed at [[Brgud]] at the start of the Morean War by [[Radan-Matic-b????-Brgud|Radan Matić]]** (doc 331) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Of [[Posedarje]] origin. The 1684 Sinobad case in [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]] (docs 391, 396) attributes his murder more directly to [[Ilija-Mitrovic-b????-Zegar|Ilija Mitrović]]'s band; the 1701 vendetta record (doc 331) names Radan Matić specifically — a long-running blood feud followed.

Same person as **Pera/Periza Sinobad**, kapetan di Budin in the [[Sinobad]] surname page.
