# Vincenzo (Vićenco) Vendramin

Venetian provveditor general of Dalmatia 1709–1711. Named **[[Marko-Smiljanic-b????.md|Marko Smiljanić]]** (minor son of [[Lazar-Smiljanic-b????.md|Lazar]]) reserved serdar of Lower Kotari (1709), and **[[Antonio-Vujanovic-b????.md|Antonio Vujavić/Vujanović]]** provisional serdar after Lazar Smiljanić's death; granted Sinobad lands in [[Kasic|Kašić]] [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
