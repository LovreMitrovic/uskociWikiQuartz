# Juraj Ozrinović (fl. 1610, Turanj)

## Summary
Recorded as *Zorzi Ozrinouich*, head of household in [[Turanj]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 7 comprised 1 old man, 3 women, 3 boys. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
