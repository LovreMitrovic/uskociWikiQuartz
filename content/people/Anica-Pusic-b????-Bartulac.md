# Anica Pušić (fl. 1610, Bartulac)

## Summary
Recorded as *Aniza Pussichia*, head of household in [[Bartulac]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. Her household of 4 comprised 4 women. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
