# Francesco Rotta (fl. 1756, Turanj)

## Summary
Recorded as *Francesco e fratello Rotta*, a landholder with no household enumerated in [[Turanj]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 30 tavole, held *senza laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
