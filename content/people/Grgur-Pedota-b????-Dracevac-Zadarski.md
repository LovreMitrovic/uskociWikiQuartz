# Grgur Pedota (fl. 1610, Dračevac Zadarski)

## Summary
Recorded as *Gregorio Pedota*, head of household in [[Dracevac-Zadarski|Dračevac Zadarski]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 6 comprised 2 men, 3 women, 1 boy. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
