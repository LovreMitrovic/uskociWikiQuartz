# Alessandro Molin (Molino)

Venetian patrician; **provveditor generale in Dalmazia** 1689–1692, between Girolamo Cornaro and Daniel Dolfin [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (docs 226–242, 257).

## Major acts in this source
- **Jan 1690** — Appointed **Cesare Casanova** capitan/governator of Obrovac; dismissed **Filippo Mitrović** as captain of a 56-foot Obrovac company [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (docs 226, 227).
- **Jan 1690** — Established the salt scale at Obrovac [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 226).
- **Feb 1690** — Granted 10 ducats/month to **vladika Visarion Borilović** of Cetinje for promoting Venetian interests in Montenegro [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 232).
- **Feb 1690** — Granted lands of the Krka monastery of Sv. Arhangel (San Michiel Arcangelo) to the Greek-rite monks Don Giuseppe Zavalac and Stefan Janoški [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 231).
- **1690** — Divided the Kotari into **Upper Kotari** (Mitrović serdarstvo) and **Lower Kotari** (Milković serdarstvo) — appointed Zaviša Mitrović and Bože Milković serdari respectively [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **24 May 1690** — Appointed **Jovan (Zuanne) Sinobad** governor of Zvonigrad with a 50-paesano company; salary 14 ducats [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 234).
- **June 1691** — Appointed **Filippo Sinobad** capitano of Zvonigrad to replace his brother Jovan, who was simultaneously promoted to governor of Knin krajina with 60 fields & vineyards near Bulin Most plus houses in Knin from late Turks Hadži Kurtović, Hadži Emorić, Ahmet-aga Livaković [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (docs 239, 240, 241).

## Notes
- Refused to meet **Ilija Mitrović** when Ilija fled Obrovac upon Molin's arrival in 1690 (doc 228) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- Succeeded by Daniel Dolfin in February 1692.
