# Vlatko Bunislavić (1406)

Vlach katunska glava in eastern Hum, 1406 [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]] (Hrabak 1981:188). Attested example of the medieval Bun-stem vlach onomastic family. Descendant of a Bunislav, indicating use of Bun-derived personal names in 14th–15th c. Hercegovinian Vlach society — onomastic evidence for the etymology of the etnik **Bunjevac**.
