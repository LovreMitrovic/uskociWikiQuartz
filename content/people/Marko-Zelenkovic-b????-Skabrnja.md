# Marko Zelenković (fl. 1686–1719, Škabrnja)

## Summary
Head of household in [[Skabrnja|Škabrnja]], recorded as **MARKO ZELENKOVIĆ pok. Martina** in the census of households and livestock 1686–1719 [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]. His household of 6 comprised the head, 1 man-at-arms, 1 boy, 1 woman and 2 girls. Livestock: 5 konji (horses), 6 goveda (cattle), 5 telad (calves), 7 krave (cows), 50 ovce (sheep).
