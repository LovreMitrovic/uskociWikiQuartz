# Ive Gašparović (fl. 1756, Sukošan)

## Summary
Recorded as *Ive Gasparovich*, head of a household of 13 souls in [[Sukosan|Sukošan]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 26 campi 10 tavole, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
