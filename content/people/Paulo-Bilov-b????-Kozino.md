# Paulo Bilov (fl. 1610, Kožino)

## Summary
Recorded as *Paulo Billou*, head of household in [[Kozino|Kožino]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 4 comprised 2 men, 1 woman and 1 boy. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
