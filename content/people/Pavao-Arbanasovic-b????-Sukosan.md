# Pavao Arbanasović (fl. 1610, Sukošan)

## Summary
Recorded as *Paulo Arbanassouich*, head of household in [[Sukosan|Sukošan]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 3 comprised 1 man, 1 woman, 1 boy. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
