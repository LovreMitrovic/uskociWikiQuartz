# Pietro Valier

Venetian noble; *Provveditor general estraordinario dell'armi* from late 1684. Successor to Mocenigo in the Dalmatian command. Presided over the disastrous April 1685 Sinj campaign.

## Timeline
- **1684 (late)** — Assumes provveditor generalship (docs 41–43 onward) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **1685-02-12** — Rules on the dispute over voting rights in the Sv. Ilija (Greek-rite) confraternity in Zadar, between Greek confrères and the Croatian/Albanian cavalry officers (doc 47) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **1685-04-07** — Present at Sinj; his unnamed son was last to leave the battlefield together with marchese Carlotti (doc 60) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. His *vice ragionato* (accountant) was killed at Sinj carrying 300 cecchini (doc 60).
- **1685-05-26** — Issues proclamation on Greek-rite slave registration: state to ransom unredeemed Orthodox slaves at 200 lire; sale forbidden without permission (doc 69) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
