# Mate Kašić (fl. 1756, Biograd na Moru)

## Summary
Recorded as *Mate Cassich*, head of a household of 6 souls in [[Biograd-na-Moru|Biograd na Moru]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 5 campi 1 quarte 53 tavole, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
