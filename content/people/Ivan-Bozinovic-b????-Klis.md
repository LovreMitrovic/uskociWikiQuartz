# don Ivan Božinović (1659)

Catholic kapelan of župa kliške, vranjicke i kamenske (svjetovni svećenik / ilirski kler). In a 1659 ćirilična isprava signs himself **"kapelan vlaški"** (Klaić 1899:V, 15) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

Documents the Dalmatian secular-clergy practice of openly using vlaško (=morlačko) ime for their flock — in stark contrast to the Bosanska franjevačka provincija practice of avoiding it. The mletačka administrative terminologija (*Morlachi catholici*) was adopted by the dalmatinski klerici and gave the morlačko (vlaško) name a continuity in Dalmatia that it lost elsewhere.
