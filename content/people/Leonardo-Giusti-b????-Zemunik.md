# Leonardo Giusti (fl. 1756, Zemunik)

## Summary
Recorded as *Leonardo e fratello Giusti*, a landholder with no household enumerated in [[Zemunik]] (villici) in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 149 campi 2 quarte 80 tavole, held *senza laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
