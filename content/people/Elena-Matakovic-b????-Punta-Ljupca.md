# Elena Mataković (fl. 1610, Punta Ljupča)

## Summary
Recorded as *Elena Matacouichia*, head of household in [[Punta-Ljupca|Punta Ljupča]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. Her household of 5 comprised 1 man, 2 women, 2 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
