# Jure Vojara (fl. 1756, Vrana)

## Summary
Recorded as *Jure Voiara*, head of a household of 8 souls in [[Vrana]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 16 campi 36 tavole, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
