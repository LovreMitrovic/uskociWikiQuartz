# Stipan Radenović (fl. 1686–1719, Škabrnja)

## Summary
Head of household in [[Skabrnja|Škabrnja]], recorded as **STIPAN RADENOVIĆ** in the census of households and livestock 1686–1719 [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]. His household of 4 comprised the head, 1 man-at-arms and 2 women. Livestock: 10 konji (horses), 5 goveda (cattle), 2 telad (calves), 4 krave (cows), 50 ovce (sheep).
