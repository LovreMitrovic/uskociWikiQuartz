# Mijo Bašić (fl. 1756, Radašinovac)

## Summary
Recorded as *Mio Bassich*, head of a household of 9 souls in [[Radasinovac|Radašinovac]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 18 campi 55 quarte, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
