# Jure Sudarević (fl. 1686–1719, Škabrnja)

## Summary
Head of household in [[Skabrnja|Škabrnja]], recorded as **JURE SUDAREVIĆ pok. Grge** in the census of households and livestock 1686–1719 [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]. His household of 7 comprised the head, 4 boys, 1 woman and 1 girl. Livestock: 1 goveda (cattle), 1 telad (calves), 1 krave (cows). [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]
