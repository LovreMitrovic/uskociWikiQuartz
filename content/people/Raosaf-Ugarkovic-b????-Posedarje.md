# Raosaf Ugarković (fl. 1610, Posedarje)

## Summary
Recorded as *Raosaf Vgarcouich*, head of household in [[Posedarje]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 2 comprised 1 man and 1 woman. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
