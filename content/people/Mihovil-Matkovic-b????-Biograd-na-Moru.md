# Mihovil Matković (fl. 1610, Biograd na Moru)

## Summary
Recorded as *Michiel Matcouich*, head of household in [[Biograd-na-Moru|Biograd na Moru]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 12 comprised 2 men, 3 women, 5 boys, 2 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]] Recorded with the title *Cap.o* (capitano).
