# Mihovil Jurasović (fl. 1610, Biograd na Moru)

## Summary
Recorded as *Michiel Jurassouich*, head of household in [[Biograd-na-Moru|Biograd na Moru]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 9 comprised 3 men, 3 women, 1 boy, 2 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
