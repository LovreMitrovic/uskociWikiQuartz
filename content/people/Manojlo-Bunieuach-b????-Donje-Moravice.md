# Manojlo Bunieuach (1670)

Vlah on the Zrinski estate at Donje Moravice in 1670 (Burić 1979:74) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]]. The personal name Manojlo + the Bunjevac surname suggests an Orthodox bearer — concrete evidence that the surname Bunjevac (etnik origin) attached primarily in Orthodox-Vlach milieux to former Catholic Bunjevac neighbors.
