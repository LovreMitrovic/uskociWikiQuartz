# Petar Devenesić (fl. 1610, Glupavac)

## Summary
Recorded as *Piero Deuenesich*, head of household in [[Glupavac]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 5 comprised 1 man, 2 women, 1 boy, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
