# Toma Kosinoga (fl. 1610, Dračevac Zadarski)

## Summary
Recorded as *Thomaso Cossinoga*, head of household in [[Dracevac-Zadarski|Dračevac Zadarski]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 7 comprised 3 men, 2 women, 1 boy, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
