# Ante Albergetti (fl. 1756, Pristeg)

## Summary
Recorded as *Antonio Albergeti*, a landholder with no household enumerated in [[Pristeg]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 35 campi 1 quarte 28 tavole, held *senza laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
