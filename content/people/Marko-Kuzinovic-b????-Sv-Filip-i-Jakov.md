# Marko Kužinović (fl. 1610, Sv. Filip i Jakov)

## Summary
Recorded as *Marco Chuxinouich*, head of household in [[Sv-Filip-i-Jakov|Sv. Filip i Jakov]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 9 comprised 3 men, 1 woman, 2 boys, 3 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
