# Stjepan Berzović (fl. 1610, Bibinje)

## Summary
Recorded as *Steffano Berzouich*, head of household in [[Bibinje]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 6 comprised 2 men, 2 women, 2 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
