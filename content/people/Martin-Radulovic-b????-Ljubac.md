# Martin Radulović (fl. 1610, Ljubač)

## Summary
Recorded as *Martin Radulouich*, head of household in [[Ljubac|Ljubač]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 13 comprised 2 men, 3 women, 3 boys, 5 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
