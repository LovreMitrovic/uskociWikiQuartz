# Pop Petar Jagodić-Kuridža (c. 1666 – 9 April 1749)

Orthodox parish priest at the church of Sv. Petar in [[Bukovica]]; principal instigator of the **1704 Bukovica Revolt** against Venetian decima reform and Canagetti's tax extortion. Spent **40+ years in Venetian prisons** (Camerotti, then the Pozzi), released 29 March 1746 at age 80 — by then blind and deaf — and died three years later in [[Zadar]].

## Timeline
- **c. 1666** — Born (back-calculation from age 80 at 1746 release). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **1704** — Together with [[Ilija-Nanic-b????-Zegar|Ilija Nanić of Žegar]] and [[Mattio-Zabetic-b????-Zaravecchia|Mattio Žabetić of Zaravecchia]], leads the **Bukovica revolt** (docs 352, 356, 357). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **1704** — Condemned to death in absentia by Marin Zane after the revolt's suppression; flees to [[Lika]] (Habsburg side) (doc 357). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **post-1704** — Eventually arrested in Venetian territory; imprisoned in the Camerotti, later the **Pozzi** (docs 385, 389). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **1734** — Nikola Stojanović named messenger to forward funds to imprisoned Petar Jagodić (doc 385). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **1737** — Daughter Magdalena (Manda) Jagodić travels to [[Zadar]] to claim her father's property (doc 387). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **1746-03-29** — Released from prison at age 80, blind and deaf; supported in his last years by Greek Pietro/Petar Spiro who was ordered to remove him from the Greek quarter at first opportunity (doc 390). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **1749-04-09** — Dies in Zadar, age ~83; recorded in *matica umrlih* of Orthodox Sv. Ilija church Zadar (doc 392). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]

## Family
- Brother: Petar Jagodić (lay) — d. ~1733; left a widow ("cognata"). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- Cousin (germano): pop Mihailo (Mijat) Jagodić — paroh of [[Bilisane]]. Managed the lay brother's estate; renounced power-of-attorney 2 January 1738 (doc 388). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- Daughter: Magdalena/Manda Jagodić — came to Zadar 1737 to claim her father's property.

## Notes
- Surname spellings in the source vary widely: *Zagotich, Curelia, Cureggia, Jagodich, Jagodovich, Giadovich, Giagodich, Godovich*. Canonical form is **Jagodić-Kuridža** (Desnica 1951 footnotes).
- The 40+ year imprisonment is one of the longest carceral cases documented in the Desnica corpus.
