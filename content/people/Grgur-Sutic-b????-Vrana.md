# Fra Grgur Šutić (padre fra Gregorio Sutich)

*Cappellano della natione* (chaplain of the Morlak community). Convinced the [[Vrana]] Morlaks to seize Vrana from the Turks; trusted by Mocenigo as informant on Vrana sentiment, docs 34, 36 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
