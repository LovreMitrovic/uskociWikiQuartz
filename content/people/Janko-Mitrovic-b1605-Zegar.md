# Janko Mitrović (c. 1605/14 – 28 February 1659)

## Summary
Founder of the *kotarska* line that became the **Janković-Mitrović** of Ravni Kotari. Originally **harambaša from Žegar** (Bukovica) under Ottoman rule with two armed sons (Ilija and Filip — the latter likely an early death; later texts give Stojan, Zaviša, Ilija) [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]] (doc. 20: "Pietro Smiglianich da Vavina ... fu il primo ad adoprarsi ... Illia antedetto ed altro Filippo" — careful, this attestation is for **Smiljanić**, not Mitrović; Janko's earliest dated attestation is doc. 49, 4 Aug 1649, "Gianco Mitrovich da Zegar, statura comune, occhi vari, pel castagno, mostachi biondi, d' anni 35 in circa" → birth c. 1614).

Stojan's 1670 supplica (doc. 170) names five brothers who "abbandonando la lor patria natia di Segar paese turchesco, si sono fedelmente portati sotto l' insegne gloriose della serenità vostra coll' haver a ciò persuaso altre settanta case, quali formavano un corpo di centocinquanta sperimentati guerrieri": **Janko, Giovanni, Steffano, Paolo, Andrea**. The Mitrović *uskakanje* thus brought ~70 households / ~150 fighting men from **Žegar** to Venetian Ravni Kotari at the start of the Candian War.

## Timeline
- **c. 1614** — Born in Žegar (per harambaša-pay terminacija doc. 49, "d' anni 35 in circa" in 1649). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **c. 1646–47** — Crossed (*uskočio*) with brothers and ~70 households from Ottoman Žegar to Venetian territory; settled at Posedarje. [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1648-05-11** — Returns from raid on Unac with brother harambaše; defeated Lika-Udbina Turks at Šuplja Crkva; brought 30 Morlach houses incl. spahija Busović (doc. 28). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1648-05-15** — Foscolo names him *poglavica Morlaka iz Bukovice* with judicial first-instance authority over their disputes (doc. 29). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1649-08-04** — 4 ducat/month pay assigned by provveditore Foscolo (terminacija doc. 49). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1651-02-01** — peso di pane added to monthly pay (doc. 54, terminacija; original parchment in *Arhiv porodice Desnica u Obrovcu*). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1651-12-10** — Foscarini orders him enrolled as *soldato di cavalleria leggera* under generalni providur konjice (doc. 58). Now of Posedarje. [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1654-01-10** — Pay raised to 20 ducati/month + peso di biscotto for repulsing Turks at the Posedarje *guazzo* (doc. 78). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1656-03-25** — Provveditore Zen names him **serdar de Morlacchi** to replace the murdered Filip Smiljanić (doc. 102) — the **first attestation of the Venetian title *serdar*** in this corpus (Desnica's footnote). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1656-08-04** — Co-signs (with harambaša Ilija Milković and all kotarski harambaše) the petition to Bernardo from Bigliane: refuses any *colonnello* over the Morlachi who is not a Venetian patrician (doc. 105). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1659-02-11** — Severely wounded at the Cetina battle alongside Ilija Milković (doc. 112, lettera Zorzi Papali); a *nipote* (nephew) of his died in Šibenik the next day (doc. 112). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1659-02-28** — Died in Zadar of those wounds; buried in Sv. Ilija (Greek-rite Orthodox), Zadar — *liber obitum* (doc. 113). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1659-03-07** — Bernardo to Senate: "il commando che egli si haveva assonto eccedente, ho diviso fra diversi" — i.e., his serdarsko ovlaštenje was split among multiple successors (doc. 116). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]

## Family
- Father: q. **Dmitar Mitrović** (cf. doc. 58 "harambassà Gianco Mitrovich quondam Dimitri da Xegar").
- Brothers (per Stojan's 1670 supplica): Giovanni, Steffano, Paolo, Andrea — and probably **Vukadin Mitrović** (d. Novigrad 1653, doc. 74 — **same kin-cluster but explicitly distinct in pay-rolls**; possibly cousin rather than brother).
- Sons: [[Stojan-Jankovic-b1635-Zegar]] (b. c. 1635), [[Zavisa-Jankovic-b????-Zegar]] (also "Sabba"), [[Ilija-Mitrovic-b????-Zegar]] (the later bandit; died in violence).
- Widow and posterity received pay-augmentations after his death (doc. 73, 116).

## Notes
- The shift from **Mitrović** (the patronymic, used by his contemporaries and on the pay-rolls) to **Janković** (the patronymic of his son's son etc., from *Janko*) happens within his own lifetime in Venetian usage: dispatches and parish entries use "Mitrovich", "Giancovich", "Mitrovich detto Giancovich" interchangeably. The single canonical form for filing is **Mitrović** as the surname stem; **Janković** is the patronymic-derived hereditary form that fixes among his descendants.
- The line is **Pravoslavna** (Greek-rite Orthodox) — buried at **Sv. Ilija u Zadru**, the Orthodox parohiska crkva.
- Per Desnica's footnote (doc. 215) Stojan in 1675 had brothers who could be confused with "Janko" — that *Janko* is patronymic shorthand for one of the actual brothers (Zaviša or Ilija), not a literal sibling named Janko.
