# Roberto Papafava

Venetian patrician; **capitano di Zara** in 1689. Received the report from governator Pietro Calčina (Posedarje pertinenza) about the defeat of the bandit Popržen [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (end of doc 224, doc 225).
