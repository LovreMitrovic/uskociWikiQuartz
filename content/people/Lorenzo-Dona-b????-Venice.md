# Lorenzo Donà (Donado)

Outgoing Venetian *Provveditor general* in Dalmatia, May 1684. When Mocenigo arrived to assume the office, Donà was ill and bedridden at *scoglio del Lantana* (Lantana islet near Zadar), doc 1 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Also referenced doc 36.
