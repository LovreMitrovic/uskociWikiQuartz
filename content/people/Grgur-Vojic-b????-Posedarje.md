# Grgur Vojić (fl. 1610, Posedarje)

## Summary
Recorded as *Gregorio Voijch*, head of household in [[Posedarje]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 6 comprised 4 men and 2 women. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
