# Juraj Dlančić (fl. 1610, Biograd na Moru)

## Summary
Recorded as *Zorzi Dlancich*, head of household in [[Biograd-na-Moru|Biograd na Moru]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 9 comprised 4 men, 4 women, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
