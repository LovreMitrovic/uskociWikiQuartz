# Petar Najčinović (Jančić)

Knez and head of the Benković Vlasi Istre dzemat in 1528–1530, residing in [[Dobruca-Vas]] [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. 1533: signed Zadar contract for 2,000 *kvarti* of wheat to be delivered to Karin. Led 1538 migration to Venetian territory; resettled in [[Podnovje]] as kapetan. His brother [[Pavel-Najcinovic-b????]] also rewarded by the Senate.

## Timeline
- **1528–1530** — Recorded in the Ottoman defter as *knez Petar Jančić*, head of a Vlach *džemat* in nahija Benković, resident at Dobruča Vas. *"Radilo se o starješini koji je u osmanskom popisu 1528.–1530. bio zabilježen kao knez Petar Jančić. Petar Jančić/Najčinović bio je starješina vlaškoga džemata u nahiji Benković i nastanjen u selu Dobrudža, odnosno Dobruča Vas."* [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]
- **1533** — As an Ottoman subject, concluded a contract in Zadar with agents of two Venetian merchants to deliver 2,000 *kvarti* of wheat to a ship at Karin. *"vlaški knez iz „istarske" skupine Petar Najčinović, sada osmanski podanik iz Dobrudže kod Benkovca, sklopio 1533. u Zadru ugovor s agentima dvojice mletačkih trgovaca, obvezujući se da će im na brod, kad pristigne u Karin, dovesti 2000 kvarti pšenice."* [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]
- **1538** — Led the migration of the Vlasi Istre to Venetian territory; he and his brother [[Pavel-Najcinovic-b????]] were rewarded by the Senate; settled at [[Podnovje]] near Novigrad as *kapetan*. *"I knez Petar Najčinović predvodio je 1538. seobu Vlaha Istre na mletačko područje, zbog čega su on i brat mu Pavel također bili nagrađeni od Senata. Naselio se… u Podnovju kod Novigrada i dobio titulu kapetana."* [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]
