# Šimun Stojmilović (fl. 1610, Bibinje)

## Summary
Recorded as *Simon Stoijmilouich*, head of household in [[Bibinje]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 3 comprised 1 man, 1 woman, 1 boy. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
