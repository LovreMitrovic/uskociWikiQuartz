# Ilija Nanić (Žegar)

Captain ("capitan") of Islam and Kašić from 1698 (doc 309) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. In 1702 named arbitrator for the Brgud villagers in their dispute with Lisičić/Podgrađe (doc 348) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. One of the **three ringleaders** of the 1704 Bukovica revolt alongside [[Petar-Jagodic-Kuridza-b1666-Bukovica|pop Petar Jagodić-Kuridža]] and [[Mattio-Zabetic-b????-Zaravecchia|Mattio Žabetić]]; banished and fled to [[Lika]] (Habsburg side) after suppression (docs 352, 356, 357). Source identifies him as "*Illia Nanich da Xegar / da Segar*" — explicitly from [[Zegar]].
