# Marko Punoš (fl. 1610, Pakoštane)

## Summary
Recorded as *Marco Punos*, head of household in [[Pakostane|Pakoštane]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 5 comprised 1 old man, 2 men, 2 women. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
