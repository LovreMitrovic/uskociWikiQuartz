# Ivan Brkljača (Ivanne Berchliazza)

Villager of [[Radovin]]. Took multiple *gecerme* (woolen vests) and silver tokens from Mihajlo Radmilović as he was escaping from Žegar to Venetian territory, doc 31 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
