# Stojan Tvrković (fl. 1610, Ljubač)

## Summary
Recorded as *Stogian Tuercouich*, head of household in [[Ljubac|Ljubač]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 3 comprised 1 man, 2 women. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
