# Miško Pelizarić (fl. 1756, Biograd na Moru)

## Summary
Recorded as *Misco Pelizarich*, head of a household of 4 souls in [[Biograd-na-Moru|Biograd na Moru]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 2 campi 3 quarte 124 tavole, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
