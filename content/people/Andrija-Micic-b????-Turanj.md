# Andrija Mičić (fl. 1610, Turanj)

## Summary
Recorded as *Andrea Micich*, head of household in [[Turanj]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 9 comprised 4 men, 3 women, 2 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
