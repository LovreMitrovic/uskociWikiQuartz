# Šimun Račić (fl. 1610, Gruhe)

## Summary
Recorded as *Simon Racich*, head of household in [[Gruhe]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 9 comprised 3 men, 2 women, 4 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
