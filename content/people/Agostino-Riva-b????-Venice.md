# Agostino (Đustino) Da Riva

Venetian provveditor general of Dalmatia 1706–1707, successor of [[Marin-Zane-b????-Venice|Marin Zane]]. Reported on hajdučija (banditry) at the confines and the **death of [[Vid-Petrovic-b????-Kosovo|Vid Petrović]]** at [[Bruvno]] 1707 (docs 363, 365) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Received the bishop of [[Cetinje]] in 1707 (doc 366). Granted [[Petar-Jagodic-Kuridza-b1666-Bukovica|pop Petar Jagodić]] a *salvocondotto* (doc 389).
