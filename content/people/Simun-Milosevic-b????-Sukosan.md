# Šimun Milošević (fl. 1610, Sukošan)

## Summary
Recorded as *Simon Miloseuich*, head of household in [[Sukosan|Sukošan]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 13 comprised 2 old men, 4 men, 3 women, 2 boys, 2 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
