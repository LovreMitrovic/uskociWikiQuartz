# Hajra (Aire) — formerly Kosa — Lakić

Daughter of Matija Lakić of [[Karin]]. Captured August 1684 by [[Gracac|Gračac]] Turks while threshing millet; sold at [[Knin]] to *sandžak-beg* [[Durakbegovic|Mehmed-beg Durakbegović]], who Islamized her ("Aire" replacing "Cosa") and made her his favorite. Recaptured March 1685 in a Klis–Trogir Morlak ambush, doc 56 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
