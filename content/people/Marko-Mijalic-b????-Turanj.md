# Marko Mijalić (fl. 1756, Turanj)

## Summary
Recorded as *Marco Mialich*, head of a household of 2 souls in [[Turanj]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 4 campi 2 quarte, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
