# Toma Matković (fl. 1610, Biograd na Moru)

## Summary
Recorded as *Thomaso Matcouich*, head of household in [[Biograd-na-Moru|Biograd na Moru]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 8 comprised 3 men, 3 women, 1 boy, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]] Recorded with the title *Cap.o* (capitano).
