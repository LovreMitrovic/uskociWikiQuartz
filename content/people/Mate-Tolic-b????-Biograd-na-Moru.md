# Mate Tolić (fl. 1756, Biograd na Moru)

## Summary
Recorded as *Mate Tolich*, head of a household of 6 souls in [[Biograd-na-Moru|Biograd na Moru]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 19 campi 47 tavole, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
