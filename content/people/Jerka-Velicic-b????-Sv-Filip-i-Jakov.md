# Jerka Veličić (fl. 1610, Sv. Filip i Jakov)

## Summary
Recorded as *Gierca Velicichia*, head of household in [[Sv-Filip-i-Jakov|Sv. Filip i Jakov]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. Her household of 3 comprised 1 woman, 2 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]

## Notes
- `birth_place` is **inferred from residence**: the census records the village where the household was enumerated in 1610, not a place of birth, and no birth record is attested. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
- *Velicichia* is the feminine form of the surname — she heads the household as a widow. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
