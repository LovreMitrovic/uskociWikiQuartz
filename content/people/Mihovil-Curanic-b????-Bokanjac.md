# Mihovil Ćuranić (fl. 1610, Bokanjac)

## Summary
Recorded as *Michiel Çuranich*, head of household in [[Bokanjac]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 8 comprised 3 men, 3 women, 2 boys. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
