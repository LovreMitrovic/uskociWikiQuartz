# Miloš Županović (1619)

Starešina of the [[Vojnic|Vlasi Vojnići]] settled in the [[Tinj]] / Županovići area after the post-Cyprus War rekolonizacija under Ferhat-beg Sokolović.

In **1619**, jointly with [[Mate-Vuksic-b????-Tinj]], negotiated with the senjski kapetan to bring **~1500 Vojnići** onto Habsburg territory; the migration ultimately did not happen (Hrabak 1982–83:391–92) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

Eponymous starešina for the village name **Županovići** that replaced (or co-existed with) **Vojnići** in the Tinj-area cadastres.
