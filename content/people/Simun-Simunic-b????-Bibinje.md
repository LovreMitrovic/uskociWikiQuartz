# Šimun Šimunić (fl. 1610, Bibinje)

## Summary
Recorded as *P. Simon Simunich*, head of household in [[Bibinje]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. Recorded with the title prefix *P.* (abbreviation, meaning uncertain — possibly a clerical title). His household of 4 comprised 2 men, 1 woman, 1 boy. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
