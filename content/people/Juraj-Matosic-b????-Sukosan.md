# Juraj Matošić (fl. 1610, Sukošan)

## Summary
Recorded as *Zorzi Matossich*, head of household in [[Sukosan|Sukošan]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 24 comprised 4 men, 6 women, 6 boys, 8 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
