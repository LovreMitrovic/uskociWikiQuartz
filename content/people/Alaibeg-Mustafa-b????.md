# Alajbeg Mustafa

Ottoman commander whose 1703 raid prompted Venetian countermeasures along the frontier; Antonio Canagetti was instructed to approach the line and advanced 200 zecchini to the Morlacchi (doc 352). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
