# Šimun Kapelan (fl. 1610, Bokanjac)

## Summary
Recorded as *P. Simon Capelano*, head of household in [[Bokanjac]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. Recorded with the title *P.* (likely *pre*, presbyter/priest); the byname *Capelano* means "chaplain" and may be an occupational designation rather than a true surname. His household of 2 comprised 1 old man, 1 woman. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
