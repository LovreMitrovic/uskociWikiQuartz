# Nikola Dalako (fl. 1756, Vrana)

## Summary
Recorded as *Nicola Dalaco*, head of a household of 2 souls in [[Vrana]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 1 campi 3 quarte 79 tavole, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
