# Franco Lilić (fl. 1610, Punta Ljupča)

## Summary
Recorded as *Franco Lilich*, head of household in [[Punta-Ljupca|Punta Ljupča]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 7 comprised 2 men, 1 woman, 3 boys, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
