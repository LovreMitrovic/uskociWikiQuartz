# Jure Beširović (fl. 1756, Polača)

## Summary
Recorded as *Giure Besirovich*, a landholder with no household enumerated in [[Polaca|Polača]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 7 campi 3 quarte, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
