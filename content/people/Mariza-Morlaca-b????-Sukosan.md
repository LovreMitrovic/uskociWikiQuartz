# Mariza Morlača (fl. 1610, Sukošan)

## Summary
Recorded as *Mariza Morlacha*, a female head of household in [[Sukosan|Sukošan]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. Her household of 3 comprised 1 woman, 2 boys. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
