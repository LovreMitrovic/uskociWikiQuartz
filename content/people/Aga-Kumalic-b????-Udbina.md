# Aga Kumalić

Udbina-area aga. Captured spring 1686 by Zaviša Janković's *četa* together with the Udbina disdar Ibrahim-aga and *spahija* Muhamed-beg, while the latter was supervising agricultural work on his estate (doc. 133, 12 April 1686) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
