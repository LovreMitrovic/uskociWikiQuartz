# Maria Bolić (wife of Lazaro Smiljanić)

*Legitima consorte* of Lazaro Smiljanić, named in his 1696 testament and pregnant that year. Distinct from her mother-in-law, also named Maria Bolić ([[Maria-Bolic-Smiljanic-b????-Zadar]]); the source does not link the two. [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
