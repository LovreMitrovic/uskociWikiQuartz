# Mate Kranjac (fl. 1686–1719, Škabrnja)

## Summary
Head of household in [[Skabrnja|Škabrnja]], recorded as **MATE KRANJAC** in the census of households and livestock 1686–1719 [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]. His household of 4 comprised the head, 1 woman and 2 girls. No livestock recorded. [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]
