# Petar Vujanić (fl. 1756, Polača)

## Summary
Recorded as *Peter Vuianich*, head of a household of 2 souls in [[Polaca|Polača]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 4 campi 93 tavole, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
