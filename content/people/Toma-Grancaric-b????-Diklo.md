# Toma Grancarić (fl. 1610, Diklo)

## Summary
Recorded as *Thomaso Granzarich*, head of household in [[Diklo]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 9 comprised 2 men, 4 women, 1 boy, 2 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
