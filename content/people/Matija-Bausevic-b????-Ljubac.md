# Matija Baušević (fl. 1610, Ljubač)

## Summary
Recorded as *Matthio Bauseuich*, head of household in [[Ljubac|Ljubač]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 2 comprised 1 boy, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
