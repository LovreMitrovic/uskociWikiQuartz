# Rade Tepšić

Lika-born Morlach, resident at Zadar after the 1685 uskakanje. In Jan 1686 sued **Ostoja Gagić** of Biljane over a debt; Gagić was imprisoned in Zadar; on 29 Jan 1686 Stojan Janković stood as *pieggio* for his release on bail (doc. 124, *Spisi Mikiel*) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].

For the Tepšić surname see [[Tepsic|Tepšić]] surname page.
