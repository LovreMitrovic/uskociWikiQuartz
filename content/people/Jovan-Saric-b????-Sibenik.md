# Jovan (Zuanne) Šarić

Son of Cvijan Šarić; his father's pay (4 ducati + peso di biscotto) was transferred to him on 1662-03-14 by provveditore Andrea Korner (doc 123). Still alive 1682-06-03, when the Doge ordered his back-pay arrears settled (doc 285). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
