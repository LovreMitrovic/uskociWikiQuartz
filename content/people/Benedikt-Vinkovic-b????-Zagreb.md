# zagrebački biskup Benedikt Vinković (1639)

Zagreb diocese. His **1639 izvješće** documents masovno prevjeravanje s pravoslavlja na katolicizam in the Krmpote, Istra, and Kras areas (Bogović 1993:107–108) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]] — concrete data point for the period before the Catholic Krmpote core of [[Lic]]/Senjsko Primorje became fully homogeneous.
