# Rade Skopelja (fl. 1756, Zemunik)

## Summary
Recorded as *Rade Scopelia*, head of a household of 7 souls in [[Zemunik]] (Albanian community) in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 21 campi 118 tavole, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
