# Rade Šiljanović

Istrian Vlach starešina; in 1549 led many peasants to former [[Vrana]] district as *zakup* on Venetian land [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Possible (but unconfirmed by Juran) continuity with medieval kraljevski Vlach katun **Šiglanovići** of Knin županija.
