# Pavao Surgatović (fl. 1610, Bokanjac)

## Summary
Recorded as *Paulo Surgatouich*, head of household in [[Bokanjac]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 11 comprised 4 men, 4 women, 2 boys, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
