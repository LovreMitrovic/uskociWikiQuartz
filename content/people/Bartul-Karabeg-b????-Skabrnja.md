# Bartul Karabeg (fl. 1686–1719, Škabrnja)

## Summary
Head of household in [[Skabrnja|Škabrnja]], recorded as **BARTUL KARABEG pok. Andrije (Jadre)** in the census of households and livestock 1686–1719 [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]. His household of 6 comprised the head, 2 men-at-arms, 2 women and 1 girl. Livestock: 2 konji (horses), 3 goveda (cattle), 1 telad (calves), 6 krave (cows), 100 ovce (sheep).
