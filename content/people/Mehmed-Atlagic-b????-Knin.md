# Mehmed Atlagić

Member of the Atlagić beg-line of [[Knin]] (see [[Atlagic]] surname page). Mentioned only obliquely in the chunk-2 documents (the *Torre dell' Atlaghich*, an Atlagić tower in the Kotari hinterland, appears in doc. 155 as a meeting point used by Stojan Janković in November 1686). His major personal documentation is in chunk 3 of this source [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
