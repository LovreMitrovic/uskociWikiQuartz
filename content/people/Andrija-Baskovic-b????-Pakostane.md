# Andrija Bašković (fl. 1610, Pakoštane)

## Summary
Recorded as *Andrea Bazcouich*, head of household in [[Pakostane|Pakoštane]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 5 comprised 2 men, 1 woman, 1 boy, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
