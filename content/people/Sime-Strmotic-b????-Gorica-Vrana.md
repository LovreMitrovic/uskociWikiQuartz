# Šime Strmotić (fl. 1756, Gorica)

## Summary
Recorded as *Sime et Ivan Stermotich*, head of a household of 3 souls in [[Gorica-Vrana|Gorica]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 6 campi 48 tavole, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
