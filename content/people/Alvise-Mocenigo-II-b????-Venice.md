# Doge Alvise Mocenigo II

Provveditor general of Dalmatia 1696–1702 (succeeding [[Daniel-Dolfin-IV-b????-Venice|Daniel Dolfin]]); subsequently became **Doge of Venice**. Signed *ducale* of 22 March 1702 (doc 342) and others (docs 345, 360, 380) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].

A **dukal of 20 August 1705 (doc 360)** granted the **hereditary count title** to Ilija, Nicolò and Stojan Janković (Mitrović).

A later dukal of 1 December 1729 removed [[Petar-Mitrovic-Stojisavljevic-b????-Vrlika|Pietro/Petar Mitrović-Stojisavljević]] and named [[Giacomo-Sinobad-b????-Posedarje|Giacomo Sinobad]] serdar of Knin (doc 380).
