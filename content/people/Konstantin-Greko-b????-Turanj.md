# Konstantin Greko (fl. 1610, Turanj)

## Summary
Recorded as *Constantin Greco*, head of household in [[Turanj]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 7 comprised 1 man, 1 woman, 3 boys, 2 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]

## Notes
The surname *Greco* ("the Greek") may be an ethnonym rather than an inherited family name. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
