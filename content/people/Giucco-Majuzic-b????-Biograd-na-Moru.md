# Giucco Majuzić (fl. 1610, Biograd na Moru)

## Summary
Recorded as *Giucco Magiuzich*, head of household in [[Biograd-na-Moru|Biograd na Moru]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 5 comprised 1 man, 2 women, 1 boy, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
