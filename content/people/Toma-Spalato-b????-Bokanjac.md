# Toma Spalato (fl. 1610, Bokanjac)

## Summary
Recorded as *Thomaso Spalato*, head of household in [[Bokanjac]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. The byname *Spalato* is the Italian name for Split and likely marks origin from there. His household of 7 comprised 2 men, 3 women, 1 boy, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
