# Martin Vranešić (fl. 1686–1719, Škabrnja)

## Summary
Head of household in [[Skabrnja|Škabrnja]], recorded as **MARTIN VRANEŠIĆ pok. Mije** in the census of households and livestock 1686–1719 [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]. His household of 5 comprised the head, 1 woman and 3 girls. Livestock: 2 konji (horses), 6 goveda (cattle), 1 telad (calves), 2 krave (cows), 50 ovce (sheep). [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]
