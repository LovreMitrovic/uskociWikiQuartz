# Elena Marunčić (fl. 1610, Turanj)

## Summary
Recorded as *Elena Maruncichia*, head of household in [[Turanj]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. Her household of 6 comprised 1 man, 3 women, 2 boys. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]

## Notes
Female head; the `-chia` surname ending is a feminine patronymic. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
