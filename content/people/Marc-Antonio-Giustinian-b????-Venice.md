# Marc'Antonio Giustinian

Doge of Venice who, by ducal of 7 November 1686 (doc 148), authorised a 500-man oltramarino regiment under Stojan Janković, with son Nikola as colonnello and son Konstantin heading an attached cavalry company. [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
