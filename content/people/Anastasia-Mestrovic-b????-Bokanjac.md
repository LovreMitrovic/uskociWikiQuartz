# Anastasia Mestrović (fl. 1610, Bokanjac)

## Summary
Recorded as *Anastasia Mestrouichia*, head of household in [[Bokanjac]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. Her household of 3 comprised 1 woman, 2 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
