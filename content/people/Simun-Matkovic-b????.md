# don Šimun Matković (1622)

Catholic missionary (Simeon Joannis Matcovich Bosnensis) — *missionarius "in patribus Serbiae, Slavoniae et Hungariae australis, Turcae subiectis"* (Fermendžin 1892:367) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

In **1622** he addressed a request to the Congregatio Propaganda Fide in Rome asking to be assigned the **parochia Bunievczi archidioecesis Colocensis** — the **first explicit written attestation of the ethnonym "Bunjevac"** in any official record [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

Notable that the petition came from a **svjetovni svećenik** rather than from the Bosanski franjevci — the Franciscans avoided the Bunjevac name (deeming it deprecative or "insufficiently Catholic") through the entire 17th and most of the 18th c.

The "parochia Bunievczi" was probably a wider community designation rather than a single naselje — barski nadbiskup's 1633 report mentions "parochia di Backa" with 400 zaselaka of "lingua illirica" speakers.
