# Dmitar (Mitar) Nikolić-Pivljanin

Brother of the hajduk **Bajo Pivljanin**; married [[Janja-Jankovic-b1655-Zadar|Janja Janković]] (sister of Stojan Janković and Ilija Mitrović) at Sv. Ilija, Zadar, on 1675-01-17 st. v. (doc 210) [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]. The Pivljanin kinship explains why he joined Ilija Mitrović's 1683–84 rebellion (Desnica's footnote on doc 376) [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]].
