# Alvise Badoer

Venetian providur of Dalmatia. Architect of the 1538 transfer of ~5,000 Vlachs from Ottoman territory ("Banadego") to Venetian Zadar contado, recognizing their military value [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].
