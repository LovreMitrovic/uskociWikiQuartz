# Jakov Arelj (fl. 1756, Nadin)

## Summary
Recorded as *Giacomo Aregl*, head of a household of 12 souls in [[Nadin]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 24 campi 156 tavole, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
