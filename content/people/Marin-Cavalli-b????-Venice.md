# Marin Cavalli

Venetian bailo at Constantinople (1560). Recommended Venice "rid itself of Morlachs" in Dalmatia even at cost of leaving land uncultivated, as their Ottoman ties created security risks [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].
