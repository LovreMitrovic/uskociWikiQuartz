# Stanko Jakov (fl. 1756, Čeranje)

## Summary
Recorded as *Stanco Giacov*, head of a household of 9 souls in [[Ceranje|Čeranje]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 18 campi 1 quarte 164 tavole, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
