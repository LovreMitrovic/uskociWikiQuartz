# Nikola Mirković (fl. 1756, Gornja Jagodnja)

## Summary
Recorded as *Nicola Mircovich e fratelli*, a landholder with no household enumerated in [[Gornja-Jagodnja|Gornja Jagodnja]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 46 campi 1 quarte 65 tavole, held *senza laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
