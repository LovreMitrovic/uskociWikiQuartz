# Ivan Matulj (fl. 1610, Turanj)

## Summary
Recorded as *Zuanne Matugl*, head of household in [[Turanj]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 8 comprised 3 men, 3 women, 1 boy, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
