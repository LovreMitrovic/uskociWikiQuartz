# Luka Samobat (fl. 1686–1719, Škabrnja)

## Summary
Head of household in [[Skabrnja|Škabrnja]], recorded as **LUKA SAMOBAT pok. Andrije (Jadre)** in the census of households and livestock 1686–1719 [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]. His household of 10 comprised the head, 2 men-at-arms, 2 boys, 3 women and 2 girls. Livestock: 7 konji (horses), 8 goveda (cattle), 5 telad (calves), 20 krave (cows), 200 ovce (sheep).
