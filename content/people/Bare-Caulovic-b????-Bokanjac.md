# Bare Ćaulović (fl. 1610, Bokanjac)

## Summary
Recorded as *Bare Çaulouich*, head of household in [[Bokanjac]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 14 comprised 1 old man, 3 men, 4 women, 3 boys, 3 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
