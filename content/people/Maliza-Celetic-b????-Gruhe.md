# Maliza Čeletić (fl. 1610, Gruhe)

## Summary
Recorded as *Maliza Celetichia*, head of household in [[Gruhe]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. Her household of 4 comprised 3 men, 1 woman. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
