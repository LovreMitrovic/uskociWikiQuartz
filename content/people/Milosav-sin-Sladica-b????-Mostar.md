# Milosav sin Sladića (1477)

Head of the [[Sladovic|Sladović]] katun in the 1477 Hercegovina defter, **nahija Mostar**, 19 houses [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

Distinctive among Bunjevac founder katuns: **organized by villages** ([[Podaca]], [[Igrane]], [[Kozica]]) rather than by transhumant pastures. Šarić: this suggests either *vlahizirano* sedentary peasants given Vlach status, or a fully *teritorijalizirani* vlach katun — a third structural type alongside the Krmpote (transhumance) and Vojnić (mixed) patterns.

Eponymous founder of the [[Sladovic]] lineage; the patronymic suffix -ić remains the canonical form, with -ović in later attestations.
