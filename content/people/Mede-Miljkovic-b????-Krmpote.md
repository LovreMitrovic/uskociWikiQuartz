# Mede(t) Miljković

"Capo di Crampote" (chief/headman of [[Krmpote-Senj|Krmpote]]) in 1696, in conflict with [[Parcic|Parčić]] villagers over [[Modrino-Selo|Modrino selo]] near St. Peter's church (Bukovica), Zadar 1696 (doc 301) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Italian rendering: *Mede Milicovich*.
