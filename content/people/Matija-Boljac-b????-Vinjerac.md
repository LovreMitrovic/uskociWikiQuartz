# Matija Boljač (Mattio Bogliach)

Villager of [[Vinjerac|Castel Venier]]. Took two *dolame* (long coats) from Mihajlo Radmilović during his flight to Venetian territory, doc 31 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
