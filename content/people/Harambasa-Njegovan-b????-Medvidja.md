# harambaša Njegovan (1647)

Krmpote starešina who led the final mass exodus of Krmpote families from Ottoman selo Krmpote (today [[Medvidja]]) during the [[Krmpote-Migration-Vinjerac-1647|1647 migration]] of the Candian War [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]] (Pavličević 1989).

Brought 40–50 Krmpote families to Venetian territory: settled primarily [[Vinjerac]] and [[Starigrad]]; some onward to [[Pag]] (later switching to Habsburg subjecthood). Crucial figure in the third and final reset of the Krmpote demographic split between Habsburg and Venetian sides of the Tromeđa.
