# ninski biskup Juraj Parčić (1692)

Ninski biskup. His 1692 izvješće documents prevjeravanje in **Posedarje, Ražanac**, and broader **"Morlakija"** (Bogović 1993) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]] — late-17th-c. data point on the confessional-shift dynamics that consolidated the Catholic Bunjevac core of the Venetian frontier.
