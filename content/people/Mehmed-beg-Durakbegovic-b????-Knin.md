# Mehmed-beg Durakbegović

*Sandžak-beg* of [[Knin]] until early 1685. Had a tower at [[Plavna|Plavno]] (where Kojadin Žepina lost his arm in 1684, doc 2). Replaced and went to [[Livno]] March 1685; ambushed; nephew Mehmed survived; favorite Hajra/Kosa Lakić captured, docs 2, 56, 57 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].

## Family
- Brother: [[Hasan-beg-Durakbegovic-b????|Hasan-beg Durakbegović]] (killed earlier at Zemunik) — father of Mehmed-nephew.
- Nephew: [[Mehmed-Durakbegovic-nephew-b????|Mehmed Durakbegović]] (son of Hasan).
