# Luka Pertović (fl. 1610, Kožino)

## Summary
Recorded as *Luca Pertouich*, head of household in [[Kozino|Kožino]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 3 comprised 2 men and 1 woman. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
