# Thomiza Hazinović (fl. 1610, Ljubač)

## Summary
Recorded as *Thomiza Hazinouich*, head of household in [[Ljubac|Ljubač]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 9 comprised 2 men, 3 women, 2 boys, 2 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
