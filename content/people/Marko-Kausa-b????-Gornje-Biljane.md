# Marko Kauša (fl. 1756, Gornje Biljane)

## Summary
Recorded as *Marco Caussa*, head of a household of 3 souls in [[Gornje-Biljane|Gornje Biljane]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 6 campi 2 quarte 184 tavole, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
