# Šimun Gracalić (fl. 1610, Sukošan)

## Summary
Recorded as *Simon Gracalich*, head of household in [[Sukosan|Sukošan]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 8 comprised 3 men, 2 women, 2 boys, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
