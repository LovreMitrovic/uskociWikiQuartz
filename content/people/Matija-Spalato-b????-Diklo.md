# Matija Spalato (fl. 1610, Diklo)

## Summary
Recorded as *Matthio Spalato*, head of household in [[Diklo]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 5 comprised 1 man, 2 women, 2 boys. The surname is a byname from *Spalato*, the Italian name for Split. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]

- `birth_place` is **inferred from residence**: the census records the village where the household was enumerated in 1610, not a place of birth, and no birth record is attested. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
