# Lovre Pajan (fl. 1610, Dračevac Zadarski)

## Summary
Recorded as *Lorenzo Pagian*, head of household in [[Dracevac-Zadarski|Dračevac Zadarski]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 4 comprised 1 old man, 1 man, 1 woman, 1 boy. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
