# Stanissaua Ugarković (fl. 1610, Ražanac)

## Summary
Recorded as *Stanissaua Vgarcouich*, head of household in [[Razanac|Ražanac]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. Her household of 3 comprised 1 man, 1 woman, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
