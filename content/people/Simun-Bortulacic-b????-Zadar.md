# Šimun (Simon) Bortulačić

Zadar patrician (Bortolazzi sub-branch q. Girolamo); *kavalier*; one of the four *capi de Morlacchi* of the Zadar territory in the early Morean War period. Brother of dr. [[Antun-Bortulacic-b????-Zadar|Antun Bortulačić]]. Already attested in [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]] (kompare at Zaviša Janković's wedding 1676; capo de Morlacchi at Vrana 1684).

## Timeline (this source: 1684–1685)
- **1684 (April)** — One of four capi over the Zadar Morlacchi; sub-region: Krmčina–Toreta–Sv. Filip i Jakov–Biograd (Zaravecchia)–Pakoštane (doc 19) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **1684-08-26** — Writes letter from Grahovo after the Glamoč–Grahovo campaign (docs 1, 5–6, 9, 10) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **1684-09** — Vrana Morlaks reject him as capo and petition for Smiljanić instead (docs 34–36) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **1684 (Oct)** — Co-leads with Alvise Marcello the failed October 1684 Sinj attempt (doc 42) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **1684-07-28** — Co-signs petition to Senate against Mocenigo's appointment of new sub-leaders (doc 22) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **1685** — Continues among the four Morlak capi (docs 27–29, 32, 42, 58, 61) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **1685-07-22** — Joint Lika raid with Janković, Posedarski, Smiljanić (docs 86, 87, 89) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **1685-07** — Wounded (doc 89) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **1686-01** — Co-accused with Janković in anonymous Lapac complaint of extortion and unlawful killing (doc 124, 126) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **1686-04** — Sent to Spalato to clarify Cornaro's orders re Lika vs Klivno (doc 93) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **1686-11** — Sent to Tinj to gather men against Bosnian pasha incursion (doc 149) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **1692-01-16** — **Murdered at [[Vrana]]** by men from Biograd and Pakoštane; aged ~31; covered with stones. Two principal perpetrators captured and condemned 1693–94 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
