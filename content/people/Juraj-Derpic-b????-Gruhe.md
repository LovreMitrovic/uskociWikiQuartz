# Juraj Derpić (fl. 1610, Gruhe)

## Summary
Recorded as *Zorzi Derpich*, head of household in [[Gruhe]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 8 comprised 3 men, 2 women, 2 boys, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
