# Seco Juričić (fl. 1610, Dračevac Zadarski)

## Summary
Recorded as *Seco Juricichia*, female head of household in [[Dracevac-Zadarski|Dračevac Zadarski]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. Her household of 6 comprised 2 women, 3 boys, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]

- `birth_place` is **inferred from residence**: the census records the village where the household was enumerated in 1610, not a place of birth, and no birth record is attested. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
