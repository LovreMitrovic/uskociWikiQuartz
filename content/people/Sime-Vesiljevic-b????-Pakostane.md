# Šime Vesiljević (fl. 1756, Pakoštane)

## Summary
Recorded as *Sime Vesiglievich*, head of a household of 5 souls in [[Pakostane|Pakoštane]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 4 campi 3 quarte 121 tavole, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
