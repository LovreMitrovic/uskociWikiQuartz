# Fra Mijo Radnić

Bunjevac, vizitator provincije Bosne Srebrene (1684). Defined the "herceštvo sv. Save" as covering the entire territory from Gacko in eastern Hercegovina to Gračac in [[Lika]] [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].

Šarić [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]] reads Radnić's "panhercegovizam" (Roksandić 1991:32) **two ways**: (1) as Roksandić frames it, a stanje svijesti formed by long-term seobe across the entire space, viewed as a unified "morlačka" cjelina; (2) as Šarić argues, also a **prostorno legitimiranje** of the bosanska provincija at the moment when Ottoman retreat made eklezijalno uređenje questions inevitable — anticipating the bosansko-dalmatinski crkveni zapletaji to come.
