# Ivan Miškić (fl. 1610, Gruhe)

## Summary
Recorded as *Zuane Mischich*, head of household in [[Gruhe]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 5 comprised 1 man, 2 women, 1 boy, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
