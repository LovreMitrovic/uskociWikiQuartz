# Elena Bosančić (fl. 1610, Punta Ljupča)

## Summary
Recorded as *Elena Bosancichia*, head of household in [[Punta-Ljupca|Punta Ljupča]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. Her household of 3 comprised 1 woman, 1 boy, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
