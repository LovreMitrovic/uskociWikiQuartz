# Tomaso (Toma) Tubić

Captain of [[Biograd-na-Moru|Zaravecchia]] (Biograd na Moru). Originally banished for participation in the **Bortolazzi murder** (~1690s); later **pardoned**. By 1704 commanded a *galeotta* serving the Inquisitori sopra dazi (doc 357) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Friend or kinsman of [[Mattio-Zabetic-b????-Zaravecchia|Mattio Žabetić]].
