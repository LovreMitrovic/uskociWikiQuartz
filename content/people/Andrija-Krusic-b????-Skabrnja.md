# Andrija Krušić (fl. 1686–1719, Škabrnja)

## Summary
Head of household in [[Skabrnja|Škabrnja]], recorded as **JADRE KRUŠIĆ pok. Mitra** in the census of households and livestock 1686–1719 [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]. His household of 2 comprised the head, 1 woman. Livestock: 1 konji (horses), 2 goveda (cattle), 2 telad (calves), 2 krave (cows), 10 ovce (sheep). [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]
