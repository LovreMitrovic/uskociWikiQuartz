# Vučen Stojisavljević

Son of *quondam* Novko Stojisavljević; from a village under [[Vrlika]]. Married [[Marija-Mitrovic-b????-Zegar|Marija Mitrović]] (daughter of [[Ilija-Mitrovic-b????-Zegar|Ilija Mitrović]]). Father of [[Petar-Mitrovic-Stojisavljevic-b????-Vrlika|Petar]], who after Vučen's death was raised in the Mitrović household and assumed the Mitrović cognomen — discovered in 1729 investigation (doc 379) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
