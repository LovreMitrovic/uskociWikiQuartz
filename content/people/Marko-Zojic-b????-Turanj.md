# Marko Zojić (fl. 1610, Turanj)

## Summary
Recorded as *Marco Zoijch*, head of household in [[Turanj]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 8 comprised 2 men, 2 women, 4 boys. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
