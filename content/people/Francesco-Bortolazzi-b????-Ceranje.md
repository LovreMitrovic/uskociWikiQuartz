# Francesco Bortolazzi (fl. 1756, Čeranje)

## Summary
Recorded as *Francesco e Simon Bortolazzi*, a landholder with no household enumerated in [[Ceranje|Čeranje]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 184 campi 2 quarte, held *senza laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
