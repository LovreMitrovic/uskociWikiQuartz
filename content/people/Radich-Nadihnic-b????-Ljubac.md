# Radich Nadihnić (fl. 1610, Ljubač)

## Summary
Recorded as *Radich Nadihnich*, head of household in [[Ljubac|Ljubač]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 11 comprised 5 men, 3 women, 3 boys. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
