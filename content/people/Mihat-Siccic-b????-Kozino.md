# Mihat Sičić (fl. 1610, Kožino)

## Summary
Recorded as *Mihat Siccich*, head of household in [[Kozino|Kožino]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 6 comprised 2 men, 3 women and 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
