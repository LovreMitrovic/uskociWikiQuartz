# Marko Ladić (fl. 1610, Gruhe)

## Summary
Recorded as *Marco Ladich*, head of household in [[Gruhe]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 7 comprised 2 men, 3 women, 1 boy, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
