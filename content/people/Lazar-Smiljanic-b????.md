# Lazar Smiljanić

Serdar of Lower Kotari; father of the minor Marko Smiljanić. After Lazar's death, provveditor general Vincenzo Vendramin named Antonio Vujanović provisional serdar (1709–1711). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
