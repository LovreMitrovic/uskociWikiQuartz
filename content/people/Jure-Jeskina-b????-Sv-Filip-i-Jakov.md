# Jure Ješkina (fl. 1756, Sv. Filip i Jakov)

## Summary
Recorded as *Giure o sia Ive Gieschina*, head of a household of 10 souls in [[Sv-Filip-i-Jakov|Sv. Filip i Jakov]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 26 campi 3 quarte 151 tavole, held *con laudemio*.
