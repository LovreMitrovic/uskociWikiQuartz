# Nikola Žila (fl. 1610, Bartulac)

## Summary
Recorded as *Nicolò Xilla*, head of household in [[Bartulac]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 5 comprised 4 women, 1 boy. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
