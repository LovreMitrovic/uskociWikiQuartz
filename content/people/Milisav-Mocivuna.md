# Milisav Močivuna

## Summary
Orthodox Morlach notable ([[Movicuna]]). On 1671-06-21, together with Smoljan Đerverščović ([[Djervescovic]]) and Luka Kožulović, he represented the *pravoslavni Morlaci* in the dispute over the church of Sv. Ivan in Spianata (doc. 193) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
