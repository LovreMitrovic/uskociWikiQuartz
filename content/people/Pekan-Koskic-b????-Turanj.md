# Pekan Koškić (fl. 1610, Turanj)

## Summary
Recorded as *Peccan Choschich*, head of household in [[Turanj]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 8 comprised 2 men, 1 woman, 2 boys, 3 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
