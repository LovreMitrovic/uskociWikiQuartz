# Grubiša Rudić (fl. 1756, Gornja Jagodnja)

## Summary
Recorded as *Grubissa Rudich*, head of a household of 6 souls in [[Gornja-Jagodnja|Gornja Jagodnja]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 11 campi, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
