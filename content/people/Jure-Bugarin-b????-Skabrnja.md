# Jure Bugarin (fl. 1686–1719, Škabrnja)

## Summary
Head of household in [[Skabrnja|Škabrnja]], recorded as **JURE BUGARIN (Bugarija?)** in the census of households and livestock 1686–1719 [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]. His household of 3 comprised the head, 1 boy and 1 woman. Livestock: 2 konji (horses), 2 goveda (cattle), 2 telad (calves), 2 krave (cows), 15 ovce (sheep). [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]
