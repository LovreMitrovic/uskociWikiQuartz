# Radan Milatović (fl. 1610, Turanj)

## Summary
Recorded as *Radan Milatouich*, head of household in [[Turanj]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 9 comprised 3 men, 1 woman, 1 boy, 4 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
