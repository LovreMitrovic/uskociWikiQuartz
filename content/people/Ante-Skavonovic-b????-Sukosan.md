# Ante Škavonović (fl. 1610, Sukošan)

## Summary
Recorded as *Antonio Schiauonouich*, head of household in [[Sukosan|Sukošan]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 5 comprised 2 men, 1 woman, 1 boy, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
