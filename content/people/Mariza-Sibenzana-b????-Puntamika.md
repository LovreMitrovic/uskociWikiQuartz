# Mariza Sibenzana (fl. 1610, Puntamika)

## Summary
Recorded as *Mariza Sibenzana*, head of household in [[Puntamika]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. Her household of 4 comprised 1 man, 2 women, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
