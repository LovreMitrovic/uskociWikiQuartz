# Doge Francesco Morosini

Doge of Venice during much of the Morean War. *Ducale* of **21 May 1690** awarding 10 ducats/month to capt. Marko Smiljanić for service in the Morean campaigns [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 233). *Ducale* of **22 October 1692** confirming Nicolò Lomazzi as Illyrian interpreter at Zadar [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (doc 257).
