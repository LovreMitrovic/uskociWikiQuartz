# Vid Gospić (fl. 1686–1719, Škabrnja)

## Summary
Head of household in [[Skabrnja|Škabrnja]], recorded as **VID GOSPIĆ pok. Stjepana** in the census of households and livestock 1686–1719 [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]. His household of 7 comprised the head, 2 men-at-arms, 2 boys and 2 women. Livestock: 2 konji (horses), 1 goveda (cattle), 4 telad (calves), 4 krave (cows), 20 ovce (sheep).
