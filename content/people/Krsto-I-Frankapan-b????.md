# Krsto I. Frankapan

Croatian magnate. Lost his estates in Istria, Kras, and Notranjska in spring 1527 after siding with Ivan Zápolya against Ferdinand Habsburg in the contested royal election [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. The dispossession of his Vlach subjects was a possible trigger for the 1528 return migration of [[Vlasi-Istre]] to Ottoman territory.
