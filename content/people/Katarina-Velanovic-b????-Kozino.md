# Katarina Velanović (fl. 1610, Kožino)

## Summary
Recorded as *Cattarina Vellanouichia*, head of household in [[Kozino|Kožino]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. Her household of 2 comprised 2 women. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
