# Šimun Mesnić (fl. 1610, Bokanjac)

## Summary
Recorded as *Simon Mesnich*, head of household in [[Bokanjac]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 11 comprised 3 men, 3 women, 3 boys, 2 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
