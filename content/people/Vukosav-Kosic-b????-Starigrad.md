# Vukosav Kosić

Lika-born Morlach who *uskočio* in 1684 and settled at **[[Starigrad]]** under Velebit. Carried Valier's and Janković's letters to Habsburg general Herberstein (camp at Jasenica) in summer 1685; on his return interrogated 8 Aug 1685 in Zadar, gave detailed information on the imperial army's strength and on conditions in Ottoman Lika — biades and orzi abundant but not yet harvested, fortifications wood-trinciere takeable without cannon, no more than 1,000 fighting men in the cities, all "impauriti et avvilliti" (doc. 92, attached to Valier's dispatch of 13 Aug 1685) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
