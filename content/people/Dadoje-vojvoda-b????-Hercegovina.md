# vojvoda Dadoje

Vlach voivode in the 1477 Hercegovina defter for **nahija Humska zemlja**. Head of a knežinska aglomeracija analogous to vojvoda [[Vladimir-vojvoda-b????-Hercegovina]] in nahija Mostar. The Krmpote katun ([[Vukic-Krmpotic-b????-Hercegovina]]) was part of his domain area [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

The 2024 article ([[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]) names him as **Radoje** ("Dadoj"); the same person, distinguishing variants in Cyrillic/Ottoman transcription.
