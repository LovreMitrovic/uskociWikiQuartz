# Ilija Dukić (fl. 1756, Gornje Biljane)

## Summary
Recorded as *Illia Duchich*, head of a household of 8 souls in [[Gornje-Biljane|Gornje Biljane]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 16 campi 183 tavole, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
