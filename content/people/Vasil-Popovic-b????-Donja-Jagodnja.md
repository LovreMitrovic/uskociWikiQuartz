# Vasil Popović (fl. 1756, Donja Jagodnja)

## Summary
Recorded as *Vassil Popovich*, head of a household of 13 souls in [[Donja-Jagodnja|Donja Jagodnja]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 1 campi, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
