# Vid Nadahnić (fl. 1610, Sukošan)

## Summary
Recorded as *Vido Nadahnich*, head of household in [[Sukosan|Sukošan]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 6 comprised 2 men, 3 women, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
