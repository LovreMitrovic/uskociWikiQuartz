# vojvoda Petar Hrabren

Notable vlach voivode of the **Donji Vlasi** in [[Hercegovina]], **ktitor** of several Hercegovinian Orthodox monasteries [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

The 1477 defter for nahija Blagaj records his katun's knežinska aglomeracija — Šarić uses this as evidence that the Vlach population around the [[Buna]] river in the 15th c. was **predominantly Orthodox**, not Bunjevac. This is one strand of the argument against the Buna-river origin legend (which would require a Catholic Vlach population east of the Neretva that the records do not support).

Among the 36 vlach džemats popisanih in nahija Blagaj 1477, none can be confidently identified as Bunjevac (Aličić 1985).
