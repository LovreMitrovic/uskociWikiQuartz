# Nikola Kosovac (fl. 1610, Dračevac Zadarski)

## Summary
Recorded as *Nicolò Chossouaz*, head of household in [[Dracevac-Zadarski|Dračevac Zadarski]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 7 comprised 3 men, 3 women, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]

- `birth_place` is **inferred from residence**: the census records the village where the household was enumerated in 1610, not a place of birth, and no birth record is attested. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
