# Petar Vladušić (fl. 1610, Ljubač)

## Summary
Recorded as *Piero Vladussich*, head of household in [[Ljubac|Ljubač]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 10 comprised 4 men, 3 women, 3 boys. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
