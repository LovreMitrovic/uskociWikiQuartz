# Frane (Francesco) Posedarski the Younger

Son of [[Zorzi-Posedarski-d1679-Zadar|Žorži Posedarski]] and *nipote* of [[Frano-Posedarski-1648-Zadar|Frano the Elder]] (doc. 232) [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]. Governator of [[Nin]] from 1672-03-20 (doc. 202); one of the four 1684 *direttori* over the Morlachi (doc. 395) [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]. Named colonnello sopraintendente of Kotari in 1691 (doc. 235) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
