# Gaetano Pasini (fl. 1756, Raštane)

## Summary
Recorded as *Gaetano Pasini*, a landholder with no household enumerated in [[Rastane|Raštane]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 48 campi 66 tavole, held *senza laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
