# Mate, sin pok. Pere Milkovića

Ottoman-subject Vlach from Kruševo near Obrovac, recorded as a debtor (to a Šibenik butcher) in 1554 [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Šarić identifies the Milkovići as a Bunjevac starešina family from [[Medvidja]].
