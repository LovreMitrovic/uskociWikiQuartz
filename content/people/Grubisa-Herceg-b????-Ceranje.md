# Grubiša Herceg (fl. 1756, Čeranje)

## Summary
Recorded as *Grubissa Erceg*, head of a household of 10 souls in [[Ceranje|Čeranje]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 20 campi 160 tavole, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
