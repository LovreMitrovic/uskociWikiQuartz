# Luka Jadresić (fl. 1610, Bibinje)

## Summary
Recorded as *Luca Jadressich*, head of household in [[Bibinje]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 8 comprised 1 man, 1 woman, 4 boys, 2 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
