# Mihovil Radasinović (fl. 1610, Biograd na Moru)

## Summary
Recorded as *Michiel Radassinouich*, head of household in [[Biograd-na-Moru|Biograd na Moru]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 6 comprised 1 man, 1 woman, 2 boys, 2 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
