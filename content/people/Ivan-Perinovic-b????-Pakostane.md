# Ivan Perinović (fl. 1610, Pakoštane)

## Summary
Recorded as *Zuane Pehrinouich*, head of household in [[Pakostane|Pakoštane]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 7 comprised 2 men, 2 women, 1 boy, 2 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
