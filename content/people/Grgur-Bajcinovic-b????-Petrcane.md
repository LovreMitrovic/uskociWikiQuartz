# Grgur Bajčinović (fl. 1610, Petrčane)

## Summary
Recorded as *Gregorio Baijcinouich*, head of household in [[Petrcane|Petrčane]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 4 comprised 1 man, 1 woman, 2 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
