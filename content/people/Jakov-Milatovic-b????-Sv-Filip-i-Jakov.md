# Jakov Milatović (fl. 1756, Sv. Filip i Jakov)

## Summary
Recorded as *Giacomo Milatovich*, head of a household of 10 souls in [[Sv-Filip-i-Jakov|Sv. Filip i Jakov]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 19 campi 3 quarte 152 tavole, held *con laudemio*.
