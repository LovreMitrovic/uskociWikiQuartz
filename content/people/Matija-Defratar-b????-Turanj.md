# Matija Defratar (fl. 1610, Turanj)

## Summary
Recorded as *Matthio Defratar*, head of household in [[Turanj]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 9 comprised 3 men, 2 women, 3 boys, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
