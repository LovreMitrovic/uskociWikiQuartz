# Petar Margitić (fl. 1610, Bibinje)

## Summary
Recorded as *Pietro Marghitich*, head of household in [[Bibinje]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 9 comprised 4 men, 2 women, 2 boys, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]

## Notes
Filename suffixed `-2` to distinguish from a second head recorded as *Piero Marghitich* ([[Petar-Margitic-b????-Bibinje]]), who yields the same croatized name.
