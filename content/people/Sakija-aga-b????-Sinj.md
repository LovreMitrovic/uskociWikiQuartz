# Šakija-aga

Sinj aga, fellow prisoner with [[Hasan-aga-Mandic-b????-Sinj|Hasan-aga Mandić]] in the Zadar captain's prison from autumn 1685.

## Timeline (this source)
- **Autumn 1685, undated** (doc. 113) — Writes to "fratello mio caro" Šain-aga Mandić from prison, complaining of the stench, asking news of Sinj, of the *effendi* and of his sister; warns Šain-aga that the Venetians have spies even in **Sarajevo** ("ne tengono tante che loro sanno cosa si fa in fino al Saragglio"). Letters carried by the ransom-cycle messenger *Janco Jankovich* (a different Janković, possibly an intermediary) and *conte Jure*. Letter written in Italian, in *Spisi P. Valier*, knj. I, str. 314 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
