# Juraj Ratajić (fl. 1610, Bokanjac)

## Summary
Recorded as *Zorzi Rataijch*, head of household in [[Bokanjac]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 6 comprised 1 old man, 1 man, 2 women, 1 boy, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
