# Luka Račić (fl. 1610, Gruhe)

## Summary
Recorded as *Luca Racich*, head of household in [[Gruhe]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 7 comprised 1 man, 3 women, 1 boy, 2 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
