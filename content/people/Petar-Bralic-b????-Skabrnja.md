# Petar Bralić (fl. 1686–1719, Škabrnja)

## Summary
Head of household in [[Skabrnja|Škabrnja]], recorded as **PETAR BRALIĆ pok. Marka** in the census of households and livestock 1686–1719 [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]. His household of 6 comprised the head, 2 men-at-arms, 1 boy and 2 women. Livestock: 2 konji (horses), 3 goveda (cattle), 4 telad (calves), 5 krave (cows), 10 ovce (sheep).
