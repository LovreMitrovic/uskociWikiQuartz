# Petar Merčić (fl. 1610, Bokanjac)

## Summary
Recorded as *Piero Merchich*, head of household in [[Bokanjac]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 8 comprised 1 man, 4 women, 3 boys. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
