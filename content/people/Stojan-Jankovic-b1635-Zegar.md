# Stojan Janković (c. 1635 – ?1687)

The most decorated *kotarski uskok* of his generation. Son of [[Janko-Mitrovic-b1605-Zegar]]. Born in **Žegar** (Ottoman zaleđe), came over with his father's *uskakanje* in the late 1640s and lived in [[Posedarje]]. Captured in slavery by Ottomans 1666–1667 (14 months in Constantinople); released; named *kavalier* of the Collegio of San Marco 1670; *capo de Morlacchi* 1681. The dispatches of Foscolo, Foscarini, Dolfin, Cornaro, Priuli, Valier, Donato, and the dukal of Marc' Antonio Giustinian (1684) document a near-continuous half-century of military service.

## Timeline (events documented in this source — 1646 to 1684 only)
- **c. 1635** — Born in Žegar (estimated; not directly attested. Father's age in 1649 doc. 49 suggests father was born c. 1614 → son c. 1635 is the conventional date).
- **1650-02-01** — As "Stojan, figlio di harambassa Gianco Mitrouich, in età tenera esistente," granted 4 ducati/month (doc. 52). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1652-11-10** — peso di biscotto added to pay (doc. 65). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1653-08-01** — Pay raised to 6 ducati/month "seben in tenera età esistente" (doc. 73). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1666-04-05** — Captured by Turks at the **Cetina rotta** (battle near Obrovac na Cetini): "harambassa di Possidaria" missing, "il figliolo del Giancovich"; Cornaro's letter from Split to Senate (doc. 148). 14 months as slave in Constantinople. [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1666-05-06** — During his captivity, the Posedarje Morlachi unanimously elect [[Pavle-Vukcevic-b????-Posedarje|Pavle Vukčević]] as their vice-harambaša (doc. 152). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1668-12-09** — Free again. Together with serdar [[Smoljan-Smiljanic-b????-Bukovica|Smoljan Smiljanić]] and governator [[Giovanni-Battista-Soppe-b????-Zadar|Giovan Battista Soppe]], he ambushed and crushed a 120-strong Turkish raiding party at the **Bribir torrent** — only 15 Turks escaped, all the rest killed (doc. 163, Priuli to Senate). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1669-01-02** — Senate approves Priuli's distribution of golden rings to Soppe, Smiljanić and Mitrović (doc. 165). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1669-11-25** — Priuli's *attestato* recapitulates his *azioni*: in person took the head of Reep-aga Filipović in Kotari, 70 heads + 30 schiavi at Cetina, 9 heads + 7 schiavi at Udbina, beheaded aga Velagić at Otres, beheaded aga Pajalitović at Kosovo polje (Kosulovo polje), 30 schiavi + 1 head at Gračac, copious herds at Rudopolje (doc. 168). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1670-01-15** — Stojan's supplica to the Doge (doc. 170): names his uncles (the five Mitrović brothers) and recapitulates his deeds; requests *cavaliere* status. [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1670-03-13** — Senate makes him **kavalier nel collegio di San Marco**, golden chain + medaglia + dolama (doc. 171). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1670-08-10** — Provveditore Barbaro grants him the houses of Jusuf-aga Tunić in **Islam** + 400 *gognali* of land (doc. 173 — original investitura in *Arhiv B. Desnice u Obrovcu*). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1674-04-23** — Birth of son **Zorzi-Alvise**, baptised Sv. Ilija Zadar by sacromonaco Athanasio Troilo. Mother *donna Vinca* (his first wife). Compare: signor Battista Sopino. (Doc. 207). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1675-01-17** (st. v.) — Sister **[[Janja-Jankovic-b1655-Zadar|Janja Janković]]** marries [[Dmitar-Nikolic-b????-Plav|Dimitri Nikolić]] (Bajo Pivljanin's brother) at Sv. Ilija (doc. 210). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1675-08-24** — Senate orders Janko (= one of his brothers, perhaps Zaviša or Ilija; the patronymic confused the chancery) and his son Nikola enrolled in cavalry (doc. 215). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1676-08-16** (st. v.) — Wife Vinka/Vuka having died, marries **Antonia Reci** (Greek of Latin rite of Zadar), at Sv. Ilija (doc. 228). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1677-09-30** (st. v.) — Birth of son **Josip Janković** (mother Antonia) baptised Sv. Ilija (doc. 235). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1679-02-09** — His son **Konstantin** named *capitano* of the company of 50 fanti raised by Stojan (doc. 255). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1681-06-26** — Senate names him **capo di tutti i Morlacchi** with 25 ducati/month, replacing the deceased Zorzi Posedarski (doc. 275 — original ducal on parchment in *Arhiv B. Desnice u Obrovcu*). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1683-09-17** — Provveditore Donà *interns* Stojan and his brother **Zaviša** to Venice "per degni rispetti" (doc. 315). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1683-12-09** — Returns from Venice; immediately deployed by Donà to suppress the Vrana uprising; intercepts the senjska četa under Vukanović and his brother Ilija at Kruševo (doc. 348). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1684-02-08** — Pay raised to 40 ducati/month (doc. 369, ducal of Doge Marc'Antonio Giustinian). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1684-03-01** — Donà appoints him *sopraintendente* of the upper-Kotari villages (Ljuba, Ražanac, Radovin, Castel Venier, Posedarje, Budin, Bagnj, Starigrad) — doc. 381. [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]

## Family
- Father: [[Janko-Mitrovic-b1605-Zegar]] (d. 1659).
- Brothers: [[Zavisa-Jankovic-b????-Zegar|Zaviša/Sabba]] (m. Maria Lase 1676-08-17, doc. 229); [[Ilija-Mitrovic-b????-Zegar|Ilija Mitrović]] (the bandit).
- Sister: [[Janja-Jankovic-b1655-Zadar|Janja]] (d. 1680-01-09 by fall, broke neck — Desnica's footnote on doc. 211; Janja was 25, so b. ~1655; m. Dimitri Nikolić-Pivljanin).
- Wife 1: **Vinka** (also documented as **Vuka**, doc. 211 fn.). Acted as *kuma* at the baptism of Bojana Novaković 1672-11-28 (doc. 203). Last attested (alive) Feb 1675. Children with V: Zorzi-Alvise (1674), Anastazija (1675), Nikola.
- Wife 2: **Antonia Reci** (m. 1676-08-16, doc. 228). The Reci/Rezzi were Greci of Latin rite resident in Zadar (Desnica's footnote). She was the daughter of donna Nicolotta and the *q. Apostolo Rezzi*. Children with A: Josip (1677), Konstantin (named capitan 1679 in tenera età).

## Notes
- His pay-roll, original *terminacije* on parchment, and the family genealogical *Trassunto* survived in **Boško Desnica's family archive in Obrovac** — Desnica is Stojan's lateral descendant.
- The **legend** of "Stojan Janković" in epic poetry collapses Janko (father, d. 1659), Stojan (son, d. 1687), and the brigand Ilija into a single figure. The dispatches separate them clearly; on the *kotarska epopeja* Desnica preface §I treats this conflation explicitly.
