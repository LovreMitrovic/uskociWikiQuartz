# Martin Janković (fl. 1686–1719, Škabrnja)

## Summary
Head of household in [[Skabrnja|Škabrnja]], recorded as **MARTIN JANKOVIĆ pok. Cvitka detto Bauer** in the census of households and livestock 1686–1719 [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]. His household of 2 comprised the head, 1 woman. Livestock: 1 konji (horses), 5 goveda (cattle), 2 telad (calves), 4 krave (cows). [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]

## Family
- Father: [[Cvitko-Jankovic-b????|Cvitko Janković]] (deceased by census)

## Notes
- Byname *detto Bauer*. No known connection to the Janković-Mitrović uskok clan (see [[Mitrovic|Mitrović]]).
