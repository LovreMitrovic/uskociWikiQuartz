# Andrija Frletić ("Andre kapetan")

Son of a [[Dracevac]] Muslim convert; among the most famous Senj uskok captains of the late 16th c., remembered in epic tradition as "Andre kapetan" [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Brother of Vuk.

## Timeline
- **1528–1530 (lineage)** — The family's likely forebears appear in the defter at the village of Tornik (nahija Podgorje) as the sons of Firultić — Pavko, Vojo, Juraj and Milovan — a name behind which the Frletići probably stand. *"U popisu 1528.–1530. u selu Tornik u nahiji Podgorje spominju se Pavko, Vojo, Juraj i Milovan kao sinovi Firultića. Iza toga prezimena vjerojatno se kriju Frletići ili Frlete."* [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]
- **1554** — The surname appears in Venetian records as newcomers "from Croatia". *"1554. Ferletich quondam Culina de Croatia."* [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]
- **1590** — Hasan-aga Frletić of [[Dracevac]] near Jasenice named as a Venetian border informant on the Novigrad Sea; Andrija's father lived there as a Muslim. *"Hasan-aga Frletić iz Dračevca kod Jasenica spominje se 1590. kao mletački doušnik s granice na Novigradskome moru, a u istome mjestu kao musliman živio je i otac uskočkoga vođe Andrije Frletića."* [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]
- **late 16th c.** — The brothers Andrija and Vuk Frletić ended up among the Senj uskoks; Andrija became the best-known of the Istrian-Morlach uskoks, the "Andre kapetan" of epic tradition. *"Krajem 16. stoljeća braća Andrija i Vuk Frletić završila su među uskocima u Senju"*; *"najpoznatiji bio spomenuti Andrija Frletić (Andre kapetan epske tradicije)."* [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]
