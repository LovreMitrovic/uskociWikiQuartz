# Rade Rkać

Lika-born Morlach, of *fede vecchia*. Companion of [[Jovan-Sudarovic-b????-Lika|Jovan Sudarović]]; jointly interrogated in Split 23 Nov 1685 (doc. 114) on the Habsburg-Venetian competition for the Lika *uskoci*'s settlement at Karlobag vs. Žegar. The original document renders his name as *Rado Grego* — Desnica's footnote notes that *Grego* in this register is a pejorative for *pravoslavni*, equivalent to *rkać*. **Rkać** is therefore the more authentic ethnonym/surname [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
