# Marko Kukrović (fl. 1610, Bartulac)

## Summary
Recorded as *Marco Cucrouich*, head of household in [[Bartulac]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 6 comprised 3 men, 3 women. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
