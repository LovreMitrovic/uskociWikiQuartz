# Gašpar Maurić

Pazinski kapetan in 1571. Wrote 4 September 1571 to the Kranjska vicedom blaming his predecessor Jakob for the bloody clash that drove many Ćići to flee back to Ottoman territory after the [[Pazin-Peasant-Revolt-1570]] [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].
