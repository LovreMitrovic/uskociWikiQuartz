# Stjepan Tomažetović (fl. 1610, Ljubač)

## Summary
Recorded as *Steffano Thomazetouich*, head of household in [[Ljubac|Ljubač]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 10 comprised 4 men, 3 women, 1 boy, 2 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
