# Kadia Jurjević (fl. 1610, Posedarje)

## Summary
Recorded as *Kadia Jurgieuich*, head of household in [[Posedarje]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 7 comprised 2 men, 3 women, 1 boy, and 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
