# Ivan Filipini

Led 40 families from Miljacki (Zemunik Gornji) back to Venetian Istria after Ottoman conquest, settling them in [[Zbandalj]] near [[Porec]] in 1570 [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].
