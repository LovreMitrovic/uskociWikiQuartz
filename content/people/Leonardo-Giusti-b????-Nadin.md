# Leonardo Giusti (fl. 1756, Nadin)

## Summary
Recorded as *Leonardo e fratelli Giusti*, a landholder with no household enumerated in [[Nadin]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 21 campi, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
