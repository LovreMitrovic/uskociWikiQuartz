# Luka Medoš (fl. 1756, Korlat)

## Summary
Recorded as *Luca Medos*, head of a household of 6 souls in [[Korlat]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 12 campi 1 quarte 62 tavole *con laudemio* and 2 campi *senza laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
