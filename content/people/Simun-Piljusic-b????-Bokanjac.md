# Šimun Piljusić (fl. 1610, Bokanjac)

## Summary
Recorded as *Simon Pigliussich*, head of household in [[Bokanjac]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 6 comprised 1 man, 1 woman, 2 boys, 2 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
