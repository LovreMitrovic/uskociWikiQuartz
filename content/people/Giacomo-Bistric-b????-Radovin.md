# Giacomo Bistrić

Villager of Radovin implicated (with Ivan Brkljača) in the theft of goods from the fleeing Morlach Mihajlo Radmilović; ordered restored by Mocenigo (doc 31, 1684). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
