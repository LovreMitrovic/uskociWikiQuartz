# Vladimir, vojvoda

Vlach vojvoda west of the Neretva who concluded an *istimâlet* contract in 1477 with [[Hamza-beg-b????]] and [[Sinan-beg-b????-Mostar]], returning Vlachs to Mostar/Hum nahija (Blato, Brotnja, Veljaci, Imotski, Radobilja) — received baštine and mlinove around Mostar, Ljubuški, Imotski [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Together with vojvoda Radoje / [[Dadoje-vojvoda-b????-Hercegovina]] in nahija Humska zemlja, head of the knežinska aglomeracija that included the Vojnić katun ([[Vukasin-sin-Vojihne-b????-Mostar]]) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].
