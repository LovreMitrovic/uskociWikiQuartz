# Ivan Antunović (1815–1888)

"Ugarski Bunjevac" — naslovni biskup, central figure of the 19th-c. **Bunjevac narodni preporod** in [[Backa]] [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

## Works
- **1870** — launches **Bunjevačke i šokačke novine** in [[Kaloca]] (parallel to [[Boza-Sarcevic-b????-Subotica]]'s *Bunjevački kalendar* 1868 in [[Subotica]]).
- **1882** — publishes in Vienna *Razprava o podunavskih i potisanskih Bunjevcih i Šokcih u pogledu narodnom, vjerskom, umnom, građanskom i gospodarskom* — the foundational text reaffirming bunjevaštvo as a distinct individuality within Strossmayer's jugoslavenstvo idea. The poticaj for [[Ivan-Murgic-b????-Perusic]]'s 1882 article in Vienac came from Antunović.

Antunović's bačka project ran in the **opposite direction** from the contemporary Lika trajectory: while Lika Bunjevci absorbed into modern Hrvatstvo, Bačka inteligencija was **reaffirming** the Bunjevac name as a defense against Hungarian magyarization [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

## Note on identity
Antunović himself wrote that as late as 1848 he openly used the **Rac** name in private and public life — *"ime Rac ni u privatnu ni u javnu životu nikad nisam zastidio"* — documenting the 19th-c. dvojna identifikacija Rac–Bunjevac in Bačka.
