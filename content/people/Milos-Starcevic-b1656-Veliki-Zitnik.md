# porkulab Miloš Starčević (1656 – ?)

Bunjevac settler at [[Pazariste]] in 1690 (during the [[Lika-Bunjevac-Colonization-1690]]). Founder of the Lika branch of the [[Starcevic|Starčević]] lineage [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

## 1712 popis (Kaser 2003:343)
- Held **168 štrikova zemlje** in [[Pazariste]], [[Vaganac]], [[Veliki-Zitnik]] ("pod kućom" + Vaganac + Žitnik + "Brizah" + sve počasbine).
- Age c. 56 (born c. 1656).
- Son **[[Jakov-Starcevic-b????-Pazariste|Jakov]]** then 20.
- 5 other family members in the household.

## 1702 — Ribnička buna
Conflict with vojvoda [[Marko-Butorac-b????-Lika]] sparked the [[Ribnicka-Buna-1702|Ribnička / Bunjevačka buna]]. Šarić uses Miloš's biography (Bunjevac at issuance, Lika porkulab in role) as paradigmatic of how, in komorske vlasti and Catholic Church eyes, settlers like him were *catholic Vlach or Bunjevac* — clearly distinct from the contemporaneous Hrvati settling Lika [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].
