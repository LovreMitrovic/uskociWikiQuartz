# Modre (il Turco Modre)

The original Ottoman owner ("*un Turco detto Modre*") of the casale at [[Modro-selo|Modro]] village near St. Peter's church in [[Bukovica]], pre-1692. Described as a *congiunto* (kinsman) of [[Jovan-Sinobad-b????-Posedarje|Sinobad]] (doc 302) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. The toponym **Modrino selo** likely derives from him.
