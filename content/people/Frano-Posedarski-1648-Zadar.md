# Frano Posedarski the Elder (c. 1601 – 1671)

Croatian noble of the [[Posedarski]] kindred of [[Posedarje]]; *governator Possedaria*, later colonnello of the *cernide*, and Foscolo's principal Venetian-side collaborator on Morlach reception from 1647 [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]. Died 1671-01-06 at [[Zadar]], age ~70 (doc. 185), buried at Sv. Domenico; lost two nephews at the 1647 Diklo/Bokanjac ambush (doc. 13) [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]].
