# Ivan Bajčinović (fl. 1610, Ljubač)

## Summary
Recorded as *Zuane Baijcinouich*, head of household in [[Ljubac|Ljubač]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 6 comprised 2 men, 2 women, 1 boy, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]

- `birth_place` is **inferred from residence**: the census records the village where the household was enumerated in 1610, not a place of birth, and no birth record is attested. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
