# Ilija Peraica

"Famoso Peraizza"; previously suspect (had served the Turks). In March 1685 led 600 armed men plus families, 30,000 small animals, and 10,000 cattle from the [[Cetina]] region to Venetian-held [[Trogir]] territory; awarded 10 ducats/month and made *sardar* over ~1,600 people, doc 59 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Also referenced docs 60, 61, 66.

## Timeline
- **1685-03** — Defects with ~1,600 souls from Cetina to Trogir territory; awarded 10 ducats/month; made sardar (docs 59, 66) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **1685-04-07** — Mentioned in connection with Sinj battle aftermath (docs 60, 61) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
