# Ilija Peraica

"Famoso Peraizza"; previously suspect (had served the Turks). In March 1685 led 600 armed men plus families, 30,000 small animals, and 10,000 cattle from the [[Cetina]] region to Venetian-held [[Trogir]] territory; awarded 10 ducats/month and made *sardar* over ~1,600 people, doc 59 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Also referenced docs 60, 61, 66.

## Timeline
- **1685-03** — Defects with ~1,600 souls from Cetina to Trogir territory; awarded 10 ducats/month; made sardar (docs 59, 66) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **1685-04-07** — Mentioned in connection with Sinj battle aftermath (docs 60, 61) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **1685 (between Aug and Oct)** — **Killed by Turkish corsair fusta off [[Rogoznica]]** while transporting his family to Zadar; 175 of his people captured (docs 104, 112). His pension transferred to brother [[Frane-Peraica-b????-Zegar|Frane Peraica]] (terminacija of Valier 1 Oct 1685) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].

## Disambiguation
Ilija Peraica is **distinct from [[Ilija-Mitrovic-b????-Zegar|Ilija Mitrović of Žegar]]** — the brother of Stojan and Zaviša Janković — who survived to die of natural causes in 1693 (per chunk-3 attestation). One of the chunk-1 agent extractions conflated the two; this wiki treats them as separate persons until further evidence emerges. The Peraica clan operated out of the Cetina valley; the Mitrovići of Žegar were Bukovica-origin Janković kin. See also [[Cetina-Migration-Peraica-1685]].
