# Šimun Ćacinović (fl. 1610, Glupavac)

## Summary
Recorded as *Simon Çacinouich*, head of household in [[Glupavac]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 9 comprised 3 men, 4 women, 2 boys. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
