# Ilija Mitrović (the bandit)

Brother of [[Stojan-Jankovic-b1635-Zegar|Stojan Janković]]. Eponymous figure in the Vrana uprising 1683–84 — Provveditore Donà repeatedly calls him *"sempre la pietra di scandalo a questo confine et principal autore delle confusioni"* (doc. 365).

## Timeline
- **1680-03-30** — Banished with capital sentence by provveditore Valier; ignored the bando (doc. 314 cites this earlier sentenza). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1683-09-13** — Donà reissues the bando: "ricerca, capi, giudici e habitanti delle ville, che capitando in alcuna delle suddette ville, debbano fermarlo vivo o morto" (doc. 314). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1683-10-22** — Joined the Senjski raid on the Vrana–Kosulovo polje area; described as "fratello del kavalier Gianco" (doc. 322). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1683-12-02** — Leads 300 men into Ottoman territory to raid Morlachi sudditi (doc. 343 — gov. Alberti to Donà). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1683-12-07** — With Vukanović and 120 Senjani depredates **Žegar** Morlachi (their own home village!) — 700 cavalli/manzi, 1 Senjanin and 5 Žegarci killed (docs. 345, 348). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1683-12-07** — Same day raids the **Erveničane** Morlaks (chained for ~16 lost compagni in earlier Vrana fighting) — 600 manzi + 1000 minuti + 2 dead villagers (doc. 344). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1684-01-02** — Occupies abandoned **Obrovac** with men of Novigrad / Castel Venier / Starigrad and demands a Venetian stipendio "or we shift to the Habsburgs"; brother Stojan dispatched to defuse (doc. 360, 365). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1684-01-11** — Brother Stojan Janković forces his men out of Obrovac (doc. 364). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1684-04-07** — Donà orders him and his Budin-based companions to restore goods plundered after the murder of Perica Sinobad (doc. 391). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1684-04-18** — Sinobad case escalates: Ilija accused of murdering [[Pietro-Sinobad-b????-Posedarje|Perica Sinobad]] (Jovan's father) and a brother, of incendij, plunder, and of armed prevention of cultivation (doc. 396). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]
- **1684-04-16** (recapitulation) — Donà to Senate: "il principal fomentatore di tali disordini si conosce sempre più essere Elia fratello del kavalier Gianco Mitrovich" — ineffective brother Stojan, asks the Senate's express command "il rimoverlo in qualche maniera da queste parti crederei alle congionture, che accorrono, molt' opportuno." (doc. 394). [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]

## Timeline (continued — 1684 to 1693, from Desnica II)
- **1684-07-15** — Mocenigo's terminacija grants him 15 ducats/month "in atto di riconoscimento" of father Janko's deeds and brother Stojan's continuing service; described as "il più ardito, e il più temerario fra Morlacchi" but profligate with earnings, generous to Morlachs, with strong following — Mocenigo notes the alternatives are to "remove him from the world or keep him content" (doc 20, 21) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **1685-09-05** — Petitions Senate to raise his stipend from 15 to 40 ducats (doc 96) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **1693 (late)** — Dies of natural causes (Desnica II attestation) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].

## Note on possible namesake
A figure called **"Peraica" / "famoso Peraizza"** appears in 1685 (Cetina-region migration leader, killed Aug-Sept 1685 by Turkish corsair fusta off [[Rogoznica]]; pension passed to brother [[Frane-Peraica-b????-Zegar|Frane Peraica]]) — see chunk-1 extraction. Some readings identify "Peraica" with Ilija Mitrović; Desnica II doc 96 references Ilija Mitrović as petitioner in Sept 1685; the chronology of that petition is hard to reconcile with a death at sea in the same window. This wiki treats **Ilija Mitrović of Žegar** and **"Peraica" of the Cetina migration** as **distinct persons** until further evidence; flagged as open question.

## Notes
- His sister was wife of [[Dmitar-Nikolic-b????-Plav|Mitar Nikolić]] (Bajo Pivljanin's brother — Desnica's footnote on doc. 376) — kinship with the Pivljanini explains why Mitar joined his rebellion.
