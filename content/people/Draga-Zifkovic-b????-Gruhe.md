# Draga Žifković (fl. 1610, Gruhe)

## Summary
Recorded as *Draga Xifcouichia*, head of household in [[Gruhe]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. Her household of 2 comprised 1 man, 1 woman. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
