# Petar Čikić (fl. 1686–1719, Škabrnja)

## Summary
Head of household in [[Skabrnja|Škabrnja]], recorded as **PETAR ČIKIĆ pok. Jure** in the census of households and livestock 1686–1719 [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]. His household of 4 comprised the head, 1 man-at-arms, 1 woman and 1 girl. Livestock: 2 konji (horses), 2 goveda (cattle), 2 telad (calves), 2 krave (cows), 40 ovce (sheep).
