# fra Bartolomej, kapucin (1649)

Vojni kapelan whose 1649 izvješće documents Catholic conversion movement in the Zadar and Šibenik zaleđe (Bogović 1993:167–170) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]] — Candian War-era data point for confessional shifts in the Bunjevac/Vlach population on the Venetian-Ottoman frontier.
