# David Marić (fl. 1756, Gornja Jagodnja)

## Summary
Recorded as *David Marich*, head of a household of 4 souls in [[Gornja-Jagodnja|Gornja Jagodnja]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 8 campi 50 tavole, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
