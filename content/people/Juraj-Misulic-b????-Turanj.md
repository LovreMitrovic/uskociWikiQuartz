# Juraj Mišulić (fl. 1610, Turanj)

## Summary
Recorded as *Zorzi Missulich*, head of household in [[Turanj]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 6 comprised 3 men, 3 women. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
