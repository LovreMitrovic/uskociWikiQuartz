# Mile Đuranić (fl. 1686–1719, Škabrnja)

## Summary
Head of household in [[Skabrnja|Škabrnja]], recorded as **MILE ĐURANIĆ pok. Ivana** in the census of households and livestock 1686–1719 [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]. His household of 9 comprised the head, 1 man-at-arms, 2 boys, 2 women and 3 girls. Livestock: 5 konji (horses), 8 goveda (cattle), 3 telad (calves), 3 krave (cows), 50 ovce (sheep).
