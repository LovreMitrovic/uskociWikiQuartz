# Radič, knez

Knez and head of a major Vlasi Istre dzemat (123 houses, 5,381 akči) in nahija [[Klicevac]] in 1528–1530 [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Shared a *timar* in nahija [[Zrmanja]] with vojvoda [[Strahinja-Bratic-b????-Unac]] (1,135 akči, split equally).
