# Matthessa Milatović (fl. 1610, Turanj)

## Summary
Recorded as *Matthessa Milatouich*, head of household in [[Turanj]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 13 comprised 3 men, 3 women, 4 boys, 3 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
