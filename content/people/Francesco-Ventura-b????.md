# Francesco (Frane) Ventura

*Capitano*; one of four "new" sub-leaders Mocenigo appointed in 1684 over the Kotari sub-region of [[Vinjerac|Castel Venier]]–[[Razanci]]–[[Ljuba]]–[[Radovin]]–[[Polesnik|Polešnik]], doc 19 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Position abolished by Senate in doc 23.
