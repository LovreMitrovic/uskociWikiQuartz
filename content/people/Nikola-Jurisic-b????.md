# Nikola Jurišić

Habsburg kapetan; from 1528 held Podgrad and Postojna estates (taken from [[Krsto-I-Frankapan-b????]]) [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. 1538: led ~40,000 head of livestock and Ćići families from Zrmanja back to N Istria/Kras under Habsburg protection, calling them "Alt Römer".
