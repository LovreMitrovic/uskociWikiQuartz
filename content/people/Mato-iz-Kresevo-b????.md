# Fra Mato (Matija) iz Kreševa

Bosnian Franciscan (provincija Bosna Srebrena, samostan Visovac). 1550: took over the abandoned Franciscan house in [[Karin]] (Bezgrešnoga začeća BDM), pastoring local Catholic Vlachs after the original brotherhood's flight in 1538 [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Catholic colonists led by him recorded in Karin area.
