# Magdalena Janković (d. 13 May 1684)

Daughter of *kavalier* Stojan Janković (Stojan Mitrović). Buried in Zadar in the Greek rite on 13 May 1684, doc 3 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Source records the burial at the church of Sv. Stošija (parish archive header) and the body laid in the Greek-rite chapel of Sv. Ilija (Zadar).

Mother not named in source.

## Notes
- Source-page header is "Parohijski arh. crkve Sv. Stošije" (St. Anastasia parish archive); body of the entry says "sepolta nella chiesa di sant' Elia" — interpreted as Sv. Ilija (Greek-rite) being administratively under Sv. Stošija.
