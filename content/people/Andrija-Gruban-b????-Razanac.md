# Andrija Gruban (fl. 1610, Ražanac)

## Summary
Recorded as *Andrea Gruban*, head of household in [[Razanac|Ražanac]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 6 comprised 3 men, 3 women. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
