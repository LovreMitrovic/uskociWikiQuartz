# Martin Dević (fl. 1610, Dračevac Zadarski)

## Summary
Recorded as *Martin Deuich*, head of household in [[Dracevac-Zadarski|Dračevac Zadarski]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 3 comprised 1 old man, 2 men. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
