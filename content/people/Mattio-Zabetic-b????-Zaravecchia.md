# Mattio Žabetić

Captain of [[Biograd-na-Moru|Zaravecchia]] (Biograd na Moru) and one of the **three ringleaders** of the 1704 Bukovica revolt alongside [[Petar-Jagodic-Kuridza-b1666-Bukovica|pop Petar Jagodić-Kuridža]] and [[Ilija-Nanic-b????-Zegar|Ilija Nanić]] (docs 352, 356, 357) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Banished after suppression. In 1704 located in Venice; observed there by the Franciscan father [[Pavle-Vuskovic-b????|Pavlo Vušković]] in Cà Mocenigo (doc 357).

His **brother was quartered ("squartato")** in the 1690s for participation in the murder of governator [[Antun-Bortulacic-b????-Zadar|Bortolazzi]] (doc 357) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. The Žabetić family was thus already deeply implicated in violent opposition to Venetian governance before the 1704 events.
