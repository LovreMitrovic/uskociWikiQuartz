# Mihat Stanković (fl. 1610, Dračevac Zadarski)

## Summary
Recorded as *Mihat Stancouich*, head of household in [[Dracevac-Zadarski|Dračevac Zadarski]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 3 comprised 2 men, 1 woman. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
