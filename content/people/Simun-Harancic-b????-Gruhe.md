# Šimun Harančić (fl. 1610, Gruhe)

## Summary
Recorded as *Simon Haranzich*, head of household in [[Gruhe]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 3 comprised 2 men, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
