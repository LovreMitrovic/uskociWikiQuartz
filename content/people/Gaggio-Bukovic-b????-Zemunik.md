# Gaggio Buković (fl. 1756, Zemunik)

## Summary
Recorded as *Gaggio Pascas da Bucovich*, a landholder with no household enumerated in [[Zemunik]] (villici) in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 8 campi, held *senza laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
