# Jure Orsada (fl. 1686–1719, Škabrnja)

## Summary
Head of household in [[Skabrnja|Škabrnja]], recorded as **JURE ORSADA pok. Ivana** in the census of households and livestock 1686–1719 [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]. His household of 5 comprised the head, 1 man-at-arms, 2 women and 1 girl. Livestock: 10 konji (horses), 6 goveda (cattle), 2 telad (calves), 2 krave (cows), 100 ovce (sheep).
