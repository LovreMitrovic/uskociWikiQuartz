# Mihovil Radobelja (fl. 1610, Turanj)

## Summary
Recorded as *Michiel Radobeglia*, head of household in [[Turanj]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 5 comprised 1 man, 1 woman, 2 boys, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
