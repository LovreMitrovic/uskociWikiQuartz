# Grgur Velanović (fl. 1610, Kožino)

## Summary
Recorded as *Gregorio Vellanouich*, head of household in [[Kozino|Kožino]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 8 comprised 2 men, 2 women, 2 boys and 2 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
