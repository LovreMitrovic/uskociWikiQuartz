# Ivan Suetanić (fl. 1686–1719, Škabrnja)

## Summary
Head of household in [[Skabrnja|Škabrnja]], recorded as **IVAN SUETANIĆ pok. Vicka** in the census of households and livestock 1686–1719 [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]. His household of 3 comprised the head, 1 woman and 1 girl. Livestock: 1 konji (horses), 3 goveda (cattle), 1 telad (calves), 2 krave (cows). [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]
