# Nikola Gracalić (fl. 1610, Sukošan)

## Summary
Recorded as *Nicolò Gracalich*, head of household in [[Sukosan|Sukošan]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 5 comprised 1 old man, 2 men, 2 women. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
