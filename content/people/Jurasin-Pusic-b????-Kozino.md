# Jurašin Pusić (fl. 1610, Kožino)

## Summary
Recorded as *Jurassin Pussich*, head of household in [[Kozino|Kožino]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 5 comprised 1 man, 1 woman, 2 boys and 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
