# Ilija Brajić (fl. 1610, Posedarje)

## Summary
Recorded as *Elia Braijch*, head of household in [[Posedarje]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 7 comprised 2 men, 1 woman, 2 boys, and 2 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
