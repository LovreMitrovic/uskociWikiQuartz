# Manojlo Sladović

19th-c. Croatian historian, author of *Pověsti biskupijah Senjske i Modruške ili Krbavske* (Trst, 1856) — a key source on the [[Senjsko-Modruska-Biskupija]] and Bunjevac integration into the Croatian sphere.

Šarić cites him for vivid description of bunjevački material culture and continued vlach-Dinaric character of compact Lika–Primorje Bunjevac villages into the mid-19th c. [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]] (Sladović 1856:28–29, 49, 435). Probably descends from the Senj branch of the [[Sladovic|Sladovići]] who later moved into [[Licko-Lesce]] in the Otočac kapetanija.
