# fra Filip Lastrić

18th-c. Bosnian Franciscan historian. Cited by Šarić for his vivid characterization of the Bosanska franjevačka provincija – Dalmatian dioceses dispute over jurisdiction over Catholic Vlachs:

> *"dalmatinskim biskupima nije stalo do spasa ovaca, nego se bore za njihovu vunu"*

(Zirdum 1982:86) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

The dispute (which structured the [[Buna]]-river origin legend that Bosnian franjevci constructed) was, in Lastrić's reading and Džaja's confirmation, a **borba za osiguranje materijalnih dobara i karijerizam sudionika**, not for vjernike.
