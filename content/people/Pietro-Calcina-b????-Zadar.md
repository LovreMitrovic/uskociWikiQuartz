# Pietro Calcina

Captain; son of "*il quondam signor kavalier Calcina*" (the deceased *cancelliere illirico actual*, who had let his son substitute for him in office). Claimed to have been invested in the *cancelleria illirica* position 1675/1680, opposed [[Nikola-Lomazi-b????-Zadar|Nicolò Lomazi]] in 1701 (doc 327) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Previously served as *cavalier* in the Levant under Venetian generals; was also a member of a 1698 commission (doc 312).
