# Toma Mršun (fl. 1756, Biograd na Moru)

## Summary
Recorded as *Toma Mersun detto Vitanovich*, head of a household of 6 souls in [[Biograd-na-Moru|Biograd na Moru]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 4 campi 3 quarte, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
