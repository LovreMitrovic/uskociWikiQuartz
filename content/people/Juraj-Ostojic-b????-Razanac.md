# Juraj Ostojić (fl. 1610, Ražanac)

## Summary
Recorded as *Zorzi Ostoijch*, head of household in [[Razanac|Ražanac]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 8 comprised 2 men, 3 women, 1 boy, 2 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
