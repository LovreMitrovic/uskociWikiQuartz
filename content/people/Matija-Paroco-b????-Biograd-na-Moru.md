# Matija Paroco (fl. 1610, Biograd na Moru)

## Summary
Recorded as *Matthio Paroco*, head of household in [[Biograd-na-Moru|Biograd na Moru]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 2 comprised 1 man, 1 boy. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]] Recorded with the title *P.* (pre', a parish priest; *Paroco* means parson rather than a true surname).
