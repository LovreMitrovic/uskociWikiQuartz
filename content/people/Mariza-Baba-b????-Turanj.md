# Mariza Baba (fl. 1610, Turanj)

## Summary
Recorded as *Baba Mariza*, head of household in [[Turanj]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. Her household of 1 comprised herself, a single elderly woman counted in the *starci* (elderly) column. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]

## Notes
- `birth_place` is **inferred from residence**: the census records the village where the household was enumerated in 1610, not a place of birth, and no birth record is attested. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
"Baba" here is a descriptor (elderly woman / grandmother) rather than a true surname; she is recorded without a family name, and it is used in the surname slot only to form a stable page id. She is the sole occupant and is entered in the *starci* column (nominally "old men"), which in her case denotes an old woman. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
