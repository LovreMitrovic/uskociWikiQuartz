# Ante Sušetić (fl. 1686–1719, Škabrnja)

## Summary
Head of household in [[Skabrnja|Škabrnja]], recorded as **ANTE SUŠETIĆ pok. Šime** in the census of households and livestock 1686–1719 [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]. His household of 4 comprised the head, 2 boys and 1 woman. Livestock: 4 konji (horses), 2 goveda (cattle), 3 telad (calves), 3 krave (cows), 20 ovce (sheep). [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]
