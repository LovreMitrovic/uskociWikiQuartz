# Katarina Kostojić (fl. 1610, Posedarje)

## Summary
Recorded as *Cattarina Costoijchia*, head of household in [[Posedarje]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. Her household of 1 comprised 1 woman (herself), suggesting a widow. The feminine surname form *Costoijchia* points to the family name Kostojić. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
