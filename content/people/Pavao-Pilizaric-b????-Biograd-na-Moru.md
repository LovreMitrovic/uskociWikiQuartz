# Pavao Pilizarić (fl. 1610, Biograd na Moru)

## Summary
Recorded as *Paulo Pillizarich*, head of household in [[Biograd-na-Moru|Biograd na Moru]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 5 comprised 1 old man, 2 men, 1 woman, 1 boy. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
