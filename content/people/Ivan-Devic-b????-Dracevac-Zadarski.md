# Ivan Dević (fl. 1610, Dračevac Zadarski)

## Summary
Recorded as *Zuane Deuich*, head of household in [[Dracevac-Zadarski|Dračevac Zadarski]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 6 comprised 3 men, 3 women. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
