# Bare Veličić (fl. 1610, Biograd na Moru)

## Summary
Recorded as *Bare Velicich*, head of household in [[Biograd-na-Moru|Biograd na Moru]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 11 comprised 4 men, 2 women, 3 boys, 2 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
