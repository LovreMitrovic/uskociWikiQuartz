# Grgur Ćurelić (fl. 1610, Ljubač)

## Summary
Recorded as *Gregorio Çurelich*, head of household in [[Ljubac|Ljubač]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 10 comprised 3 men, 2 women, 2 boys, 3 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
