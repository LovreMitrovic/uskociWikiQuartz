# Ćiro Graovac (fl. 1756, Donje Biljane)

## Summary
Recorded as *Chiro Graovaz*, head of a household of 6 souls in [[Donje-Biljane|Donje Biljane]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 12 campi 135 tavole, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
