# Rade Račić (fl. 1610, Gruhe)

## Summary
Recorded as *Rade Racichia*, head of household in [[Gruhe]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. Her household of 3 comprised 1 man, 1 woman, 1 boy. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
