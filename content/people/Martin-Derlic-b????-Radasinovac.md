# Martin Derlić (fl. 1756, Radašinovac)

## Summary
Recorded as *Martin Derlich*, head of a household of 8 souls in [[Radasinovac|Radašinovac]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 16 campi 1 quarte 162 tavole, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
