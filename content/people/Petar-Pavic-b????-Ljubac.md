# Petar Pavić (fl. 1610, Ljubač)

## Summary
Recorded as *Piero Pauich*, head of household in [[Ljubac|Ljubač]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 4 comprised 1 man, 2 women, 1 boy. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
