# Petar Čalić (fl. 1686–1719, Škabrnja)

## Summary
Head of household in [[Skabrnja|Škabrnja]], recorded as **PETAR ČALIĆ pok. Jakova detto Itković** in the census of households and livestock 1686–1719 [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]. His household of 10 comprised the head, 3 men-at-arms, 1 boy, 2 women and 3 girls. Livestock: 13 konji (horses), 8 goveda (cattle), 5 telad (calves), 8 krave (cows), 300 ovce (sheep).
