# don Stjepan Sorić (?– 17 July 1648)

The **same Sorić** appears across all three sources covering this period: 1636 župnik of Gorica/Vojnić Catholic Vlachs (Šarić 2008/2024), then 1647 *vođa uskakanja* of those same Vlachs into Venetian territory, then *governator de Morlacchi* and finally killed at Ribnik (Desnica 1950).

## Pre-1646 (per Šarić)
Don, župnik in **Gorica** (parish of [[Tinj]]) in 1636, tending ~500 "Morlaka latinskog obreda" [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. In the 2008 article (Šarić), župnik of the **župa Županovići** in 1636 covering 6 villages (Gorica, Raštane, Prkos, Bubnjane, Lišane Tinjske, Brkljačina Polača) with ~1550 "duša" — Vojnić-area Catholic Vlach population [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]]. In **1638** in dispute with Visovac Franciscans over jurisdiction.

## 1647–1648 (per Desnica)
Foscolo's *attestato* of 1647-12-25 (doc. 19) traces his career from the start of the Candian War: previously *paroco di Gorizza e ville adiacenti sotto Halil Begh dalla Vrana, Sanzaco di Lica*, he opened a confidential channel to Foscolo through a brother who lived in **Maidan di Bosna**. He gave warning of Ibraim-paša's army descending on Novigrad (1646), penetrated nightly to a Venetian galley moored at Pašman to deliver intelligence during the Ottoman attack on Zaravecchia. Wounded by *Zagaja* in the back during the rescue of Vezir's nephew (which Foscolo's narrative compresses incoherently). Promoted to *governator delli Morlacchi* — Foscolo's terminacija doc. 26 (1648-04-20) recapitulates: razed Zvonigrad mills, helped take Drniš and Klis, captured the standard of paša Tekeli at Klis. [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]]

- **1647-04** — Doc. 16, Foscolo on the deceptive *Goričani* uskakanje (Sorić's own *uskakanje* with 20 families).
- **1648-04-18** — Doc. 25 — Pietro Paolo Roviglioni buys from "don Stefano Sorich governator delli Morlacchi" the Turkish slave-girl Rahe of Islam (notarial Skoci sv. 132, Šibenik).
- **1648-05-07** — Doc. 27 — Sorić personally vouches that the *rob* Mehmed Jelovac of [[Babodol]] was not paid by the State; signs *bosanskom ćirilicom*: "ja Pop Sorić potvrđujem gore rečeno."
- **1648-06-21** — Leads the great raid on **Udbina** with Petar and Ilija Smiljanić — burns Mazin valley, Rudopolje (3 sela: **Tomičić, Vodena Glava, Pancino selo**), Klapavica, Mračaj, Bruno (doc. 32). Brings 70 *Žegar* families with 250 fighters into Venetian protection.
- **1648-06-20** — Senate sends him a gold chain (doc. 31), Foscolo to deliver it on his return.
- **1648-07-16/17** — **Killed at Ribnik** (doc. 37, 40, 41). Foscolo describes (doc. 40): captured wounded after *valorosamente combattuto* and ferito d' arcobuggiata in a coscia; questioned by the Turks about his brother (i.e. they wanted to know whether his Bosnian-Maidan brother had betrayed them); when he answered the brother had been killed, the Turkish executioner stabbed him with a lance and finished him off. *"È moro certo un bravo e valoroso soldato, ma dalla natione non molto ne obedito ne amato."*
- **1648-07-31** — His brother **Mate Sorić** elected by the Goričani as the new capo (doc. 41); the gold chain is later transferred to Mate (doc. 41 footnote: a *parastos* in Sv. Stošija Zadar with all morlački glavari present, 1648-08-19).

## Family
- Brother in **Maidan di Bosnia** — confidant, killed before pop himself (per the Turkish executioner's question).
- Brother **Mate Sorić** — *capitan*, succeeded him as Goričanski leader; received the gold chain.
- (1683) **Gregorio Sorić** — implies him as a *defunct* — sues for relics: Margarita Ivančević is keeping the *uščuvana ruka* (preserved hand) of pop Stjepan Sorić as a relic; provveditore Donà orders her on 1683-09-02 to deposit the hand and his papers in the chancery (doc. 311).
