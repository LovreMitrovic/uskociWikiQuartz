# Nikola Kolar (fl. 1756, Tinj)

## Summary
Recorded as *Nicola Colar*, head of a household of 7 souls in [[Tinj]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 7 campi 24 tavole, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
