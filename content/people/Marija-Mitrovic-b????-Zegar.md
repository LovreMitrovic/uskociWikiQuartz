# Marija Mitrović

Daughter of [[Ilija-Mitrovic-b????-Zegar|Ilija Mitrović]] (the rebellious brother of [[Stojan-Jankovic-b1635-Zegar|Stojan]] and [[Zavisa-Jankovic-b????-Zegar|Zaviša]]). Married [[Vucen-Stojisavljevic-b????-Vrlika|Vučen Stojisavljević]] of a village under [[Vrlika]]; after her husband's death, raised son [[Petar-Mitrovic-Stojisavljevic-b????-Vrlika|Petar]] in the Mitrović household, hence Petar's adoption of the Mitrović cognomen (doc 379) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
