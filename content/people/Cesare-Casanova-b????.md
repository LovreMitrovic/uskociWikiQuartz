# Cesare Casanova

Italian; previously kept a *galeotta* in the Dalmatian waters and spent years at Spalato; **appointed by Molin capitan / governator of Obrovac in January 1690** [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (docs 226, 227). Died c. 1693; succeeded as governor of both Obrovacs by **Dimo Vlastò** (Dolfin's appointment 10 Jan 1693, doc 260) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]] (also docs 256, 260).
