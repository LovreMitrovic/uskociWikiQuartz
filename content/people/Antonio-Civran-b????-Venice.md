# Antonio Civran (1615)

Venetian kapetan gulfa (commander of the Adriatic). His **18 March 1615 relacija** records oral tradition collected directly from the **Krmpoćani** in negotiations to relocate them from [[Lic]] / Bakar area to Venetian territory (Commissiones et Relationes Venetae VI/1960:223) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

The relacija is the **single most important narrative source for the Krmpote migration**: Civran reports that the *Carapotani* had moved ~150 years before from somewhere into the area "around [[Gracac]]" (likely the older form *Gradac*); when they became suspect there ("in sospetto"), they relocated to the area around [[Novigrad]]; eventually, "fastiditi dell'Imperio de'Turchi", they moved to "un certo luoco sopra Buccari quindeci miglia" (= [[Lic]] above Bakar) on the Habsburg nadvojvoda's territory [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

Šarić cross-validates this oral tradition against the 1477 defter, the Kijani stećak ([[Ivan-Balenovic-b????-Kijani]]), and Pavličević 1989 to construct the canonical Krmpote itinerary: Hercegovina → Gračac (~1465?) → Medviđa/Krmpote (1528 / post-Cyprus War 1573–74) → Lič (1605, 1627) → Vinjerac (1647).

Likely descendant of [[Andrea-Civran-b????-Venice]] (providur Dalmacije 1524) — same Venetian patrician family.
