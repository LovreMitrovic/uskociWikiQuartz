# Žakman Gleđevac

Vojvoda of the Karin Vlasi Istre dzemat (53 houses) in 1528–1530, residing at "Dasidžmani" with four other Gleđevac houses [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Held a shared *timar* in Vrbljani near Ostrovica with [[Marko-sin-Lipovica-b????]]. 1538: led migration to Venetian territory, settled in [[Podnovje]] (novigradski distrikt) as *comes Murlacorum* (vlach knez) in *stipendiati* military service; tracked in sources to 1559. Name "Žakman" is a variant of Šikman.

## Timeline
- **1391 (lineage)** — The Gleđevci, an old Vlach *katun* from the Hum land, attested in the Zadar hinterland since 1391 as subjects of the counts of Krbava; by a record of 1496 their *katun* had territorialized in the village of Bukovica near Karin. *"Gleđevci su starinom vlaški katun iz Humske zemlje koji se u zadarskom zaleđu spominje još od 1391. – kao podložnici knezova Krbavskih. Prema jednom podatku iz 1496., njihov katun teritorijalizirao se u selu Bukovica kod Karina."* [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]
- **1528–1530** — Vojvoda over the Karin *džemat*; the return of the Gleđevci from Istria to their old Karin hearth. *"Gleđevci su se tako iz Istre vratili na svoje staro ognjište u karinskom kraju."* [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]
- **1538** — Led the migration to Venetian territory and was rewarded by the Senate; settled at [[Podnovje]] in the Novigrad district, styled *comes Murlacorum* and enrolled in mercenary service (*stipendiati*); traceable in the sources to 1559. *"Vojvoda Žakman Gleđevac predvodio je 1538. seobu na mletačko područje, zbog čega ga je Senat i nagradio. Nastanio se u Podnovju… U mletačkim izvorima vodio se kao vlaški knez (comes Murlacorum) i bio je uključen u plaćeničku vojnu službu (stipediati). U izvorima ga možemo pratiti do 1559."* [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]
