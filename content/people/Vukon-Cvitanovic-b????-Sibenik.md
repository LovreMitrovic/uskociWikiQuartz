# Vukon Cvitanović (Vuconie Zuitanovich)

Morlach harambaša dispatched (with Nakić) on 12 Nov 1686 by Stojan Janković from Bribir to traverse the whole Zadar contado, alerting villages to the imminent Bosnian-pasha raid: each household to send one armed man to Bribir, the families and animals to be moved to safety (doc. 155) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
