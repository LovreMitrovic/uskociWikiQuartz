# Raosaf Kovač (fl. 1610, Ražanac)

## Summary
Recorded as *Raosaf Chouaz*, head of household in [[Razanac|Ražanac]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 6 comprised 2 men, 2 women, 2 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
