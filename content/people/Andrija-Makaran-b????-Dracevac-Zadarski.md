# Andrija Makaran (fl. 1610, Dračevac Zadarski)

## Summary
Recorded as *Andrea Macaran*, head of household in [[Dracevac-Zadarski|Dračevac Zadarski]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 3 comprised 2 old men, 1 woman. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
