# Alberto Fortis (1778)

Italian-Padovan friar and naturalist. **First to record the Buna-river origin legend of the Bunjevci** in the appendix to the English edition of his *Viaggio in Dalmatia* (London, 1778), describing the inhabitants of the [[Velebit]] coast [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]] (Fortis 1984:276–277):

> *Stanovnici planina danas su posve različiti od onoga što su tada bili. Sami sebe nazivaju Bunjevcima, jer su došli s područja Bune u Bosni…*

His attestation marks the moment when the legend of Buna-origin entered the European etnografska literature. Šarić's structural argument: this is a legend in 18th-c. literary recording, not a 16th-c. historical narrative — its real origin is Bosanska franjevačka provincija pastorally-jurisdictional propaganda.
