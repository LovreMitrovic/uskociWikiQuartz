# Andrija Josip Mandal (1733)

Vojni svećenik for all of [[Lika]] and [[Krbava]]. In his 1733 izvješće he no longer calls Bunjevci Vlachs but says they are *similia* (in dress, customs, life) to Vlachs (Burić 2002:132) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

Marker for the moment when **vlaški** drops out of the official designation in [[Senjsko-Modruska-Biskupija]] and only **Bunjevac** remains, after biskup [[Martin-Brajkovic-b????-Senj]] introduced the dvojni naziv "catholici Valachi alias Bunievczi" in 1702.
