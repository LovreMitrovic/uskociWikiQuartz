# Ivan Stratico (fl. 1756, Sv. Filip i Jakov)

## Summary
Recorded as *Giovani Battista Stratico*, a landholder with no household enumerated in [[Sv-Filip-i-Jakov|Sv. Filip i Jakov]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 5 campi 142 tavole, held *senza laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
