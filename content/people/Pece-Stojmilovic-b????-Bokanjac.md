# Pece Stojmilović (fl. 1610, Bokanjac)

## Summary
Recorded as *Pece Stoijmilouich*, head of household in [[Bokanjac]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 5 comprised 2 men, 2 women, 1 boy. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
