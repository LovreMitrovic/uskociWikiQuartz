# Nikola Hrabrić (fl. 1610, Petrčane)

## Summary
Recorded as *Nicolò Hrabrich*, head of household in [[Petrcane|Petrčane]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 5 comprised 3 men, 1 woman, 1 boy. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
