# Veronika Bukio (fl. 1756, Tinj)

## Summary
Recorded as *Veronica Buchio*, a landholder with no household enumerated in [[Tinj]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 150 campi, held *senza laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
