# Ivan Šimičić (fl. 1756, Sv. Filip i Jakov)

## Summary
Recorded as *Ivan Simicich*, head of a household of 16 souls in [[Sv-Filip-i-Jakov|Sv. Filip i Jakov]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 35 campi 1 quarte 107 tavole, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
