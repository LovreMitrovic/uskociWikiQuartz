# Ivan Vitković (fl. 1610, Kožino)

## Summary
Recorded as *Zuane Vitcouich*, head of household in [[Kozino|Kožino]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 4 comprised 1 man, 1 woman, 1 boy and 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
