# Matija Sasinić (fl. 1610, Sukošan)

## Summary
Recorded as *Matthio Sassinich*, head of household in [[Sukosan|Sukošan]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 8 comprised 5 men, 2 women, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
