# Girolamo Cornaro

*Provveditor generale in Dalmazia et Albania* from 1686, succeeding Petar Valier. Brother of cavalier Catterino Cornaro of "ben degna memoria" who had earlier defended the tower of **Lapsach/Lapčanj** in the Makarska primorje (Senate dispatch of 27 July 1686, doc. 138).

## Timeline (this source)
- **1686-07-19** (doc. 136) — Cornaro reports to the Senate from Split on the **first Livno raid**: 5,000 Morlach foot and 1,500 horse under Janković, Smiljanić, Posedarski (tenente collonnello) and Bortulačić — 400 Turks killed, 30 Christian slaves liberated, 1,800 houses burned. Requests medals and rings for the most valorous.
- **1686-09-17** (doc. 144, "from the tent at Salona") — Reports the war-council decision to attempt **Sinj** with prince of Parma and S. Polo; raising 2,000 from garrisons + 1,000 sbarcati + cavalry + 3,000 Morlachi. Notes the death of provveditor general de cavalli **Polo Michiel** (with regret).
- **1686-09-30** (doc. 145, from camp at Sinj) — Reports the **conquest of Sinj** (assault night of 29 Sept; piazza taken). 150+ Turks killed in assault; 25–30 Venetians dead, 120 wounded; cannon recovered. Antonio Bollani named provveditore di Sinj at the Klis salary.
- **1686-10-30** (doc. 147) — Letter to the *Inquisitori di Stato* warning that the Ragusans are pushing the Habsburgs toward Bosnia and Herzegovina to forestall a Venetian conquest of Hercegnovi, and forwarding the suspicious **Iljanović letter** (doc. 139) addressed to Stojan Janković as "generale". Receivedat Venice 9 Nov 1686.
- **1686-11-12 to 11-19** — Coordinates the response to the **Bosnian pasha's raid on the Kotari** (16,000 Turks, 5 paše); writes from Šibenik and Skradin, urges Stojan Janković at Bribir, sends 200 reinforcements; the Turks ultimately retreat to Knin without significant booty (docs. 150–157).

## In this source (1689 — chunk 3)
- **1689-03-11** — Promulgated the **Liga statute of 1689**, dividing the Zadar contado into 9 *pertinenze* with named governors and judges (Cassio at Nin, Calčina at Posedarje, Belglava at Novigrad area, Begna at Nadin, Spingaroli at Zaravecchia, etc.) — the canonical organizing document of the post-1684 administrative landscape (doc 224) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- **1689-03-30** — Issued the *attestato* on Zaviša Mitrović's warlike deeds (Sinj, Zara, Vrcerev Vakup wounded, Knin) (doc 225) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- Succeeded by **Alessandro Molin** later in 1689.
