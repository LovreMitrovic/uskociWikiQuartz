# knez Mihovil (1528–30)

Vlach knez attested in the 1528–30 Ottoman defter for the ličko-pounsko krajište. Settled the depopulated krajište with **hajmane** (newly-arrived Vlach colonists not yet on the regular tax rolls; paid only *resm-i haymane*) by Ottoman command (Vasić 1963:244) [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

Šarić proposes Mihovil is the **father** of [[Ivan-Balenovic-b????-Kijani]], whose mid-16th-c. krstača-stećak in selo Kijani near [[Gracac]] reads "totu leži Ivan Balenović, sin Mihovilov." This would associate Mihovil with the Krmpote re-settlement of Lika c. 1528 — converging the genealogical, archaeological, and register evidence.
