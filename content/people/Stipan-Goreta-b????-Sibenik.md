# Stipan Goreta

One of the principal *capi* of the Šibenik-territory Morlachi, paired with **Palikuća** and **Žinović** (doc. 155). On the evening of 15 Nov 1686 came to Stojan Janković at Bribir with the news that the Šibenik-side men were about to flee, and despite Janković's attempts to rally them, they bolted — forcing the abandonment of Bribir before the Bosnian pasha's arrival [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
