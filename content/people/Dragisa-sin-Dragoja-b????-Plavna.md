# Dragiša, sin Dragoja

Vojvoda in [[Plavna]] in the 1540 register — replaced the fugitive [[Zakman-Gledjevac-b????-Karin]] as the leading vojvoda among the Vlasi Istre after the 1538 mass defection [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]. Resident in selo Podlužje, leading 37 houses.
