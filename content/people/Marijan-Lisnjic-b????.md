# fra Marijan Lišnjić, makarski biskup (1670–71)

Makarski biskup; visitator of mountains Vitorog and [[Ljubusa]] in summer 1670–71 [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]] (Jurišić 1972:58).

His report is among the rare Bosnian-Franciscan opaske about *catholic Vlachs* — describing *pastire "Morlake"* and their dušobrižnik [[Anto-Ripcanin-b????]] in not-flattering terms. A characteristic example of Bosanska franjevačka provincija reluctance to use either *vlaški* or *bunjevački* for their flock.
