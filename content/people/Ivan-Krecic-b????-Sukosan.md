# Ivan Krečić (fl. 1610, Sukošan)

## Summary
Recorded as *Zuane Chrechich*, head of household in [[Sukosan|Sukošan]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 15 comprised 5 men, 4 women, 4 boys, 2 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]

- `birth_place` is **inferred from residence**: the census records the village where the household was enumerated in 1610, not a place of birth, and no birth record is attested. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
