# biskup Martin Brajković (1702)

Senjski biskup who in **1702** introduced the dvojni naziv **"catholici Valachi alias Bunievczi"** into official ecclesiastical record — the first formal adoption of the Bunjevac ethnonym in Catholic administration [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

In subsequent years under his successors (Bedeković, Ratkaj, Pohmajević, Benzoni, Čolić) the *vlaški* part dropped, leaving only *Bunievczi*. The 1733 izvješće of [[Andrija-Josip-Mandal-b????-Lika]] documents the completed transition.
