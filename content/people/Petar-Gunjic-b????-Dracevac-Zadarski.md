# Petar Gunjić (fl. 1610, Dračevac Zadarski)

## Summary
Recorded as *Piero Gugnich*, head of household in [[Dracevac-Zadarski|Dračevac Zadarski]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 5 comprised 1 old man, 2 men, 2 women. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
