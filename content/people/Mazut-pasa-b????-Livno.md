# Mazut-paša (Mazul-paša)

Turkish pasha at [[Livno]]. Came to Grahovo to avenge the Glamoč defeat August 1684; defeated; his *kjehaja* (lieutenant) killed by [[Tomo-Sinovcic-b????-Novigrad|Tomo Sinovčić]], docs 30, 32 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
