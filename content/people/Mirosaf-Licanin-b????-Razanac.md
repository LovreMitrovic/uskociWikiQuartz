# Mirosaf Ličanin (fl. 1610, Ražanac)

## Summary
Recorded as *Mirosaf Liçanin*, head of household in [[Razanac|Ražanac]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 4 comprised 1 man, 1 woman, 2 boys. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
