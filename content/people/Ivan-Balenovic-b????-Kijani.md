# Ivan Balenović sin Mihovilov

Buried in selo Kijani near [[Gracac]] in [[Lika]]. Cyrillic-inscribed krstača (cross-stećak) from the first half or middle of the 16th c. (Bašagić 1971:72–73) reads:

> *totu leži Ivan Balenović, sin Mihovilov*

Šarić [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]]:
- The given name **Ivan** indicates a Catholic.
- The surname **Balenović** is a Krmpote Bunjevac surname (the Balinovići/Baleni were one of the 1605 Lič-migration families).
- The patronymic father **Mihovil** is plausibly knez [[Mihovil-knez-b????-Pounje]] of the 1528–30 Lika–Pounje popis.

Together this is read as a **mid-16th-c. Catholic Vlach (proto-Bunjevac) burial in Lika**, anchoring the Krmpote re-settlement of the Gračac area c. 1528.
