# Ante Dražina (fl. 1756, Pristeg)

## Summary
Recorded as *Antonio Drasina*, head of a household of 15 souls in [[Pristeg]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 31 campi, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
