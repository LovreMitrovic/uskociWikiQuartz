# Vukadin Mitrović (d. 1653)

Žegarski harambaša; "basso, occhi varii, pel nero, barba castagna, d' anni 40 in circa" (doc. 51, 1650-01-08 — i.e. b. c. 1610) [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]].

- **1650-01-08** — 4 ducati/month pay; from Žegar (doc. 51).
- **1652-11-10** — peso di biscotto added (doc. 65).
- **1653** — Died defending the post of Novigrad against Ottoman attack: *"toccò gloriosamente lasciar la vita"* (doc. 74, 1653-08-01). Pay transferred to son **Jakov Mitrović**, who had earlier been enslaved (doc. 74, 274).

Likely brother or cousin of [[Janko-Mitrovic-b1605-Zegar|Janko]] (the kin-cluster is one *uskakanje* from Žegar).
