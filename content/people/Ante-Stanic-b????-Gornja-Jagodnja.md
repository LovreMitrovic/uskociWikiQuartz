# Ante Stanić (fl. 1756, Gornja Jagodnja)

## Summary
Recorded as *Anton Stanich*, head of a household of 12 souls in [[Gornja-Jagodnja|Gornja Jagodnja]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 24 campi 54 tavole, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
