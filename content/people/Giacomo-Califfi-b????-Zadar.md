# Conte Giacomo Califfi (q. Francesco)

Conte; son of *quondam* conte Francesco Califfi. **Sold half of [[Olib]] island to [[Zavisa-Jankovic-b????-Zegar|Zaviša Mitrović]] for 8000 ducats** by contract of **26 November 1701** (doc 336) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. The sale included peculiar dynastic conditions binding the half-island to alternate between Zaviša's male line and his deceased brother Ilija's male line.
