# Bože Tolić (fl. 1756, Biograd na Moru)

## Summary
Recorded as *Bose Tolich*, head of a household of 8 souls in [[Biograd-na-Moru|Biograd na Moru]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 14 campi 3 quarte 70 tavole, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
