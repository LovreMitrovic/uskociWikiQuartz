# Bunac Rušović

Named on a tall medieval stećak in [[Buncici]] (Radmilović-Dubrava, near Bileća). Cyrillic inscription. Bešlagić 1971:390 [[Saric-2008-Bunjevci]].

The size of the stećak suggests an imućnija osoba, likely a vlach starešina. Šarić connects him to the **Vlasi Banjani** aglomeracija, in which Rušovići are documented in dubrovački sources as starešine.

Personal name **Bunac** is itself a slavicized hipokoristik of the vlach name **Bun/Bunj** (< Latin *Bonifacius*).
