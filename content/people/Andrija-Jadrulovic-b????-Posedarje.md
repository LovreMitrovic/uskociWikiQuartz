# Andrija Jadrulović (fl. 1610, Posedarje)

## Summary
Recorded as *Andrea Jadrulouich*, head of household in [[Posedarje]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 3 comprised 1 man and 2 women. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
