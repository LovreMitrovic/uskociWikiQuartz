# Doge Silvestro Valier

Doge of Venice. Signed multiple dukals in 1697–1698 (docs 303, 306, 310) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]:
- 1697 ducal addressed to provveditor Mocenigo, **raising [[Zavisa-Jankovic-b????-Zegar|Zaviša Janković's]] pay from 30 to 50 ducats per month**.
- Ducal of 18 May 1698 granting a soldier's pay in cavalry to wounded priest [[Lazar-Lazaric-b????|Lazar Lazarić]].

## In this source (chunk 3 — 1696)
- *Ducale* of **30 January 1696** investing **Jovan Sinobad** as cavalier of San Marco (doc 283) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
- *Ducale* of **27 June 1696** investing **Zaviša Mitrović** as cavalier of San Marco for his deeds at Sinj, Cyclut, and Klivno (doc 288) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
