# Marko Pelarić (fl. 1756, Stankovci)

## Summary
Recorded as *Marco Pelarich*, head of a household of 41 souls in [[Stankovci]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 81 campi 3 quarte 33 tavole, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
