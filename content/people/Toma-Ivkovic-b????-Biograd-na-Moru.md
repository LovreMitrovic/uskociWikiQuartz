# Toma Ivković (fl. 1610, Biograd na Moru)

## Summary
Recorded as *Thomaso Ifcouich*, head of household in [[Biograd-na-Moru|Biograd na Moru]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 5 comprised 2 men, 2 women, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
