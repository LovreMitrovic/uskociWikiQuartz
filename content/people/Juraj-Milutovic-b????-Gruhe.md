# Juraj Milutović (fl. 1610, Gruhe)

## Summary
Recorded as *Zorzi Millutouich*, head of household in [[Gruhe]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 2 comprised 1 old man, 1 man. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
