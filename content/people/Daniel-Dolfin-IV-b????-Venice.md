# Daniel Dolfin (IV)

Venetian provveditor general of Dalmatia. Predecessor of Mocenigo II in the first generalate; gave the **1697 *relazione* to the Senate** documenting Morlach manpower, character, and deployment (doc 305) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].

A later **Daniel Dolfin "quarto"** (likely the same office-holder dynasty's descendant) served as provveditor general 1737–1738, handling pop Petar Jagodić's correspondence (docs 386, 387, 388) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
