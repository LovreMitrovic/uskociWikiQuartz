# Catussa Perhatov (fl. 1610, Bibinje)

## Summary
Recorded as *Catussa Perhatoua*, head of household in [[Bibinje]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. Her household of 5 comprised 1 woman, 2 boys, 2 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
