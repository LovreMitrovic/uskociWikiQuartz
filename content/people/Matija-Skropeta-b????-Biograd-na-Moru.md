# Matija Škropeta (fl. 1610, Biograd na Moru)

## Summary
Recorded as *Matthio Schropeta*, head of household in [[Biograd-na-Moru|Biograd na Moru]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 3 comprised 1 old man, 2 women. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
