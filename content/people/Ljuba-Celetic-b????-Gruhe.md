# Ljuba Čeletić (fl. 1610, Gruhe)

## Summary
Recorded as *Gliuba Celetichia*, head of household in [[Gruhe]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. Her household of 6 comprised 2 women, 2 boys, 2 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
