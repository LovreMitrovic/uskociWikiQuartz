# Modre ("il Turco Modre")

Ottoman-era owner of the casale and lands of [[Modro-selo|Modro (Modrino selo)]] in [[Bukovica]], the eponym of the place. After 1692 his lands passed to the [[Sinobad]] family [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
