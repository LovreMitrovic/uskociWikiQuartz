# Ibrahim-aga, disdar of Udbina

*Disdar* (fortress commander) of [[Udbina]], age ~24 (in 1687). Captured spring 1686 by Zaviša Janković's *četa* along with [[Muhamed-beg-b????-Udbina|Muhamed-beg]] and [[Aga-Kumalic-b????-Udbina|Aga Kumalić]] (doc. 133, 12 April 1686).

Zen's report (doc. 134): *"huomo molto accorto, soldato coraggioso e ardito, temuto, stimato, e da Turchi di sua giurisdittione obedito."* Salary from sultan: 20 aspri/day. Bought by Zaviša Janković and partners for 260 reali at auction [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].
