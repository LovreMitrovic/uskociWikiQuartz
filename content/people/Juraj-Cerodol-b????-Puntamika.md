# Juraj Čerodol (fl. 1610, Puntamika)

## Summary
Recorded as *Zorzi Cerodol*, head of household in [[Puntamika]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 8 comprised 1 elder, 3 men, 2 women, 1 boy, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
