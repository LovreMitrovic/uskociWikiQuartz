# Petar Gospić (fl. 1686–1719, Škabrnja)

## Summary
Head of household in [[Skabrnja|Škabrnja]], recorded as **PETAR GOSPIĆ pok. Jure** in the census of households and livestock 1686–1719 [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]. His household of 6 comprised the head, 2 boys, 1 woman and 2 girls. Livestock: 1 konji (horses), 4 goveda (cattle), 1 krave (cows), 30 ovce (sheep). [[Razov-Milkovic-Skabrnja-Monografija]]
