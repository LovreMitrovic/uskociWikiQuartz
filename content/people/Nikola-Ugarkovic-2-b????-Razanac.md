# Nikola Ugarković (fl. 1610, Ražanac)

## Summary
Recorded as *Nicolò Vgarcouich*, head of household in [[Razanac|Ražanac]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 3 comprised 1 man, 1 woman, 1 boy. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]

A second head named *Nicolò Vgarcouich* appears in the same Ražanac census list; this page (filename suffix `-2`) distinguishes him from the earlier entry [[Nikola-Ugarkovic-b????-Razanac]]. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
