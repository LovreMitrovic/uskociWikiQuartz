# Mare Vulinović (fl. 1756, Pakoštane)

## Summary
Recorded as *Mare Vulinovich*, head of a household of 15 souls in [[Pakostane|Pakoštane]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 4 campi 121 tavole, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
