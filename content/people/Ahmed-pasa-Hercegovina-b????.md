# Ahmed-paša (of Hercegovina)

Pasha of [[Hercegovina]]; commanded a 3,000-cavalry attack on Kaštela (Trogir coast) April 1685, doc 62 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Defeated by 3,000 castellani plus Morlaks.
