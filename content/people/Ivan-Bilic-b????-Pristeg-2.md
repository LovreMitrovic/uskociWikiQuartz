# Ivan Bilić (fl. 1756, Pristeg)

## Summary
Recorded as *Ivan e fratelli Bilich*, a landholder with no household enumerated in [[Pristeg]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 6 campi 23 tavole, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
