# Mariza Raduljević (fl. 1610, Gruhe)

## Summary
Recorded as *Mariza Raduglieuichia*, head of household in [[Gruhe]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. Her household of 5 comprised 3 women, 1 boy, 1 girl. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
