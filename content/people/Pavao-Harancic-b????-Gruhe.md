# Pavao Harančić (fl. 1610, Gruhe)

## Summary
Recorded as *Paulo Haranzich*, head of household in [[Gruhe]] in the 1610 Venetian census of the Zadar mainland district [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]. His household of 10 comprised 1 old man, 3 men, 3 women, 3 girls. [[Dundovic-1610-Zadar-Census]]
