# Bogdan Prejić

Son of [[Petar-Prejic-b????]]. As a child fled to Habsburg territory c. 1538; later returned to Ottoman side, converted to Islam in mature age, and in 1557 received a *timar* on the recommendation of [[Malkoc-beg-b????]] (Klis sanjakbeg) — sign of the family's continued standing [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]].

## Timeline
- **c. 1538** — As a child, fled to Habsburg territory, probably during the turbulent events around 1538. *"Bogdan Prejić izbjegao je kao dijete na habsburški teritorij, vjerojatno tijekom burnih događanja oko 1538., ali se poslije vratio na osmansko područje i prešao na islam."* [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]
- **1557** — Having returned to "Turkish land" and converted to Islam in mature age, received a *timar* on the recommendation of [[Malkoc-beg-b????]], sanjakbeg of Klis — evidence of the family's standing. *"Bogdan Prejić, sin kneza Petra, koji se u zreloj dobi povratio u „tursku zemlju" i 1557. po preporuci Malkoč-bega, sandžakbega Klisa, dobio timar, također govori u prilog tome da je riječ o uglednoj obitelji."* [[Saric-2024-Vlasi-Istre]]
