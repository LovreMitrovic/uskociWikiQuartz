# Luka Zekuša (fl. 1756, Zemunik)

## Summary
Recorded as *Luca Zecusa*, head of a household of 5 souls in [[Zemunik]] (villici) in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 10 campi, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
