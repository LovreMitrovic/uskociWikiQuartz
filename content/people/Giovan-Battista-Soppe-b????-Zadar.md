# Giovan Battista Soppe ("Soppe Fortezza")

*Kavalier*; Zadar noble; sub-leader appointed by Mocenigo in 1684 over the sub-region of [[Vrana]]–[[Tinj]]–[[Jagodnja]]–[[Skradin]]–[[Ostrovica]]–[[Bribir]]–[[Kosovo-Polje-Knin|Kosovo Polje]] (near Knin), doc 19 [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]. Led a successful expedition (doc 24); also docs 15, 23, 32. Already attested in [[Desnica-1950-Kotarski-Uskoci-I]] as "governator Battista Sopino" (kompare at Stojan Janković's son's baptism 1674; at Janja Janković's wedding 1675).

## Notes
- Distinct from [[Giulio-Soppe-b????-Zadar|Giulio Soppe]] (different sub-region).
- The epithet "Fortezza" (fortress) distinguishes him from other Soppe bearers.
