# Jovan (Zuanne) Sinobad

"Kavalier sopraintendente," chief Venetian Morlach intermediary of the [[Knin]]–[[Zvonigrad]] area; principal investigator and witness across 1697–1715. Son of *quondam* [[Pietro-Sinobad-b????-Posedarje|Pietro/Pera Sinobad]]; killed in clash with Ottomans at [[Glamoc-Polje|Glamoč]] in **1715** (doc 372) [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]].

## Timeline (selected)
- **1701** — Witness in oath at [[Knin]] (son of *quondam Pietro*); pursued [[Radan-Matic-b????-Budin|Radan Matić]] in vendetta (doc 331). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **1702** — His slave Seifulah confessed in Travnik, ransom recorded; nephew (sister's son) and [[Vid-Petrovic-b????-Kosovo|Vid Petrović]] reported as raiders on Mt. Prolog (docs 343, 346). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
- **1715** — Killed at [[Glamoc-Polje|Glamoč]] in clash with Ottomans; [[Andrija-Butkovic-b????-Lika|Andrija Butković]] succeeded him as sopraintendente of Knin/Vrlika (doc 372). [[Desnica-1951-Kotarski-Uskoci-II]]
