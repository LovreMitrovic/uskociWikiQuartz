# Ilija Modrić (fl. 1756, Galovac)

## Summary
Recorded as *Illia Modrich*, head of a household of 3 souls in [[Galovac]] in the 1756 Grimani land-and-souls survey of the Zadar district [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]. Landholding: 6 campi 3 quarte, held *con laudemio*. [[Stanojevic-1756-Zadar-Census]]
